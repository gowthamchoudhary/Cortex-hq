Summary:
CedarPulse is evaluating Redwood for a rerank-heavy retrieval stack. Primary concerns: tail latency at high fanout, cost per query (esp. during peak), and ability to run in VPC/private for PHI-sensitive docs. They want a short pilot to compare hosted vs dedicated vs private with particular focus on fanout sizes and quantization effects on recall/latency.

Topics covered:
- Current vector store + neural reranker pipeline
- Fanout sizing (k candidates) and rerank cost model
- Dimensionality & quantization tradeoffs (float16 vs int8/4bit)
- Dedicated capacity and VPC/private deployment questions
- SLAs, security (SOC2, KMS), and next steps for pilot

Action items (quick in-meeting list):
- Redwood to share benchmark sheet and suggested test configs
- CedarPulse to share telemetry and sample documents
- Schedule pilot kickoff and align success criteria
