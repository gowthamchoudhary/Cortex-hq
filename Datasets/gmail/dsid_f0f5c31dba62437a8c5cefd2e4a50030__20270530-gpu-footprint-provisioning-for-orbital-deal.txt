I ran quick throughput math against our 70B LLM baseline (the model Orbital plans to serve in the sheet):

Assumptions we used in the sheet:
- Model: open-70b-rlhf-ish (dense), context avg 1.2k tokens, generation avg 200 tokens per reply
- Median request work: 220 tokens (Samir's number) => ~0.18 seconds of model compute on an H100 at batch=1 equivalent
- Observed token throughput per H100 (mixed Q4 pruning path) ≈ 55k tokens/sec sustained for inference mix (this is conservative; could be 70k in optimized kernel path)

Compute derived numbers:
- Effective tokens/sec need at median * peak multiplier: 220 tokens/request * (RPS) => we need RPS estimate.
- If peak minute multiplier is 6x and their current steady RPS is ~55 req/sec (Samir to confirm), peak RPS ≈ 330 req/sec => peak tokens/sec ≈ 72.6k tokens/sec.

GPU sizing: with conservative per-H100 throughput = 55k tps, peak 72.6k tps implies ~1.32 H100s => round up to 2 H100s for peak only. For steady-state (55 req/sec => ~12.1k tps) with 1.5x headroom steady => ~18k tps => ~0.33 H100s => 1 H100 to cover steady with redundancy.

Burst pool: to support 6x sustained for 10 minutes and allow for failover, recommend a shared burst pool equal to one additional H100 (or equivalent 2 A100s).

Recommendation (quick):
- Recommend 3 H100-equivalent GPUs committed (1 steady + 1 redundancy + 1 burst) calibrated in the contract as H100-eq units.
- Include autoscale ability to bring additional capacity (up to 1.5x) for short-term peaks with billing on usage.
