Hi Sara — great to hear the demo landed well. High-level answers below; I attached a short pricing draft and a proposed pilot scope spreadsheet.

1) Ephemeral retention: we can offer a 7-day full-trace retention profile where we purge raw traces after 7 days but keep aggregated metrics (summaries, counters) for 90 days. This reduces storage and PSI footprint. For pilot sizing you should expect ~15% lower storage cost vs our standard 30-day default.

2) Live trace dashboards: priced as an addon with two components — streaming UI compute (per concurrent view) and trace ingest indexing (per million spans). For a 200k requests/month pilot, the incremental run-rate is modest (we estimate $1,200–$1,800/month) depending on sampling rate.

Pilot credits: we typically offer a pilot credit in exchange for a short case-study and a commitment to review results. For a 6-week pilot we can propose a one-time usage credit (see attached obs_pricing_draft_v3.pdf) and a reduced SLA for the pilot window.
