Subject: Triage notes (internal) — Chiron4b 5xx spike

Quick notes from the first 15m of triage:
- Correlation: spike aligns with a rolling kernel/optimization push we shipped to the Chiron4b pool at 07:45 PDT (optimizer v2.1.4).
- Observed symptoms: increased OOM/kcache churn on multiple GPUs, KV-cache eviction thrash under long-context requests; tokenization queue growth leading to timeouts at ~30s.
- Hypothesis: optimizer change increased memory footprint for large attention heads, exposing fragmentation during high-concurrency spikes.

Suggested immediate actions (we recommended these on the bridge):
1) Roll back optimizer v2.1.4 (hotfix) to restore previous memory profile.
2) Temporarily reduce max concurrency per instance and enable prefix caching for BrightCart sessions.
3) Backfill requests onto Chiron3b-compat for non-deterministic endpoints.

I'll start coordinated rollback with infra and confirm the build hash. If rollback not possible within 20m, we fallback routing only.
