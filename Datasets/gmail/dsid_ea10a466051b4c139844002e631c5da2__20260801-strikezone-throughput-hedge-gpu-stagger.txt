Team —

Reviewed Alex's ask re: trimming to 7 H100s in exchange for a larger fallback window. Quick math from the spreadsheet:

- Baseline single H100 effective throughput (with our batching & KV hit rates ~38%): ~1.25k tokens/sec.
- 7 H100s -> nominal capacity ~8.75k t/s. With 30% headroom target we need ~12.5k t/s total to cover peak bursts (12k). So 7 H100s alone is short by ~3.75k t/s.
- Distill model acceptance at 25% of traffic reduces peak required by approx 0.25 * 12k = 3k t/s (because distill runs faster, ~2.1k t/s per H100 equivalently), but that still leaves ~0.75k t/s deficit.

Options:
A) Keep 8H100 reserved + 2 A100 burst. Contract fits target SLO without increasing fallback above 15%.
B) Commit 7 H100s reserved + explicit monthly burst budget that guarantees +1 H100 for scheduled peak windows (e.g., campaign hours) — reduces committed cost but adds burst spend variability.
C) Agree 7H100 + permit 30% distill fallback cap during pre-announced peak windows. Lower SLO on those minutes but under budget.

Operationally I also recommend setting a strict cooldown policy: after routing >20% to distill for >8 minutes, trigger a manual ops alert and spin additional burst capacity. This avoids long-tail regressions.

I put numbers in the attachment (tab: commitment-scenarios). If we want to hit $85k/mo cap, B is the most likely path (keeps average spend in-band but leaves occasional overage risk we can mitigate with pre-purchased burst credits).
