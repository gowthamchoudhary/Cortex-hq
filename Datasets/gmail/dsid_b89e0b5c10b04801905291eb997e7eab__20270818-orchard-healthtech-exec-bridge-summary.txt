Team — initial triage notes from the bridge: 
- Symptom: high token-generation error rates (internal 5xx), stack traces point to an internal stack overflow when KV-cache exceeds expected size on a hot-shard sequence. This correlates with a sequence-length distribution spike from Orchard (long prompts appended via custom metadata).
- Scope: impacting a small subset of model shards in us-west-2 serving LLM-v2-medium quantized 8-bit. Error surfaces at peak concurrency and long-context requests.

Immediate mitigations applied (09:30–09:33):
1) Automated prefix-caching disabled for Orchard routes to avoid KV inconsistencies.
2) Temporary routing rule: route Orchard traffic to model-variant LLM-v2-medium-slim (same quality with truncated context) to reduce KV pressure.
3) Throttled per-customer concurrency from 20 -> 8 for affected routes.

Early impact: errors dropped from 4% -> 0.8% within ~6 minutes after routing change; median latency trending down toward normal. We'll monitor 30m.

Next steps (30–180m):
- Run a targeted replay of Orchard's failing traces in sandbox to confirm build/codec issue.
- Prepare a model patch to improve KV handling for long contexts (ETA 24–36h).
- Ops to collect detailed perf counters and a focused runcard.
