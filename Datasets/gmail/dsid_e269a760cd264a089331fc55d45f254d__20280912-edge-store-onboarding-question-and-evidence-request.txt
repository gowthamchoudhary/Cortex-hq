Hi Rafael — thanks for the quick reply and good questions. Answering below inline: 

1) Retention & caching: EdgeStore's default transient cache is 90 seconds for full encrypted payloads; certain routing metadata is kept up to 14 days for debugging and metrics. No payloads are written to persistent disk by default; caches live in memory with disk fallback encrypted at-rest (AES-256) only in failover situations. We will provide a short config that forces memory-only caching for EU customers.

2) Access controls & keys: EdgeStore operates as a forwarding proxy and will not have access to plaintext for Redwood customer payloads. Valencia will maintain the encryption envelope; keys are stored in Valencia's KMS. EdgeStore may have access to TLS termination certificates for inbound connections but cannot decrypt application-layer payloads.

3) Processing scope: EdgeStore performs header normalization, geo-based routing, and optional token redaction for telemetry. No content transformations that alter customer data are planned.

4) Data residency: Logs are written to EdgeStore-managed log clusters in the POPs listed; backups are only in-region unless a support-level escalation requires cross-region access, which Valencia will approve in advance.

5) Evidence / docs: attached are the SOC 2 redacted PDF (control references called out on pages 12-16 for Access and Encryption) and a pen-test executive summary (scope: external network, edge services; no customer data exfiltration found). We have also uploaded the formal subprocessor add form to DocuSign: https://docusign.com/valencia-edgestore-subprocessor-addition/ABC123
