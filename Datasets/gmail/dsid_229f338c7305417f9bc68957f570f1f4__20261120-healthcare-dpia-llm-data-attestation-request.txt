Hi Amy —

Thanks for the note and the clear checklist. Short answers up front:

- Training: Redwood does not use customer prompt/response data from paid Hosted or Dedicated customer workloads to improve publicly-shared model weights by default. Customers can opt in explicitly for research/aggregation, but that is a separate agreement.
- Retention: We retain prompts/responses and associated metadata according to the customer-configured retention policy; default retention for Hosted accounts is 30 days for request/response bodies and 90 days for logs/metadata, unless the customer configures longer retention or enables audit-log export.
- PHI/HIPAA: We offer HIPAA controls for customers who execute a BAA + Private deployment or Dedicated with encryption + logging controls. For Hosted customers with PHI we recommend Private/Dedicated.

Hi — following up with the detailed responses below (summarized then expanded). Stephanie and Jonas helped craft language for your assessor; Jonas has suggested a short clause we can append to the Master Services Agreement.

Summary answers:
- Aggregation: No aggregation or use for public model training occurs unless a customer explicitly opts in via a signed data-sharing addendum. De-identified data is treated the same — it is not used for model improvement without opt-in.
- Retention table: Default Hosted retention: request/response bodies = 30 days; embeddings/cache = 7 days; logs/metrics = 90 days. We attached an export CSV to the shared Drive link (see related links).
- Telemetry: For BAA/Dedicated/Private we do not send customer telemetry to third-party analytics partners. For standard Hosted accounts, anonymized telemetry metrics (non-PII) may be sampled and sent to our analytics provider, Snowflake/Looker for aggregated dashboards; this is configurable and disabled by default for accounts with HIPAA designation.

Quoted detailed text for record: 

-----Original answer (for DPIA template)-----
1) Training and opt-out
Redwood Inference operational policy: Customer-provided prompt and response data from paid Hosted, Dedicated, or Private customer accounts are not incorporated into Redwood’s public model training datasets or model weights by default. Customers who wish to allow their operational data to contribute to model research must sign an explicit data-sharing addendum; this addendum details scope, deletion rights, and compensation if applicable. The opt-in flow is contractually managed and requires observable consent from the customer admin.

2) Data retention (default values)
- Request/response bodies: 30 days (default).
- Embeddings and intermediate cache artifacts: 7 days.
- Tokenized logs / trace metadata: 90 days.
- Long-term audit exports / S3 buckets: customer-configurable (manual export or automated retention policy).

3) PHI & HIPAA controls
- Redwood offers HIPAA support under a signed BAA for Dedicated and Private deployments; Hosted customers handling PHI should migrate to Dedicated/Private or sign a BAA and enable our HIPAA-safe configuration (end-to-end encryption at rest, customer-managed keys, audit log retention).
- We do not use third-party analytics partners to process identifiable PHI for BAA customers.
