Naomi — thanks for the summary and the redline. Quick legal read:

- Liability: $5M aggregate for direct damages is acceptable as a commercial concession, but we should add explicit language excluding punitive, exemplary, and incidental damages. Add a clause limiting recovery to fees paid in the prior 12 months if they object to the $5M figure.
- Tail: 12-month tail for data-breach-related claims is reasonable; do not extend more broadly.
- Indemnity: we must preserve the IP/third-party claim carveouts. Freyr’s ask to remove the carveout is a material change — push back. Instead, offer a narrow covenant that we will cooperate on defense for downstream claims but not assume liability for customers’ downstream products.
- Warranty: converting to a 12-month SLA-based warranty is okay if remedies are limited to service credits and the SLA metrics are clearly scoped in the MAP. Do not agree to financial remedies beyond credits.
