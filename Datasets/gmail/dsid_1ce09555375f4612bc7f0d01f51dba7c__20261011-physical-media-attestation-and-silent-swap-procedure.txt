Offline silent-swap minimal steps (operator-run, no network):
1) Insert standby SSD (B) into slot as labeled.
2) Run: sudo dd if=/dev/sdX of=/dev/sdY bs=4M conv=sync,noerror status=progress (verify exact device nodes in checklist).
3) Run provided sha256sum files against manifests shipped on the crate: sha256sum -c shipped_manifest.sha256
4) If hashes match, touch /var/run/acceptance.complete; bring services up in maintenance mode via local init script: sudo /usr/local/bin/start_redwood_offline.sh --maintenance
5) If any mismatch, follow rollback: re-seat SSD A and contact Redwood support hotline on emergency card (phone on paper receipt).
