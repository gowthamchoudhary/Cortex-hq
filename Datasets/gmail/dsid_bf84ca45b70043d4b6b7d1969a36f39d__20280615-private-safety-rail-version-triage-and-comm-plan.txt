Sara — thanks for the heads up. I've sketched a conservative triage + comms plan below and attached a version-gating matrix for the Acme fleet. High-level:

- Pinning model: all customer production clusters start pinned to the current GA bundle. We create a canary variant (canary bundle) and only target it to 1-2 non-critical hosts per cluster in a single AZ.
- Canary observation: 24h metrics + automated health checks (latency/tail-latency, error-rate, kv-cache hit rate).
- Rollback criteria: if 3+ SLO breaches within the window OR any data-path errors (KV corruption, persistent OOM), trigger an immediate rollback to the pinned bundle.
- Progressive rollout: after 24h stable canary, expand to 20% of capacity, watch 48h, then full at 48–72h with a staggered 10% step schedule.
- Emergency escape: immediate pin-shelve (freeze new scheduling) + manual drain of canary hosts if rollback triggers.

Attachments: version-gating-matrix.xlsx (matrix mapping components to pin/fallback behavior)
Links: runbook draft at https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/0A1B2C3D/Private-Upgrade-Runbook
