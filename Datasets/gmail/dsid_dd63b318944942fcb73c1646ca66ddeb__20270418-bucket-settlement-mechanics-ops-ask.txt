Alicia — thanks for the clear questions and the export. High level answers first, then I can send a short one-pager (and a worked example against your CSV) by EOD: 

1) Overages: billed at your committed tier's overage rate, not the full on-demand sticker. In practice we apply a per-token overage multiplier tied to the tier band you land in after the quarter closes. That avoids surprise full-rate billing.

2) Underuse / settlement: we run a quarterly settlement (we call it a bucket settlement) at quarter close. If you underuse, there are two options we support (set in SOW):
   - Credit: generate a credit equal to the unused committed tokens valued at your committed per-token rate (applies to future Redwood invoices).
   - True-up payout: for enterprise SOWs, optionally process a refund for the monetary value of unused tokens (requires finance coordination and may take an extra billing cycle).

3) Make-goods: we can do a one-time make-good if an under-provisioning error occurred on Redwood's side (e.g., a misapplied discount). Those are handled case-by-case and recorded as manual credits.

Thanks, Diego — adding two operational clarifications from the pricing side: 

- Measurement window: we calculate token usage against the commit on a per-quarter basis aligned to calendar quarters unless the SOW specifies a customer fiscal quarter. That determines the settlement period.
- Rounding / attribution: token counts are measured at the request level and rounded to the nearest whole token for billing; we do not prorate partial tokens. Overflow that pushes you into the next tier will trigger the overage band for the quarter, not retroactive re-pricing across prior months.
