Hi Priya — Laura here, Redwood Privacy Counsel. I started populating the questionnaire and attached a few artifacts for your review (subprocessors list, redacted SOC2, and a DPA summary). High-level notes below: 

1) PII classification: Redwood treats customer-supplied content as Customer Data. For the pilot, typical PII elements we see are names, email addresses, and healthcare identifiers when provided by the user. Persistence depends on deployment:
   - Hosted: request metadata and model outputs may be persisted for up to 30 days by default for monitoring and billing; configurable per-customer retention.
   - Dedicated & Private: retention and persistence are controlled by the customer (you may opt out of retention).

2) Data flow & KV cache: the runtime uses ephemeral KV caches to accelerate generation; in Hosted/Dedicated these caches live in-memory (GPU RAM) and in ephemeral SSD on the instance. For Private, caches remain within customer VPC/on-prem and can be configured to avoid any disk persistence.

3) Encryption & keys: in-host envelope encryption is used; at-rest encryption uses cloud provider disk encryption, and you may bring your own KMS keys (BYOK) for Dedicated/Private. We support HSM-backed KMS integration for enterprise customers.

4) Access controls & audit: role-based access, SSO (SAML/OIDC), just-in-time elevated access for support with approval workflows, and request-level audit logs are available. We can provide a redacted sample audit log.

5) Subprocessors & transfers: attached CSV lists subprocessors and primary locations. For cross-border transfers, we operate under SCCs for EU -> US transfers and support in-region hosting.

6) Certifications: SOC2 Type II report available (redacted), ISO 27001 in progress — compliance references attached.

Hi all — Rishi on infra/security. Quick technical notes (attached: kv-cache-crypto-note.txt; redwood_log_sample_redacted.txt):

- Persistent writes: by default KV cache is in-memory (GPU RAM) and ephemeral SSD. We do not write KV cache to durable object storage or backups in our hosted offering. Ephemeral disks are encrypted and destroyed on instance termination. For Dedicated/Private customers who opt into long-term caching, we surface config controls but do not enable durable caching without explicit customer consent.

- Crypto & key management: keys are envelope-wrapped. For hosted customers we manage KMS; for Dedicated/Private you can BYOK and we support HSM-backed keys. Key rotation is configurable; default rotation cadence is 90 days for wrapping keys and we support automated rotation via KMS.

- Access boundaries: support engineers have role-based, audited just-in-time access; automated services use service accounts with narrow scopes. The updated diagram can label those boundaries — we’ll produce a version that highlights support-access scopes before the call.
