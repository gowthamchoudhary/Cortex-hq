Hi Sarah — thanks for the docs and the clear summary. Short answers up front: we need to confirm a couple of things before we can confidently apply reverse-charge (customer self-assess) for your account. In most cases for SaaS consumed in Australia by an Australian-registered business, reverse-charge is appropriate — but we need evidence to support our tax treatment and to avoid future restatements.

Could you please confirm/provide the following so our billing team and tax ops can proceed?
- Confirmation that Kaizen Health Pty Ltd is the billed legal entity (ABN 52 123 456 789) and that you are claiming GST liability (i.e., not a non-resident for GST).
- A signed place-of-supply statement: one short line confirming that the primary users/consumption of the service is in Australia (we’ll attach a template if helpful).
- A contact at your tax advisor if you want them to co-certify (optional).

- Redwood’s Australian registration summary (redwood_tax_registration_summary.pdf) and our AU ABN/GST number.
