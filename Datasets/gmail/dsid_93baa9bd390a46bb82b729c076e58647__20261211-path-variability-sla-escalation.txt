Quick findings from initial triage (15:45–16:55 UTC):
- VPC peering metrics: packet retransmits increased by ~12% for Cartica's peer connection starting ~15:38 UTC. No peering route flaps recorded, but sustained retransmits indicate congestion or MTU anomalies.
- DNS: Our edge resolvers observed intermittent NXDOMAIN -> SERVFAIL flapping for the customer's internal resolver chain; median DNS resolution 20ms -> 320ms at p95 in same window.
- Proxy/firewall: HAProxy front-tier logs show elevated 502 responses where backend never accepted the connection; some proxies returned 504 due to read timeouts. The proxy cluster also shows a recent config reload at 15:35 UTC on a subset of nodes (automated rolling reload).

Interim hypothesis: the automated proxy reload combined with a transient DNS resolver instability pushed more traffic over a congested peering path, causing retransmits and backend timeouts => elevated 5xx and tail latency.

Recommended immediate actions (within 30m):
1) Shift Cartica traffic to alternate peering (peer-b) for us-east-1 (temp route) — avoids congested path.
2) Increase DNS cache TTL at edge for Cartica hostnames to reduce resolver load (and mitigate flapping).
3) Roll back automated proxy reload to stable config on affected front-tier nodes; restart only after traffic stabilizes.
