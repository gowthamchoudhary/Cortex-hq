Technical notes from Security (things we need to align on):

1) Retroactive capture feasibility
- If events are removed from hot storage after 48 hours with no extra buffer, there is no way for us to reconstruct them unless we keep a short grace buffer (e.g., 24–72h) or store a compact index (hash-only) that lets us verify later. Retroactive ingestion would require either: (a) an ingestion-side hold that prevents deletion for a grace window, or (b) a continuous stream-copy to cold storage gated by a legal-hold flag (but that has cost implications).

2) KMS and proof-of-wrap
- For hosted tenants we can capture wrap metadata and include attestations, but true customer KMS control (HSM-backed keys) is only available on Dedicated/Private deployments today. We can prototype an envelope-wrapping handshake for hosted, but it will need product sign-off and a short pilot.

3) Archive immutability
- We should provide append-only archives with signed manifests and store a digest in our immutable ledger (SHA256) and expose the ledger entry in the archive as extra proof.

4) Operational hooks
- Need a runbook for: trigger archive -> evaluation -> create bundle -> sign & wrap -> store -> notify legal & auditor. Who approves trigger? Sunrise wants legal to do it, but product/ops need a fail-safe.

I recommend we offer two options to Sunrise:
A) Recommended: Dedicated deployment with customer-managed KMS and a 24h grace buffer (fast to guarantee capture).
B) Alternative: Hosted pilot with an agreed policy: 72h grace buffer + signed-manifest prototype and explicit contractual terms on evidence scope.

Happy to walk through this on the call.
