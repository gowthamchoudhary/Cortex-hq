Lucy — thanks, happy to outline. I attached a compact matrix (commit_matrix_v3.xlsx) and a short TDS (tds-token-pricing.pdf). High level here so procurement can share a single page: 

Overview (plain language)
- Starter: 5M tokens/year — designed for evaluation/light production. Effective price range: $0.0016–$0.0010 per token depending on optional smoothing.
- Growth: 50M tokens/year — typical mid-market production usage. Effective price range: $0.00095–$0.00065 per token.
- Enterprise: 300M+ tokens/year — large scale with dedicated capacity options. Effective price range: $0.00055–$0.00030 per token.

Key mechanics
- Overage: billed monthly at 125% of the committed per-token rate for the nearest tier unless you convert to a higher tier mid-term.
- Smoothing: we offer monthly smoothing (even spend) at contract stage — spreads the annual commit across 12 invoices.
- Carryover: unused committed tokens carry over up to one billing period (30 days) by default; Enterprise can request extended carryover (up to 90 days) in SOW.
- Ramp credits: For growth ramps we offer front-loaded credits to offset initial higher per-token cost (example in matrix).

Example ramp (6-month) — Growth tier baseline (50M annual commit)
- Month 1–2: 2.5M tokens/mo (pilot) — effective price $0.0012/token, covered by 3-month pilot credit of $6k.
- Month 3–4: 6M tokens/mo — convert to smoothing schedule; effective blended $0.0009/token.
- Month 5–6: 10M tokens/mo — steady-state. If you elect carryover or increase tier, we reprice with new breakpoint.
