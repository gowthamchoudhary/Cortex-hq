Subject: RE: Paxion Health — investigation update (hypothesis + mitigation)

Internal — latest from bridge (summary):

Findings so far:
- Root symptom: GPU memory pressure causing rank-local OOMs during high-concurrency batching, leading to graceful-failures surfacing as 503s at the gateway.
- Trigger: a batching config tweak rolled out at 02:50 UTC increased max_batch_size on LLM v4.2 for latency optimization. That change interacts poorly with certain payload shapes from Paxion and increased memory transient peaks.
- Autoscaler behavior: pod churn increased as nodes scaled down then back up, creating cold-start latency and cache misses.

Actions taken / planned:
- Rolling back the 02:50 batching config (in progress).
- Temporarily lowering max_concurrency to reduce peak memory.
- Forcing a subset of traffic to a smaller, stable model variant while we validate rollback.

Impact assessment (preliminary):
- Estimated affected Paxion requests: ~18% of traffic during window.
- Customer-visible errors: 503s and delayed responses.

Attachments: sample request ids, short timeline (incident_timeline.csv). Expect another internal note in ~35 minutes or sooner if rollback completes.

Sara / Thomas / Paxion team,

Summary (resolved at 11:40 UTC): we mitigated the issue that produced increased 503s by reverting the batching config rolled out at ~02:50 UTC and applying a conservative concurrency cap while we validate stability. Service is now within normal error/latency bounds.

Root cause (confirmed): an aggressive batching optimization interacted with specific request shapes from Paxion, which caused transient GPU memory pressure and pod churn. The autoscaler exacerbated the effect by triggering additional cold starts during the churn window.

Customer impact:
- Window: ~03:05–11:40 UTC (intermittent 503s and degraded throughput).
- Estimated affected Paxion traffic: ~15–20% during peak minutes.
- Business effect: increased retries on several critical endpoints; no data loss reported.

Actions we have taken:
1) Reverted the 02:50 batching configuration and rolled out a safe default across affected clusters.
2) Applied a temporary concurrency cap and pinned a small percentage of Paxion traffic to a stable model variant.
3) Increased telemetry (heap tracking and per-pod memory histograms) and added an alert for GPU memory transient spikes.
