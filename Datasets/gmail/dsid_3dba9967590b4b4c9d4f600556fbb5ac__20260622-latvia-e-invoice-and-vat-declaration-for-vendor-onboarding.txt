Hi Ilze — thanks for sending the docs and for the clear checklist. We're excited to get RigaPrints live. Below are the steps and what we need from you to complete vendor setup and enable PO/payments: 

1) E-invoice support and operator: Redwood Finance prefers PEPPOL UBL where available. Our current AP operator for EU suppliers is "Redwood-AP (operator code: RDW-PEPPL)". Please send PEPPOL invoices to that operator and include your supplierId as documented in your sample UBL.

2) Invoice fields & PO reference: Please include the Redwood PO EXACTLY as "PO 789123" (we'll issue this first) in both the Invoice/OrderReference and the InvoiceLine/BuyerReference fields. Missing PO values are the most common cause of routing delays.

3) VAT handling: We can accept invoices gross or net depending on the line items. For software/subscription services, we typically apply the reverse charge when the supplier is outside the EU VAT obligation for the buyer; however, because RigaPrints is VAT-registered in Latvia (LV12345678901) and has provided a fiscal rep letter, our tax team will treat you as a standard VAT supplier. Please include your VAT number on every invoice and flag when VAT is charged.

4) Fiscal representative & CORRESPONDENCE: We need a DocuSign-signed fiscal representative letter on your letterhead (the signed-fiscal-rep-letter.pdf you attached is fine) and a confirmation of the fiscal rep's Latvian tax agent registration. If possible, upload the registered agent number or certificate.

5) Bank details & remittance: Please include IBAN + SWIFT and the beneficiary name matching the VAT certificate. For EU domestic payments we prefer SEPA (IBAN). For cross-border, include intermediary bank details if relevant.

6) Sample files: Your sample-invoice-peppol.xml looks good but a small note — please add the paymentMeans element with payment due date to reflect our Net30 terms.
