Below is a short summary and a couple of asks so we can move this into the SOC/security review.

Summary (defaults for Redwood Hosted API):
- Transit: all requests are TLS-encrypted in flight.
- Short-term logging: request/response payloads are stored in transient logs for 24 hours for debugging and billing correlation.
- Long-term telemetry: token-level billing and aggregate latency metrics are stored indefinitely as aggregated metrics (no raw text).
- Prompt/output storage: by default, we do not persist full prompt/output payloads beyond 24 hours unless a customer enables persistent logging.
- KV cache & prefix caching: cached content is encrypted at rest; caches are ephemeral and cleared per session by default.

Configurable options we mentioned that NovuGen might enable:
- Persistent audit logs (customer can opt-in to retain full payloads for X days).
- Data residency pinning: store logs and persistent artifacts in a chosen region (US/EU/APAC).
- Redaction rules: automatic regex-based redaction for fields identified as PII before storage.
- HSM/KMS integration for encryption management.
