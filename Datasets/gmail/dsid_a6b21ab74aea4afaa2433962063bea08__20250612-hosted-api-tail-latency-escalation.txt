Quick update from infra: initial traces show a classic amplification pattern. Observations: 
- Our global rate limiter transitioned to hard 429s for several customer tokens when the token-bucket depth was exhausted.
- A subset of SaaSCo client processes appear to be retrying immediately (no jitter, low backoff) so we see lots of retry requests arriving within seconds — that created a retry storm.
- Retry storm increased queueing on front-line workers, amplifying tail latency (p95/p99) and causing timeouts.
- There were a couple of autoscaling events, but the spike was faster than the scale-up window; some requests got routed to fallback slower workers which worsened p99.

Immediate mitigation we suggest (we're running these now):
1) Apply customer-specific soft-throttle: change SaaSCo from hard 429s to a 2xx micro-degrade for non-critical routes and add Retry-After header for others.
2) Apply short-term quota relaxation (10-20%) for SaaSCo token to reduce hard 429s while we analyze.
3) Blacklist a handful of noisy IPs/instances identified in logs for 30m while we evaluate.
4) Turn on per-token smoothing (leaky-bucket smoothing) to spread bursts across a 10s window.

Longer term: circuit breakers at the gateway, faster warm-up for autoscaling, and a client library push to encourage exponential backoff + jitter.

I'll attach the incident trace + logs to the ticket. ETA for the soft-throttle change is ~12 minutes.
