Timeline (short)
- 2027-05-03 06:07 UTC — autoscale event triggered for shard-12.
- 2027-05-03 06:08–06:46 UTC — elevated queueing and token latencies observed for requests routed to shard-12.
- 2027-05-03 06:48 UTC — rollback of the autoscale placement and re-routing completed; median latency recovered.

Immediate actions taken (already done)
- Re-routed Finova traffic off shard-12 and pinned routing to the healthy pool for your account (complete).
- Rolled back the autoscale change that placed workers on the affected host (complete).
- Temporarily disabled background maintenance jobs during peak in that AZ (complete).
