Short technical summary and recommended actions (ETA estimates in parentheses):

Root cause hypothesis:
- The kernel v2.3 introduced a slightly different KV eviction ordering under heavy multi-tenant prefix churn. For NovaCorp's traffic pattern this produces a higher cold-start miss rate and extra host-level serialization at the cache tier.

Immediate mitigation (can do within ~20-35 minutes):
1) Soft redirect NovaCorp to model-10b (variant pinned) — safe and low-risk. (20m)
2) Enable fallback routing: requests that exceed 400ms will be automatically re-routed to model-10b at the edge for the next 2 hours. (25m to enable feature flag)
3) Re-provision a small pool of instances with kernel v2.2 (previous kernel) and warm KV cache for a controlled roll-forward test. If metrics normalize we can switch traffic back. (60-90m to provision warm pool)

Operational notes:
- The runbook for controlled failover is attached (runbook_model10c_failover.md). It contains the exact CLI commands to pin routing and the signal thresholds for rollback/rollforward.
- We recommend keeping a 4-hour SLA carveout window while we validate; commercial to confirm credit terms.

Authorization request:
- I need a go-ahead from Stephanie/Laura to execute 1+2 now, and from leadership if we extend the carveout beyond 4 hours.

I'll stand by to run the commands as soon as you confirm.

— Marcus
