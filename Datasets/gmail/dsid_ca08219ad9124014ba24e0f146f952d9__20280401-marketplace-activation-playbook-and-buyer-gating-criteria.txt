Erin — thanks for the clear questions. TL;DR: AWS Marketplace private offers are flexible but we recommend a 1-year PO with a 6-month pilot milestone (pro-rata billing) to keep activation simple. Details + MAP below.

Options on term/pricing (high level):
- Genuine 6-month private offer: possible but adds additional activation steps on AWS side and sometimes requires a marketplace registration exception. Adds ~5 business days to activation.
- 1-year PO with 6-month pilot milestone (recommended): we set the contract term to 12 months, invoice prorated for the first 6 months at the pilot rate, then convert to full-year billing on month 7 if both sides sign off. Cleaner for buyer approval and AWS onboarding.
- Usage-first pilot: usage-based pricing with a capped commit for 6 months. Lower upfront, but buyer must accept usage reconciliation terms.

Suggested pricing bands for a 6-month pilot (examples to kick off procurement discussion):
- Usage (tokens / runtime): $0.006 / 1k tokens equivalent for the pilot usage band (capped at $30k).
- Committed pilot: $24k for 6 months (equivalent annualized ~ $48k) with a 20% discount if customer converts to a 12-month commit within 60 days.

Buyer approvals and docs we typically need to complete activation:
- Signed PO (buyer business unit signature + billing org) — required for AWS Private Offer activation.
- AWS account ID for the buyer (to attach the private offer).
- SOC 2 Type II report (we provide a redacted copy) + DPA signed.
- Standard ATO / data residency notes (if you need specific region isolation, tell us now).

4) Test activation and SSO + service account exchange (Day 5–7)

Adding a marketplace activation operational note — internal checklist for attaching a private offer on AWS Marketplace:

1) Confirm buyer AWS Account ID and target marketplace region (us-east-1 preferred).
2) Receive signed PO (scanned PDF) and verify billing entity name matches the AWS account.
3) Upload PO to the AWS private-offer attachment field and submit request.
4) AWS will validate buyer eligibility (2 business days typical) — include contract reference and MAP in the submission.
5) Once AWS attaches the offer, we exchange service account keys and enable tenant access.

Edge cases & gating that cause delays: mismatched billing entity, missing SOC2, non-standard procurement terms (e.g., net-90), or legal redlines on the DPA. For net-90 asks we need RevOps + Finance sign-off and manual billing cadence.
