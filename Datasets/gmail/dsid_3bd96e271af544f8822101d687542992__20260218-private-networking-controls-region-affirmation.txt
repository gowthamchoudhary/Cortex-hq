Dylan — quick confirmations below, attaching a short checklist (vpc-control-checklist.txt) with implementation notes.

1) Audit logs / S3 exports: default behavior is to retain logs in the pinned region. For Private (VPC) deployments we write logs to a customer-managed S3 bucket in the same region if you configure Customer Export; Redwood-managed export endpoints are also provisioned in-region. Cross-region export only happens if a customer requests replication.

2) KMS / BYOK: Customer-managed keys are supported via BYOK — keys remain in-customer KMS and we never copy the CMK out of the region. For Redwood-managed keys we generate and store keys in the deployment region. For air-gapped on-prem deployments: we provide an offline key-configuration flow; we do not export keys outside the appliance.

3) Transient copies: maintenance tasks (e.g., snapshotting for upgrade) may create short-lived encrypted artifacts. Those artifacts are encrypted with the region-local keys, access-logged, and deleted after the maintenance window. We do not create uncontrolled cross-region copies without explicit contract-level configuration.

Edge caveats:
- If you enable third-party managed backups that are global by design, those vendors may store data in additional regions — we flag this in the contract and controls matrix.
- Failover to a different region is available as an opt-in DR policy; it's not enabled by default.
