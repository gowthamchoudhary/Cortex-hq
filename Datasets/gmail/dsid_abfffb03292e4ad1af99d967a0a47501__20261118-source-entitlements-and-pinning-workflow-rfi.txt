Thanks Sarah — we can do this. I attached our completed workbook (tabbed answers + evidence references), a short executive playbook that describes pinning and entitlements, and a CSV export of the entitlements matrix for programmatic checks.

High-level notes here:
- Provenance artifacts we produce per model version: model weight SHA256, build Docker image digest, signed SBOM for third-party libs, training-data provenance statement (summary + access policy), and optional reproducible seed/recipe where allowed by upstream licenses.
- We provide a signed provenance bundle (COSE/JSON-LD) that includes a machine-verifiable chain: commit -> build -> artifact hash -> KMS signature. Our private deployments can include HSM-signed snapshots on customer demand.
- License surfacing: our catalog exposes license IDs for each model and dependent component, plus a risk flag for copyleft/AGPL-like terms. We run continuous license-monitoring and emit alerts + recommended mitigations to the customer's console and via webhook.
- Pinning: customers can pin by model_version_id or by upstream commit hash. We support two pin types: immutable pin (no automatic updates) and managed pin (customer opts into security backports within semantic-stability windows).

See the workbook rows for specific artifacts and example manifest URIs. I flagged areas where we need permission to share parts of the training-data provenance due to third-party NDAs.
