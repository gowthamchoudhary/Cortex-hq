Maya — thanks, good clarifications. Below is the measurement language we typically propose for streaming SLOs (keeps it narrow and auditable):

PROPOSED STREAMING LATENCY MEASUREMENT (95th PCTL TOKEN APPEND):
- Measurement unit: per-token append duration measured at Redwood's edge service (time between token send event and client-visible byte emission).
- Window and aggregation: compute per-request 95th percentile of per-token append across the reporting window (daily), then report the 95th percentile across those per-request values for billing period.
- Exclusions: scheduled maintenance windows >15m (with 72-hour prior notice), customer-origin network outages (verified via traceroutes), third-party dependency outages (CDN or customer proxy), and force majeure events.
- Burst policy: sustained bursts >3 minutes will be counted against SLOs; short bursts (<=3 minutes) are excluded from SLO calculations but included in anecdotal reports.

I've attached a short CSV (measurement-examples.csv) that shows a synthetic sample and how the aggregation works (per-request per-token p95 -> billing p95). For remedies, we can draft either: (A) graduated service credit tiers based on monthly p95 exceedance, or (B) defined monthly bill credits tying to impacted percentage of requests. My recommendation for BrightLeaf is (A) since you asked for deterministic credits.
