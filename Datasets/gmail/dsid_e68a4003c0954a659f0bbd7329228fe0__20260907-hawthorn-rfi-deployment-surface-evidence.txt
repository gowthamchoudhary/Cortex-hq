Thanks for the questions. Short answers here, full evidence linked in the packet at https://redwoodcloud.drive/hawthorn/HAW-2026-091/evidence/. We'll also provide a redacted SOC 2 report under NDA; happy to accept your standard NDA or send ours.

1) Ephemeral KV on hosted: We run KV/state in RAM and favor in-memory only for the hosted API. In rare scale-recovery scenarios (container restart, node OOM), we do not write KV blobs to durable customer-visible disk. If a crash dump is captured for debugging it is scrubbed and encrypted; permanent persistence only occurs for Dedicated or Private deployments where the customer explicitly opts in. Ephemeral filesystem usage is enforced on tmpfs for any transient artifacts and mounted with node-level encryption policy where supported. See sidecar_kv_persistence_note.txt attached.

2) Allowlisted connectors: Connector configuration is an admin-tier control. For hosted customers, account admins may enable approved connectors; self-service connectors are restricted via account-level feature toggles and an allowlist. We can map your approved connectors against our allowed set during onboarding.

3) SOC 2: summary and auditor contact are available. Link above.
