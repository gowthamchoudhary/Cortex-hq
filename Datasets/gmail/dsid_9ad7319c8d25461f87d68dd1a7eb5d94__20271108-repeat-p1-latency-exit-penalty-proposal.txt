Maria —

Appreciate the clear ask and for sharing the incident log. Short answer: we understand the need for stronger protections but an open six-month escrow plus unconditional termination without cure could materially impair our risk posture. Suggested counter (we think meets both sides):

1) Fee-at-risk: escrow equal to three months of committed spend (not six), held in escrow and drawn down only after an uncured set of objective events (see below).
2) Termination trigger: allow termination only after 3 uncured breaches in a trailing 90-day window where each breach remains uncured after a 30-day cure period. During cure Redwood will provide a remediation plan with milestone tracking.
3) Liquidated damages cap: express cap equal to 6 months of committed spend (including any escrow applied) — this keeps total exposure bounded.
4) Objective metrics: we will accept your P1 >2s for >5% of requests in a 24h window as a breach definition; we propose an independent telemetry snapshot (mutually agreed query) as the evidence.

If that sounds broadly acceptable, we can produce a redline. I’ve looped in Marissa and Aditya to confirm commercial and legal appetite. For now, we’ll attach a draft mutual action plan and a sample escrow term sheet.
