Hi Aisha —

Appreciate the quick response. Attached is our AP onboarding packet (Redwood_AP_Onboarding_Packet_v2.pdf) and a PO acceptance spec spreadsheet (PO_Acceptance_Specs.xlsx). Summary below to make the portal registration painless: 

1) Invoicing cadence: We support (a) per-PO invoice, (b) consolidated monthly invoice per Redwood legal entity, or (c) consolidated monthly per Helio legal entity if you send a single remittance token. Please confirm which you prefer — we typically default to consolidated monthly per supplier legal entity unless the customer requires per-PO matching for automated finance workflows.

2) PO matching rules (Coupa examples): For 95% auto-match we require either: 
   - PO Number + Invoice Amount + Supplier Redwood Tax ID (TIN or VAT) OR
   - PO Number + Remit Token (16-char alphanumeric) that we embed in the invoice UDF.

   Fields we include in invoice header: Invoice Number, Invoice Date (ISO), PO Number, Buyer Legal Entity, Seller Legal Entity (Redwood Inc.), Currency, Net Amount, Tax Amount (breakout by VAT/GST where applicable), Gross Total. Line-item-level tax codes only required if you do tax reclaim at line level.

3) Remit token: We can generate a Helio-specific remit token that your Coupa integration can store as a supplier custom field. Using the remit token avoids per-PO reconciliation noise and allows consolidated invoices to be allocated across multiple POs on our side for your AR/GL mapping.

4) VAT / bank details: The packet includes our US EIN, Redwood EU VAT ID (EU-REDACTED), and primary remit bank stub. If you need certified bank letter or W-9/W-8, tell us and I will loop in Finance.

Hi Aisha —

Quick clarifications from Legal/Finance side: 

- Buyer legal entity on PO: Please ensure the "Bill To" legal name matches the buyer legal entity value in the Coupa header; mismatches can prevent automated acceptance. Acceptable synonyms are listed in the attached spreadsheet under "BillToAltNames."
- Tax paperwork: For US payments we need a completed W-9 (attached in packet). For EU customers requiring supplier withholding proof, we can provide VAT certificates on request.
- Wire remittance: We can provide a remit instruction PDF if your finance team requires bank verification for large transfers.

Also FYI — if you choose consolidated invoices we will include a mapping CSV (PO->amount) as part of the invoice package so your AP can auto-apply lines back to individual POs. That mapping file is produced by our billing engine and matches the Coupa PO numbers you send during portal registration.

One-page checklist (below) — feel free to paste into your Coupa supplier onboarding notes: 
- Supplier legal name (exact)
- Redwood remit token (to be placed in supplier custom field)
- Accepted invoice types: consolidated-monthly OR per-PO
- Required header fields on invoice: Invoice Number, Invoice Date, PO Number, Buyer Legal Entity, Currency, Net, Tax Breakouts
- Attachments: PO PDF (if available) + mapping CSV when consolidated invoicing used
