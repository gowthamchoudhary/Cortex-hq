Lisa — thanks for the clear ask and for looping Emma. Great to see the requirements up front; that helps us tailor options. I’ve sketched three practical commercial pathways below (high level) and attached a short SOW draft you can share with procurement.

1) Hosted – Add-on billing (fastest to go-live)
- EU-only routing + residency flag: one-time residency activation fee + recurring per-region surcharge
- Audit logs: metered by GB/month; retention tiers (1y / 3y / 7y) priced per GB; archival/restore fees apply
- HSM: billed as "HSM seats" (managed key wrapping + attestation) or via a pass-through if you want direct vendor HSM; 1 seat included at enterprise commit levels
- Pros: fastest onboarding, minimal infra ownership
- Concessions: pilot discounts available; volume discounts for permanent commits

2) Reserved capacity (Dedicated) – committed throughput + credits for add-ons
- Commit to throughput (tokens or QPS) for a 12/24/36 month term
- Add-ons (audit log, HSM, residency) can be converted to credits and amortized against the commitment over an agreed period (we typically amortize onboarding over 24–36 months)
- Pros: predictable unit economics, lower per-token price, easier to bundle HSM costs into commit crediting

3) Private deployment (customer-managed network)
- One-time implementation fee (onboarding, attestation), monthly platform fee; lower recurring add-on rates
- Pros: full control for Healthly, suitable if strict procurement prefers on-prem/VPC

Ballpark numbers (order-of-magnitude; final pricing requires volume profile):
- Audit log retention (7y): ~ $0.02/GB/month (can be lower with commit and if archived to deep storage)
- HSM seat (managed): ~$30k / seat / year; on-prem HSM pass-through typically 1–3x capex amortized
- Residency activation: one-time $20k–$50k depending on footprint and regional certs

Suggested concession structure we can offer for pilot → scale:
- Pilot (3 months): reduced residency activation (50% off) and waived first-month platform fee
- If you convert to a 24-month commit within the pilot, we can amortize residency and implementation fees across the 24 months and provide a 10%–15% usage discount (tiered to volume).
