Team — initial findings from quick triage:
- The client traces show the HTTP/1.1 chunked body stops being sent from our edge at specific intervals (10:21:12.6, 10:27:03.9, 10:41:50.2 PST).
- At those times, we see an increased syscall timeout in the nginx worker logs followed by a short spike in connection queue depth on the gateway.
- The runtime logs for the associated model containers DO show token emission continuing for ~2–3s before the chunked frames stop propagating to the client. The KV cache and decoder logs show no OOM or model stall.

Hypothesis: backpressure/congestion between gateway and edge proxy resulting in dropped chunk flushes. That explains the client-side token counter but no chunks reaching the browser.

Mitigation attempted (10:35): temporarily increased gateway chunk flush interval and disabled a new batching heuristic rolled last week. Early tests show the client receives steady chunks on a repro harness.

Next: collect full tcpdump for the affected nodes and escalate to infra to check kernel-level socket drops.

-ben
Senior SRE, Redwood Inference
