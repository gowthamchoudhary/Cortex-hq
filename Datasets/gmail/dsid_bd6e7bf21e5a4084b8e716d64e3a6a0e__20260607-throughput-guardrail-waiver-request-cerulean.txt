Checked the spreadsheet and the token burst profile looks predictable (daily spike ~90–120m with consistent job sizes). Two operational paths we can take:

Option A — Scheduled soft carveout (lower risk)
- Reserve an isolated headroom slice in the existing dedicated pool for Cerulean during 02:00–04:00 PT. This is implemented as a time-bound soft-reservation: other tenants can use unused headroom but are preemptible only outside the 02:00–04:00 window.
- Behavior: no preemption of Cerulean jobs in the 02:00–04:00 window; outside that window, normal guardrails apply.
- Risk: moderate — requires scheduler bump and nightly capacity monitoring.

Option B — Deterministic time-slice (higher isolation)
- Create a deterministic time-slice on a small set of reserved nodes (N+1 nodes) dedicated to Cerulean at 02:00–04:00. This guarantees isolation but increases cost (you get dedicated nodes guaranteed).
- Behavior: full non-preemption and deterministic throughput during window.
- Risk: lower, but material cost impact to Cerulean or Redwood depending on commercial terms.

Recommendation: start with Option A for a 60-day pilot with telemetry gates: (1) average tail latency < 350 ms for priority requests during the window, (2) no more than 2% job retries due to scheduling, (3) observed headroom usage stable for 14 consecutive days before extending. If Option A proves insufficient, move to Option B.
