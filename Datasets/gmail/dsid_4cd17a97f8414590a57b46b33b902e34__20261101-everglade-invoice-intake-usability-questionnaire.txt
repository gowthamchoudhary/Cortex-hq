Hi Nina — thanks for sending these over and for the sample CSV. A few clarifications from our side so we can give a clean mapping to AP: 

- Portal vs email fallback: for the pilot we recommend the portal for production uploads (better parity, idempotency, and line-item parsing). However, we can accept invoices by email as a temporary fallback during the first 90 days. After that we expect vendors to upload via the portal for SLA and audit reasons.

- Minimum CSV fields we need for the pilot (required): invoice_number, invoice_date (YYYY-MM-DD), vendor_tax_id, PO_number, line_item_description, quantity, unit_price, line_total, invoice_total, currency, remit_address.

- Tax fields: vendor_tax_id at the vendor level is required; VAT/TIN per line is optional for now unless you operate across multiple tax jurisdictions.

- PO stamping: If you include PO_number in the CSV we can auto-match. If you only send PDFs via email, please put the PO in the subject and also in the top-right of the PDF to help our OCR.

- Filename convention suggestion (optional but helps routing): EVGL_INV_<invoice_number>_<YYYYMMDD>.ext (example: EVGL_INV_20260123_20261031.csv)

I'll loop in Jordan and Laura for the mapping preview and anything AP-specific. Can you confirm whether your system exports line-level tax columns and whether you have multi-PO invoices (more than one PO per invoice)? That affects whether we require a PO row per line or a PO column per line.

Thanks — I can send a short mapping example once you confirm.
