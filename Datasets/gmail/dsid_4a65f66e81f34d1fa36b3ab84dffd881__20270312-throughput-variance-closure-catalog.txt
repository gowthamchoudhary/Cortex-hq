Quick RCA (summary):
- Root cause: selector kernel mismatch on a subset of model variants after a recent kernel-selector change merged on 2027-03-10. The selector triggered a slower attention kernel for long-context batches when the runtime observed bursty sequence-length variance. This caused sustained CPU->GPU handoff contention and increased queuing.
- Contributing factors: high variance in incoming sequence-lengths from Trellis’s long-prompt feature, default batching window set to 12ms (too aggressive for variance), and the recent selector flip did not have a long-tail synthetic test case for sequence variance.

Proposed corrective actions and owners (first pass):

3) Prefix/KV cache tuning for Trellis long-prompt flows (config toggle) — owner: Kimberly Park — target: 2027-03-13 08:00 UTC
