Thanks for sending these over and for the quick turnaround. A couple of notes so we can move PO 8921 forward:

- Our standard procurement policy requires evidence that the tax ID for non-US vendors maps to the legal entity on the invoice. For US payees we typically accept EIN + 147C (IRS). For EU/UK vendors we accept an official government-issued tax ID confirmation (your Irish letter is a good start) but Finance needs something that directly ties the signatory authority to the entity.

- If you can provide either: (a) an official tax authority letter with company registration number + tax ID (PDF), or (b) a notarized signatory certificate that shows Julian Mercer is authorized to bind Nimbus Analytics, that will usually satisfy Finance. A scanned notarization is acceptable provided it includes the notary seal / jurisdiction and date.
