Rajesh — thanks, this is straightforward. Summary answers below; I attached a compact retention matrix and our SOC 2 summary for your compliance team.

1) Data locality / backups
- Live request/response data is persisted to object storage endpoints located in the region selected at account creation. For US-region accounts that is our US data plane in us-west-2 (primary).
- Automated backups and scheduled snapshots for tenant metadata and configuration are stored in the same region by default. Cross-region replication only occurs if the customer explicitly enables multi-region failover; otherwise snapshots remain region-bound.

2) Retention windows (default, non-enterprise customization shown)
- Request payloads (raw request/response text): 30 days by default; can be configured down to 0 days for enterprise customers.
- Generated outputs (responses): 30 days default.
- Transient model cache / KV cache: not persisted beyond active session; ephemeral.
- Debug logs (token-level traces for troubleshooting): retained 90 days by default; aggregated diagnostics for telemetry retained 180 days.
- Embeddings and indexes: not retained by Redwood unless explicitly stored by the customer; if customer enables server-side embeddings storage, retention follows the tenant config (default 365 days).

3) Erasure (customer-initiated) SLAs
- Request to confirmation on live systems: we acknowledge within 24 hours, and finish deletion from primary live stores within 14 calendar days in typical cases.
- Backups/snapshots: we mark objects for deletion and they are purged according to backup lifecycle; in normal operations full removal from immutable backup copies may complete within 90 days.
- Legal hold: deletions are deferred and documented while an active legal hold exists; we will provide notification of hold and cannot purge during that period.

4) Deletion proof / audit exports
- We provide an audit export that includes: tenant_id, deletion_request_id, timestamp (UTC), effected_keys/object-paths, operator (system vs. support agent), and final_status (deleted/held/partial). Audit exports are available for 365 days via the Console or API.

Rajesh — quick responses to your follow-ups and a sanitized sample export attached.

- EU region: confirmed. If you select an EU-hosted tenant during onboarding, primary storage, snapshots, and backups remain in the EU region. Cross-region replication will not be enabled without explicit customer opt-in and a change request.

- Audit export: we include a SHA-256 hash of each object key in the audit CSV so you can correlate without exposing payloads. The export fields in the sample: tenant_id, deletion_request_id, utc_timestamp, object_key_hash, operator, final_status. See the attached 'audit-sample-export.txt' for a masked example. Audit exports are signed by our key (we provide the public cert) on request; we can also provide a short signed attestation PDF after a completed purge for your records.

- Signed attestation: yes — for enterprise contracts we can provide a signed purge attestation (human-signed + machine-signed stamp) that includes the deletion_request_id, timestamp, and list of top-level buckets affected. Allow 3 business days for issuance after final_status is achieved.
