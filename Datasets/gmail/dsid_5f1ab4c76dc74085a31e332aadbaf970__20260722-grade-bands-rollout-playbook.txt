Thanks — reviewed the sheet and Mercer appendix. A few notes from the market/finance side: 

- Benchmark signals: for senior IC levels (IC5–IC7) the data Mercer shows for SF vs national gap is currently +18% median; Laura's proposed +12% anchor understates the market delta for senior hires. I suggest a split-anchor approach: +18% for senior IC roles in Bay Area, +12% for mid IC. This keeps offer competitiveness where the market is most tight and avoids across-the-board inflation.
- Headcount impact: modeled P&L using the new bands + headcount plan shows a ~3.4% run-rate increase in comp expense across 2027 if we adopt immediately. We should consider phasing across quarters (Q4 2026 adoption for new offers, Q1 2027 promotions window) to smooth budget impact.
- Operational ask: if we move to city-level sliding anchors we need recruiting offer automation updated (two fields: city_index, band_anchor). Recruiting should scope the engineering work and estimate time.

I attached a short checklist (rollout_checklist_v1.docx) with suggested gating and owner assignments. Happy to present this at the Wednesday working session.
