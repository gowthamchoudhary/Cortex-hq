Subject: Post-incident summary — us-west streaming latency surge (Apr 16)

Hi Arjun,

TL;DR: On 2027-04-16 at ~16:48 PDT a subset of edge connectors in us-west experienced a KV cache eviction storm during planned micro-rebalancing, which temporarily forced some requests to decode from cold path and increased token emission latency for ~14 minutes. We rebalanced the shard and applied targeted cache warming; customer-impact window was ~16:48–17:02 PDT.

What happened (short):
- Root cause: Edge shard rebalancing combined with a transient traffic microburst produced coordinated KV cache evictions on 3 host-tiles. Evictions caused higher per-token compute and I/O for affected streams.
- Impact: ~4% of FreshCart chat sessions observed token emission pauses (median extra latency 2.3s, 95th percentile 5.9s). No dropped or corrupted responses were observed.

Immediate mitigations we recommended & results:
- Client polling fallback (150ms) + reduce max_tokens_per_chunk to 48: restored session responsiveness within ~60–90s of application.
- Re-route to redwood-q-lat: marginally improved tail latency for sessions that could accept lower-precision weights.
- Server-side: replaced the three hot host-tiles, executed targeted cache-warm on affected shards, and added a conservative admission control to prevent simultaneous aggressive cache eviction during future rebalances.

Follow-up actions (what we’re doing):
1) Short-term (completed): admission-control tweak and targeted cache warm during rebalances.
2) Medium-term (2–4 weeks): implement per-tenant cache isolation heuristics to limit cross-tenant eviction blast radius.
3) Long-term (Q3 roadmap): enhance shard rebalancer to perform staggered evictions with probabilistic warming and add an automated client guidance page for graceful streaming fallbacks.
