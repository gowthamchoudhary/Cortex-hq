Logan —

Thanks for the detailed note. A few constraints from the infra side that will affect the math:

1) NICs & Oversubscription: Standard hosts in the target VPC provide dual 40Gb NICs. We strictly allow 2:1 oversubscription on NICs for tenant workloads. That reduces sustained per-host egress from 40Gbps burst to ~20Gbps sustainable if other tenants present.  
2) Cross-AZ egress: Our current inter-AZ backbone guarantees 200 Gbps but shared with other infra. Reserving 80 Gbps exclusively for your pool requires a formal request and SLA signoff — lead time 3–4 weeks.
3) Firewall/NAT: Outbound egress to public endpoints is SNAT'd through a pool of 2 NAT gateways per AZ. Burst scaling of NAT can introduce 30–50ms variability for flows that re-establish connections.
4) Procurement note: procurement requires final host count + reserved subnet CIDR before purchase order.

We can support a dedicated subnet and tag-based egress carveout, but that will add a one-time infra provisioning window (~5 business days) and a small charge for static IP reservation.

Adding Katie for procurement and to validate ordering constraints.
