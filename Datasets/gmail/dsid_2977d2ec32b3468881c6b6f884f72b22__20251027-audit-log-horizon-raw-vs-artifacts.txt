Hi Laura — thanks for the clear ask. Putting a concise baseline here and attaching the draft retention matrix for visibility.

Short answer (hosted API defaults):
- Raw requests (prompts, request payloads): retained for 45 days by default for billing, QA, and anomaly detection. After 45 days, records are purged unless a retention hold is applied.
- Model responses (generated outputs): retained for 45 days by default, same purge behaviour.
- Derivative artifacts (embeddings, rerank scores, vector indices used in private deployments): embeddings generated via the embeddings endpoint are persisted only if the customer explicitly stores them — by default embeddings derived during on-demand generation are discarded after processing unless the customer uses the dedicated embeddings store feature.

Exceptions we include: legal holds, active support traces (up to 90 days internally unless escalated), and telemetry that is aggregated/hashed and retained for 12 months for product analytics (no PII/cleartext prompts retained in analytics data).
