Hi Sam —

Thanks — great questions. Below is a concise thread-level summary that you can include with your legal packet. I also attached our No-Training Addendum (PDF) and a machine-readable retention matrix; we can produce a redacted version for public sharing.

Short answer: Redwood does not use customer content for model training or improvements unless a customer explicitly signs a training consent addendum. That commitment covers:
- Offline fine-tuning: not performed on customer content without explicit written consent.
- Online/continual updates: customer content is not used as training signal.
- Embeddings corpora / evaluation datasets: we do not incorporate raw customer content into open or internal model corpora.

Retention & operational categories (default hosted API profile):
- Request payloads (full request/response): retained for 30 days for debugging, caching and KV replay. Ephemeral by design; not used for training.
- KV cache / prefix caches: retained up to 7 days by default for latency optimization; can be disabled per-customer.
- Audit logs (routing, API key usage, auth events): retained for 365 days; logs contain metadata but not full customer prompts (only in exceptional debug snapshots — see below).
- Debug snapshots & crash dumps: retained up to 90 days; snapshots may contain request context to reproduce an issue. We exclude snapshots from training pipelines and they are access-controlled.
- Full backups / storage snapshots: retained up to 90 days for disaster recovery; backups are encrypted and can be excluded in Dedicated/Private deployments on request.
- Aggregated telemetry & metrics: retained as aggregated, non-identifiable metrics indefinitely for product telemetry and performance monitoring.

Options for stricter profiles and contractual guarantees:
- No-training + short-retention add-on: we offer a product add-on that enforces shorter retention windows (request payloads 7 days, KV disabled, snapshots disabled by default) and a written no-training warranty in the DPA.
- Dedicated / Private deployments: increased control — customers can opt out of managed snapshots/backups, provide their own KMS, and set custom retention windows in the deployment config.

Deletion timeline & scope:
- Live deletion request: once validated, we purge request payloads and caches within 7 business days in the hosted environment.
- Backups & snapshots: deletion from immutable backup chains can take up to the backup retention cycle (up to 90 days) unless an escrow or expedited deletion flow is negotiated.
- Audit trails: metadata and high-level audit records are retained for compliance (configurable by customer) but do not contain raw prompt/response content in normal operation.
