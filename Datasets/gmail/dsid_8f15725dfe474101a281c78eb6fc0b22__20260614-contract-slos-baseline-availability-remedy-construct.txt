Hannah —

Thanks for the feedback. Quick internal suggested compromise for your procurement team to consider:

- Baseline availability: 99.90% for the first 6 months from go-live, moving to 99.95% thereafter if utilization patterns match the provisioning assumptions. This gives both sides time to observe production behavior.
- Remediation: tiered credits as Soojin proposed but with a monthly cap at 35% of invoice initially. Credits for separate incidents in the same month are additive up to the cap (we propose additive, with a clear formula in the SLA doc).
- Latency: define "batched" as any request where the API payload contains more than 1 user-turn OR uses the batch endpoint explicitly. Proposed SLOs for production-hosted: p95 single-turn < 350ms; p95 batched < 700ms. For customers on Dedicated capacity we can commit to stricter p95 < 250ms (hardware dependent).
- Exceptions: scheduled maintenance (with 48h notice) and emergency maintenance (best-effort notice) — we will publish a monthly maintenance calendar for major regional upgrades.
