Hi Angela / team —

Quick technical clarifications to address your asks ahead of the SIG-OPS call. Forwarding summary of the proposal and why we think it maps to your constraints.

1) Static egress IP for prod control-plane: we can allocate a small, reserved static IP block with an associated SLA for control-plane calls (health checks, billing, model orchestration). This is implemented via a dedicated NAT gateway managed by Redwood in the customer-dedicated routing fabric. Expect a single /30 (2 usable IPs) for primary+failover in the simplest case; we can expand if you need multiple egress ASNs.

2) Managed proxy-only model traffic: yes — Redwood can route model-serving egress through our managed proxy (mTLS on the outbound leg). That proxy supports SNI allowlisting so you can enforce hostname-level inspection on your side. Note trade-offs: proxying model traffic adds ~15–35ms p95 depending on region and model size; we include options to bypass for latency-sensitive calls.

3) Audit logs and schema: control-plane audit entries include timestamp, request_id, caller_identity (account_id), source_ip, destination_hostname, mTLS_subject (when present), request_path, response_code, and token_anonymized_flag. We can deliver filtered log exports to a storage bucket you own (S3/GCS/Azure) daily or stream them to your SIEM via our forwarder.

4) Pen tests / SOC 2: Laura has the pointer links; we will share a redacted penetration test summary and SOC 2 attestation in the Drive folder ahead of the meeting.
