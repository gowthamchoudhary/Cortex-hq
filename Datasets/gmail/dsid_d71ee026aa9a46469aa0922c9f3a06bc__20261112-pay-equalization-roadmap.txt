Team — attaching a short proposal (pay-eq-proposal-v1.pdf) that attempts to standardize how we adjust offers for remote candidates across regions. Quick summary below and I'd like approvals to move to SOW/vendor selection and People Ops playbook updates.

Why this now
- Multiple hiring queues in APAC and EU where managers are asking for ad-hoc COLA/stipend adjustments.
- We need consistent rules for candidate communication and for finance to provision budgets.

Proposal (high level)
- Baseline: use local market median as the baseline for base salary in candidate offers (regionally sourced comp data).
- Remote adjustment stipend: up to 10% of base for APAC candidates relocating to higher cost timezones; up to 6% for EU; US handled via existing banding.
- One-time onboarding stipend vs ongoing allowance: default to one-time where below $2k USD; ongoing monthly allowance for larger differentials (> $2k/yr).
- Cap rule: absolute cap at 15% of base or $18k, whichever is lower (to limit runway).

Operational asks
- Finance: confirm whether we can route ongoing allowances through payroll vs vendor (PayAdjust).
- People Ops: update offer templates and acceptance language (taxes, benefits, local deductions).
- Legal/Security: verify any data residency or withholding implications (cc'd Neha for initial check).

Attached: pay-eq-proposal-v1.pdf (3 pages), pricing sketch placeholder.

Timeline
- Approvals this week, vendor SOW by 11/18, pilot hires in APAC/EU in early December.

Are we aligned to proceed? If so I'll ask procurement to run PayAdjust for a 6-month pilot.

Thanks Ben — good framework. A few inline notes and a handful of tweaks I'd like before we lock this as policy: 

On 2026-11-12 09:12, Ben Carter wrote:
> Baseline: use local market median as the baseline for base salary in candidate offers (regionally sourced comp data).

- Agree on local median as baseline but we should specify which data provider(s) we accept (e.g., Radford for enterprise roles, Payscale for smaller markets). I'll add a short appendix listing approved sources.

> Remote adjustment stipend: up to 10% of base for APAC candidates relocating to higher cost timezones; up to 6% for EU; US handled via existing banding.

- Request: convert percent rules into concrete bands for managers to apply quickly (e.g., Tier A APAC: 8% up to $6k; Tier B APAC: 4% up to $3k). Percentages alone cause inconsistent offers.

> One-time onboarding stipend vs ongoing allowance: default to one-time where below $2k USD; ongoing monthly allowance for larger differentials (> $2k/yr).

- Suggest lowering the ongoing allowance threshold to $1.5k/yr to reduce payroll overhead. Also, if an ongoing allowance is used, require a 12-month review clause.

Operational note for Neha: we need language on taxability and claim handling; if we route through vendor, what is the SR from employee side (expense form vs payroll)?

I'm happy to step in and map the banded table if you want — I can have a draft by EOD Thursday.
