Hi Sofia — thanks for the certificate and context. Short answer: yes, we can support reverse-charge treatment for EU B2B customers where the place of supply is outside the supplier’s taxable presence, but we need to validate a few items and decide whether to use standard invoicing with 0% VAT or a self-billing arrangement. Practical next steps below.

What we need from CloudMerchant: 
- Official EU VAT ID (full number) and the country code you want us to validate against (you already attached the certificate; please confirm the VAT ID string).
- Billing entity to appear on Redwood invoices (legal name + registered address).
- Confirmation of customer country for place-of-supply (is the consumption in PT/ES/DE etc.).

What Redwood will do: 
1) We will validate the VAT ID via VIES (or national registry) and keep a validation audit log on file.
2) If validated, we can issue monthly invoices with reverse-charge wording and 0% VAT; invoices will include the validated VAT ID and a note like: "Reverse charge: VAT to be accounted for by the recipient under Article XX."
3) If CloudMerchant prefers self-billing we can use our self-billing template and run a short DocuSign to enable monthly net invoicing (requires PO and a signed self-billing agreement).

I attached a draft invoice showing how we would present the net amount + reverse-charge note and a template self-billing agreement for your review.

Questions for your team: do you want us to proceed with a standard invoice (0% VAT + reverse charge wording) or start the self-billing DocuSign? Either works; self-billing can speed reconciliations but requires the signed agreement.
