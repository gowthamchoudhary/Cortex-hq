Alex / Samir — quick note with concrete math and an example, plus confirmation on a couple operational points.

Pro-rata (day-weighted) calculation example:
- Month has 30 days, Finvera experiences 18 minutes total unavailability across two incidents.
- Availability = 1 - (total_unavailable_seconds / total_seconds_in_month). For 18 minutes (1,080 seconds) in a 30-day month (2,592,000 seconds): availability = 1 - (1080 / 2,592,000) = 99.9584% => falls in 99.95+ bucket (no credit under our proposed bands).

Monthly cap options:
- Hosted standard: 25% cap (Redwood standard)
- Hosted PRAXIS (enterprise tier): 50% cap if customer signs 12 month committed MRC >= $X and agrees to 99.95% for dedicated pool.

Regional maintenance carve-out: Redwood can support regional blackout exceptions for scheduled maintenance provided we receive a 30-day rolling calendar of blackout dates. For end-of-month EU reporting windows we can add a standing exception for last 3 business days of each month if you need it.

Latency: 95th pct <200ms is achievable on our Redwood-hosted medium models for text generation; we'll document model-specific latency SLOs in the appendix.
