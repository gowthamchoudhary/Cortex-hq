Summary of positions:
1) Publicity: add a short clause to Order Form/Exhibit saying: "Customer may reference Redwood by name, display Redwood's logo, and include a short Customer quote in a press release or on Customer marketing materials, subject to Redwood's prior written approval (which will not be unreasonably withheld). 2) Logo usage: limit to (a) partner page and (b) press PDF distributed by Acme; any other reuse requires separate written approval. Provide size and color constraints as an exhibit.

Suggested sentence: "Redwood may redact or decline to publish any excerpt that would disclose Customer's confidential technical, security, or pricing information."

4) Attribution: customer quote must be mutually agreed; we propose a short approval period (48 hours) for minor edits after initial review to avoid looping cycles. Major edits require further review.

If Acme needs a limited embargo (we treat embargoes case-by-case), we can accept a reasonable 14–30 day embargo with written notice.
