Draft redline options below. I kept the math explicit so procurement can paste it into their T&Cs:

Option A (Pro‑rata refund):
"If Customer terminates in whole or in part prior to the end of the Term, Customer shall be entitled to a refund equal to the lesser of (i) the Remaining Committed Value and (ii) the Unused Committed Usage Value multiplied by (1 – 0.10). Remaining Committed Value means the total committed spend remaining in the Term. Unused Committed Usage Value means the portion of committed units not consumed as of the date of termination. Refunds will be applied first as credits against outstanding invoices; any cash refunds will be paid within 60 days."

Option B (Buy‑out):
"In lieu of the pro‑rata refund, Customer may elect to pay a Buy‑Out Amount equal to 60% of the Remaining Committed Value. Upon payment of the Buy‑Out Amount, Customer shall have no further payment obligations under the terminated portion of the Term."

Negotiation guidance: Option A -> better optics for customer, smaller wind‑down revenue for Redwood. Option B -> faster close, predictable cash, simpler contract language. If Sales wants to split the difference, suggest a blended tier: 70% for year‑1 term remaining, 65% for year‑2 remaining, 60% thereafter (encourages longer commitments).

I also recommend we keep the audit clause: one audit per 12 months; if variance > 5% customer can request additional reconciliations and Redwood will cooperate; if variance is < 5% then customer pays the cost of the audit.
