Joanna —

Technical context on batching/caching trade-offs (high level):
- Cacheable fraction: based on the sample logs you provided, we confirm ~32% average cacheable prefixes if you enable lightweight canonicalization and session-based prefix keys. That aligns with the workbook’s conservative line.
- Prefetch strategy: for interactive workflows we can prefetch likely continuations which increases cacheable fraction but requires predictable prompt patterns; benefit varies by use case (search gains most).
- Batching windows: 40–60ms windows give ~8–12% token cost improvement with negligible 95th pct latency impact for median prompt lengths (~220 tokens). Once windows exceed ~120ms you start to see tail latency degradation for sub-50-token prompts.

Risk callouts:
- If PulsePanther’s live traffic has higher short-prompt volume than your sample, the batch/window trade will hit 99th pct targets unless we keep smaller windows and rely more on KV caching.
- Quantization: switching to int8 inference in Dedicated reduces servo costs by ~20% but may increase hallucination risk for some sequence-completion prompts; we recommend adding a small eval bucket to capture LM quality if you push aggressive quantization.
