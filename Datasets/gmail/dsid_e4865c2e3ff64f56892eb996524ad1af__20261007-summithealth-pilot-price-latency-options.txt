Option B — Balanced reserved + adaptive batching (Mixed tier)
- Configuration: reserved pool with adaptive small-batch windows, prefix caching for repeated prompts, opportunistic streaming for short requests.
- Best for: mixed interactive + background where you want lower unit cost without sacrificing p95 too much.
- Typical unit cost (pilot band): $1,250–$1,700 per 1M tokens.

Option C — Cost-optimized batch (Analytics tier)
