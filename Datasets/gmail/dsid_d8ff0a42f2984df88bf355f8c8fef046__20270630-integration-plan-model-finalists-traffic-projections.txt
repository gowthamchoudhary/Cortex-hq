Hi Alex —

Thanks for the brief and the SLA — good context. Quick answers up-front, then a proposed next step.

Model finalists (shortlist):
1) LlamaX-13B-finetuned (approx LLM family): strong on summarization when given 8k context; recommended context: 4k–8k tokens for clinical notes. Good latency/cost balance for pilot.
2) OpenMed-7B-quant (quantized variant): lower cost per token, recommended context 2k–4k, good for Q&A where concatenated notes are shorter.
3) Redwood-arch-13B (optimized runtime kernels): best-in-class latency for 8k windows, recommended if you need long-window synthesis.

Token volume / ramp (rough):
- Baseline month (pilot): 250k tokens/day (~7.5M tokens/month)
- Expected steady ramp month: 600k tokens/day (~18M tokens/month)
- Peak (marketing/dev spike / batch reprocessing): 1.1M tokens/day (~33M tokens/month)

Estimate average tokens per request: ~1,800 tokens (incoming context ~1,200 + generation ~600) — this will vary per flow (Q&A vs summarization).

Private/VPC constraints (high level):
- Network: outbound to our control plane on port 443, mutual TLS optional (we support customer-managed certs).
- Sizing: for your expected peak profile, we recommend reserving 4–6 GPU capacity nodes (A100-equivalent) behind Dedicated pools for steady throughput; autoscale/burst policies will absorb spikes.
- Logs & audit: we can push audit events to your Splunk/Cloud logging or deliver daily log bundles.
