Hi Angela / team — thanks for the redline. I reviewed the procurement comments and sketched a narrow compromise below that has worked for similar customers; happy to discuss on a call and send a tracked-redline if youd like.

Proposed compromise (high level):
1) Governing law: the MSA to be governed by the laws of the State of California, but the parties acknowledge that certain local enforcement actions (see below) may be brought in the customers principal place of business if necessary for provisional relief.

2) Dispute resolution: Binding arbitration for all commercial disputes (claims for money, breach, etc.) administered by JAMS (or AAA) in San Francisco with a single arbitrator and limited discovery.

3) Injunctive / provisional relief carveout: Either party may seek emergency injunctive or provisional relief in any court of competent jurisdiction prior to, or during, arbitration for the limited purpose of preventing: (a) unauthorized exfiltration or disclosure of Confidential Information or Personal Data; (b) infringement or misappropriation of a partys intellectual property; or (c) a compelled disclosure arising from regulatory or lawful process where expedited access is required. The parties will meet and confer in good faith to limit the scope and duration of relief; any access to data for compliance will be logged and, where feasible, subject to audit by RedWood (sic) and the customer.

Notes and operational considerations:
- We must avoid open-ended venue exceptions that defeat the benefits of arbitration. Suggested language restricts provisional relief to measures strictly necessary to preserve rights pending arbitration.
- For the provisional access/export ask: Security will need a statement of purpose, data types, retention, and a named contact at TidalWorks. Neha, can your team confirm operational SLAs for expedited log exports (e.g., 48 hours for metadata, 5 business days for full session exports) and acceptable transfer mechanisms?
- If TidalWorks insists on NY governing law as a condition of signature, we can consider contract-level concessions (e.g., shorter survival periods on certain indemnities, or a capped exposure) rather than an unrestricted venue swap. Karthik, flag any commercial levers we want to offer.
