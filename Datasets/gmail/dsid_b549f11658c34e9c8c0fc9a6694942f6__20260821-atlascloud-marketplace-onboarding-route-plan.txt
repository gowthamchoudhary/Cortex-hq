Finance prefers a single vendor PO per Redwood legal entity where possible to simplify accounting, but marketplace platforms sometimes require separate POs and routing depending on billing relationships. My notes:
- For AWS Marketplace we typically accept an umbrella PO referencing the vendor subscription; AWS billing flows to the marketplace contract and we record it to the AtlasCloud SKU.
- GCP Marketplace often wants a cloud-specific billing arrangement (we've routed separate POs in two prior deals).
- Azure Marketplace can be flexible but our tax team requested a distinct PO number for offers listed by cloud in a couple of instances.
