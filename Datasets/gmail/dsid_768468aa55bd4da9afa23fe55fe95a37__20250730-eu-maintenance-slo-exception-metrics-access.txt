Hi Lucy — thanks for the clear list. Short answers below, and I've looped in Marissa (SRE) and Ben (Sales Ops) to provide the formal evidence packet and to confirm exception timelines.

1) Uptime and p95 definitions:
- Uptime (availability) is calculated as the percentage of successful requests (2xx/3xx) over total requests to the selected production endpoints, evaluated at daily and 30-day windows.
- p95 latency is computed on server-side request durations measured at the ingress proxy (start of request to first byte of response) with outlier trimming for synthetic/health-check traffic. Our production p95 uses a rolling 1-hour sampling window aggregated to hourly and 30-day summaries.

2) Monitoring stack and runbooks:
- We run Prometheus for metrics ingestion, Grafana for dashboards, and PagerDuty for alerting. We maintain runbooks for common alert families (latency spikes, ingestion backpressure, model OOMs). Full runbook links are in the evidence packet.

3) Customer metrics access:
- We can provide read-only Grafana access scoped to the customer's account and region for a default 30-day period. Tokens are timebound and IP-restricted on request.

4) Temporary SLO exceptions:
- We do support approved, documented temporary exceptions for scheduled maintenance that are mutually agreed. Exceptions are limited to the maintenance window plus a short buffer (typically up to 24 hours) and recorded in the contract amendment. Approvals usually take 48–72 business hours once we receive the maintenance plan and impact analysis.
