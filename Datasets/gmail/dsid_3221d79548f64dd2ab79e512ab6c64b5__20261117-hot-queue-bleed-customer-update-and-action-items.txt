Subject: Re: Hot queue bleed in us-west-2 — regional findings and mitigations

Sam / Neha — technical update from ops: we confirmed that a head-of-queue saturation event occurred in rsv-usw2-14 starting ~14:42. Key findings:

- Root immediate cause: the regional autoscaler missed several scale-up signals due to a recent scheduling-edge change in the allocator (a circuit condition when node warmup rate exceeded the backfill window). The autoscaler did not allocate new warm nodes fast enough.
- Secondary effect: continuous request retries caused KV-cache thrash on active nodes, increasing p99 token latency and triggering request timeouts.
- Impacted surface: all Dedicated tenants pinned to rsv-usw2-14 with high concurrency; Aurora observed ~30% throughput drop and elevated 504s.

Mitigations applied (15:00–15:25 PST):
1) Manually forced a partial pool rebalance and increased warm standby count for rsv-usw2-14.
2) Enabled temporary routing to compatible spillover pool rsv-usw2-spill-2 for 20% of traffic to lower head pressure.
3) Applied a short-lived throttle to extremely high-concurrency keys (via per-tenant rate cap) while we stabilize.

Artifacts attached: ops_snapshot_20261116.log (truncated), alloc-graph-uswest2.png (allocation timeline).

Next steps:
- We'll conduct a controlled rolling restart of the allocator subservice across the pool to clear warmup backlog (scheduled 17:00 PST tonight).
