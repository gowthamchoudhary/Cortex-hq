Claire — thanks for the clear brief and the RFP summary. Putting three pragmatic, procurement-friendly packages together — short summary below and a SKU matrix attached. Full matrix and term sheet attached (VPC_Sku_Matrix_v1.xlsx, Redwood_Term_Sheet_for_ClearVista.pdf).

1) Pilot: "Starter Pilot"
- Scope: single-site, up to 8 GPU-equivalent nodes for 6 months
- Commercial: waived first-month platform fee; metered runtime invoicing monthly; one-time onboarding professional services (PS) estimate (we can scope SOW after technical interview)
- Support: Business hours SLA, email + Slack channel; optional on-call add-on at standard hourly rate
- Migration credit: we can offer a $20k implementation credit that applies to the first renewal if you commit to an enterprise SOW within 90 days of pilot end

2) Committed Rollout: "Anchored Commit"
- Scope: multi-site option (US + EU), node-anchored SKUs; customer selects node count per site, platform fee amortized over the committed term (12 or 24 months)
- Commercial: flat per-node runtime pricing + amortized platform fee applied monthly; 10% discount for 12 month commit, 18% for 24 month commit
- Support: premium SLA (4 hour P1 response, 24/7 incident hotline), 3 days/year of included engineering hours for runbook creation and major upgrades
- Price protection: platform fee increases capped at 3%/year for the committed term

3) Enterprise: "SRE+"
- Scope: enterprise entitlements, cross-region routing, dedicated SRE retainers
- Commercial: bespoke SKU bundling, volume tiers on runtime; deeper discounts tied to GPU-commit and minimum revenue commitments
- Support: 24/7 on-call rotation, dedicated engagement manager, quarterly business reviews, optional white-glove upgrade windows
- Onboarding/migration: PS retainer options (time & materials or fixed SOW), migration credit negotiation depending on multi-year commit

A few clarifications up front: the platform fee is applied at the account level (not per site) but can be amortized and modeled per-site internally for internal chargeback. The attached XLSX has a worked example of amortization across a 24-month commit and a sample invoice cadence.
