Hi Ellen —

Quick operational notes from the Platform/Delivery side:
- Deletion certificate: we can provide a signed PDF statement that lists the dataset identifiers, deletion job ids, timestamps, and the operator (automation) that performed the purge. We usually return that within 30 days for bulk requests; we can move to 21 days in the order form if Clarion requires it.
- Audit logs: we will retain enriched deletion logs (who/what/when) for 24 months as requested; we will provide S3 links (or secure portal access) to download those logs.
- Backups: Clarion needs to accept that immutable snapshot retention policies are controlled by a separate backup lifecycle. For Option A we will mark backups identified at time of deletion and guarantee final purge at 180 days. For stronger guarantees (no backup carve-outs), that becomes Option B but has higher operational cost and contractual penalties for emergency restores.

If Clarion wants 45 active days + 7-business-day export window, we will schedule a joint runbook rehearsal before signature and include a clause that requires 48-hour notice for restore/evacuation ops.
