Hi Sara —

Appreciate the detail. I wanted to respond with a technical tradeoff summary and a concrete POC proposal so your security and procurement teams have what they need. High level: all three deployment paths are viable, but they differ in isolation guarantees, lead time, and cost.

Isolation and noisy-neighbor controls (concise):
- Hosted Private VPC: logical isolation via customer VPC peering and network controls; runtime still shared at the scheduler level but we provide strong namespace isolation (per-tenant KV cache, per-tenant disk encryption keys, and strict RBAC). Good if you prioritize time-to-value and network isolation.
- Dedicated capacity (recommended for your profile): dedicated GPU pools assigned to CareLumen — no other tenants scheduled on those nodes. We enforce compute-level tenancy using topology-aware allocation and explicit GPU reservations. This removes noisy neighbor risk on the GPU layer and lets us offer tighter tail-latency SLOs (example: median 120ms, 95th 320ms at 200 qps in our internal tests for a 70B quantized model; numbers vary by model and sequence length).
- Private on-prem (air-gapped): full control over hardware and networking; we ship a private control plane and runtime that can run without Internet egress if required. Longest procurement and onboarding timeline but maximum control for PHI.

Operational features we provide across options:
- KV cache per-tenant and configurable prefix caching to reduce token costs and tail latency.
- Per-tenant telemetry and request tracing (token-level costs, latency percentiles, model versioning).
- Rolling canary and automatic fallback to smaller model variants if SLOs are breached.
- Audit logs with immutable append-only storage and configurable retention for legal/forensic needs.

POC proposal (recommended: Dedicated pool)
- Duration: 1 week (proof-of-performance) followed by optional 2-week extension for scale test.
- Resources: 2x A100-equivalent nodes (or same vCPU/GPU mix you specify).
- Deliverables: performance report (p50/p95/p99), cost per 1k tokens, isolation verification steps + audit log export, and runbook for failover to hosted fallback.
- Timeline: we can provision a Dedicated POC environment in ~5 business days after contract/PO.
