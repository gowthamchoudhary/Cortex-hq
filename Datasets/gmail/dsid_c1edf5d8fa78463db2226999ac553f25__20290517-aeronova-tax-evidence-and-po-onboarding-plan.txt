Hi Sara / team — thanks for starting this. Procurement checklist and expectations below.

Procurement minimum requirements to create a PO:
- Completed W-8BEN-E (entity classification box completed; signatory matches corporate registry)
- Certificate of incorporation or government registration (PDF)
- Bank letter with account name and routing (we prefer bank letter >30 days old)
- Remit name and billing email exactly as you want them on the PO

Tax team next steps (from Procurement):
- We'll submit the TIN/foreign-TIN to our compliance tax verification flow. If TIN matching fails or the entity is high-risk, we may request notarized signature pages and an explicit UBO declaration.
- Typical turnaround is 2–3 business days after full packet is received.

Please place all docs at the vendor questionnaire link (preferred) or reply-all with the attachments. Once validated, I will create the PO and send the PO number and standard Redwood payment terms (Net 30 unless otherwise negotiated).

If you expect to invoice immediately, please include a cover note in the invoice with your HubSpot deal ID (HS-789456) to help match to the PO.
