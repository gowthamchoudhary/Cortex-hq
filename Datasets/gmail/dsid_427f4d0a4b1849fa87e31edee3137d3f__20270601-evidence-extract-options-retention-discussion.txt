Eve / team — short update on throughput and options.

1) Throughput & sizing: Our extractor currently streams ~45 GB/min when operating with 8 workers and the default serialization to NDJSON. For a ~1.2B event window, that maps to roughly 5–7 hours to produce the full NDJSON payload (gzipped). We should budget 8–10 hours total end-to-end for staging and checksum verification.

2) Formats: supported — NDJSON (preferred), gzipped CSV, and Parquet (columnar; useful if they plan to run large analytics). We can include a manifest file with checksums and a small schema dictionary.

3) Object-level fidelity: We keep per-object access events at full fidelity for 365 days by default for hosted accounts. For accounts on Dedicated or Private, retention can be configured longer, but Finvero is on hosted with an extended retention add-on they purchased (+1 year). That means we can provide full fidelity for the requested window.

4) Deletion/retention logs: We retain purge-event records (who triggered, what objects, timestamps) for 13 months in the audit index; we can include a filtered extract of those events.

5) Delivery: US‑only S3 delivery is supported. We'll do a signed PUT into their bucket (we request a pre-authorized bucket policy) or else we can stage into our secure transient S3 and grant access. For their GRC ingestion, NDJSON gzipped is the safest.
