Hi — thanks for the clear asks. Below is a short checklist and the operational notes. I'm attaching a one-page checklist (redwood_retention_checklist_v1.xlsx) you can include in your report.

Short checklist (customer-facing):
- User requests (what you send): retained by default for 30 days on hosted API. These are considered request-level artifacts. Customers can request shorter retention via support; see note below.
- Model responses (outputs / generated artifacts): retained by default for 30 days to support quality monitoring, billing reconciliation, and safety investigations.
- Transient runtime artifacts (KV caches, streaming buffers, in-memory session state): ephemeral and not persisted to long-term stores by default. Runtime caches are kept only for the duration of a session/connection and are dropped after inactivity; they are not accessible via console/exports.
- Billing & telemetry aggregates: non-reversible billing records and aggregated telemetry are kept for longer (90 days default for aggregated metrics; billing records per contract longer).

Operational notes / opt-out paths:
- Hosted API: account-level retention can be changed to a shorter window via a support request; for 0-day retention (no storage of requests/outputs) we offer a scoped opt-out but there are SLA and feature trade-offs (no replay for troubleshooting, limited quality/regression tooling).
- Dedicated / Private deployments: customers can configure retention in their deployment manifest / config and may keep none of the data if they self-host. Private deployments with our control plane can also request custom retention settings under enterprise contracts.
- Deletion requests: we accept formal data deletion requests through Support + Account team. For hosted API, deletion actions are queued and executed with standard audit logging and an email confirmation back to the account owner.

Exceptions & investigative retention:
- We may retain copies of specific items longer if required by legal hold, security investigations, or fraud detection. Any such hold is recorded and limited in scope.
- We keep audit trails of deletion actions and policy changes beyond the per-item retention window for compliance (these trails contain pointers, timestamps, and operator IDs but not full request/response payloads).
