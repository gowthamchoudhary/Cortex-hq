Hi Lena —

Thanks for outlining the priorities. I attached a short Renewal Playbook (renewal_playbook_v1.pdf) that covers common structures we offer and a sample staged ramp we use for customers scaling production LLM usage. High level options that map to your asks:

1) Multi-year annualized commit with quarterly true-ups
   - Customer commits to an annual token/throughput spend, invoiced monthly, with a quarterly reconciliation and ability to top-up capacity.
   - Benefit: predictable monthly unit pricing, easier budgeting.

2) Staged ramp (12-month schedule) + burst pool
   - Example: Year1 baseline 50% of expected peak, + scheduled unlocks at month 4 and month 8 to reach 100%. Burst pool for temporary spikes.

3) Co-term + alignment credit
   - If you co-term other vendor contracts to Redwood's contract date, we can offer a one-time alignment credit (applied to first invoice post-co-term).

4) Auto-renew flexibility
   - We can extend the opt-out notice to 60 days in the SOW for enterprise customers; we can also add a mutual right to renegotiate pricing for material usage changes.

Next steps I suggest: you share a 12-month usage forecast (tokens/peak concurrency) and your preferred invoicing cadence. Procurement typically likes the playbook + a short AMP (mutual action plan) so I'll prepare an AMP and a ramp schedule spreadsheet for review.
