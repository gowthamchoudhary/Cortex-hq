Thanks for the loop — EdgeWorks can take the delegated zone for acme.internal.redwood under the following constraints (summary):
- We require a single delegated subzone (acme.shadow.redwood) that we will manage via NS delegation. No apex transfer.
- We can support three TLS patterns: 1) full termination on our managed proxy (we provide ACME/HSM-backed certs and can provide an attestation), 2) CSR-only termination where we sign a CSR in our HSM and return the signed cert (we do not keep private keys), 3) SNI passthrough to origin.

For option (2) CSR flow, our ops ask for: 
- Signed CSR in PKCS#10 DER format (csr_sample.pem is fine)
- A short HTTP-based challenge endpoint we can verify under acme.shadow.redwood for provisioning (we can also support DNS TXT challenge if you prefer)
- A binding proof document from Redwood that outlines retention/rotation windows.

Attached: acme-delegation-playbook.pdf — includes our delegation steps, sample CSR handling, and expected telemetry (NS propagation times we observe).

Quick security constraints from compliance: 
- HSM-backed signing is required for any vendor-signed leaf certs for Acme (we cannot accept vendor software-only key storage for prod).
- We will not accept vendor-held private keys for more than staging/test environments. For production, prefer Redwood-managed leaf certs or vendor-signed CSR where the vendor explicitly returns the cert and provides an HSM attestation.
- Rotation policy: max 90-day validity for leaf certs, with automated renewal start at 14 days before expiry. For sensitive endpoints we prefer 30-day validity.
- OCSP: stapling required on proxy; for SNI passthrough ensure origin supports stapling or we fallback to direct origin revocation checks.

Attached a sample CSR (csr_sample.pem) — use this as a template for the format we expect. Also confirm that EdgeWorks can produce an HSM attestation artifact (serial-signed) as part of the signing response.

On the call, we'll want a short checklist: attestation, rotation windows, audit log export (syslog/S3), and proof that private keys are not exported.
