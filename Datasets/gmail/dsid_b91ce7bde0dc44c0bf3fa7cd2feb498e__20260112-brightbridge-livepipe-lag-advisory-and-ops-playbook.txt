Carlos — thanks for the quick heads-up and for attaching the traces. We're seeing the same symptom in our telemetry: increased inter-chunk latency for a subset of streaming sessions in us-east-1 between 08:45 and 09:20 PST. SRE is investigating a capacity scheduling regression that increases kv-cache pressure for some model shard placements.

Immediate suggestions you can deploy client-side while we triage (short-term mitigations):
- Disable streaming for critical flows (switch to single-response/non-streaming completion) — this avoids chunk reordering and gives deterministic latency.
- Reduce max_tokens per request (we recommend lowering by 30% for now).
- Implement a simple client-side retry with exponential backoff for failed/slow streams (start 250ms, max 2s, 3 attempts).
- Enable smaller streaming chunk size on your end (if you control chunking) or fall back to our compact model variant by setting model=redwood-compact-1 in the request. Compact reduces per-token compute load and has lower observed tail latency in these conditions.

We do not expect token loss when switching to non-streaming; you'll receive the same completion content. We'll confirm consistency across model variants in our follow-up.

I've looped in Laura (SRE) and Ben (Customer Success) to coordinate an ops-level response and update your account for any potential SLA impact. I'll follow up with a timeline once we establish the root cause.

That heuristic increased memory pressure on a small subset of inference nodes, which in turn elongated attention-kernel scheduling and caused inter-chunk latency spikes. Short-term operational playbook we recommend for BrightBridge (we can push some flags on our side if you prefer):
- If you need immediate stability and can tolerate slight latency increase, flip to non-streaming for high-risk flows (UI paths that cannot tolerate chunk jitter).
- For sessions that must remain streaming: set model=redwood-compact-1 OR send requests with smaller max_tokens and enable client retry on 5xx / stalled streams.
- Optional: add header X-Redwood-Fallback: compact-1 to requests; we can honor it server-side to route your sessions to the compact pool with minimal change.

Carlos —

Final update from our side and next steps:
- Root cause: an autoscaler heuristic change (deployed 2026-01-07 03:12 UTC) allowed tighter packing of model shards which increased KV-cache pressure on a small subset of nodes. Under certain session mixes this increased attention-kernel contention and produced the inter-chunk latency spikes you saw.
- Mitigation applied: we rolled back the heuristic and deployed a targeted scheduler patch to redistribute affected shards; the canary completed successfully in us-east-1 at 2026-01-09 08:45 PST and the full rollout finished by 2026-01-12 15:10 PST. Real-time telemetry shows chunk latency returned to expected baselines.
- Customer impact: transient tail latency increases on streaming responses for a subset of sessions between 2026-01-08T08:40:00-08:00 and 2026-01-09T09:00:00-08:00. We do not see evidence of token loss or generation corruption.
