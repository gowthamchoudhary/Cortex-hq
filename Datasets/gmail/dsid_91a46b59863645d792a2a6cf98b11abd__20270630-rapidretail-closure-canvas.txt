Bethany —

Thanks for the heads up and the logs. We saw the same spike from ~07:40–08:20 PDT originating from two serving clusters in us-west-2 that momentarily exhausted ephemeral KV cache memory during a burst of long-tail sequences (avg seq len + 3x expected). Autoscaling did not trigger fast enough because the spike pattern looked like short-lived tail burst rather than sustained CPU/GPU load.

Immediate steps taken (in-bridge):
- Throttled non-critical background requests for RapidRetail routing to free KV pool.
- Pinned a hot-fix kernel path to reduce per-token memory amplification.
- Scaled an extra dedicated pool and re-routed 15% of live traffic (manual) to it.

We are preparing a short RCA and a proposed mitigation timeline. Monica is working on the mitigation worksheet and will share a timeline for permanent fixes (KV guardrails + adaptive burst autoscaler).
