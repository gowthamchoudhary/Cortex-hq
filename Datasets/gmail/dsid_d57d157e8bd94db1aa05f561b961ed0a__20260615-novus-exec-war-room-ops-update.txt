War-room update (initial triage):
- Observed: two GPU hosts serving shard A reached sustained 98% memory utilization; KV-cache growth caused longer-than-expected token generation times.
- Error signature: increased inference timeouts leading to cascading retries and 503 responses from the fallback router.
- Root cause hypothesis: traffic pattern change (batch size and sequence length) + autoscaler not provisioning fast enough for the hot shard.

Immediate mitigations we've initiated:
- Provisioned two additional instances into the Dedicated pool (scaling event in progress, ~6–8 minutes to be in steady state).
- Adjusted routing policy: heavy traffic routes will temporarily be routed to a compatible v2_small variant to reduce latency risk for critical requests.
- Rate-limit tolerances increased for background/low-priority jobs to relieve pressure.

We are monitoring; pushing live metrics to the war-room board. Will update in 10 minutes or sooner if we see recovery.
