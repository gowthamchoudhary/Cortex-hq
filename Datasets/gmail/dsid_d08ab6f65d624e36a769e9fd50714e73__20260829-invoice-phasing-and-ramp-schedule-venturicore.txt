Great to see the forecast — thanks for sending. Below is a quick set of workable invoice/ramp templates and how onboarding credits typically work at Redwood. I'll attach an illustrative ramp spreadsheet for visibility.

Options (high level):
1) 3-year full upfront prepay (biggest term discount)
   - Single invoice at signature for entire committed spend.
   - We can provide an onboarding credit equal to X% of year-1 spending (example: 10%) applied against invoices as a credit memo; if fully prepaid, credit reduces the initial invoice net cash but remains on ledger and is visible on invoice line items.
2) 3-year committed, annual invoices
   - 3 invoices (one per 12m). Discount is slightly lower than full-upfront; credits amortized monthly against monthly usage or as line-item credits on the annual invoice.
3) 1-year prepay
   - Single year invoice (lowest term discount). Onboarding credit available but typically amortized over first 6 months.

Ramp mechanics (example approach):
- Tranche A (Month 0): onboarding — N GPUs, TPM baseline for integration.
- Tranche B (Month 6): additional GPUs/TPM to reach mid-scale.
- Tranche C (Month 12): full production capacity.
- Each tranche has acceptance criteria (network tests, latency SLOs, runbook sign-off) before subsequent tranche billing is considered final.

Credit handling:
- Credits apply to overage first, then to usage if there are no overages. Unused credits roll forward for up to 12 months by default; beyond that they expire unless otherwise agreed.
