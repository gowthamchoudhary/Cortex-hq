Hi Rishi/Neha/Vanessa,

Short answer: yes, Microsoft Commercial Marketplace supports both (meter-based SKUs with separate committed/overage SKUs) and retroactive marketplace credits, but there are process constraints to be aware of:

- Merchant of Record: For private offers where Redwood is Merchant of Record (MoR), you can set up a discounted committed SKU and a full-price overage SKU. Usage is reported through the meter and settlement follows the SKU pricing.
- Retroactive credits: The marketplace allows invoice adjustments/credits but they must be requested within 60 days of the month being adjusted, and year-end true-up credits are typically processed as a separate rebate line item during reconciliation (not editing historical invoices). Microsoft requires an authorized finance contact and a reconciled usage report uploaded to the marketplace portal for audit.
- Channel splits: Marketplace settlement will distribute revenue according to the MoR/partner split you configure, but if you want a custom waterfall (different margins on committed vs overage), that needs to be configured as separate settlement rules tied to SKU IDs. That means the committed SKU would have one settlement percentage and the overage SKU another.

Operational notes: DocuSign amendments are fine for the PO, but credits need a marketplace reconciliation workflow (we can coordinate with the commercial team). I'll share the private-offer guidance doc and a sample settlement config after this mail.
