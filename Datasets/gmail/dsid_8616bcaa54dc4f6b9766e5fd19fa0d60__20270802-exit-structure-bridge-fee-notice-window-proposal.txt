Laura / Ben — attaching a first-pass redline (Redwood_ExitTerms_Proposal_20270802.docx). Key points I recommend for negotiation with Nimbus: 

- Auto-renew opt-out: require notice at least 60 days prior to the renewal effective date to opt out of auto-renew. This aligns with our operational lead time for capacity reallocation.

- Mutual termination for convenience: allow either party to terminate for convenience with 60 days' written notice after the initial 12-month commitment. If termination occurs during the first 12 months of a committed term, propose a bridge fee equal to 25% of the remaining committed fees to cover setup and committed capacity. After 12 months, bridge fee would step down to 10% of remaining committed fees.

- Mid-term deprovisioning: require 30 days to complete customer-requested export/hand-off of data; Redwood will retain audit logs for 90 days unless a different retention schedule is agreed.

Rationale: the staggered bridge fee protects Redwood from rapid churn while giving Nimbus a clear, gradually reducing economic cost to exit. It also avoids a heavy one-time termination penalty that scares procurement.
