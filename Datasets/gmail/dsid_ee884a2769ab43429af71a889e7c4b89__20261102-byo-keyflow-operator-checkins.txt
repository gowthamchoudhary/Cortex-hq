Looping in a few technical notes from the runtime side before the call. High-level proposal: ephemeral-wrapped keys with customer BYOK root. Key points:

- Root key (customer-managed HSM) is used only for wrapping/unwrapping a short-lived envelope key. The envelope key is what the inference runtime stores in its KV cache (encrypted at rest).
- Envelope keys expire by default after X hours (configurable per workload). On rotation we rotate the envelope and re-wrap; root rotations are handled as an operator event with explicit audit hooks.
- Recommended operator ports: 5696/TCP (KMIP over TLS) + 443 for management callbacks. We avoid opening unrestricted admin ports to the HSM network.
- Rotation evidence: operator produces a signed rotation manifest (SHA256) plus the KMIP request/response logs with timestamps. We suggest retention 12 months by default.

I'll bring a short draft of the runbook to the call. Also linking a Confluence draft: https://confluence.redwood.ai/display/OPS/BYO+KMS+Patterns
