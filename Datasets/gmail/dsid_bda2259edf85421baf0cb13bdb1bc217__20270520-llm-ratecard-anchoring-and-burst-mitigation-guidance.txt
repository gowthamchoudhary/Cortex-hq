Ana — thanks for the checklist. Per Rishi's note, here's the commercial appendix and example invoice math. I'm sending the workbook (RDW_Medicom_Pricing_20270521.xlsx) and pasting the key sections below so you can quickly copy into your comparison table.

--- Commercial appendix (summary) ---
1) Token rates (hosted API list prices)
   - Short-context chat (<=2k tokens): $0.0009 / token (list)
   - Long-context generation (>=8k typical): $0.0016 / token (list)
   - Embeddings (dense vector): $0.00040 / vector

2) Commit tiers (example bands, monthly committed tokens)
   - Band A: 0 - 5M token/mo -> committed unit = $0.00090
   - Band B: 5M - 25M token/mo -> committed unit = $0.00072 (20% off list)
   - Band C: 25M+ token/mo -> committed unit = $0.00060 (33% off list)

3) Overage and smoothing mechanics
   - Overage rate = 125% of the committed unit price for the band that was consumed (e.g., Band B committed unit $0.00072 -> overage $0.00090).
   - True-up cadence = quarterly by default. Quarterly true-up reconciles committed vs actual usage; overages invoiced on the reconciliation invoice.
   - Smoothing credits: up to 10% of committed monthly volume may be carried forward one quarter as a smoothing credit (only within the same contract year).
   - Graceband: first two months after contract start receive a 15% overage grace (helps pilots).

4) Multi-year and volume discounts
   - 24-month anchor: additional 8% off the committed unit after applying band discounts.
   - 36-month anchor: 12% additional off committed unit.
   - Discount stacking capped at 40% off published list rates.

5) Private / Dedicated specifics
   - Private (VPC) control plane surcharge: +12% on committed spend (covers KMS, audit logs, private networking).
   - Dedicated GPU pool: base reserved capacity fee (starts at $35,000/mo for a 4-GPU pool) + reduced per-token compute charge (negotiated).
   - Data egress & storage: billed separately at cost plus 8% handling fee.

6) Invoice examples (see attached workbook)
   - Example A: 1M token/mo commit (Band A)
       * Committed unit: $0.00090 -> monthly commit $900. If actual = 1.5M: overage 500k tokens at 125% -> overage unit $0.001125 -> overage = $562.50. Quarterly true-up accrues to invoice.
   - Example B: 4M token/mo commit (Band A->Band B crossing logic)
       * We apply banded calculation: first 1M at Band A rates, remaining at Band B committed unit; workbook shows exactly how we pro-rate and bill.

7) Commercial terms & protections
   - SLA credit schedule included (details in SOW).
   - Usage anomaly protection: configurable surge cushion (pre-purchased burst credits) available to cap overage exposure.

Next steps: we can 1) lock a small pilot commit for 3 months to baseline usage, 2) finalize commit bands, and 3) circulate SOW + PoF for internal legal review.

Ana / team — adding a few commercial mechanics we typically include in the SOW to avoid confusion later:

- Invoice cadence & payment terms: Net 30 standard. We can support monthly invoicing with quarterly true-up reconciliations (detailed in the workbook).
- Overage calculation: billed at the band rate that corresponds to the actual consumption band; if consumption moves up bands mid-month we pro-rate by token buckets (workbook shows the function).
- Escrow / purchase order: we accept PO references; for larger multi-year deals we typically require an initial deposit equal to one month of committed spend.
- Minimum seat for priority Dedicated capacity: 8 seats (or equivalent GPU reservation) for guaranteed throughput SLAs — we can provide an alternate priority queueing SLA for smaller seat counts at a lower cost.
