Laura — thanks for the clear list. I've pulled together answers below and attached a short implementation checklist and our current subprocessor roster. High level responses first, then the checklist (attached).

Summary answers:
- Legal mechanism: Redwood currently operates on SCC 2021 for controller-to-processor transfers and offers EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF) supplemented arrangements where customers require it. For country-specific needs we can include SCC addenda / UK module.
- In-region hosting: Yes — we support Dedicated and Private deployments to keep processing inside an EU region. Typical lead time for Private (VPC on customer infra) is ~8-12 weeks depending on procurement; Dedicated (reserved capacity in our EU region) can be enabled in 2-4 weeks.
- Subprocessors: we maintain a live CSV of subprocessors and notify customers 30 days in advance of material changes.
- Encryption & KMS: encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), at rest using AES-256. Customers can choose Redwood-managed KMS or bring-your-own keys (BYOK) backed by HSM (AWS KMS / Azure Key Vault integration). Rotation policies default to 90 days for ephemeral keys; master key rotation can be coordinated with customers.
- Auditability: we provide region-tagged access logs, processing location metadata in trace events, and deletion receipts for deletion API calls. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 audit reports are available under NDA.

Laura — quick technical clarifications from the security/product side:

On DPF / SCC interplay:
- We recommend SCC 2021 for EU controller transfers to Redwood as processor. For US-hosted processing customers who require DPF-specific controls, Redwood implements a control mapping (available in the attachment) that aligns DPF principles with our operational safeguards (processing location, vendor attestations, access controls). Where customers need DPF-only assurances we can provide an additional attestation and include specific operational commitments in the DPA annex.

On isolation & in-region behavior:
- Dedicated: tenant isolation at the software level with dedicated GPU pools; no co-mingling of customer inference requests.
- Private: option for customer-managed network + private control plane; supports on-prem or VPC deployments.
- For in-region guarantees we include an explicit processing-location clause in the DPA and a verification procedure (periodic location logs + audit support).

On keys & HSM:
- BYOK flows use customer KMS + envelope encryption. We record KMS key IDs in audit logs and provide key rotation events.

Evidence artifacts we can deliver (examples):
- Subprocessor ledger (attached CSV)
- Processing-region audit trail (sample extract)
- KMS usage logs (redacted)
- SOC 2 Type II summary + ISO 27001 cert under NDA

I also attached a proposed DPA annex redline that limits cross-border transfer mechanisms to SCC 2021 with an opt-in for DPF addenda. We're flexible on the wording but want to keep an explicit clause for in-region deployment and BYOK options.

Laura / team — adding operational details from the privacy/program side and a short execution checklist below (I also attached the PDF checklist referenced earlier).

Operational checklist (high level):
- Step 1: Choose transfer mechanism (SCC 2021 default) and indicate DPF addendum if you require DPF attestations.
- Step 2: Decide hosting mode: Hosted (Redwood API), Dedicated (EU region pool), or Private (VPC/on-prem). Provide preferred region and estimated go-live window.
- Step 3: Select KMS model: Redwood-managed or BYOK. If BYOK, provide KMS details and we will schedule an onboarding call with infra.
- Step 4: Subprocessor review: review attached CSV and confirm any objections within 15 business days.
- Step 5: Annex execution & doc sharing: we provide redlines; legal teams sign and we schedule a short technical onboarding.

Typical evidence and deliverables we provide during due diligence:
- DPA annex redline (word) + signed DPA on execution
- Subprocessor roster and standard subprocessors attestation
- Region-tagged processing logs + deletion receipts
- Audit pack (SOC 2 Type II summary, penetration test summary) under NDA

Timeline notes: if you need a Private deployment inside a specific EU country (on-prem or isolated VPC), allow 8–12 weeks for infrastructure and legal setup. For Dedicated capacity in our EU region we can often enable within 2–4 weeks once contracting is complete.
