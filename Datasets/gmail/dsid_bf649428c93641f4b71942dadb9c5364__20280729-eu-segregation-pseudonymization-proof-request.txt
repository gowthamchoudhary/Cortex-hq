Hi Oliver —

Following up with the detailed guidance Alex promised. High-level positions and what we can provide now: 

1) EU-only processing and in-region backups
- We support tenant-level region pinning. For Everest Health we can configure production, replicas, and backups to remain in EU regions only; cross-border replication is disabled by default for in-region tenants.
- Operational evidence we can share: (a) architecture diagram with region annotations, (b) S3/Blob bucket location manifests, (c) snapshot of our backup retention config for the tenant.

2) SCCs and supplementary measures (Schrems II)
- We execute the current EU Commission standard contractual clauses (SCC, 2021 text) between Redwood and the customer. For high-risk PII, we recommend and include the following supplementary technical/organizational measures: strong in-region KMS with per-tenant key separation (customer-managed KMS optional), pseudonymization of identifiers at ingestion for analytics pipelines, and strict access controls with audited keystores.
- We will provide a redlined SCC template and a short memo describing the supplementary measures we implement (encryption key custody, access logging, legal process for government requests).

3) Deletion and evidence of deletion
- Our deletion process: customer API request or console action triggers immediate removal from primary stores, initiation of backup purge jobs, KV cache invalidation, and generation of an immutable audit record. Backups are truncated/rewritten per retention schedule; for expedited deletions we run a targeted purge with certificate issuance.
- Evidence we can provide: sample deletion certificate (sample-deletion-cert.pdf), redacted audit log extract, and the deletion runbook. We cannot share unredacted customer logs; we will provide redacted examples that preserve timestamps and operation ids.

4) Pseudonymization specifics
- For analytics-only copies we apply deterministic hashing + salt per-tenant and remove direct identifiers. For higher assurance we can provide a technical spec (eu-pseudonymization-spec.xlsx) describing hashing, tokenization, and which fields are pseudonymized by default.
