Hi Alex / Sophie —

Thanks again for your patience yesterday. Summary and next steps from the emergency maintenance we performed on 2026-06-02:

What happened
- At ~09:00 PT we observed a steady memory increase on the serving pool handling some high-concurrency workloads. The root cause was a regression in the KV-cache eviction heuristic deployed on 2026-05-31 (change set r3184). Under certain prompt-length distributions, entries were not being evicted promptly, causing memory to grow and, in some cases, worker restarts.

What we did
- 09:30 PT: deployed patch (v1.2.9) to canary subset; monitored latency, memory, and error rates.
- 09:40 PT: canary passed acceptance (no elevation in request errors, memory growth stabilized).
- 10:05 PT: rolled to full serving pool for Acme traffic; completed rollout by ~10:40 PT.
- No customer requests were intentionally dropped. A small percentage (<0.5%) of in-flight requests were retried automatically by the platform; retry logic completed without manual intervention.

Impact to Acme Analytics
- Brief latency spikes during canary (5–12 minutes) for a subset of requests routed to canary nodes.
- No data loss and no requirement for manual reconciliation.

Technical fix and artifacts
- The patch increments eviction aggressiveness for large-session keys and adds a guard for long-lived sessions (see attached fix-notes.txt).
- Attached: patch-v1.2.9.tar.gz (release artifact) and postmortem-20260602.pdf (non-sensitive summary).

Preventive measures
- We’ve added a synthetic workload test that reproduces the prompt-length distribution, gated into pre-deploy checks.
- We’re adding an alert that triggers at lower memory growth thresholds for affected pools.
- We will run a two-week monitoring sweep to ensure no regressions.

If you’d like, we can schedule a 30-minute technical debrief with our reliability engineers and your SREs to walk the postmortem and the new guardrails.

Apologies again for the disruption. If there’s any customer-visible behavior you still see, reply to this thread and I’ll escalate immediately.
