Logan / Marissa —

Short legal read: Ventra's asks raise three discrete risks:
1) IP carveout removal — opens unlimited indemnity exposure and undermines our existing indemnity model.
2) 3x ARR liability cap — exceeds playbook thresholds for enterprise (we normally cap at contract value or 1x ARR depending on term).
3) 24-month warranty survival for operational SLAs — extends exposure period materially.

Concrete counterproposal we recommend for leadership:
- Keep a tiered liability cap: IP claims uncapped only where Redwood is adjudicated to have materially infringed third-party IP (and only for direct damages); otherwise cap at 1.5x ARR.
- Warranty survival: 12 months standard, 24 months limited to discrete code defect remediation (not performance fluctuations) and subject to timely reporting.
- Third-party connector carveout: specify reasonable efforts and explicit exclusions for connectors maintained by Ventra.

These reduce tail risk while giving Ventra meaningful concessions. However, the IP language and increased cap will require executive approval (VP+ legal concurrence). If leadership signs off, legal can draft precise clause language and an exception letter.
