Quick update — lab runs completed this morning. Summary below (full CSV attached).

Executive summary (short):
- Baseline (fp16): p50=120ms, p95=360ms, p99=780ms for 350-token outputs (512 prompt).
- Quantized (int8): p50=145ms, p95=420ms, p99=900ms — ~18% cost reduction, small tail impact
- Burst (50 concurrent spikes): TTFT cold=520ms, TTFT warmed=160ms. We observed transient queueing; peak queue depth ~12 requests, average wait 320ms during spike windows.
- Steady (200 sustained concurrency): throughput stable at 380 tokens/s per shard, p95 drifted up to 410ms at 22–24 minute mark under sustained KV load (cache warming matters).
- Rate-limit policy tests:
  * immediate 429: lowest operational impact but poor UX for client (no retry coordination)
  * queue-with-timeout (10s): best tradeoff — 80% of requests complete, average wait 600ms; tail latencies better than immediate drops
  * adaptive throttling: good for prioritized traffic but requires client hints and orchestration

Recommendation for POC MAP: enable server-side queue with 8s timeout + client-specified priority header for interactive sessions. Suggested per-tenant soft limit = 250 concurrent requests, hard limit = 320. Backoff: exponential with jitter, max retries 3.
