David / Kimberly — quick operational notes to back Kim's points. I'm summarizing what we can commit to without impacting service reliability: 

- Regional access controls: EU customer telemetry is stored in our EU tenancy. Support-facing debugging access for EU tenants is scoped to EU-hosted tooling. Non-EU engineers may be granted ephemeral, time-limited access only via a break-glass process that requires manager approval, ticket justification, and 4-eyes supervision.

- Session auditing: All break-glass sessions are recorded (command audit, session start/end, operator identity) and pushed to our SIEM. We can provide the session audit record as part of deletion evidence.

- Ephemeral telemetry retention: Data is tagged with a TTL. We can set a customer-specific TTL at onboarding (14 / 21 / 30 days). Lower TTLs will reduce window for live debugging; we can provide an alternate: a short-term snapshot export for troubleshooting under explicit consent (and then automatically deleted).

- Deletion certification mechanics: When a purge is requested we will run a coordinated multi-step workflow: mark, run background erase (including index pruning), validate via checksum/logs, and generate a signed certificate with timestamps and job ids. Our SOC runbook expects ~10 business days for full validation; we can contractually commit to 7 business days for non-exception cases but will enumerate exclusions.
