> Customer ConnectPay (Asha Rao) reported a p99 climb to ~3.2s and a ~36% throughput drop starting 08:50 PDT.

Checked infra telemetry and runtime logs. Quick summary:
- No host-level CPU/memory pressure on the dedicated fleet for CP; GPUs are stable.
- There was a kernel selector change rolled at 08:43 that enabled an experimental attention kernel for long-seq paths (artifact id: kernel-0.9-expB). Rollout was to 10% of requests on the model family used by CP.
- At 08:49 we observed an unexpected KV cache miss storm correlated to requests routed to hosts with kernel-0.9-expB. Miss rate jumped from 2% -> 22% for that cohort.

Immediate actions taken:
1) Throttled kernel-0.9-expB rollout to 0% at 09:02 (effectively paused).
2) Triggered KV cache warmup on affected hosts; starting to see miss rate normalize after 09:18.
3) Enabled temporary adaptive-batching to limit worst-case latency on those hosts.

Preliminary hypothesis: kernel change interacts poorly with our LRU-based KV cache partitioning under certain request fanouts; it increases cache churn. This yields higher per-token recompute and batching inefficiencies, hence both p99 and throughput impact.

Next steps:
- We'll run a canary rollback of kernel-0.9-expB on the small cohort and collect histograms for p50/p95/p99 across 15-minute windows.
- If rollback confirms, we will not promote kernel-0.9-expB and will open a post-mortem with the runtime team.

I’ll update the ticket with metrics and a short timeline.
