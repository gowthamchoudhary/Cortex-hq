Thanks for the context, Evelyn — this is actionable but there are a few technical clarifications we need before we can commit:

1) Residency: are they insisting on tenant-dedicated nodes (single-tenant residency) or region-affinity (multi-tenant nodes pinned to us-west-2 but shared)? Their compliance wording was a little fuzzy.
2) Traffic profile: what are the expected qps peaks and the idle window after which cold starts happen? Warm-pool sizing depends heavily on expected concurrency distribution during business hours.
3) RTO/RPO expectations: besides cold-start ms, do they want guaranteed failover behavior across AZs / regions?

Prelim thoughts from runtime side: region-affinity (pinned placement in us-west-2) is doable without heavy infra changes — it’s a routing policy plus placement constraint. Exclusive single-tenant nodes (full isolation) requires tenant scheduling and capacity reservation; we can do it but it adds provisioning lead time and cost.

Warm-pool approaches:
- Pre-warmed long-lived instances reserved per tenant for business hours (fastest, predictable latency, higher cost)
- Proactive KV/prefix caching + background keepalive (lower cost, variable guarantees)
