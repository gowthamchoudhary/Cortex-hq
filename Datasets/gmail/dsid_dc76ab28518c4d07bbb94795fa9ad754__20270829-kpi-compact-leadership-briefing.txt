Irene / team — quick read-through. I dug into the latency spike (15–17 Aug) and here’s what I have:

- Root cause: a kernel scheduling change deployed 15 Aug that altered our continuous-batching thresholds for short sequences. This increased scheduling overhead for small requests under 128 tokens.
- Impact window: 15:07–18:40 UTC on 15 Aug and a smaller regression 16 Aug morning. P95 for small-hosted rose from ~210ms to ~320ms.
- Mitigation already in flight: reverted the threshold change (PR #7421) and queued a tuned threshold with an A/B canary targeting 10% traffic. Canary shows P95 back to ~215ms.
- Ask for QBR: approve reverting to the tuned threshold (low risk) and call out the plan to add a microbenchmark before batch-tuning changes.
