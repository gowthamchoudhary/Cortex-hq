Hi Lena — thanks for the clear summary and timeline. Short answer: yes, we support both patterns but there are trade-offs and one flows better operationally. Outline below and a conservative recommendation.

Option A — pooled account credits + redistribution (recommended for speed):
- Redwood issues a single promotional credit allocation tied to your accelerator account (sponsor-funded pool).
- LaunchWell sponsor provides payment to Redwood; we apply the pool at account level and we can generate per-startup, single-use credit codes (non-transferable).
- Accounting: we can provide a CSV showing code -> startup -> amount to satisfy your bookkeeping.
- Expiry: configurable; we can set 180 days.
- Notes: credits applied via promo codes reduce consumption immediately; sponsor gets one consolidated invoice/receipt.

Option B — consolidated invoice reduction (sponsor rebate / credit memo):
- Redwood invoices the sponsor for the consolidated amount then issues credit memos / invoice adjustments to reflect the stipend.
- This is cleaner for sponsor GL treatment but requires coordination with finance and may take longer (PO/invoice cycle).

Operational limits / constraints:
- We can generate up to 2,000 single-use codes per issuance without manual ops; larger volumes we route through a CSV import workflow.
- Non-transferability: we can mark codes as single-use and require redemption against specified account IDs.
- Legal/terms: we provide a short MOU + Promo Addendum specifying expiration and usage rules (no cash value).

Recommendation: Option A with a consolidated sponsor invoice sent at time of payment. We will generate single-use codes for each startup and provide the reconciliation CSV. That hits your deadline and minimizes manual invoicing.
