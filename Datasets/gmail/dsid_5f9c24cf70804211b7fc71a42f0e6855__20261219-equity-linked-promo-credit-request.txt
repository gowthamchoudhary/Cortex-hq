Diego — thanks for the forward. I like the structure but suggest we tighten terms to reduce churn and exposure:

- Reduce cohort pool to $20k (redeemable by cohort companies; each voucher capped at $5k). This keeps exposure predictable and still gives them a meaningful starter.
- Reserve pilot credits: 2 pilot companies at $7.5k each (total $15k) for hands-on engineering support and case study commitment.
- Credits valid for 120 days from issue (gives startups time to onboard).
- Add a conversion incentive: if a company converts to a paid plan within 6 months, we can apply a one-time onboarding credit equal to 20% of usage in their first paid month (capped at $5k).
- No equity acceptance — legal confirmed this is messy unless Corp Dev signs off; don't want to open that path for an accelerator program.

Also recommend requiring a signed acceleration partner addendum that clarifies marketing commitments, measurement, and a short SLA for engineer office hours. If LaunchFoundry is pushing for equity, ask them to route the intro to Corp Dev (cc me).

Happy to approve $35k total if Sales/Deals want to accept the conversion incentive above.
