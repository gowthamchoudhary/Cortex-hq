Mitigation applied (09:07 PST):
- Rolled back the last deployment to previous image for the affected AZs.
- Pushed a temporary runbook patch to force agents to reconnect to the previous control-plane endpoint while we prepare a forward fix. See runbook_fix.patch attached.

Next steps:
- Create a unit/integration test to catch header-negotiation regressions (in progress).
