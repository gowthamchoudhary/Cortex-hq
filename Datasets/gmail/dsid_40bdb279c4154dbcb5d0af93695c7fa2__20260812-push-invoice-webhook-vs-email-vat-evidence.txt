Hi Elena — thanks for the clear notes and the attachments. Short answers up top:  

• Yes, we support a push/webhook delivery option for invoices. Payloads can include a machine-readable XML, invoice metadata (VAT number, billing entity, tax breakdowns), and a separate PDF attachment link.  
• We also support email delivery to your AP inbox as a fallback; emails include a PDF invoice and metadata in the message/body for automated parsing.

A few implementation details and tradeoffs to call out:  
- Webhook push (recommended): we POST to your endpoint and include both XML and a signed PDF URL. We can optionally include embedded base64 PDF when requested, but most customers prefer a signed S3/GCS URL plus a JSON invoice object so their ingestion pipeline handles retrieval.
- Email: we deliver a standardized PDF filename (redwood-invoice-<invoice_id>-<date>.pdf) and include an XML attachment when requested. Email is simpler but has higher chance of being delayed/blocked by mail filters and is harder to reconcile for "machine guaranteed" delivery.
- VAT evidence: for EU customers we can include explicit VAT identifiers and line-level tax amounts in the XML per your sample. We do not currently support PEPPOL endpoints natively; that would be a custom integration.

Hi all — thanks for the clear thread. Quick operational answers and action items from billing ops:  

1) Webhook support: yes — we can POST a JSON wrapper that contains a link to the signed PDF plus an embedded XML invoice. Authentication options: API key (recommended) or mTLS for customers that require mutual TLS. Please note mTLS requires a cert exchange and will add ~3–5 business days for setup depending on networking teams.

2) Payload details: our current production schema includes fields for buyer_vat_number, seller_vat_number, tax_amounts (per-line), currency, invoice_issue_date, invoice_total, and a line_items array. The XML sample you provided maps cleanly to our XML export and we can provide a mapping doc before integration.

3) Inline/base64 PDFs: we can support including a base64 PDF in the payload upon request, but we prefer providing a signed, short-lived URL to the PDF (better for large files and avoids bloating the payload). If your parser requires inline PDFs, we will enable it for GreenField as part of the onboarding checklist.

4) Email fallback: if you choose email, we will deliver PDFs named: redwood-invoice-<invoice_id>-YYYYMMDD.pdf and include the XML as invoice-<invoice_id>.xml. We recommend also enabling a copy to your portal mailbox so you have a retrievable canonical copy. Emails are subject to standard SMTP delivery caveats (spam filters, quarantines).

5) EU VAT evidence & retention: Redwood will include invoice-digitally-signed metadata and keep copies for audit for 7 years by default. If you need extended retention or a signed timestamp (qualified electronic signature), that's an add-on and we can provide pricing.
