Thanks Sabrina — great to meet you and Tom today. Short answers up front, then a concrete next-steps list and links.

Quick answers:
- Hosted evaluation rate limits: default trial accounts are rate-capped per-API-key; we provide a sandbox API-key with a baseline of 60 requests/min and 10 concurrent connections. We can temporarily raise burst caps for trial customers on request (we often do for demo days).
- Endpoints parity: sandbox endpoints mirror production paths and routing. You can expect the same URL conventions and parameter schemas.
- Quickstart: 1) sign the evaluation access SOW, 2) we provision sandbox API-key + workspace, 3) install SDK or use REST endpoints, 4) run smoke tests (we provide test prompts), 5) we enable any requested transient burst increases. Typical turnaround: 24–48h after SOW.
- Burst behavior: Hosted evaluation will accept short bursts up to configured burst cap; anything beyond will get 429 throttles. For your 500 req/min burst we recommend pre-requesting a temporary burst increase; Aditya can coordinate.
- Data residency / PII: Hosted trial keeps logs for diagnostic retention only; we can obfuscate or disable retention for specific sandboxes on request. If you need strict on-prem or VPC data residency we can move to Private evaluation, but that's typically a longer setup.

Attachments: I attached a short eval access SOW and a one-page quickstart checklist.
