DPA & retention options:
- Option A (default, recommended for evaluation): 90-day retention for request/response content plus automatic purge of temp logs.
- Option B: 365-day retention (audit-focused) with periodic reconciliation.
- Option C: Customer-managed export — we provide a scheduled encrypted export to your S3 bucket (you control retention).

Notes on exports / logs:
- We do support push exports of audit logs and request-level metadata to customer S3 (TLS + SSE), on a daily cadence. Exports include request timestamp, model/version, and anonymized tokens unless you request token-level exports (those need explicit approval).
- Audit logs are logically separate from customer content retention; you can choose independent retention for logs in your S3.

KV cache & context persistence:
- During evaluation we do not persist KV cache to customer buckets by default. The runtime manages ephemeral KV caches per request to reduce latency; caches are cleared on session termination. For production we offer an optional export of summarized session artifacts for debugging under tight controls (encrypted, access-audited).

Escrow for logs: Redwood does not currently offer a third-party escrow service for raw logs. If BridgePoint requires escrowed copies, we can set up scheduled encrypted exports to a mutually agreed S3 (Redwood-side retention limited) and include transfer proofs in the MAP.
