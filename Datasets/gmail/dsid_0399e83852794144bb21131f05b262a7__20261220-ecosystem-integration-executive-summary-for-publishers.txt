Camila / Marcus —

After a quick scan of the 1-pager you sent (thanks), here are the legal items I want on the table for the initial commercial MSA / publisher addendum:

1) Permitted Use and Redistribution: need explicit language for whether Redwood may host, cache, mirror, or redistribute weights. If mirroring is allowed, require checksums and a mutually-agreed access control list.
2) Attribution & Branding: publisher logos in the catalog require a trademark grant and a short brand guidelines addendum.
3) Liability Cap: standard Redwood terms (mutual cap tied to fees), but need to confirm OpenMux is comfortable with explicit warranty disclaimers for training-data provenance.
4) Security & Evidence: lightweight SOC2 Type II snapshot or equivalent, and a signed statement for dataset provenance for any models trained on third-party proprietary corpora.
5) Export Controls: if models are trained on data from restricted jurisdictions, include a clause for export restrictions and compliance cooperation.

I’m attaching a short onboarding checklist and a redline summary template I’d like to use in the first pass. If OpenMux’s counsel prefers, we can exchange DocuSign or a tracked-redline PDF.
