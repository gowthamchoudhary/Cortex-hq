Liam / team —

Thanks for the quick call this morning. Below are the three commercial constructs we discussed with the key assumptions we used in the workbook. I kept the math intentionally simple so you can map to the RFP columns. All numbers are normalized to your reported monthly token volume (18B tokens).

Summary of scenarios
1) Consumption-first (no seat commitment)
   - Unit price: $0.000023/token (generation), $0.000012/token (embeddings)
   - Expected monthly run-rate: ~$414k
   - Migration credit: one-time onboarding credit equal to 2 months of projected run-rate (applied first invoice)
   - Upside: no long-term commitment; downside: higher marginal cost at scale

2) Hybrid (staged credits + reserved capacity for priority)
   - Unit price (consumption): $0.000019/token gen, $0.000010/token emb
   - Reserved capacity for priority traffic 99th p95 SLO: $18k/mo for burst pool sizing (covers peak day skew)
   - Expected monthly run-rate: ~$355k (consumption) + $18k reserved = ~$373k
   - Migration credit: $120k amortized over first 6 invoices (20k/mo), plus 2 weeks of dedicated onboarding support
   - Notes: recommended if Novus needs predictable latency SLO and a click-to-scale priority pool

3) Seat + bundled credits (enterprise-style)
   - Minimum annual commitment: $3.6M (prepay / commit) mapped to a blended unit rate equivalent to $0.000015/token gen, $0.0000085/token emb
   - Upfront credit: $250k applied to professional services and migration assistance
   - Expected effective monthly run-rate after credits (year-1 amortized): ~$240k/month equivalent (given credit absorption and prepay)
   - Notes: best for customers who want lowest unit economics and are able to commit to run-rate predictability; includes extended SLA options and on-call engineering handoff windows

Assumptions / clarifications
- Token mix: 60% generation family-A, 40% embeddings family-E
- We assumed an average generation token length of 64 tokens and embedding request size consistent with your RFP samples
- Billing cadence: monthly; prepay option available for seat construct
- Latency: Hybrid / Seat constructs include a priority pool for <180ms p95 on your stated concurrency; consumption-only targets best-effort latency but often hits p95 ~250–300ms under peak unless caching/batching is tuned
