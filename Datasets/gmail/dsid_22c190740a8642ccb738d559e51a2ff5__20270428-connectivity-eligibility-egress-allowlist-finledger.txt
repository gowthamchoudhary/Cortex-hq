Sam — quick answers from the infra side so we can unblock planning: 

1) Egress IPs: For our Private VPC offering we can allocate a small static egress pool. The typical minimum we can guarantee per environment today is a /30–/28 range depending on capacity and whether you need HA across multiple AZs. For production we'd recommend a /28 to avoid cross-talk with other customers; Laura to capture exact allocation once we finalize the account/region.

2) PrivateLink vs Peering: PrivateLink is ENI-based on the customer side and does not require VPC peering. You will see an interface endpoint in your account and generally do not need to alter route tables for normal call flows; outbound flows from your infra to our connector use the interface endpoint. We will provide the exact interface endpoint DNS name and the ENI IPs for allowlisting in the checklist.

3) TLS termination: We support customer-managed TLS termination in front of the connector (recommended for maximum control). Control-plane connections (management/health checks) will still use mutually authenticated TLS to Redwood-managed endpoints; we can provide cert fingerprints for your allowlist/inspection.

4) Audit logs / retention: We can deliver request/response logs to your secure S3 bucket or a dedicated log-forwarding endpoint. Retention 180 days is fine; we will include log-forwarding delivery options in the SOW addendum.
