Hi Sophia — thanks for the PO and VAT cert, helpful. Short answers up front, then next steps.

1) Invoice entity proposal:
   - SaaS subscription: invoice from Redwood Inference (EU operations) / Redwood EMEA where available — we try to invoice digital services from the buyer's region to simplify VAT.
   - Gateway hardware: invoiced by Redwood UK trading entity (we ship from our UK partner warehouse).

2) SaaS subscription (B2B): if Globex provides a valid DE VAT ID, we can issue an invoice net of VAT and reference reverse-charge (Article 196/199 EU rules). We'll need a copy of the VAT record and to validate it via VIES before final invoice.

3) Hardware shipment: because goods are physically shipped from the UK into Germany, import VAT is typically applied at customs on import. If we (Redwood UK) arrange DDP vs DAP terms it affects who pays and declares import VAT. For straightforward procurement, we usually:
   - Ship DDP and include German VAT on the hardware invoice (collected by Redwood then reclaimed where applicable), or
   - Ship DAP and let the buyer handle import VAT at customs.
