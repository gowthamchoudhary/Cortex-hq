Lisa — thanks for the clear asks. Short answer: yes, we can support a presigned S3-based ingest pattern and supply the mapping to Whistic/Vendr for import. Below is the proposed flow plus a quick checklist; I attached a short playbook and a small helper script for presign generation/validation.

Proposed flow (high level):
- CloudNova creates a dedicated S3 bucket for evidence (you already noted the KMS alias; good).
- CloudNova scripts produce presigned PUT URLs limited to the object key namespace: cloudnova/evidence/<account>/<year>/<artifact> and TTL=12 hours by default.
- CloudNova uploads object and records sha256 hash and length into a manifest.json in the same prefix (manifest.json is write-once).
- Redwood runs an automated ingest job that: (1) fetches the manifest via presigned GET, (2) downloads artifacts via presigned GET, (3) validates sha256 and content-length, (4) extracts metadata and maps to the portal schema (Whistic/Vendr/OneTrust).
- On validation success Redwood returns a signed proof-of-ingest record (timestamp + internal job id) to CloudNova and pushes a single-line verification CSV to a CloudNova-managed callback URL (or your SQS).

Operational defaults we propose: TTLs: 12 hours for PUT, 72 hours for GETs used by our ingestion (to account for retries). Allowed headers: x-amz-server-side-encryption: aws:kms, x-amz-meta-evidence-type, content-type. Hash algorithm: sha256 (hex). Manifest format: line-delimited JSON with fields key, sha256, size_bytes, uploaded_at.

Security notes:
- Redwood will validate KMS envelope encryption metadata but will not have access to your KMS key (we only access presigned URLs).
- We keep an ingestion audit log (immutable) with object key, sha256, job id, and timestamp. We can surface the audit log lines in the verification CSV for procurement/legal.
