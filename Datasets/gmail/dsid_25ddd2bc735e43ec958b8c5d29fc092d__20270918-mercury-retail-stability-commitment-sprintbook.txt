Adding technical context and immediate plan from infra/serving:

Observed signals (preliminary):
- Error mode: elevated 500s from the inference runtime tied to async KV-cache misses and transient OOMs on hosts serving sequences >2048 tokens. Error pattern shows "KVCacheOverflowError" in the debug logs and increased GC churn.
- Latency: p95 increase correlated with synchronous cache refill and our fallback to a higher-cost path when the cache miss ratio exceeded ~12%.
- Throughput: autoscaler reacted but cooldown and warm-up latency caused effective capacity drop.

Probable root causes (hypothesis):
1) A regression in our recent preprocessor change that changed tokenization for extended customer prompts, producing longer-than-expected sequences for a subset of requests.
2) KV cache retention policy inadvertently reduced allowed entries after the last deploy (config drift), causing cache churn and refill storms.
