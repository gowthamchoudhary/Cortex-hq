Hi Elena — thanks for the heads up, and congrats on the launch planning. Short answers up front, then a couple of options:

Short answers:
- We support both per-token overage billing and temporary quota (burst) purchases.
- True-ups are standard: overage is billed at the agreed overage rate for the billing period, but we can structure a capped true-up or a prorated SOW for planned events.

Option A — Temporary burst quota (recommended for predictable spikes):
- Purchase a weekend burst allocation (e.g., additional 5M tokens/day for 3 days) as a one-time add-on.
- Pricing: flat $1,200 per million tokens/day for the burst window (example tier). The burst is invoiced up-front or net-30 depending on your terms.
- Benefit: fixed cost, predictable invoice, no surprise per-token overage calculations.

Option B — Per-token overage with protective discount/true-up:
- Standard overage rate: $0.0009 per token.
- For planned launches we can offer a promotional 20% discount on overage for the first billing period (first month after launch).
- We can also cap the maximum overage liability for that billing period (e.g., cap at $25k) and amortize any remainder into the following month as a controlled true-up.
