Angela — thanks for the clear asks. We can absolutely structure something along those lines. Early thoughts: 

1) Term: 24 or 36 months, with annual price bands and a true-up cadence quarterly (preferred by finance) or monthly (preferred by engineering).

2) Uplift windows: define three enrollment windows per year (Jan 1 / May 1 / Sep 1) where OliveTech can elect a higher bucket. Elections take effect immediately and are billed pro rata for the remainder of the period.

3) Credit tranche: Redwood can offer a front-loaded credit (prepay credit) equal to 5–10% of year-1 committed spend that applies to Q1 overages. If you early-exit, remaining credits are pro-rated back.

4) Auto-renegotiate: we can add a conditional clause that triggers a pricing review (good-faith renegotiation) when usage >150% for 2 months. This is non-binding but commits both sides to a decision window and an interim uplift.
