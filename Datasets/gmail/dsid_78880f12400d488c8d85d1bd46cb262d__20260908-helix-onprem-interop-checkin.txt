Rafael — thanks for the summary and the attachments. Quick technical read:

- Telemetry: we have per-tenant KV cache and request tracing already, but the export pipeline will need a new sanitization stage that strips any user-provided prompts and masks probable PII tokens. I estimate 3-4 engineer-weeks to build an exportable, configurable sanitizer + S3-compatible dump job; add another 1-2 weeks to make it configurable per-deployment (Helix will want toggles).

- Entitlement for on-prem: we can support a signed entitlement token (JWT) that the appliance validates locally and pings our entitlement service periodically (offline-first with 30-day grace). That will require a small auth shim and a lightweight licensing daemon for the appliance. 2-3 engineer-weeks.

Caveats: we must be explicit about data residency and KMS — if Helix requires customer-managed keys on-prem we'll need support from the Private deployment team and possibly new docs. Also curious if they expect us to support upgrades/patching of the runtime inside their appliance or they manage it entirely.

I can draft an engineering scoping note by EOD tomorrow.
