Hi Alex / Lakeshore SRE team,

We wanted to let you know we are investigating an issue affecting hosted inference for a subset of Lakeshore API keys that may result in truncated generation outputs (responses cut short) and higher p95 latency on the /v1/generate and chat endpoints.

Impact:
- ~3% of requests for your production key are returning partial responses (newline/truncation mid-sentence).
- p95 generation latency has increased from ~420ms to ~1.9s for some regions.
- No data exfiltration observed; requests are processed and responses returned, but sometimes cut short.

What we're doing:
- Engineering has opened an active incident (RED-7891) and is running an ops bridge.
- We have enabled a temporary routing bypass to reduce queuing on the affected pools and are adjusting KV cache thresholds.
- We will provide an ETA for full mitigation within 2 hours.

Workaround you can use now:
- Retry on 5xx/early-close with exponential backoff (we recommend up to 2 retries).
- If you rely on stream completeness, we suggest enabling our prefix-cache pinning per the attached runbook_extract.txt for sensitive prompts.

Attachments: runbook_extract.txt (short guidance), incident_snapshot-0406.json (ops snapshot).

I will keep this thread updated. Apologies for the disruption — we know this affects your user flows and we are prioritizing a fix.

Alex — quick update from the ops bridge and engineering:

Summary of root cause (interim):
- We identified a KV-cache eviction path that triggers under particular request patterns that include long prompt prefixes + simultaneous small-batch high-concurrency traffic. On affected nodes this causes backend kernels to evict prefix cache entries mid-request which manifests as truncated output and spiking p95 while the runtime rehydrates state.
- Scope: affected pools in us-west-1 and us-east-1 used for hosted inference for some shared-capacity customers (Lakeshore keys fall into the affected pool list). Not observed in Dedicated instances.

Mitigation applied @ 10:40 PDT:
- Disabled aggressive eviction heuristics on affected pools and increased per-session cache pin window.
- Activated an emergency routing policy to shift new requests away from saturated nodes (this will remain for ~4 hours while we validate).

Current status:
- Error rate for truncation has dropped from ~3% to ~0.3% for your key; p95 latency has fallen to ~650ms on routed traffic.
- We are monitoring; full normalization expected within 2–3 hours while we complete a safe kernel patch and targeted canary.

Next steps and asks:
- We will run a controlled canary of the runtime patch at ~13:00 PDT; we'll notify you before widening.
- Please avoid bulk batch replays from your side during the next 2 hours — this helps reduce noise during canary.
- We'll share postmortem and offer a credit as appropriate once the incident is closed.

Attachments: incident_snapshot-0406.json (ops traces), runbook_extract.txt (recommended temporary flags).

Apologies again for the impact — I will message you as soon as the canary completes.

Alex / Lakeshore SRE,

Status: RESOLVED (2027-04-06 14:25 PDT).

What we changed:
- Deployed a safe runtime kernel patch that fixes the KV-cache edge eviction and added backpressure limits to prevent mid-request cache rehydration.
- Re-enabled normal routing after validation canaries showed stable behavior across test traffic and production samples.

Impact:
- Truncation rate for your key is back at baseline (<0.01%).
- p95 latency returned to expected levels (~420–480ms).

Post-incident actions we will share in a formal postmortem by EOD tomorrow:
- Detailed timeline, root cause analysis, remediation steps, and planned long-term mitigations (improved cache instrumentation, apportioning hot-prefix protection, and a change to our autoscaler backoff behavior).
- Proposed customer remediation: a service credit covering the window of elevated error rate and latency; Monica (cc'd) from Customer Success will follow up with specifics.
