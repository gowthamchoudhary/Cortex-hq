Thanks Linda — good notes. Quick technical clarifications from networking: 

- Approved peers: we can mark specific CDNs and partner-cache ranges as region-local by ACL. Those will be the only allowed egress exceptions; everything else will be dropped at the VPC edge unless an explicit warm-standby route is activated.
- Failover artifacts we can provide:
  * Flow logs (VPC-level) with s3 export: daily rollups + raw hourly files (retention 180d)
  * BGP session logs and RIB snapshots (pre/post) — we will capture via control-plane hooks and store SHA256 checksums in the audit bundle
  * Control-plane events: all operator actions (API call logs) with request IDs, operator id, and timestamp (retention 365d)
- Practical constraint: immediate warm-standby cutover will transit encrypted tunnels between regions. We will provide a staged runbook with a 15-minute maintenance window for live cutover to capture consistent snapshots.
