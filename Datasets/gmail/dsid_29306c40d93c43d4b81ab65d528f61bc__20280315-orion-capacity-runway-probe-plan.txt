Mike / Hannah —

We completed the probe on the six racks this morning (attached: sizing-probe-results.csv). Summary highlights:

- Baseline: sustained aggregate ~505 tokens/sec (measured over 12m window) with p95 = 230ms; individual rack variance +/- 6%.
- Burst window: we achieved 2x sustained for 10 min with p95 = 380ms and 0.6% request timeouts when bursts coincided with cross-rack traffic peaks.
- Long-tail: overnight batch runs completed without queue growth; KV cache hit-rate stabilized at ~84% after 12 warmup requests per session.
- Network observation: spikes aligned with periodic east-west backups on the leaf switch, observed 1.2% packet drops (RX) during the busiest minute on rack-03. MTU was consistent. No DPU driver faults were observed.

Recommendations (actionable):
1) Increase capacity runway to 30% above current sustained target for 12 months to absorb feature growth and observed burst instability.
2) Apply a conservative batch size bump to 6 for daytime chat flows on racks with >90% KV hit rate; leave at 4 otherwise.
3) Add a small queuing buffer on the Request Router (1.5s max) to smooth cross-rack microbursts — reduces p95 spikes with <0.1% added tail latency.
4) Network mitigation: move scheduled backups to off-peak windows and enable QoS policing on leaf switches for inference VLAN.

Attachments: sizing-probe-results.csv (CSV summary), probe-test-script.sh

Let me know if you want a short 30-min review this afternoon. If you want us to produce a formal SOW and procurement-ready capacity slide, we can draft that and include cost delta for the +30% runway.
