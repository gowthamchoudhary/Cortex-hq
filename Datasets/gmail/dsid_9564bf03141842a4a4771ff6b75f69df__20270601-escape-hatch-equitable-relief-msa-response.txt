Thanks Tessa — reviewed the attached Stellar_Retail_MSA_redlines.docx and drafted a response redline with our recommended compromise (attached: Redwood_response_redlines.docx). Summary of our positions and rationale below.

1) Governing law: we prefer California, but we can accept New York law for this Enterprise deal provided that we obtain two concessions:
   - A mutual forum carveout allowing Redwood to seek provisional injunctive relief in any federal court that has jurisdiction over Redwood (to enable urgent injunctive relief in appropriate venues).
   - A limitation on forum selection to the state or federal courts located within the State of New York for disputes that are exclusively monetary in nature (so non-monetary emergency relief can be sought where Redwood reasonably needs it).

2) Arbitration / forum: Stellar asked for arbitration in NYC. We are open to arbitration but need an explicit emergency relief carveout that allows parties to seek interim equitable relief in court prior to arbitration. Our proposed language narrows injunctive relief requests to cases involving imminent data exfiltration, model theft, or material breach affecting production serving.

3) Equitable relief / injunctions: we pushed back on the requirement to exhaust internal escalation steps before seeking court relief. Instead we propose a short, documented notice process (24-48 hours) for issues that are operational, but no exhaustion requirement for clear, time-sensitive harms (e.g., theft of customer data, key material breach). This preserves both parties’ ability to move quickly while reducing unnecessary back-and-forth for routine escalations.

4) Multi-entity/affiliate issues: Stellar’s draft attempts to broaden consent-to-serve and waiver provisions to subsidiaries not on the Order Form. We recommend restricting waivers and service provisions to contracting entities listed on the Order Form and a defined group of affiliates to avoid open-ended exposure.

5) Order Form / DocuSign path: to keep the commercial timeline, I propose we accept a narrow New York governing law clause with the above carveouts and agree to a redlineize Order Form now (Jordan, Tessa — I will prepare a DocuSign-ready Order Form with the negotiated carveouts noted).
