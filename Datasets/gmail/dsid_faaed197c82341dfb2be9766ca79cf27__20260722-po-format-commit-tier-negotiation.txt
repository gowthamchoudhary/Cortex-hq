Hi Lisa / Soojín,

Looping in to propose a pragmatic mapping that has worked for other healthcare customers:

Proposal (sample, negotiable):
- Commit tiers: Quarterly commitment with three bands:
  * Tier 1: 0-50B tokens — list rate (no discount) — PO line: "Tier 1 usage reserve"
  * Tier 2: 50B-200B tokens — 18% discount off list — PO line: "Tier 2 usage reserve"
  * Tier 3: 200B-500B tokens — 30% discount off list — PO line: "Tier 3 usage reserve"
- PO treatment: Redwood provides a quarterly not-to-exceed amount per the chosen tier. The PO will reference the Redwood quote PDF and state the commit in dollar terms. Invoices draw down against the reserved amount; overages are billed monthly.
- Overages cap: We can commit to a 25% cap on per-token overage rate above the committed unit price for the first 12 months; after that we re-evaluate.
- Vendor form: Redwood accepts SOC 2 Type II and will sign a standard DPA. For indemnity/lability we propose a mutual liability cap at the greater of $X or 2x the annualized committed spend — we’ll provide specific clause language to your legal team.
