Grounding & quality metrics to monitor (proposed):
- Grounding Precision@1 (is the top reply supported by at least one retrieved doc)
- Grounding Recall@5 (coverage of supporting docs in top 5)
- Hallucination rate (human-evaluated, per-100 responses sample)
- Citation coverage (percent responses with at least one document citation)
- Response latency (p50/p95 token-latency with batch size=1)
