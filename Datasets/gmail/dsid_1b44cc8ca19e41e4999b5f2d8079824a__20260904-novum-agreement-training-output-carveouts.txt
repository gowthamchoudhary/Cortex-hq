3) Feedback/Telemetry License- Position: Provide a limited license for telemetry (metrics, error rates, latencies) while avoiding a broad feedback license that would allow commercial reuse of customer outputs.
- Draft: "Customer grants Redwood a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use anonymized telemetry and usage data for the purpose of operating and improving the Services. Such telemetry shall exclude verbatim Customer Output and Customer Input and shall not be used to train models to generate outputs targeted to Customer's data without Customer consent."
