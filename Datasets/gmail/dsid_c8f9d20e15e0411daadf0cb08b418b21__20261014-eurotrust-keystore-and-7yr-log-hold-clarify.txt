Chloe / Priya / Martin —

Technical artifacts we can offer (Dedicated / Private):
- Keystore audit export: CSV/JSON manifest with key_id, rotation_timestamp (ISO 8601), operator_principal (internal id), rotation_reason, and a signature block (SHA256) — available on request and delivered with a signed envelope (GPG) for chain-of-custody.
- Snapshot evidence: We can produce point-in-time snapshots of the tenant audit index and compute a manifest (file hashes + sha256 root) stored in an immutable object store. We can provide the manifest and an audit playbook describing retention and retrieval steps.
- Proof-of-retain: For holds lasting multiple years we recommend periodic (quarterly) retentions snapshots with notarized manifests (we have a partner workflow for timestamping).

Operational notes / constraints:
- Storage and indexing: 7-year tiled retention for high-volume tenants can increase costs substantially and may require sharding/indexing changes that affect retrieval speed. We need to size and price this for EuroTrust based on average daily log volume (DDLV).
- Evidence delivery SLA: We can commit to producing evidence artifacts within 10 business days for Dedicated/Private customers; shorter windows (48–72 hrs) are possible for managed-priority incidents at an added cost.
