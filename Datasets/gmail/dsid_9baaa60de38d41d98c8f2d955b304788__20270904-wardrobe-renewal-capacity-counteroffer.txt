Elena / Lauren — I reproduced the workload from Wardrobe_bakeoff_requirements.pdf against the same model variant we ran in the bakeoff. High-level findings below (full log in the shared Drive):

- Observed behavior: the 503s correlate with concurrent KV-cache eviction under long sequences and an aggressive client retry pattern. When client concurrency spiked above 1,200 active streams, we saw transient connection queueing on the serving layer.
- Tail latency: median=42ms, p95=380ms, p99=1.6s on our test harness; LatticeAI numbers you reported show lower p99 for long sequences but we observed better stability when we apply an adaptive batching config.
- Recommendations to reach 20% uplift:
  * Provision a small dedicated pool (2x the current reserved nodes) for your Black Friday window and enable prefetch + prefix caching.
  * Apply adaptive batching with a 40ms wait window for long-form jobs.
  * Short-term mitigation: add an ingress rate limiter per-tenant to avoid retry storms.

I attached a short proof PDF (Redwood-proof-report-20270902.pdf) with test config, kernel selection, and token-level cost delta. With the dedicated pool and config changes we validated steady-state ~1.4x throughput without higher p99.
