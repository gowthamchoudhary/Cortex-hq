Benchmark credit (example):
- Acceptance benchmark: p95 generation latency under a 1,000 qps synthetic workload <= 80ms across a rolling 24-hour window.
- Measurement & runbook: Redwood runs a joint benchmark during onboarding, results written to an acceptance report (attached as perf-benchmark-report.xlsx).

- Default: monthly retainer (SRE on-call) with guaranteed response windows and 24/7 escalation.
- Swap path: after 6 months of successful SLAs, customer may elect to remove the retainer and instead pay per-incident engineering time at $250/hour with a three-month notice. We can also provide a blended incident cap (e.g., up to 40 hours/month at a discounted rate) as interim.

Discounts & incentives:
- Early conversion (sign within 30 days of pilot): 20% conversion credit on platform fees for year 1.
- Volume anchor (>= 3 site deployments): additional 10% platform fee discount.
