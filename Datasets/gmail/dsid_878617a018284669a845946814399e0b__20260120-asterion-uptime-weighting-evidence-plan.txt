Team — attaching Redwood's response and a short evidence plan (see attachment). Highlights below; full answers are in the attached PDF and the completed worksheet.

1) Uptime definition and weighting- We propose a single blended availability metric per billing month computed as a weighted average across regions where:
   - weight_region = (prod_request_count_region / total_prod_request_count_month) capped with a minimum region floor of 5%
   - blended_availability = sum(weight_region * availability_region)
   - Availability_region = 1 - (service_unavailable_seconds_region / region_total_observable_seconds) where observation excludes scheduled maintenance windows with 72-hour advance notice.

2) Measurement window and sampling- Primary measurement = 30-day rolling window (aligns with your preference). Secondary reference = calendar month for final contract reconciliation if needed.
- Sampling cadence: continuous telemetry captured at 1s resolution in our tracing pipeline; availability sampling uses 30s aggregation (sample window) to reduce transient noise. Latency SLOs reported on request/response times aggregated per minute and then percentile-computed across the 30-day window (p50/p95/p99).
- Warm-up handling: initial 10 minutes after a model instance start are excluded from p99 calculations; KV cache priming is considered part of normal operation (we document expected cold-starts per model profile).

3) Latency commitments (example classes — full matrix in attachment)
- Chat (small context, 512 tokens): p95 ≤ 220ms, p99 ≤ 420ms
- Generation (long context, 4k tokens): p95 ≤ 1.1s, p99 ≤ 2.8s
- Embeddings (single vector): p95 ≤ 180ms, p99 ≤ 350ms

4) Evidence and reconciliation- Accepted evidence: (a) telemetry exports (pre-specified query set + CSV with signed timestamp ranges), (b) client-side synthetic probe bundles signed by Asterion's probe service, (c) sample request traces (redacted) and hash manifests.
- We will provide a packaged evidence bundle monthly on request and within 10 business days of a formal incident claim. Each bundle includes: query results, raw samples (redacted), and a signed manifest for tamper-evidence.

5) Service credit formula (proposed, negotiable)
- Monthly blended_availability >= 99.95%: no credit
- 99.5% ≤ availability < 99.95%: 10% monthly service credit
- 99.0% ≤ availability < 99.5%: 25% monthly service credit
- availability < 99.0%: 50% monthly service credit + joint post-mortem and remediation plan
- Credits applied to the next billing invoice; dispute window 30 days from delivery of evidence bundle, arbitration clause in Master Services Agreement.

6) Private (VPC/on-prem) measurement- We support hybrid reconciliation: Asterion runs probes and pushes signed bundles to a secure S3 endpoint we provide, or optionally we can deploy a lightweight probe collector in your VPC that will forward signed samples. We do not, by default, require customer data to leave the VPC for measurement artifacts — only signed probe metadata and hash manifests are exchanged.

7) Escalation and dispute process- Initial evidence handoff within 10 business days; Redwood response with counter-evidence in 10 business days; joint review meeting within next 5 business days. If unresolved, binding independent auditor (pre-agreed) step within 30 days.
