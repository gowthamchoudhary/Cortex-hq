Marcus — thanks for the traces. Quick notes from runtime:
- Root cause hypothesis: a recent kernel selection heuristic (deployed 2026-03-18) chooses a higher-memory attention kernel for sequences >1024 tokens. For workloads with bursty long prefixes that churn KV caches, that kernel increases temporary working set and on a small subset of hosts it caused transient OOMs and serve exceptions.
- Short-term mitigation steps applied:
  1) Fallback routing: BrightStream routes to rw-9b-v1 (confirmed, now live).
  2) Reverted the new kernel heuristic on hostpool-a to previous selector (rollback planned cluster-wide after validation).
  3) Increased KV cache retention on impacted hosts to reduce churn.

I’ve pushed the routing config (fallback-routing-config.txt) and will monitor. Please note rw-9b-v1 is functionally compatible but has slight latency/cost differences — we informed the customer.

If we remain stable for 2 hours, we’ll roll the rollback cluster-wide and follow with a controlled re-release after adding a guardrail.
