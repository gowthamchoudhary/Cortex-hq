Hi Lydia — thanks for the clear list. Answers below — I also attached our SLO measurement playbook and a short invite file for a read-only Grafana embed we can provision for a time-boxed window.

1) Measurement scope: we compute per-route and per-model SLOs at the service ingress. Uptime is derived from a combination of internal health checks (per-host) and end-to-end synthetic probes at the route level. We aggregate both sources to produce customer-facing availability numbers (route-level dominates).

2) Aggregation/sampling: primary SLO aggregation windows are 1m for raw telemetry, a rolling 5m for short-term smoothing, and hourly rollups for historical SLOs. For high-volume customers we sample at the tagging step (1:10 or configurable) to keep tail-latency telemetry feasible; sampling is applied before aggregation and tagged in the metric payload so sampled spans are traceable.

3) Alerting guidance: typical defaults we recommend — error-rate alert at 0.5% (5xx) over a 5m window; p95 latency alert at route-specific thresholds (for general-purpose routes we suggest 500ms p95 over 5m). We include multi-stage alerts: 5m early-warning, 15m severity, and 60m on-call escalation. These are customizable per-customer as part of Dedicated/Private agreements.

4) Temporary access process: we support time-limited read-only dashboard access (Grafana embed) or a streamed sampling export (S3/secure channel) for up to 30 days. Approval path: procurement request -> security review (existing SOC2/contract review) -> engineering ops (to set sampling policy and provisioning). We can provision a 7-day window on short notice; 30-day windows require a signed MSA addendum. Exceptions are approved by our Security & Compliance lead and the Eng Ops manager.
