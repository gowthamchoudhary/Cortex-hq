Attached is the margin waterfall PDF and two tabs in the model: "UpfrontCredit" and "ConsumptionRebate". High level notes:
- To preserve Pondera's target net, distributor (DataFlow) needs ~8% fee on top of a 12% reseller uplift from list. That yields ~PonderaNet = List * (1 - reseller_uplift - distributor_fee) +/- rounding.

- Clawback language: suggest a 60-day repayment window if committed consumption < 60% of target in Q1. Legal flagged a simpler 30-day CVR but finance prefers 60 to protect against measurement noise.
