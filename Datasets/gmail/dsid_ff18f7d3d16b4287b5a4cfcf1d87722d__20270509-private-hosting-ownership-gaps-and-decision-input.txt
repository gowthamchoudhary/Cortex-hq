Thanks Steph — quick take inline:

- VPN termination vs appliance NAT: customer should own VPN termination into their VPC; we must own the appliance NAT that translates to internal containers. Rationale: security boundary stays with customer; NAT translations and port mapping remain our operational responsibility. This means we need an ops playbook for troubleshooting customer VPNs early.

- IaC templates: Redwood should deliver opinionated IaC (Terraform modules) plus a partner-signed variant for teams who want managed provisioning. Rationale: reduces support churn; partner/pinebrook can maintain one-off templates for larger customers.

- SLA for pilot: suggest 99.5% availability for inference (measured at the gateway) with a 4-hour P1 response window. Notes: full 99.9% needs Dedicated capacity and additional runbook staffing.

I'll update the ownership_matrix_v1.xlsx with these flags and send version 1.1 by EOD.
