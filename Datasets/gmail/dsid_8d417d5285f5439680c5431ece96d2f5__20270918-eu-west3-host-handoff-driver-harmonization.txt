Hi Tom (and Acme ops team),

We wanted to give you a heads-up about a planned, low-impact maintenance window in the eu-west3 region next week. Purpose: to harmonize the NVIDIA driver and control-plane host agent across a subset of host pools to improve compatibility for newer quantized kernels and reduce transient compilation variance.

Planned window (staged):
- Start: 2027-09-20 22:00 UTC
- End: 2027-09-21 04:00 UTC
- We will operate in short, staged host handoffs by host-group (groups of ~80 hosts). Each host-group will undergo a brief drain + driver update; typical eviction time is expected to be under 2 minutes per host.

Potential customer impact:
- Short-lived evictions for running hosted inference sessions (in-flight requests will get automatic retries).
- Best-effort to preserve KV cache for pinned model sessions, but for sessions that are actively migrating there may be a cold-start for a small percentage of requests.
- No scheduled data-plane model redeploys; models pinned to Dedicated pools are unaffected unless you are on a host in a targeted group.

What we are doing to minimize disruption:
- Canary group first (5 hosts) to validate driver + kernel interaction with the serving runtime.
- Rolling handoffs with health checks; automatic rollback on elevated error rates.
- Autoscaling headroom reserved for customer-facing pools during the window.
- We will exclude Dedicated reserved capacity where customers have requested an exemption (see options below).

Actions for Acme:
- If you want an exemption for a specific pool, reply with pool IDs or hit reply-all and we will route to the on-call runbook.
- If you have long-running batch jobs scheduled during the window, consider queuing to outside the window or request a transient exemption.

Hi Tom — quick answers inline, and then proposed mitigation: 

1) KV cache persistence: our goal is to preserve cached KV for pinned sessions where the session is migrated within the same host pool and the serving runtime can attach to the persisted cache. In the handoff case where a session is reconstituted on a different host with a different kernel/driver ABI, there is a ~12% chance of a cold KV miss for pinned sessions during the first 10 minutes after handoff. That's based on recent canaries with the same quantization profiles.

Mitigation: if you proactively tag the sessions/pools (provide pool IDs), we can put them into the exclusion set for the lifecycle so they are not migrated during the staged window.

2) Canary timing: canary will not be run in the peak window for customers who flagged peak times in the schedule CSV. Acme's peak is now noted; we'll move your canary outside 22:15-23:30 UTC.

3) Exemption process & effects: to request an exemption, reply-all with the pool IDs; we will create a temporary exemption ticket. Exempt pools will retain capacity on existing hosts and will not be part of the host-group drain. There is no additional billing for the exemption, but capacity headroom we reserve may limit autoscaling burst during the window if your traffic spikes beyond committed capacity.

If helpful, we can offer a compensated validation canary: we will run an isolated canary against your sample traffic in an adjacent host-group and provide a short report on observed latency and KV hit-rate before we start the full roll. Let me know if you'd like us to schedule that.

I'll coordinate with Marcus for the on-call runbook if you provide pool IDs.

Hi all — final update after the maintenance window completed earlier today: summary below and a short postmortem attached.

Summary of what happened: 
- Maintenance window start: 2027-09-20 22:00 UTC; end: 2027-09-21 03:48 UTC.
- We proceeded through all host-groups as planned and completed the driver harmonization.
- Canary group (5 hosts) validated expected behavior; health checks passed and we proceeded with staged handoffs.

Customer impact observed: 
- Overall, the event completed with no sustained service degradation and no SLOs violated for the region.
- Acme Retail experienced a brief latency spike and a higher-than-expected KV miss rate during the handoff for one of their pinned pools at 22:37 UTC. The spike lasted ~4 minutes and affected ~0.6% of their requests.
- Root cause for the Acme blip: an intermediate autoscaler decision placed traffic onto a host group that was in late-notice draining status due to an edge health-flap. We have a preventative action to tighten the autoscaler drain-aware placement policy (see actions).

Actions & next steps: 
- Immediate: Acme pool was re-pinned and we validated KV cache rebuild within 8 minutes normally. Customer ops were notified in real-time (thank you for quick replies).
- Short-term mitigations: tighten autoscaler decision horizon and disable placement onto groups with >1% recent drain events during maintenance windows.
- Long-term: we will add an additional pre-drain gating check for the control-plane to avoid late-stage placement during handoffs.

Attachments & links: eu-west3-postmortem-summary.pdf (summary), detailed runbook and action tickets (link below).

We apologize for the transient impact to Acme and appreciate the team for flagging it quickly. If you'd like a 20-minute walk-through of the postmortem and the planned mitigations, reply to this thread and we will schedule.
