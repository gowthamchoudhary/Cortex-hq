Anna, Ben — attaching the subprocessor roster and a short ops note. Quick technical clarifications below and a recommended runbook excerpt: 

- Normal operation: compute, KV cache and KMS crypto calls occur in eu-central-1 for EU-pinned customers. We pin by tenancy/cluster and network ACLs prevent outbound replication to other regions unless a failover is triggered.

- Failover trigger & window: failover can be manual (ops) or automatic (health check thresholds). When triggered, we follow the pinned deployment's runbook: 1) promote standby cluster (if available), 2) if standby not available, route traffic to cross-region compatible variant. We aim to resolve within 72 hours; the runbook contains checkpoints at 2h, 8h, 24h, 48h. For Dedicated customers we can commit to 24h.

- KMS specifics: with BYOK, keys remain in the customer region. During cross-region routing we do not export raw keys; instead, we either: a) perform wrapped key ops via a regional proxy, or b) require a temporarily provisioned regional key (customer choice). We note the risk and added time for key material provisioning in the runbook.

- Subprocessors roster & activation order: see attached CSV (subprocessors_roster_v3.csv). Each entry includes role, region, standby_order. During failover we escalate to the next available subprocessor per that standby_order. We can provide escrowed contact points and activation notifications as part of the addendum.

- Audit artifacts: we capture an egress event record with fields: event_id, start_ts, end_ts, source_region, target_region, policy_id, affected_request_ids (sample sanitized). We will include a 1-page sample in the evidence packet.
