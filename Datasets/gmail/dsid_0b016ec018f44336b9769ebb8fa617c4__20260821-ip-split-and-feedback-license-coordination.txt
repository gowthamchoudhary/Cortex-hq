Mateo / Clara —

Quick legal read and suggested approach (internal summary for Mateo, copying Clara for transparency):

1) Output ownership/assignment
- Problem: Blanket assignment of output risks transferring rights to artifacts that are materially tied to Redwood models or preexisting components.
- Proposal: Redwood will grant Skybound an exclusive, perpetual, worldwide right to use, reproduce, and sublicense customer-specific "Deliverables" (defined in the order form) for their productization purposes. We will resist assignment of underlying model or model weights. Suggested clause: "Customer Deliverables shall be owned by Customer; Redwood retains all underlying model rights and preexisting materials. Customer shall receive perpetual, exclusive rights in Deliverables as defined in Exhibit X."

2) Training/data use
- Problem: Unlimited training permission is a regulatory and product risk.
- Proposal: Default: No training on Customer Data. Exception: Customer may opt-in to a specific, time-bound, purpose-limited training license. If agreed, telemetry will be restricted to aggregated and de-identified metrics. Add an audit right limited to scope and frequency.

3) Feedback license
- Problem: Feedback is useful for product improvement; mutual license is reasonable but must exclude resale as a standalone product (i.e., no selling of customer feedback as insights to third parties).

Next steps: I attached a response redline with compromise language: "Redwood_response_redline.docx". If Skybound can accept the Deliverables definition approach and the opt-in training carveout, we should be able to finalize the order form with a short signature path.
