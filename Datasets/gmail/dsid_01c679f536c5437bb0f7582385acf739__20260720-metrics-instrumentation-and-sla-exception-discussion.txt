Hi Olivia —

Thanks for the clear list. I'm attaching our formal responses and a small CSV export of the metrics you asked about. High-level answers below — happy to jump on a quick call tomorrow morning if you'd like to go line-by-line.

1) SLO definitions: 
- Uptime SLO: 99.95% availability measured as successful request rate (2xx/3xx) over a rolling 30-day window. Outage is defined as service availability under 99.0% over any rolling 60-minute window for core inference endpoints.
- Latency SLOs: p95 <= 280ms and p99 <= 900ms for requests with <1024 token input on the standard hosted tier; for long context variants we provide separate SLOs noted in the doc. Measurements are per-customer aggregated at the ingress proxy layer before load-balancing.
- Error budget policy: we consume error budget on any availability or latency breach per the measurement rules in the attached PDF.

2) Monitoring & access: 
- Stack: Prometheus (metric ingestion), Cortex (long-term metrics), Grafana (dashboards), and an internal tracing layer (Jaeger-compatible).
- Customer access: read-only Grafana dashboard and a metrics export (CSV for last 90 days) by default for enterprise customers. We can provide a scoped Prometheus remote_read endpoint under an approved MSP agreement for Dedicated/Private plans.
- Sampling & retention: 1s scrape for ingress latency counters, aggregated to 10s series stored for 90 days, 1y for summarized hourly rollups. Logs are retained 30 days; full request traces 14 days.

3) SLA exception & credits: 
- Exception approval: Customer success + legal sign-off for credits; engineering provides incident analysis. For temporary exceptions (e.g., planned migration), we accept formal exception requests with at least 7 business days' notice.
- Credit calculation: total seconds of unavailability beyond the SLO window, normalized to monthly committed units, multiplied by the monthly fee to produce a prorated credit. Example calculation is in the PDF.
- Escalation: level-1 support -> CS -> engineering on-call -> SRE review.

Hi Olivia — looping in a few implementation details from the instrumentation team. Short answers: 

- Measurement point: latency SLOs are measured at the server-side ingress (our LB edge) after TLS termination but before model runtime. This excludes client-side rendering and upstream CDN caching variance. Network transit inside cloud regions is included in the measurement. We also publish tail-latency traces so you can correlate client observations to server timelines.

- RBAC & tokenization: We provide a tokenized, time-bound Grafana link scoped to your org and routes (read-only). Role options: org_admin_read, eng_read, billing_read. You can request additional scopes for Dedicated/Private where we map to your IAM.

- Exception form & timeline: submit the attached SLA Exception Request Form (filled and signed by your CS or Procurement). We aim for a 5-business-day review for standard exceptions; expedited reviews (2 business days) are possible with an on-call SRE escalation. Approved exceptions are recorded and can be annotated to the monthly SLO reports to avoid retro-active credit triggers.
