Hi Arielle — Vanessa asked me to provide the technical appendix. Quick answers to the questions you raised: 

- Processor role: confirmed, Redwood will act as a processor under the DPA.
- Storage & encryption: Data at rest is AES-256 encrypted. For hosted customers we store in the selected cloud region(s) (US-West-2 or US-East-1 by default). We offer customer-managed keys via AWS KMS/HSM integrations; we also support Bring Your Own Key for Dedicated and Private deployments.
- Transit: All RPCs and API endpoints enforce TLS1.2+; internal service traffic uses mTLS.
- PHI handling: Our platform does not de-identify or re-identify content by default. If Santorini elects to store PHI, we recommend using customer-managed KMS and a Dedicated or Private deployment for additional isolation and contractual controls.
- Subprocessors: We use a small set of cloud providers and CDN partners; full subprocessors list is published in our DPA. Any subprocessor with potential access to unredacted customer content is listed there — we can call out the ones that apply to Santorini's chosen region.
- Retention & deletion: Default retention for transient inference logs is 30 days; customers can configure shorter retention or opt-out of logging. Permanent artifacts (model snapshots, backups) follow our backup retention policy; we can provide the exact retention schedule in the mapping.

I’ve put together a short mapping table and a bullet list you can paste into the DPIA (see attached). If you want we can redact the system diagram to highlight only the flows that involve Redwood.
