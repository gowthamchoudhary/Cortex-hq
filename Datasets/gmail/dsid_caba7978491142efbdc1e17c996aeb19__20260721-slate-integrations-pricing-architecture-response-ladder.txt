Jacob, copying some technical detail here to help procurement and your infra team. High level tradeoffs: operator license vs platform fee

- Operator license (per-site): best when you need total air-gapped control or local GPU licensing. Lower per-month run cost at scale but upfront operator license and on-site install/support.
- Platform fee (control plane & observability): pays for hosted control plane features, rollouts, RBAC, telemetry retention. Easier for multi-site because we handle routing, rollouts, and tracing out of the box.

Recommended pilot configuration for Slate (based on yesterday's usage patterns):
- 2 x A4000-equivalent nodes (dedicated) with KV cache warmers
- Platform fee: can be amortized across the pilot (we apply a credit if you convert)
- Support: Priority (1-hr P1) during pilot; add 24/7 in conversion phase if desired

Notes on credits & discounts:
- We can include a one-time implementation credit (applied as invoice credit) for pilots that sign a 12-month committed renewal.
- For multi-region/burst, we recommend a burst credit bucket rather than on-demand overprovisioning. That can be priced as an add-on.

I've attached a technical appendix in the pricing sheet (tab: recommended-config) and a short SOW excerpt for install tasks. If your infra team wants, I can put a short runbook together for node sizing during the pilot.
