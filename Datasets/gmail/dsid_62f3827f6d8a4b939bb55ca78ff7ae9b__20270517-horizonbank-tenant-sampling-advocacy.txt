Quick engineering readout after a 30-min sync with infra and runtime leads:

Summary: Feasible with a scoped implementation, estimated 3 sprints (6 weeks) for an MVP that covers:
- A tenant predicate in the runtime that allows per-tenant sampling policies (0-100%) and a whitelist for "full capture" IDs.
- A filtered export hook to S3 with tenant metadata and per-export manifest. We would reuse existing export pipeline but need ACL metadata and retention flags.

Risks / caveats:
- Backfill/retention plumbing for 7-year retention is heavy; for MVP we can provide export guarantees and hand the export object to the customer's archive (they manage long-term storage).
- Need security review for ACLs & KMS usage (2-3 business days).
- This will require a canary rollout and extra e2e telemetry tests (adds ~1 sprint for QA).

Option A (fast): 6 weeks, limited support for backfill; we deliver a feature-flagged path for Horizon only, with on-prem-like export to their S3 and handover docs. We mark it as "partner-exception" rollout.
Option B (robust): 10-12 weeks, includes integration with long-term retention and audit automation.
