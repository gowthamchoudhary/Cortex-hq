Emma — thanks for the numbers, that's helpful. A few technical clarifications and what we'll change in the matrix: 

- Admission strategy: Redwood uses a hybrid approach by default: prefix/KV cache hits are prioritized, admission controller applies a fast-path token-budget check (drop/429) only when the burst band is exhausted; otherwise we favor queueing with a max queue latency budget of 150ms (configurable).
