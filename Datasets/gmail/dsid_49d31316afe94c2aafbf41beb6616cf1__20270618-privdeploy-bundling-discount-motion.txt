Proposed commercial structure (initial):
- Term: 36 months (preferred for meaningful amortization)

- Operator licensing: two options — (A) per-GPU seat (recommended for early pilots) or (B) site license (flat for unlimited operator access across one site). We will size operator license based on the GPU pod count you shared in the RFP.
- Support tiers: Standard (24x5), Priority (24x7, 4 hr P1), Enterprise (24x7 + named SRE retainer and runbook ops hours).

Indicative pricing and discount framework (for discussion):
- Base offer: platform fee + operator per-GPU = list pricing per our ratecard.
- Package discount: 15% off platform fee if you pick the site-license model and commit to 36 months + minimum 2-site deployment.
- Pilot credit: $50k credit on platform fee applied against first-year invoices if you sign pilot SOW within 30 days.
- Onboarding SOW: we can bundle a one-time onboarding block (40 hours of SRE onboarding) at a 25% discount if executed with agreement signature.

Optional concession (internal approval needed, quick call to discuss):
- If Helio signs a 36-month commit with an annual committed spend >= $1.2M, we can increase platform discount to 22% and include one quarter of named SRE retainer for free during year 1 (up to 120 hours).

Operational notes / escalation guardrails:
- Burst capacity: we can include a burst clause (pay-as-you-go GPU hours) to avoid lockout during peak months.
- Data residency & audit: SOC2 evidence and audit logs are included in the Private package; additional compliance attachments can be added as a line item.

Pricing mechanics we should be explicit about
- Platform fee amortization: define invoice schedule and a simple explanatory line in the term sheet: “Platform fee may be invoiced annually or amortized across term; Redwood will provide an invoice schedule to support capital accounting.” That reduces procurement pushback.

Support tier language to include
- Response times, escalation path, and included runbook creation hours per tier. For Enterprise we will include 1 named SRE (up to 40 hours/month) in year 1 only (unless retainer purchased).
