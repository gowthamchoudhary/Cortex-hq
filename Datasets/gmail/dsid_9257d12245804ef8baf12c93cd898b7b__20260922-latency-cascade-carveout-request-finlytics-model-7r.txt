- Enable automated fallback gate for model-7r -> model-7r-lite for requests with rolling p95 > 400ms (20% initial traffic split).
- Temporarily lower batch window to 8ms for impacted pools (reduces batching-induced tail compaction).

Medium plan (30m–3h):
- If p95 remains > 400ms after fallback, trigger traffic shift: move 60% of FinLytics requests to model-7r-lite and limit max seq length to 512 for their sessions.
- Run quick quantization sanity check on v2.1 to ensure no kernel fallback path introduced a regression.
