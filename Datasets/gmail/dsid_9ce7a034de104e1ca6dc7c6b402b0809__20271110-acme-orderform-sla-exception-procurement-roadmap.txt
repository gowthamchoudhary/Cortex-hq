Draft response below (I tried to balance commercial and operational risk):

-- Proposed SLA language (short-form) --
Maintenance windows: Customer may schedule up to one (1) Maintenance Window per calendar quarter not to exceed six (6) consecutive hours. Maintenance Windows must be scheduled with at least forty-eight (48) hours prior written notice. Redwood will not schedule Maintenance Windows during scheduled enterprise-wide holidays as set forth in Exhibit A. Emergency maintenance required to address critical security vulnerabilities or stop-the-presses incidents remains excluded from this limitation.

Service credits: For unplanned downtime, Customer will be eligible to receive Service Credits as set forth below. Notwithstanding the foregoing, aggregate Service Credits payable in any single billing month shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of Customer's then-current monthly fees ("Monthly Credit Cap"). Service Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for downtime.

Response times: Critical incidents: initial response within one (1) hour and continuous engagement until mitigation; High: initial response within four (4) hours; Medium/Low: next business day.

-- end draft --

Thanks Marissa — reviewed. Legal comments in-line and high-level notes below: 

1) Monthly Credit Cap: Legal is comfortable with a 10% Monthly Credit Cap, provided we add an explicit definition that Service Credits are calculated based on the monthly recurring fees for the affected Service and exclude usage-based overages. This avoids surprises if they have burst consumption.

2) Maintenance window: Accept quarterly request as drafted but add a requirement that Redwood will provide two mutually acceptable scheduling options within 5 business days of request. Also add language that prohibits back-to-back quarterly windows affecting the same calendar month (i.e., no two windows within a 60-day span) to prevent churn.

3) Emergency maintenance carve-out: keep as-is but add notification requirements (notice within 24 hours post-event where possible) and a brief postmortem within five business days for any emergency maintenance that impacts customer availability.

4) Response SLA: Suggest tightening Critical incident engagement to 30 minutes initial ack and a 1-hour continuous engagement target for high-severity customers (e.g., those on Dedicated). Propose we tie that tighter target to customers on Dedicated or customers who have purchased an Enhanced Support add-on.

I added these as tracked changes in redlines_sla_v1.docx. Commercial should decide if we want to make the faster response a conditional term (recommended) or a blanket promise (risky).

Hi Katie — thanks for the note and for surfacing Acme's preference. We discussed this internally and propose the language below for inclusion in the Order Form. This is intended to keep your team operationally comfortable while keeping our platform commitments realistic.

-- Proposed counter (to be inserted into Order Form) --
1) Maintenance Windows: Customer may request up to one (1) Maintenance Window per calendar quarter not to exceed six (6) consecutive hours. Maintenance Windows must be requested with at least forty-eight (48) hours prior written notice and Redwood will provide two proposed dates/times within five (5) business days of request. Redwood will not schedule Maintenance Windows on the list of enterprise holidays in Exhibit A, and will not schedule two Maintenance Windows within any sixty (60) day period. Emergency maintenance remains excluded and will be subject to a post-event notice and a five (5) business day postmortem.

2) Service Credits: Service Credits for unplanned downtime will be calculated as set out in Exhibit B. Aggregate Service Credits in any calendar month shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the Customer's then-current monthly recurring fees ("Monthly Credit Cap"). Service Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy. Service Credits are calculated only against recurring subscription fees and do not apply to usage-based charges or one-time fees.

3) Response Times: For Critical incidents Redwood will provide initial acknowledgement within thirty (30) minutes for Dedicated customers or those with Enhanced Support; otherwise initial acknowledgement within one (1) hour. Continuous engagement until mitigation for Critical incidents is the standard. High: initial response within four (4) hours; Medium/Low: next business day.
