Evelyn / all,

Root cause (initial findings): during the June 24–27 rollout of the new request router we enabled a temporary mirror mode to validate traffic parity. A bug in the mirror deduplication logic meant mirrored requests were being written to the billing ledger with a distinct session_id instead of the "mirror_tag" flag. Downstream billing matched on session_id, so mirrored requests were counted as additional customer requests for approximately 28 hours on June 25. Impact: ~2x counts for affected endpoints for that 28 hour window for a subset of customers (Starlit.ai included).

Actions we've taken so far:
- Stopped mirror mode and rolled back router config (June 27 02:14 UTC).
- Identified affected ledger entries and marked them as candidate duplicates (internal flag).
- Generated list of affected accounts and event timestamps (attached: affected_customers-june-window.csv).
- Drafted incident summary for customers (attached: incident_summary-ledger-mirroring.pdf).

Estimated financial impact for Starlit.ai: $12,345.67 (rounding to nearest cent) as excess charges on INV-2027-06-ST. Recommendation: issue credit for that amount and send a formal post-incident note with assurance steps (fix and monitoring).
