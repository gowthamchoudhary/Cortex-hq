Samir —

Appreciate the clear summary. Looping in Ben (sales lead) and Lauren (Dedicated product) so we can get a concise answer and a proposed carve-out.

High-level answers up front:
- Reserved GPUs: by default, reserved capacity is designed to avoid involuntary preemption in the firm's committed window. However, there are edge cases during severe capacity exhaustion where we route to preemptible pools unless a carve-out is negotiated.
- Fallback routing: we can route to a lower-precision (4-bit/8-bit) variant in the same region; that usually preserves median latency but can increase token generation time by 10–25% depending on prompt length.
- Short bursts: our runtime supports fine-grained queuing and burst tokens; the tail depends on concurrency and whether requests can be batched. Expect 95th percentile latency to degrade first; we can provide modeled numbers after looking at your traffic shape.

Samir,

Following up with a short proposal attached and a few clarifying Qs below.

Proposal highlights (see attached Dedicated_SLA_Draft_Stellarus_v2.pdf):
- No involuntary preemption for reserved pool during business window, subject to a 15% uplift on committed capacity (to cover headroom and multi-tenant variance).
- If we detect an emergent hardware failure or region-wide outage, requests will failover to a regional compatible variant; in that case we commit to an end-to-end 95th percentile latency target of <= 450ms for small chat/QA requests (<256 tokens) and a mitigation credit equal to 10% of monthly dedicated fees for SLA misses.
- Queueing behavior: short bursts will be smoothed by a combination of request shaping and automatic short-window autoscaling. For 200 concurrent 1–2s calls, modeled 95th latency is ~380–520ms depending on batching and model size. We'll include a small traffic-shaping header option you can use to declare high-priority flows.

Clarifying questions:
1) Can you share a representative 24‑hour request histogram for your busiest API key? We only need a sample to model headroom.
2) Are you open to a grace-tier credit (50% credit for first month of production errors) to accelerate go-live while we tune autoscaling?
