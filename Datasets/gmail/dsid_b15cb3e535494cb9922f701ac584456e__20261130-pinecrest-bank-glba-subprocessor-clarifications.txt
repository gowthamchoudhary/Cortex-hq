Hi all,

I've drafted a redline of our standard DPA to address the GLBA appendix. Key points we proposed:

- Data residency: we agreed to add a clause that for GLBA accounts Redwood will maintain processing within US regions unless customer requests an exception in writing. (This covers hosted region pinning for logs and inference.)
- Subprocessor disclosures: we'll provide an up-to-date matrix and 30-day notification window for new subprocessors that materially affect GLBA workloads.
- Audit rights: we can provide SOC 2 report and a scoped inspection protocol; for most customers SOC 2 + attestations suffice, but for high-risk regulated customers we will consider read-only auditor access on a case-by-case basis.
- Liability/indemnity: standard limits will remain but we will add narrow language around confidentiality obligations specific to financial data.

Open items I'd like Pinecrest to confirm before finalizing the addendum:
1) Do you require the subprocessor matrix to include vendor contract start/end dates and hosting countries?
