Hi OliveTech team — welcome and thanks for the quick follow-up. Portal-first is our preference for two reasons: 1) automated matching to POs and 2) faster AP routing. Please register your billing user at https://vendor.redwood.com/signup and use the portal for regular submissions.

Fallback rules (email):
- Send to ap-invoices@redwood.com with subject: INVOICE|PO-{PO_NUMBER}|{VENDOR_NAME}|{YYYYMMDD} (example: INVOICE|PO-45321|OliveTech|20271115)
- Attach a single PDF invoice; include the PO number in both the subject and the invoice body (top-right).
- Required invoice fields: invoice number, invoice date, PO number, line-item description, qty, unit price, currency, tax id (if applicable), and remit address.
- Line-items: we accept both consolidated (single invoice with multiple lines) and per-shipment invoices. For automated matching, include a column with our internal cost center if you have it — we can map later.

PO stamping / vendor-ack: procurement (cc'd Karthik) manages activation. Most vendors are activated in 3–5 business days after onboarding form + W9 / bank details. We do not require a physical stamp; we do require that the PO number on the invoice exactly matches the PO provided in the portal/email to allow auto-apply.

I attached our lightweight onboarding invoice template (fillable). If you can export to PDF from that template and name the file per the subject token above, AP processing is usually same-day once the vendor is active. If you cannot use the portal immediately, send the first two invoices by email as a fallback and then switch to portal — please note that emailed invoices may require manual matching and can take an extra 2–3 days to clear.

Let me know if you need SFTP credentials or an alternate ingest (we occasionally enable SFTP for high-volume partners).
