- Redwood sends initial SOW + standard terms: Day 0

- Finalization and signature: target within 10 business days after first redline iteration, subject to complexity

Thanks, Rachel & Sally. Adding a couple of operational notes from the engineering side and flagging our legal/terms owner:

Operational/technical:
- Week 0: data access and connectivity checklist (we’ll need a short data flow diagram and a dedicated test bucket). Marissa will own onboarding tasks.
- Week 2: smoke tests (latency and end-to-end ingest) — I’ll post the test scripts in the shared drive.
- Week 5: quality sign-off — we’ll run the agreed eval set and produce a short report.

Contracting / redlines: Olga Petrov (olga_petrov@redwood.com) is our primary contracts attorney for this region and will handle redlines. Typical process from our side:
1) We accept tracked redlines on the SOW and standard terms; Olga will reply with a cover email summarizing any commercial exceptions.
2) For standard pilot SOWs, we try to resolve the first round of redlines within 5 business days. If there are material legal requests (data residency, indemnity changes), that can add another 3–7 days depending on the counterparty.
3) If Helios has mandatory clause language, please put those in a short doc or list so Olga can triage quickly.

I’ll add the test scripts link and a short checklist to the MAP tab once we agree to a call time.
