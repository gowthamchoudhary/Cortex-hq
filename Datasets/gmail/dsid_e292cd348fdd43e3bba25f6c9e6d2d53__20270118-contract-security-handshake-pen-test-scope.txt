Jared / Tessa / Laura — I reviewed the questionnaire overnight and drafted a redline for the Security Exhibit and the Order Form. High-level notes up front:

- SOC 2: Redwood will provide SOC 2 Type II report on an annual basis; customers may request read-only access to the report under NDA. We will not provide control-level evidence on demand; instead we offer mapping and point-in-time artifacts per the runbook.
- Pen test: we accept third-party pen-tests with 30 days prior notice. Tests must be non-destructive, and Redwood requires a scoped statement of work and a single point of contact. We propose a mutually agreed 5-business-day notice for operational blackout windows.
- Audit rights: propose language that limits on-site audits to once per 12 months and requires use of Redwood-approved auditors for sensitive systems; for remote evidence collection we propose a structured cadence rather than ad-hoc requests.
