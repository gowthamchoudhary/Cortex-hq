We joined the call. Quick triage summary for the thread:

- Root cause (prelim): KV-cache miss amplification when larger-than-expected batched prompts hit a subset of nodes that had recently rebalanced shards. The miss rate increased backend work per request by ~3x for affected sequences.
- Contributing factors: recent autoscaler tweak that reduced buffer headroom; safety fallback routing that didn't warm KV caches; request-size distribution spike due to Copperline's promotion.
- Immediate mitigations taken: temporarily routed Copperline to a dedicated CPU/GPU slice; temporarily increased autoscaler headroom; manually warmed caches for the tenant shard.
- Customer impact: throughput drop ~35-45% for 40-50 minutes; elevated 5xx during fallback; no data loss.

Attaching extracted token-level slices and runtime logs. Next steps: full RCA doc, exec one-pager for Copperline leadership, and an internal checklist for changes to autoscaler safety thresholds.
