Hi all — quick technical note on the AWQ / GPTQ / FP8 tradeoffs and a suggested A/B plan: 

Short summary
- AWQ-4: lowest compilation friction for smaller models and best latency delta in our hosted stacks because it maps well to our optimized int kernels. Risk: for long-context prompts it can change tokenization-side rounding behavior; we should test for that specifically in your long encounter notes.
- GPTQ-4: typically preserves logits ordering better across head outputs; slightly less throughput than AWQ in our infra but usually safer for extraction tasks where boundary tokens matter.
- FP8 (mixed-precision): best fidelity to FP16, but requires GPU drivers + runtime flags; on some older SKUs FP8 falls back to software paths which kills throughput.

Numbers from our recent internal runs (illustrative, same infra family we'll use for your pilot)
- Llama-2-13B FP16: entity-extract F1=0.947, latency p95=240ms, tokens/sec=1800
- Llama-2-13B AWQ-4: F1=0.939 (-0.8 pts), latency p95=160ms, tokens/sec=3100
- Llama-2-13B GPTQ-4: F1=0.943 (-0.4 pts), latency p95=180ms, tokens/sec=2800
- Falcon-40B FP16: F1=0.956, latency p95=360ms, tokens/sec=1200
- Falcon-40B FP8: F1=0.952 (-0.4 pts), latency p95=210ms (on supported SKUs), tokens/sec=2600

Proposed evaluation matrix (we'll run all configs on your 5k sample):
- Models: Llama-2-13B, Mistral-Instruct-7B (if you prefer smaller), Falcon-40B-Instruct (for highest quality check)
- Quantization: FP16 baseline, AWQ-4, GPTQ-4, FP8 where supported
- Metrics: entity precision/recall per type, F1, latency p50/p95, cost per 1k tokens, flagged regression samples (examples we will attach)

Safety/quality guardrails
- Any model/quant config that reduces precision on high-priority entities by > 1.5 pts will be flagged as FAIL for clinician workflows; we'll surface exact sample failures and backtrace logits where useful.
