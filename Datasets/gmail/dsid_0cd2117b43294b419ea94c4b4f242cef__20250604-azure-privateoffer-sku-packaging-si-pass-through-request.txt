Maria — quick note from channel/ops side.

We typically preserve a 12–18% channel margin in Azure private offers when a reseller/SI is involved (this is not a public rule — it varies by deal economics). Mechanically we can do one of two things: 

1) Redwood invoices Aurelia for the net amount (Private offer price less the reseller fee), and we pay BrightLine separately per a partner disbursement agreement; or
2) Redwood invoices Aurelia for the full committed amount and passes an explicit invoice line to the SI for clarity (BrightLine issues its own invoice to Aurelia).

Option 1 keeps the financial flow simple from Aurelia's POV (one payment to Redwood), but requires a partner disbursement/commission agreement. Option 2 provides the clearest audit trail for your teams (they can see the SI line on the same invoice).

If Aurelia's AP needs SI to appear on the invoice itself for compliance, we recommend Option 2 and will add the pass-through line with matching evidence attached.
