Thanks for the clear asks and the redline. Quick responses below and a suggested approach:

- Audit logs vs telemetry: We can support a two-tier retention model. Proposed contract language: "Customer audit logs (as defined in Appendix A) will be retained for up to 24 months. Operational telemetry and debug traces will be retained per a configurable TTL (default: 45 days) unless Customer requests an extended retention period via a written service order."

- Onshore backup: We offer an onshore backup option as part of our Dedicated/Private plans. For Hosted customers we can provide an onshore restore copy held for disaster recovery with the following caveat: it is a DR copy used only for recovery and not for regular processing. Proposed clause to avoid double-pricing: "Redwood will maintain a geographically-restricted DR copy (US-region) for Customer, at no additional charge for the first 30 days following a declared incident; continued onshore custody beyond 30 days is available as an optional add-on at a mutually agreed rate."

I think this preserves Helix's control needs without creating perpetual dual-billing. If that sounds acceptable I can draft specific DPA language and mark the redline.
