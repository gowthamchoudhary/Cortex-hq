Confirming technical details for the debug snapshot/export flow:
- Retention: default automatic purge after 30 days; 90-day retention only with documented incident ticket and approval from security;
- Storage: encrypted-at-rest with customer-scoped KMS keys for Private customers; for Hosted customers we use Redwood-managed KMS with customer-access review;
- Access: role-based access via central IAM; debug snapshots require explicit SRE/Support approval and are logged in the audit trail;
- Re-identification: snapshots are treated as PII-containing by default; we redact/obfuscate where possible and label as sensitive; any manual re-identification requires approval and POA (purpose of access) in ticket.
