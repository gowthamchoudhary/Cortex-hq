- Impact: ~6-9% of generation requests to the affected variant experienced >1.5x token latency; some upstream clients retried and timed out. No data exfiltration.

- 2026-09-24 16:07:12 — First elevated L2 cache miss rate observed on pool-34 (serving hosts: rwd-srv-34a,b).
- 2026-09-24 16:12:47 — Autoscaling triggered but placement on new hosts experienced slower KV warmup due to a regression in rehydration backoff (recent change rolled 09-22).
- 2026-09-24 16:18:55 — Customer-facing error rate and retries increased; our router began diverting traffic to backup variants but warmup penalty persisted.
- 2026-09-24 16:42:10 — Mitigation: we disabled the rehydration backoff on pool-34 and initiated a targeted cache pre-warm; tail latency normalized over the next 6–15 minutes.
- 2026-09-24 16:50:00 — Monitoring shows return to baseline.

Actions taken (already completed):
- Disabled new rehydration backoff on impacted pool.
- Applied targeted cache pre-warm and adjusted kernel selection to reduce per-request setup work for the short term.
