Root cause details (short):
- A recent optimization change (internal deployment window 06:00–07:50 UTC) modified the kernel shape inference for quantized attention. For inputs above specific lengths the inference logic misclassified the layout and chose a generic attention path which requires additional per-step recompiles and causes KV cache fragmentation under our continuous-batching regime. The result is repeated small stalls that amplify with sequence length.
