Hi Asha — thanks for the dataset and the heads-up. We dug into the report and ran a short internal simulation against three runway scenarios (steady, seasonal-peaks, and high-burst). Highlights here:

- Root cause: short (2–8 minute) fan-outs during business-hour model retrains and customer demo windows cause KV cache churn and reduce effective concurrent slots by ~18–24% during peaks.
- Short-term recommendation: Add a 10–GPU top-up pool (RTX-equivalent) that is billed at a discounted burst rate and auto-attaches during defined peak windows. This maintains your committed slab but gives headroom for the p95 latency SLO.
- Long-term: Introduce KV-prefetching on the model serving path + prefix caching we can enable; that reduces peak token cost by ~12% and lowers the size of a required top-up.

I've attached a one-page sketch and a pricing delta we can fold into an amendment. If that sounds okay, we'll draft a formal amendment with staggered billing (commit stays, burst pays-as-used with a monthly cap).
