Olga / Marissa — thanks for the context. Below is a succinct legal approach that typically closes quickly:

Proposed compromise (redline language):
"Notwithstanding the foregoing, either party may seek temporary injunctive relief, provisional remedies, or other equitable relief in the state or federal courts located in San Francisco, California, without first submitting the dispute to arbitration, solely for the purpose of preserving rights relating to: (a) infringement or misappropriation of intellectual property; (b) protection of confidential information or customer data; (c) immediate cessation of conduct that threatens irreparable harm to a party and its customers. All other disputes arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware under JAMS rules, and the arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding."

Rationale: this preserves rapid access to courts for injunctive relief (we generally ask for a district/county near Redwood ops) while giving BlueOak the arbitration predictability they want for monetary claims. If BlueOak pushes back on San Francisco, we can accept "Northern District of California" or "Santa Clara County" as alternative.
