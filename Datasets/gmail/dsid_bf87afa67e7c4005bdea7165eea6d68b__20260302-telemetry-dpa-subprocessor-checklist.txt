Hi Sofia — thanks for the clear list. Short answers up front, then details and next steps below. I attached a succinct cookie map and a DPA redline that highlights the telemetry/marketing clauses.

Quick summary: 
- Console telemetry (API call logs, per-account usage metrics, and operational traces) is treated as product/operational data and is NOT shared with marketing analytics vendors. That data is retained per our customer-configured retention policies (default 30 days for detailed traces, 13 months for aggregated billing metrics).
- Website tracking & marketing pixels (site visitors, marketing forms) are considered marketing telemetry and are sent to our marketing vendors. Those vendors are listed as subprocessors and are covered by our DPA.
- We do not send account-level PII to marketing vendors. Where we send identifiers (e.g., hashed user IDs for conversion tracking), those are pseudonymous, salted hashes; retention is 90 days by default.
- For EU transfers: Redwood relies on SCCs for subprocessors outside the EEA and can adopt additional contractual safeguards if required. We can also support customer-requested Data Processing Amendments for specific controls.

Hi Sofia — quick technical confirm from the infra side. 

- Console telemetry is ingested into our customer-dedicated namespaces where possible; logs that include sensitive fields are redacted before any downstream pipeline. The operational path for Console metrics does not route to our marketing analytics stack. There is a separate pipeline for marketing events generated from our public website.
- Regarding hashed IDs: we use HMAC with a rotating salt for any identifier sent to marketing vendors; we do not append account email addresses or un-hashed identifiers. That said, if Acme wants all marketing events suppressed entirely, we can toggle that at the tenant level and include the toggle in the DPA addendum.
- Subprocessors: the canonical, regularly-updated list is at the link Camila included. We add or remove subprocessors with 30 days' notice; emergency short-notice changes are handled with the customer success team and we provide mitigation write-ups.
