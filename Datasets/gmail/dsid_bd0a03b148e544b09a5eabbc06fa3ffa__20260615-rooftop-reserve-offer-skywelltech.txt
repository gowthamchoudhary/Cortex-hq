Option A — Pilot / Warm Start
- Cluster: 8x A100-equivalent GPUs (single zone)

Option C — Production / High Availability
- Cluster: 128x A100-equivalent GPUs (multi-AZ footprint)
- Minimum guaranteed: 96 GPUs
- Use case: enterprise SLAs, multi-region failover, steady high QPS
- Ramp: phased over 8–12 weeks (can be compressed with upfront commitment)
- Burst: guaranteed hot spare pool + predefined surge credits for incidents

Activation notes:
- Warm-starting reduces token burn and cold-cache latency; we recommend a 2–3 week warmup before peak traffic.
- We include a one-time tuning window for batching and kernel selection after model pin.
