Claire — really appreciate the concise list. Attached are two files: a proposal PDF that shows our recommended commercial paths and an Audit Log tiering spreadsheet with sample math. High level:

Option A — Slab (recommended for pilots that want predictable cost and lower procurement friction)
- Audit logs: $8k/mo slab for up to 100M events; add-on tiers at $5k increments. Retention add-on (7yr): +$1.5k/mo.
- KMS/HSM: $6k/mo per HSM seat (includes key rotation support); or $2k/mo per-seat lightweight KMS with shared HSM.
- Residency: US-only regional bundle $3k/mo; active failover add-on $1.2k/mo.
- HIPAA attestation: one-time paperwork + readiness package $12k (can amortize over 12 months as $1k/mo).

Option B — Usage (better if volume is uncertain)
- Audit logs: $0.02 per event ingested; retention priced per GB-month: $0.05/GB-month.
- KMS/HSM: per-request crypto API surcharge $0.0005 / crypto op; HSM-seat still available as optional commit.
- Residency: per-GiB egress + $0.01/GB storage surcharge.

Pilot -> Production conversion approach:
- Offer a pilot credit equal to 50% of the first 3 months of slab charges (if they commit to 12-month post-pilot).
- If they pick usage during pilot and convert to slab within 90 days of pilot end, provide a one-time credit equal to 25% of the first 6 months.

Internal — quick suggested approach for Monday call / proposal revision:

1) Hybrid pricing (recommended): slab baseline for audit logs up to 80M events (we set slab slightly below expected pilot peak so Mediflow gets predictability but we still monetize spikes). Overage at $0.015/event. This maps to an effective blended rate that sits mid-way between the slab and usage rows in Ava's spreadsheet.

2) HSM flexibility: offer a small monthly HSM-seat token ($1k/mo) + per-request surcharge for heavy crypto ops. Cap monthly per-request charges at 150% of expected baseline; if they hit cap we'll pause and require a short sync (protects them and us).

3) HIPAA paperwork: split $12k as $6k upfront + $6k on go-live. For pilot we can amortize the $6k as a $500 credit/month over 12 months if they commit to 12-month post-pilot.

5) Negotiation levers: escrow of evidence delivery (reduced HIPAA fee), shortened retention windows (reduces audit-log storage), and optional SLO waivers during pilot.
