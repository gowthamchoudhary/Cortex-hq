Hi Alicia —

Appreciate the submitted docs — I pulled them in. A few quick notes and proposed next steps so we're aligned: 

1) Standard terms: Redwood's default payment term is Net30. That helps with cash forecasting across our hosted and dedicated supplier base.

2) Exceptions: We can consider Net45 for strategic suppliers or to match a customer's AR cycle, but it requires a short approval path (Procurement + Finance manager sign-off).

3) PO & invoicing flow:
   - Vendor profile creation in our vendor portal: 3–5 business days once we have onboarding docs + bank remittance.
   - PO generation: typically within 1 business day after vendor activation; PO number must appear on invoices.
   - Cutoff: AP runs two weekly disbursements; invoice posting is continuous but payments hit the next scheduled run.

4) Early-pay: We do offer early-pay incentives (vendor-side opt-in). Typical structure: 1–2% discount if invoice paid within 10 calendar days. Treasury can present formal terms if you opt in.
