Hi Liam — thanks for the spreadsheet. I pulled the legal/ops position together below and included the exact wording we recommend you include in the SOW so procurement can close quickly.

Quoted clause (recommended SOW language):
"Redwood Inference represents and warrants that Customer Data (as defined in this Agreement) will not be used to train or improve Redwood's publicly or privately released machine learning models, model families, or research datasets. Operational telemetry that does not contain Customer Data payloads (e.g., anonymized platform metrics) may be retained for platform monitoring and capacity planning, but such telemetry will not be ingested into model training datasets. Backup images, disaster recovery snapshots, or crash dumps are maintained solely for resilience and will not be used for model training or research.

Upon Customer request for deletion of Customer Data, Redwood shall: (a) delete active copies from primary and replication stores within 30 calendar days where deletion does not compromise availability under the agreed SLA; (b) mark backups and archives containing Customer Data as tombstoned and exclude them from restore operations going forward; (c) provide Customer a certificate of deletion for active stores and for tombstoned archival items within 60 calendar days, to the extent allowed by immutable-retention legal holds.
"

Retention windows (typical defaults — configurable per-customer):
- KV/Token caches (ephemeral): automatic eviction within 48 hours by default.
- Short-term request logs (for debugging/trace): 30 days (can be shortened to 7 days on request).
- Aggregated telemetry (metrics, not payloads): 365 days for capacity and anomaly analysis.
- Backups/DR snapshots: default 90 days; long-term cold archives may persist longer depending on the account's chosen policy.

Edge cases & notes:
- Snapshots taken for regulatory e-discovery or legal hold are handled per the legal hold scope and cannot be auto-deleted until release.
- Debug snapshots that contain customer payloads are rotated and access-controlled; they are not used for model training.
- If you require a contractual "no-derivative-models" clause (i.e., not only no training but no derived artifacts), we can add explicit language and an audit appendix.

Hi all — operational runbook below (short form):

1) Receive formal deletion request via contract portal or support ticket (we log request id).
2) Validate scope with customer (data ranges, endpoints, and affected resources).
3) Execute deletion in primary stores immediately; replicate deletion commands to secondaries.
4) Initiate tombstone flagging for backups/archives and add to queue for immutable-store review.
4a) For typical backups, the tombstone and exclusion from restores completes within 10 business days.
5) Issue deletion certificate for primary stores within 30 days. Archive/tombstone certificate delivered within 60 days, subject to immutable retention holds.
6) If FinHealth requests confirmation that deleted data never entered any training pipelines, we provide a signed attestation and (if needed) a 3rd-party audit summary.
