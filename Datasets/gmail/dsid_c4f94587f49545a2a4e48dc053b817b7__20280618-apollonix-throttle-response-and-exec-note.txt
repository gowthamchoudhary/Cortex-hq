Jordan / Aditya — initial findings:
- Not capacity. Backends show normal CPU/GPU utilization for the region.
- Rate limiter configuration for the Apollonix tenant had a burst_window parameter set to 1s instead of the intended 60s; combined with the global bucket mapping for multi-region routing it caused premature token exhaustion when clients use short high-frequency bursts.
- We also discovered a mismatched quota binding between the US-EAST and US-WEST pools — requests routed to a fallback pool were counted against a much smaller quota.

Action taken so far:
- Prepared a config patch (config_diff.patch) to change burst_window -> 60s and remap quota binding to correct pool.
- Opened Jira SUP-2147 and linked monitoring alerting dashboards.

Short-term mitigation I propose:
1) Apply a hotfix to burst_window and remap quota binding in the tenant config (canary to 5% of traffic in 10 minutes).
2) Temporarily increase quota for Apollonix by 2x for 2 hours while canary stabilizes.

I can push the hotfix within 20 minutes if you authorize the temporary quota increase.
