Olga — quick update from sleuthing (initial notes attached: initial-sleuth-notes.txt):

Findings (preliminary):
- Edge routers reported long request queues and elevated 5xx (backend timeouts) starting ~08:42 UTC.
- Many 429s are originating from the per-tenant rate-limiter at the router layer when bursts exceed token-bucket capacity.
- FinPay client logs show aggressive retry policy (0s -> immediate retry then exponential) that produced a retry storm once transient latency hit 100–200ms.
- Autoscaler lag: our scale-up path for the model pool takes ~4–6 minutes to reach steady-state under bursty traffic; during the ramp we hit timeouts and additional retries.
- KV cache churn observed for large sequence lengths which increased tail latency.

Immediate mitigation I recommend (doing now):
1) Apply a short-duration tenant-level throttle (soft cap) to finite window for FinPay to bound requests.
2) Enable per-tenant circuit-breaker to eject traffic to a lightweight fallback model for non-critical paths.
3) Increase router rate-limiter burst allowance slightly for authenticated tenants while keeping a hard cap.
4) Kick off a warm-pool spin (add warm reserve instances) to reduce scale latency.

I'll start 1–3 now and monitor. We need a postmortem for detection/runbook gaps: specifically there was no alert that would have indicated client-driven retry amplification vs steady increased latency.

—Marcus
Principal SRE, Redwood Inference

Team / Emily — thank you for the rapid response. Below is a concise postmortem and the committed follow-up actions. Please review and flag anything missing.

Summary of impact:
- Window: 08:42–10:20 UTC on 2027-09-15.
- Symptom: Elevated 5xx and 429 responses on /v1/generate; client-side timeouts and amplified retries caused increased user failures for FinPay merchant flows.
- Customer impact: FinPay reported merchant checkout failures and increased support tickets; revenue-impacting for two major merchants.

Root cause (primary):
- Client retry policy on FinPay (immediate retries) + a transient backend latency spike caused a retry amplification effect that saturated router queues.
- Router-level token-bucket rate-limiter returned 429s when token buckets burst-depleted.
- Autoscaler scale-up latency and lack of warm capacity increased backend timeouts.
- Detection gap: alerting does not differentiate between gradual latency increase and short, sharp retry-amplified error spikes. No per-tenant throttling playbook existed.

Immediate remediation performed:
- Tenant-level soft throttle and hard cap applied to FinPay (Dylan) — reduced user-visible errors.
- Circuit-breaker/fallback model enabled for non-critical flows (Dylan).
- Warm pool invoked (+6 workers) to reduce further scale-up lag (Marcus).
- Customer comms: FinPay notified at 10:45 UTC and updated at 12:00 UTC (Lauren handled customer email cadence).
