Neha / team — see suggested compromise below. High-level thoughts first:
- 60 days is reasonable for opt-out of auto-renewal; operationally we can support that with a 60-day cutoff when notices must be received.
- For termination-for-convenience, 60 days is workable but we should include a limited termination fee to protect revenue on short pilots.

Suggested redline (clean language to propose to Acme):
"Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to the other party. If Customer terminates for convenience within the first twelve (12) months following the Effective Date, Customer shall pay a termination fee equal to one (1) month of the then-current monthly subscription fees (the \"Termination Fee\"). Termination under this Section shall not relieve Customer of any fees accrued prior to the effective date of termination.
"
Additional operational notes:
- Recommend we add a transition assistance item: 30 days of read-only access to the environment after termination for convenience, limited to data export and KV cache retrieval (no serving).
- For auto-renewal, accept 60-day opt-out provided a notice is delivered by Customer to billing@redwoodinference.com and our console receives the cancellation flag prior to the 60-day cutoff.

I attach a short proposed redline to insert into their doc (redwood_proposed_redline_msa.docx). Happy to tighten the termination fee language if Sales prefers a dollar-capped alternative.
