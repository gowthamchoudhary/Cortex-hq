On Mon, Jun 20, 2027 Ben Carter wrote:
> Following today’s POC call with Apex ...

Quick feasibility note from Eng: short answer — doable with caveats.

Model residency options (tradeoffs):
- Tag & route: lowest effort. We route requests for apexfin tenant to a chosen model variant and keep routing in the control plane. Pros: no per-tenant storage change; Cons: if the model is evicted or topology changes, outputs could diverge. No strong guarantees for exact deterministic outputs across infra changes. Estimated effort: ~1-2 dev-weeks for config + rollout.
- Persistent residency: persist a copy of model artifacts (or pinned weights) for the tenant on a dedicated node pool. Pros: stronger determinism; Cons: storage + licensing + infra cost; more invasive. Estimated effort: ~4-8 dev-weeks + infra lead time.

Warm standby options:
- Priority scheduling onto shared pools with pre-warmed workers at queue front: medium effort (2-3 dev-weeks) and relies on scheduler changes and SLI guardrails.
- Dedicated reserved pool (hard reservation): higher infra cost but simplest from SLA perspective; needs reservation policy and billing/usage separation (3-5 dev-weeks + infra provisioning).

Short-term mitigation path we can ship quickly (1–2 weeks):
1) Use Tag & route for model selection + a temporary dedicated small reserved pool sized for demo bursts (manual provisioning).
2) Instrument tracing and snapshot outputs for the 30-day window so Apex can verify determinism during review.

Risks: rollback semantics for pinned model during canary/rollback, and increased operational burden for manual pool management.

ETA rough: if we scope as short-term workaround (tag+manual pool) — 1–2 dev-weeks and ~$1.5k/mo extra infra for demo support. For product-grade solution (persistent residency + reservation) — 6–10 dev-weeks and design work for rollback/SLI, plus a small infra recurring cost.

Happy to sketch an RFC and an incremental rollout plan if Product wants to push to roadmap committee.
