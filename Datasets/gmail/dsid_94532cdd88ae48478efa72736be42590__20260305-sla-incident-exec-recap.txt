Subject: Postmortem draft: INC-1423 (SLA degradation) — timeline & action items

Hi all — attaching a short postmortem draft (postmortem-draft.docx) and linking the JIRA ticket. Key points below to slot into the exec 1-pager: 

Root cause (finalized):
- A scheduler refactor (CL 5321) deployed 2026-03-01 22:58 UTC changed the order of cache invalidation and KV checkpointing for long-lived sessions. Under a specific traffic pattern (heavy prefix reuse + low tail entropy) this produced a race that caused cache churn and high lock contention on our IO threadpool. The effective result was increased per-request latency and lower throughput until caches warmed.

Impact summary:
- Customers affected (not exhaustive): Acme Retail (primary), two smaller ecommerce customers that share the same routing shard.
- Duration: 23:40 UTC — 00:30 UTC (about 50 minutes).
- SLA breaches: Acme experienced 3.7% of requests above SLA threshold; we recorded 12 distinct 5-minute windows with p99 > SLA.
- Revenue estimate: preliminary estimate ~0.4% monthly ARR attrition risk if left unmitigated (we will model with Finance).

Immediate mitigations completed:
- 00:12 UTC: Reverted scheduler refactor and deployed guard for KV invalidation ordering.
- 00:18 UTC: Route-level fallback to v2-small model variant for requests with short context length.
- 00:26 UTC: Warming job to repopulate prefix caches across affected shard.

Action items (owners & recommended due dates):
- A1: Revert verification gating: add automated regression test for scheduler ordering (owner: aditya_rao) — due 2026-03-10
- A2: Add a chaos test that simulates heavy prefix reuse patterns (owner: grace_oconnor + infra team) — due 2026-04-01
- A3: Customer communication draft and credit proposal (owner: grace_oconnor) — due 2026-03-04
- A4: Instrumentation: emit cache-miss heatmap to 90d retention and add alert on sustained cache-miss rate (owner: hana_ops alias / hannah_schmitt) — due 2026-03-12
- A5: Post-release canary policy update: hold 15-minute canary with load generator on scheduling-sensitive routes (owner: platform-release team) — due 2026-03-15
