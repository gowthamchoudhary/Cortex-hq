Hi Tom / Maya —

Following up with more detail from engineering after initial triage. Short summary and mitigations you can apply immediately: 

Root-cause (current hypothesis):
- A regression in autoscaling interactions with streaming-prefetch logic caused suboptimal worker placement under a burst of small concurrent sessions. This increased KV-cache misses and caused a higher frequency of synchronous kernel stalls, pushing tail latency up for streaming endpoints.

Immediate customer workarounds (recommended order):
1) Route reads to non-streaming generation endpoints for sessions that can accept full response payloads (POST /v1/generate). This eliminates chunking pauses and reduces dependence on streaming prefetch.
2) If you must keep streaming, reduce token chunk size by setting smaller max_tokens_per_chunk on your client and enable client-side incremental render timeout to avoid UI stalls (see mitigation-playbook-0619.pdf).
3) Temporarily lower concurrency per connection (limit concurrent streaming sessions per host) while we validate autoscaler fixes.

What we’re doing:
- Patching autoscaler decision logic and testing in us-west-2 canary cluster now.
- Forcing warm workers with KV-cache prefill for high-priority BlueWave tenant to reduce miss rate.
