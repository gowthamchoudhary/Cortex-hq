Quick note on SLA topology: to meet PioneerGrid's regional redundancy ask with a 99.9% SLA and reasonable cost, we recommend an active-primary/standby in a second region with automated failover (30s–90s target). Active-active would be materially more expensive and unlikely necessary for their workload profile. I'll add this as an option in the final commercial pack with delta pricing.

Rollback criteria (high level) for the runbook:
- Failure conditions: token latency > 2x baseline for 5 consecutive minutes for 2 critical routes, or sustained error rate > 2% for 10 minutes.
- Auto-fallback: route to a validated Turbine-compatible model variant (if PioneerGrid signs off) or route back to their current vendor for a short window while we triage.
- Rollback execution: SE lead initiates, MigrateOps runs traffic cut, engineer on-call monitors for 60 minutes before confirming rollback complete.
