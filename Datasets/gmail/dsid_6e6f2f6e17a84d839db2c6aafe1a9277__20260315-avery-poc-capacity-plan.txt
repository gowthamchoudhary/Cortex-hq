Hi all,

Quick technical confirmation and proposal:

Recommended baseline: 2x A100-equivalent GPUs (or 2x H100 if you want headroom for larger context lengths). Baseline will be pinned to your trial to ensure consistent latency during business hours (9am–6pm local).

Burst/autoscale behavior:
- Autoscale target: allow bursts up to 4x GPUs (2 baseline + 2 burst) to handle demand spikes up to ~150 concurrent short requests. Bursts are provisioned from a shared pool and are best-effort but very reliable for short windows (sub-10 minute bursts are routinely honored).
- If the shared pool is exhausted, Redwood will fallback to a compatible lower-cost model variant (slightly higher latency) rather than fail requests. We'll include a per-request header indicating fallback occurred for observability.

Performance estimates (empirical):
- With 2x A100-equivalents and the model variant we discussed, expect ~40 concurrent sessions with P95 around 200–240ms for 512 tokens.
- Scale to 4x GPUs for bursts; that should push effective concurrency to ~140–180 depending on prompt length and KV cache hit rate.

Cost and billing for POC:
- Reserved capacity during the POC will be billed pro-rata at our standard reserved-hour rate unless we agree to credit it as part of the trial. For sales expediency we can offer up to $4,000 in trial credits to offset reserved hourly charges for a three-week POC (subject to approvals). Any burst consumption beyond reserved hours is billed at standard on-demand burst rates.
