Hannah / Monica — quick internal note before we reply to James:

I reproduced the "Account not linked" behavior on a test tenant when an email address is already associated with another Coupa customer tenant. Two remediation options:
1) Tenant-unlink + resend: remove the email binding on the other tenant (requires coordination with Coupa support or the other tenant admin) then resend the invite.
2) Alternate invite address: ask the customer to provide a different invite email (eg invite+echo@echoport.com) that isn't already bound.

Operational details: if we do the unlink we must also invalidate the old activation token and send a new one. I'll run the unlink script during a low-traffic window — please confirm the timestamp window (I'll aim for 10:30 PDT).

If we expect a PO cutoff tomorrow, option 2 is fastest. Option 1 is cleaner for their tenant but may take longer if the other tenant admin needs to sign-off.
