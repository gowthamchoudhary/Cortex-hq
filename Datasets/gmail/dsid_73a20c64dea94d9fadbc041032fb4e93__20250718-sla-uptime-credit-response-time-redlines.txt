Thanks, Megan — and great to meet you yesterday.

High level answers first:
- Uptime metric: Redwood's standard is per-region availability for customer-dedicated endpoints (we measure 5xx/500-class error rates + ingress reachability). For hosted customers we provide a per-region and account-level view in the Console; contract language can be written as 'per-deployment (region) availability' with a clause for multi-region failover behavior.
- Fallbacks: we consider automated fallback to compatible model variants part of our reliability playbook, but the SLA scope depends on whether fallbacks are configured and tested as part of the customer's dedicated environment. We can include fallback coverage in the Order Form as an optional extension.

On the specifics: 99.95% is a material commitment for our standard hosted tier. For Dedicated or Private deployments we can discuss 99.95% with standard maintenance blackout windows. For hosted, 99.9% with a 30-minute P0 response is our more typical baseline.

Proposed path to resolve quickly:
1) Redwood to propose a redline counteroffer that keeps 99.9% for hosted with a tighter P0 response (30 minutes) and a tiered credit formula that caps at X% of monthly bill — we will quantify X in the counter.
2) For Dedicated deployments, include a 99.95% option with a higher reserved capacity commitment and a different credit ladder.
3) Agree on maintenance window definition: prioritize scheduling during off-peak hours (weekends) and allow emergency patches with notice and limited credit exposure.

Megan — quick appendix from an implementation and billing perspective:

Operational notes:
- Measurement window: we recommend a rolling 30-day measurement window for availability calculations to avoid credit volatility from single-day blips.
- Credit formula: propose a staircase: 99.9–99.5% = 5% credit, 99.5–99.0% = 15% credit, <99.0% = 30% credit of monthly service fees. For Dedicated, the caps move +10 percentage points per slab.
- Response times: 30m for P0 (phone + Slack), 2h for P1, 24h for P2. Escalation path documented in the Console and in the Order Form.
- Maintenance windows: standard weekly maintenance excluded windows should be: Saturdays 02:00–06:00 UTC and low-impact deploy windows on Wednesdays 00:00–04:00 UTC. Emergency maintenance allowed with 4-hour notice and postmortem.

We can make the credit ladder configurable in the Order Form; if Tideway wants 99.95% for hosted we'd need a minimum monthly commit and a dedicated pool or private deployment. Happy to run numbers; looping Rishi for SLA cost delta.
