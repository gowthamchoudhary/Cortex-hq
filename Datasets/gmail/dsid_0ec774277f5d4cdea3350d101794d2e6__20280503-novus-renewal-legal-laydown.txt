Marissa / Lucy — I reviewed Novus's redline and prepared an internal summary. I attached a PDF with inline comments (Redwood_Markup_MSA_comments.pdf). High-level position follows.

Quick summary of legal positions (high priority):
- Liability cap: Novus proposes unlimited liability for certain data breaches. Redwood cannot accept open unlimited liability; propose cap at 5x fees over term or standard $5M cap (option to discuss).
- Data retention & deletion: we can agree to more granular retention but need to keep carve-outs for backups and aggregate telemetry for optimization. Suggest language that requires Novus to request data deletion with agreed SLAs; Redwood will comply subject to lawful hold exceptions.
- Audit access / SOC evidence: provide SOC 2 summary and audit scheduling window vs. full onsite audits; propose replacing onsite audit right with written evidence + 30-day remediation window.
- Export controls & sanctions: add mutual representation that both parties comply with applicable export laws; limited carve-out for government requests.
- Termination for convenience: Novus asked for a 60-day termination-for-convenience window with pro-rata refund; counter-propose 90 days with defined wind-down fees for committed capacity.

Commercially relevant legal asks for sales direction:
- If we accept a higher liability cap, we should ask for a larger non-refundable onboarding fee or a price uplift.
- Any SLA credit formula requested by Novus will increase our expected refund exposure; suggest we cap the total SLA credits at 10% of monthly fees.

Attachments: Redwood_Markup_MSA_comments.pdf (tracked suggestions).

Proposed options for Marissa / Ben to present back to Novus:
A) Minimal edits: accept limited redlines, keep 12-month default renewal but add order-form for 4M committed tokens and a 12% price uplift.
B) Compromise: 18-month term, 4M committed tokens, liability cap moved to $5M, onboarding credit of $15k, SLA credits capped at 10% monthly fee.

If Novus insists on unlimited liability or on-site audit rights we will need legal escalation and [possible] executive approval.
