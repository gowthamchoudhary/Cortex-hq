- Net terms: we can make an exception to Net30 and accept Net45 provided: (a) Purchaser issues a PO before service commencement; and (b) Purchaser accepts a 1.5% monthly late fee after 60 days past due. We prefer Net30 but will accept Net45 for customer relationship reasons.

- Withholding / gross-up: recommend this clause (suggested redline):
"If Purchaser is required by applicable law to withhold or deduct any taxes from payments due hereunder, Purchaser shall provide Redwood with evidence of such withholding and paid taxes promptly. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the Parties shall treat the amounts actually received by Redwood as full payment and the Parties agree to cooperate to minimize tax withholding where permitted by law. If withholding reduces Redwood's net receipts below the amount owed, Purchaser shall either (i) gross up the payment so that Redwood receives the full amount due, or (ii) provide valid tax documentation (e.g., tax residence certificate) that causes the applicable tax authority to exempt or reduce the withholding."

- Evidence required: if Purchaser expects withholding, we ask for a current tax residency certificate for the paying entity plus the PO. If Purchaser cannot provide the certificate in advance, require a 30-day remediation plan and escrow obligation for the difference.
