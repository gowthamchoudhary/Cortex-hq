Subject: Re: Private endpoint firewall / outbound IPs — exclusivity and rollback

Alex — quick answers from ops: 

- Static block exclusivity: yes. When a customer chooses the per-customer static option we reserve those four IPv4 addresses for that tenant for the length of the contract. We do not assign them to other customers. I'll add a short clause to the SOW to make this explicit.

- Managed proxy rollback: we maintain a publish-and-stable model — a rotation is staged in our canary region and tested against active telemetry. If a rotation has unexpected impact we can revert to the prior nat mapping within our maintenance window; documented rollback steps are in the rotation PDF. We also provide a 72-hour pre-notice so customers can prepare.

- Address ownership: the static blocks we allocate for per-customer mapping are drawn from our BYO-range allocations on our fabric and are not from ephemeral public pools; they are ASN-backed and announced consistently. The managed proxy pool is drawn from our cloud provider ranges and therefore could be subject to upstream reassignment in emergency scenarios (hence the 72-hour notice and rollback plan).
