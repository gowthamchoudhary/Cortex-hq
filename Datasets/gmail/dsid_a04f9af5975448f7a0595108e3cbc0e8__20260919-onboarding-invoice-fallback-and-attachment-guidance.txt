Hi Aisha — thanks for the sample.

Short answer: portal preferred; email is allowed as a fallthrough but we need a stable subject token (PO or Vendor ID) to avoid manual routing.

High-level details:
- Portal: single source of truth. Preferred because it captures structured line items and guarantees acknowledgement.
- Email fallback: acceptable when customers can't use the portal, but PDFs must include a PO visible on page 1 and the email subject must contain the PO in the canonical form: "PO:<PO_NUMBER>" (e.g., PO:BF-12345).
- File types: PDFs and single CSVs accepted. ZIPs add manual steps; please avoid unless necessary.
- Max size for email ingest: 10 MB per message. Larger files should be uploaded to the portal or SFTP.

Internal note for Marissa/Vanessa: we need to confirm whether we accept multipage PDFs where line items are embedded in images (OCR risk). Suggest we set the fallback acknowledgement to "Received (manual review)" when OCR fails, and include a 48h SLA for manual triage.

Thanks Ben — adding the recommended minimal template for the onboarding form and the ingest rules we should surface to vendors. Please review/comment.

Minimal invoice template we prefer (email fallback):
- Invoice number (required)
- Invoice date (required)
- PO number (required for auto-match)
- Vendor name & Redwood vendor ID (if assigned)
- Total amount (gross)
- Line items: description, qty, unit price (CSV only; PDF allowed but reduces automation)

Ingest rules to publish in onboarding doc:
- Preferred: portal (https://portal.redwood.ai/vendor/onboard)
- Subject token format for email: "PO:<PO_NUMBER>" — must be in the subject or first line of the email body
- Accepted attachments: .pdf, .csv (single file). Max 10 MB.
- ZIPs: accepted only with prior coordination (add support@redwood.ai)
- If we can't auto-match: invoice will be placed in manual queue and vendor receives an acknowledgement with a link to provide missing PO/metadata
