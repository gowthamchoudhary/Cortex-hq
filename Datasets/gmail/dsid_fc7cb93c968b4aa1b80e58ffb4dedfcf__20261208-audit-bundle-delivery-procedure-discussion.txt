Hi Olivia —

A few technical clarifications from the exports team to help you plan ingestion and verification:

- Schema and fields: Exports include a strict schema: event_timestamp (ISO8601), request_id (UUID), sequence_number (monotonic per-tenant), event_type (inference.request | inference.response | model.update | policy.decision), model_version, tenant_id, user_id (if present), prompt_digest (SHA256), response_digest (SHA256), tokens_in/tokens_out, and optional raw_payload bundle when enabled. We will provide a canonical JSON Schema in the attached workbook.
- Export types:
  * Snapshot (point-in-time): single snapshot export of an account range; produces a manifest + N data files.
  * Incremental delta: sequence-based ranges that can be replayed to reconstruct state; includes first_sequence and last_sequence in manifest.
- File layout and compression: For >200MB files we produce parquet + snappy compression and gzip the transfer package. Filenames are timestamped with UTC and sequence ranges (e.g., audit-20261207-0001-of-004.parquet.gz).
- Integrity and verification: Each data file has a SHA-256 checksum; the manifest lists these plus a signed manifest digest (RSA-2048 signature). We can provide a signed manifest timestamped by our audit KMS.
- Encryption: Exports at rest and in transit are encrypted with AES-256 + optional KMIP/HSM-wrapped keys for Dedicated/Private customers. S3 deliveries can be to a customer-owned bucket with SSE-KMS.
- Legal hold flow: When a legal hold is applied, deletion jobs skip flagged objects; holds are recorded in the audit ledger with operator, case id, and TTL (until release). Release requires two-person approval for Hosted enterprise customers or single-operator release in Private deployments (configurable).
- Cost and quotas: Large full-account exports are charged as a one-time export job fee (est. $0.02/GB for Hosted default) and separate egress if S3 cross-region. Dedicated customers can subscribe to an export plan that includes N expedited exports per year.
