In normal operations, Redwood engineers do not access request payloads; access is limited to anonymized telemetry and aggregated metrics. Jordan / team — technical confirmation below. High level: when Acme supplies and manages keys, our service components cannot decrypt customer data without the key material presented by your KMS. Practical details: 

- VPC deployment: the runtime nodes that process requests are provisioned inside your VPC and authenticate to your KMS for per-request decryption. Redwood control-plane maintains orchestration metadata but does not receive plaintext payloads. Our logs capture request IDs, latencies, and non-sensitive metrics; payloads are not logged.

- On-prem deployment: runtime and persistent storage remain on your infrastructure; Redwood staff access is limited to what you grant for debug sessions.

- Operator access / emergency: We do not have standing access to decrypt content protected by customer keys. In the rare event of lawful process (subpoena/court order) where Redwood is compelled to assist, we notify customers promptly unless prohibited. If Acme requires an escrow or break-glass mechanism, we can discuss a mutually agreed technical escrow with strict access controls and audit logs (this would be an exceptional, documented arrangement).

- Transfers: Our subprocessors list indicates a small set of support vendors with EU-based processing for telemetry only; core compute and storage for Private deployments stay in customer region when required by contract. If you need SCCs or specific transfer mechanisms, we can add them to the DPA.
