Hi — thanks for the RFQ and numbers, attached is an initial framing. High level: we generally offer three packaging patterns for Private/VPC deployments — we can map your RFQ into these:

1) Committed + amortized platform fee
   - Platform fee is billed as a term fee (annual or multi-year) and amortized across the committed node count. Best for customers wanting predictable monthly P&L treatment.
   - Surge: included burst credits (X hours/month) + capped overage at a reduced per-hour node rate.

2) Metered core + optional platform retainer
   - Lower upfront platform fee, metered usage for excess inference. Better if you prefer opex model.
   - Surge handled with auto-burst; overage token pricing with volume discounts after thresholds.

3) Pilot-to-scale hybrid (6 months)
   - Deeply discounted platform fee for pilot; fixed number of burst credits; if converted, pilot fees apply toward first-year amortization.
