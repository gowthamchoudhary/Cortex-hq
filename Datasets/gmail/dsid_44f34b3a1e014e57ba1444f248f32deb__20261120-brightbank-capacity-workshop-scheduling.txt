Adding a quick note on the Dedicated vs Hosted decision. TL;DR: for short-term spike testing, Hosted quota bumps are fastest; for sustained high rps during the pilot, Dedicated reserved pools reduce per-token cost and give predictable tail latency.

Checklist for sizing Dedicated (if you lean that way):
- Peak simultaneous streams (LLM token generation streams)
- Max tokens/session and average truncation length
- Desired latency SLO (p95 < 300ms target for our recommended config)

If BrightBank can share a small sample of typical prompts (or rough token stats) we can run an internal simulation and recommend either a small Dedicated pool (1–2 nodes) or a Hosted bump (quota and rate-limit changes).

On the quota bump process: we can generally do Hosted token increases within 24–48 hours once procurement signs the temporary addendum. Dedicated provisioning typically needs 3–5 business days to schedule capacity depending on region.
