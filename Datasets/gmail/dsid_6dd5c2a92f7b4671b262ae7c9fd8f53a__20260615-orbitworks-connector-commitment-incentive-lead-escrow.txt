Monica / team —

Thanks for the forward. My suggested baseline for a pilot-stage ISV/referral partner:

Proposed commercial terms (pilot -> initial 12 months post-conversion):
- Referral fee: 10% of gross revenue recognized by Redwood on converted customers during the first 12 months post-conversion.
- Resale/Co-sell (if OrbitWorks invoices and remits): 15% commission on gross revenue for the first 12 months.
- Tiered accelerator: if cumulative ARR from OrbitWorks-sourced conversions > $250k within 12 months, bump to 18% for incremental revenue.

Lead registration & protection:
- Standard registration window: 90 days from initial lead submission or first demo (whichever is earlier).
- Protection requires a qualifying contact record and a minimal MQL definition (contact, company domain, one qualifying note).
- Dispute window: 30 days to raise claims post-close; after that, lock applies.

Onboarding/demo credits & clawbacks:
- One-time onboarding credit: $5k credit applied against Redwood services invoiced in the first 60 days post-go-live (clawback if customer churns or refunds within first 90 days).
- Demo credits: 2 free demo environments (up to 30k tokens each) for joint PoC.

Legal & ops asks:
- Include explicit clause for marketplace-sourced deals (if the customer comes via Redwood Marketplace, OrbitWorks gets referral credit only if registered and validated pre-close).
- Standard 12-month clawback on credits for refunds/churn.
