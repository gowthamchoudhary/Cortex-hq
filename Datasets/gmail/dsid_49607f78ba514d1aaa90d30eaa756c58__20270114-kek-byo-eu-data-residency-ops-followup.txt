Hi Sarah — thanks again for taking the demo earlier today. Quick write-up from our side with the concrete options we discussed and the immediate asks so your security and legal teams have something to work from.

High-level agreed points from call:
- Priority: ClearBank requires EU data residency for customer content and PII.
- Encryption: Must support TLS in transit and AES-256 at rest.
- Customer-managed keys (BYOK/KEK) is a gating requirement for production.

Redwood options (recap / technical summary):
1) Envelope encryption model (default): Redwood generates a data encryption key (DEK) per model/session; the DEK is encrypted (wrapped) with a key-encryption-key (KEK/CMK). For Hosted customers who integrate a cloud KMS, the CMK lives in the customer’s cloud KMS (recommended). For Private deployments we can allow an HSM-backed CMK that never leaves the customer environment.
2) BYOK via cloud KMS cross-account integration: Customer provisions an HSM-backed CMK in their AWS/GCP/Azure KMS and grants Redwood a limited encrypt/decrypt role for envelope wrapping. Redwood never has access to the CMK plaintext.
3) Importable key material / dedicated HSM: For customers that require FIPS/HSM guarantees we support two paths — (a) Private deployment where the CMK is hosted in customer-controlled HSM (preferred for strict custody), or (b) a co-managed HSM model where key material is imported into a dedicated HSM instance with contractual controls.

What Redwood DOES / DOES NOT persist:
- We persist DEKs encrypted at rest (wrapped). We do NOT persist customer KEKs in plaintext.
- Audit logs record KMS wrap/unwrap operations and key identifiers; access logs are available for compliance review.

Logging and retention:
- Hosted default: operational access logs retained 90 days (can be extended).
- Dedicated/Private: default retention 2 years; longer retention is available as an add-on.
- We can provide sampled key-usage logs and sign them for audit when pairing with customer KMS.

Suggested contract items (short):
- Clear definition of key custody (customer holds CMK in their KMS)
- Audit access window and log retention guarantee
- SLA fallback if KMS unwrap failures occur (fail open/closed policy)

Ava / Monica — answers below, please feel free to paste verbatim into a response to ClearBank. I’ve also uploaded a short playbook to the shared drive (link in related_links).

A) DEK handling (customer-facing):
- Redwood never persists plaintext DEKs to disk. DEKs are generated in-memory and immediately wrapped with the configured CMK. Any transient materials that touch RAM are zeroed after use; we do not write DEKs unencrypted to persistent storage.

B) AWS cross-account privileges (minimal):
- We require an IAM role with kms:Encrypt, kms:Decrypt, kms:GenerateDataKey, kms:DescribeKey on the specific CMK ARN(s). We recommend a scoped role with resource restrictions and an assume-role trust policy for Redwood's account ID. Sample policy attached (sample-clauses.docx includes the policy snippet).

C) Key rotation and old wrapped DEKs:
- Rotation is customer-controlled for customer CMKs. When a CMK is rotated, previously wrapped DEKs remain decryptable if the KMS maintains the previous key material (standard KMS behavior). If you import new key material or destroy previous key versions, previously-wrapped DEKs will be irrecoverable — we will not be able to unwrap them. We recommend maintaining historical key versions for the regulatory retention period you require.

D) Retention extension (Hosted):
- 90 days is our default for Hosted access logs. We can extend to 12 months for an add-on (estimated +15% to monthly Hosted fee for the routes that capture KMS usage and audit logs). For Dedicated/Private deployments retention is configurable and typically provisioned for 2+ years. Lead time: ~2–3 weeks to enable extended retention and to wire in storage and retention policies.

Suggested concise contract language (paste-ready):
- "Customer will retain sole ownership and custody of the customer master key(s) (CMKs) provisioned in Customer KMS. Redwood will be granted scoped, auditable encrypt/decrypt permissions for KEK wrapping operations only and will not store CMK plaintext. Audit logs for key wrap/unwrap operations will be retained for at least 12 months as agreed in the applicable service addendum."
