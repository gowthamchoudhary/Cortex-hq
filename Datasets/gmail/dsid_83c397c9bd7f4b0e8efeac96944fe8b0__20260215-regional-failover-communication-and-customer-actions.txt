Hi Lisa — quick answers:

1) Data residency: we will not route EU-resident customer payloads outside of EU where the customer has regional pinning enabled. For customers who can accept eu-central-1 as a compliant EU region, switching to region="eu-central-1" will keep data within the EU. Our legal/compliance team is standing by for any formal confirmation if you need it.

2) SLA credits: we are logging all failed requests and will evaluate SLA impacts after the incident is closed. If your account qualifies for credits, we'll surface that via your account manager.

3) Preferred retry headers: add X-Redwood-Prefer-Fallback: true to encourage immediate fallback attempts (helps for some SDKs). Example curl:

curl -X POST https://api.redwood.example/v1/generate 
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <key>" 
  -H "X-Redwood-Prefer-Fallback: true" 
  -d '{"model":"redwood-13b","region":"eu-central-1","input":"ping"}'

What happened (short):
- A regional network fabric issue at our cloud provider caused loss of connectivity to a subset of hosts in us-east-1 and eu-west-1 between 07:05–09:12 UTC. Our serving runtime detected degraded node health and attempted automated failovers. Some tenants with strict region pins saw failed requests while we completed the evacuation.

- Add header X-Redwood-Prefer-Fallback: true to accelerate fallback behavior for clients that support it.
