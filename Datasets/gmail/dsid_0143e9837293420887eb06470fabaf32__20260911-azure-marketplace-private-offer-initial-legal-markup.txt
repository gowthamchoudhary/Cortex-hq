Ava / team,

Draft language below for the billing entity and payment terms that I recommend we propose back to Skyline:

1) Billing entity and tax handling: "Seller invoices will be issued to the billing entity specified on the customer purchase order. All invoices will reference the Seller Azure Marketplace Offer ID and the Customer Purchase Order number. Taxes will be handled in accordance with applicable law; customer is responsible for providing any required tax exemption certificates prior to invoicing."

2) Payment terms and PO timing: "Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt (Net-45). To ensure timely processing, customer shall provide a valid purchase order at least five (5) business days before invoice issue date. For invoicing delayed by late PO submission, Seller reserves the right to adjust the invoice date to the date of PO receipt for payment term calculation."

3) Invoice format / Azure mapping: "Seller will provide a sample invoice indicating the Seller Offer ID and Azure billing line items for mapping purposes. Seller's Azure seller identifier for Marketplace billing will be provided upon execution of the private offer."

If you want a version with a Net-60 option we can include a fallback clause: "If Customer requires Net-60, Seller will consider with written approval from Seller finance and may apply a 1.5% financing administrative fee on outstanding balances beyond 45 days."

I think Net-45 + PO-timing clause is a reasonable middle ground. If procurement pushes hard for Net-60, expect finance approval to take 48–72 hours.
