Team — reviewed Pacta's attachments. Operational thoughts and recommended legal framing below: 

1) Data residency: We can support EU-only inference logging by pinning Pacta's Dedicated pool to EU regions and disabling any cross-region backups for the raw logs. Must be explicit in the SOW and limited to logs (not model artifacts).

2) Audit and forensics windows: Approve time-boxed access where Redwood provides read-only aggregated logs and a pre-authorized forensics window (48 hours x2/year) that is executed under mutual NDA and requires 48 hours' written notice. 3) Retention: 36 months retention for aggregated logs is acceptable if Pacta agrees to a carveout that excludes raw KV cache material and any debug traces. We'll include an attestation clause limiting Redwood's obligation to commercially reasonable efforts and require Pacta to pay incremental storage at list price if retention exceeds standard terms.

If approved, I'll draft SOW language and a narrow playbook for initiating and executing forensics windows (timeline, scope, post-mortem obligations). Recommend Exec approval (Soogin) to sign off on credit and the limited operational carveouts.
