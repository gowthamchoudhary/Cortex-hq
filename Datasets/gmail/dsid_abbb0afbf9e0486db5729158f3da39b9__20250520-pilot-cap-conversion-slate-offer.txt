Hi Sam —

Thanks for the clear ask. We can definitely support converting remaining pilot days into a prepaid credit pool with a simple PSI (pilot services invoice) that your procurement can accept. To make this efficient, here are two options we commonly provide: 

1) Time-to-credit conversion (recommended for short pilots)
   - We convert remaining calendar days to an equivalent token-credit amount based on your observed average daily token consumption.
   - Credits valid for 60 days from invoice date.
   - Overage: $0.50 per 1k tokens (flat) once the cap is exhausted.

2) Monthly capped plan (recommended if you expect bursty usage)
   - Purchase a monthly-cap SKU for 1 month: includes a higher cap and a 10% discount vs pure pay-as-you-go.
   - Auto-renew disabled unless you opt-in.
   - Overage: $0.45 per 1k tokens.
