Short summary of findings from infra triage (timeline in attached PDF):

- Root cause: cascading retry amplification from client-side backoff misconfiguration + our overconservative connection pool threshold on the affected inference fleet.
  - BlueCart toggled a new client library flag that reduced jitter on retries; their retry interval went from randomized 50–200ms to fixed 100ms, which aligned request bursts.
  - Our connection pool saturates under aligned bursts: when pools hit max inflight, we start rejecting with 502s and then 504 timeouts as queues back up. The downstream model worker queue also experienced longer KV cache lock contention.
- Why it escalated: traffic amplification from retries pushed the affected pod group over the burst tolerance; automatic scale-up lagged because our autoscaler uses 1m smoothing and we were inside a 10m SLO window.
- What we did immediately: increased pool threshold on the cluster and enabled short-term permissive queuing to drain inflight requests; applied emergency rate-limit override to BlueCart API key to cap QPS while we stabilized.
- Observed effect: error rate dropped from 14% to 1.6% over 22 minutes after mitigations; latency tail reduced by ~210ms.

Gaps discovered (high level):
- Alerting: we did not have a composite alert for synchronized retry patterns (high retry density + rising 5xx). Existing 5xx alerts fire after error-rate breach, not on correlated retry signals.
- Runbooks: our retry-storm runbook assumes exponential jitter at client side; it does not cover synchronized fixed-interval retries. Playbook lacked a quick checklist for connection-pool temporary tuning and per-customer QPS overrides.
- SLO tuning / autoscaling: smoothing window and scale-up aggressiveness needs review for customer-critical fleets during business hours.

Proposed immediate follow-ups (owner in parentheses):
1) Add a composite alert: retry-density >= X and 5xx >= Y (oncall + infra) — target: 5d
2) Update runbook to include pool-threshold tuning and emergency per-key QPS overrides (dev_patel) — target: 3d
3) Short-term SLO/ASG tweak: reduce autoscaler smoothing on hot fleets to 15s during business-hour rampback windows (rishi_malhotra + infra) — target: 7d
4) Customer coordination: write an incident exec note for BlueCart and recommend client-side retry config with jitter + server-suggested backoff (naomi_feldman) — target: 24h
5) Capacity planning: evaluate dedicated short-burst spare capacity for top-20 accounts (product + sales) — owner: marissa_cole — target: 14d
