Key points we need to resolve:

1) Edge caching / CDN routing (transient telemetry)
- Your team asked whether our edge cache and occasional CDN logs are a "sale" or otherwise constitute a transfer under CCPA/CPRA. Our position: these are transient connectivity/operational logs used solely to deliver the service (caching, prefix routing, bandwidth metrics) and are not used for ads or saleable profiling.
- Proposed contract language: "Provider may use third-party CDN and caching subprocessors for ephemeral request routing and delivery. Such usage is limited to delivery and performance optimization and does not constitute a Sale or a disclosure for direct marketing purposes."

2) Onshore fallback routing for high-priority accounts
- For customers who require onshore fallbacks, we can route inference and telemetry to an onshore Dedicated pool. This involves a shift in routing policy (region-based) and may engage additional subprocessors (onshore cloud partner). We want to include a narrow fallback clause rather than an expansive transfer right.
- Proposed clause: "In cases of capacity or emergency failover, Provider may route Requests to an onshore fallback environment; Provider will notify Customer within 72 hours and ensure equivalent technical and contractual safeguards for data processing."

3) Subprocessor notice and onboarding timelines
- Healthful asked for 30-day advance notice for any new subprocessor; our standard is a 14-day notice with the ability for a customer to object. For enterprise accounts we can extend to 30 days but that impacts rapid security patching for critical infra.
- Compromise: 14-day notice for routine infra subprocessors; 30-day notice for new persistent data-storage subprocessors. Shorter emergency onboarding (48 hours) may be required for security patches and will be accompanied by an incident rationale and post-hoc evidence.

4) Telemetry / observability exports and retention
- We capture telemetry to monitor model performance and platform health (latency, token counts, error traces). We propose retention and minimization commitments and a telemetry pseudonymization step for US state privacy.
- Suggested SOW appendix: retention windows, fields filtered (PII redaction), and a transit encryption statement.
