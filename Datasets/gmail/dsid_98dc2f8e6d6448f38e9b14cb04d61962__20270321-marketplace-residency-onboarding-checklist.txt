Thanks Kim / Sara. Quick clarifications from ops:

- AWS: We support region pinning at listing time; logs are stored in S3 buckets in the same region unless the customer requests cross-region replication. Marketplace listings default to the selected region; for GovCloud we require a separate contract.
- GCP: We support single-region and dual-region options. Note: certain GCP marketplace partners require explicit mention of multi-region behavior in the listing (we've added text to the Nimbus draft).
- Azure: Similar to AWS; for Azure Government and Azure Sovereign (e.g., China) we require an additional review and may need a private deployment.

Retention specifics:
- Request logs (raw): 90 days default, can extend to 365 days for Dedicated with a billing delta.
- Aggregated telemetry: 12 months by default.
- KV cache: session-only unless customer configures persistent session caching (not recommended for regulated data).

Deletion playbook (short):
1) Customer request received via HubSpot/Support
2) Ops ticket created and verified
3) Automated purge job for logs/backup; manual verification for dedicated resources
4) Deletion confirmation email + evidence export (screenshot of purge job ID)
