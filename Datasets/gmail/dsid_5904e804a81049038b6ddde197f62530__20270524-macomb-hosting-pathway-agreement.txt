Samantha —

Quick technical note to answer the on-prem vs dedicated question and to give perf/cost ballparks for the MAP. Short version: Dedicated (Redwood Dedicated) gives you isolation, predictable throughput, and VPC peering; Private (on-prem) is available but adds integration effort (networking, K8s/operator deploy, and HSM/KMS integration).

Key deltas:
- Latency: Hosted (multi-tenant) typically adds 30–80ms vs Dedicated in same region.
- Cost: Dedicated PoC (reserved short-term) estimated at ~20–30% higher Opex than Hosted for equivalent QPS, but unit cost stabilizes with commit.
- Security: Both Dedicated and Private support customer-managed KMS; Private gives physical control required by some auditors. SOC2 artifacts are identical; Private adds customer responsibility for patch cadence.

Recommended PoC config for Macomb:
- Dedicated pool in us-east-1; 2x A100-equivalent instances, quantized model variant for eval; KV caching enabled for session-heavy flows; perf tests with 1k synthetic users and provided test prompts.
- Acceptance criteria: 95th pctl <200ms for standard flows, no PHI leaks in logs (we’ll run redaction tests), and end-to-end prompt accuracy within 2% of baseline.

I attached a short tech note with the config and delta checklist. Happy to walk through it on the call.
