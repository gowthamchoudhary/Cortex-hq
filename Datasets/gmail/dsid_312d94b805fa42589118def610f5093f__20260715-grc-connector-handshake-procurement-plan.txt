Elaine — thanks for the clear list and for the doc. Quick answers up front, then proposed next steps: 

- Exchange flow: we prefer a brokered evidence bucket managed by Redwood (short-lived objects, strict ACLs). That gives us better auditability and avoids cross-account permissions for customers. We can support presigned URL flows for customers that require it, but default will be brokered S3 with enforced encryption-in-transit and server-side encryption with customer KMS where requested.
- Keys: Redwood can accept KMS-wrapped objects or host encryption keys under a customer-managed CMK. For inter-company handoffs, we typically use envelope encryption: partner encrypts with their key, we re-encrypt under our CMK upon ingest, and we surface key metadata in audit logs.
- Retention: default 90 days for shared artifacts; we can expose a per-customer TTL (30/90/365) in the connector config.
- Pen-test vendor: SentinelPen is fine from a tooling perspective. We’ll need their ATO / insurance certs and a PO or SOW to engage.
