Short answers follow; expanded notes are in the attached PDF.\n\n1) Region-specific hosting:\n- Supported regions: us-west-2, us-east-1, eu-west-1, eu-central-1, ap-southeast-1.\n- Default: US (us-west-2). Customers may elect EU or APAC placement during onboarding or via a configuration change.\n\n2) Default retention (Hosted API):\n- Request/response payloads: 30 days by default for operational debugging and abuse detection; configurable shorter retention upon request and generally reducible to 0 days with a private/dedicated plan.\n- Embeddings: Ephemeral by default (48 hours) unless persisted by customer or requested to be retained for a specific feature (e.g., semantic search). Longer retention tiers available as an add-on.\n- Backups / snapshots: Encrypted backups retained for up to 30 days; snapshots used for disaster recovery are scoped per-tenant and region.\n\n3) Cached artifacts (prefix/KV cache):\n- Prefix caching / KV used by runtime to speed repeated prefix generation is memory/ephemeral-NVMe backed and is not written to long-term durable storage.\n- Eviction policy: LRU with a maximum lifetime of 24 hours; explicit eviction occurs on model restart or when the cache node is decommissioned.\n- Persistent cache for Dedicated/Private customers is configurable and documented separately.\n\n4) Customer-initiated deletion / erasure process & SLA: \n- Erasure request path: open a support ticket (support@redwood.com) or use the Console > Data Requests. We acknowledge requests within 24 hours.\n- Hosted API SLA: standard deletion confirmation within 72 hours for request/response payloads and logs; embeddings and persisted artifacts: 7 calendar days to delete + audit confirmation.\n- Evidence: deletion is recorded in our audit log (timestamped entry) and we can provide a deletion report (JSON lines export) and a signed attestation on request.\n\n5) Training and model usage:\n- We do NOT use customer request/response content to train or fine-tune public models. Optionally, customers can opt in to a dataset-sharing program (explicit and contractually bound) — this is not default.\n\n6) Evidence and artifacts:\n- We can provide: audit-log export (scoped to the tenant & date range), deletion report, and a signed letter of attestation.
