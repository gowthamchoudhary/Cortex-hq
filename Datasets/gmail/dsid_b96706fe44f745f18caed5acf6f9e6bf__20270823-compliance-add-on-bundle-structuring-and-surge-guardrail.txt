Elliot — good thread. My recommended approach: 

1) Implementation credit amortization: Finance will approve amortizing a one-time implementation credit across up to 6 months for pilot agreements <= 12 months. Use a $25k credit as you suggested; show equivalent monthly credit so procurement can see OPEX impact.

2) HSM pricing anchor: Offer a pilot seat rate that covers provisioning/attestation (~$3k/mo) + one-time onboarding of $6k. For scale, include a transition clause: after month 6, we reprice to throughput (example: $0.50 per 100k HSM ops) with a migration credit equal to remaining amortized implementation. This gives them predictability and an exit to unit economics at scale.

3) Audit logs: Tiered retention pricing. Example packaging: 90-day hot at $0.12/GB/mo, 1y standard at $0.04/GB/mo, archive at $0.01/GB/mo with retrieval fees. For 7y legal retention, recommend pushing oldest data to archive with index shards retained at low cost.

4) Surge guardrail: Define a soft commit and a 48-hour free burst up to 2x soft cap. For sustained overage, stepped overage rates (e.g., 1-2x -> base overage, 2-5x -> +20%, >5x -> +50%). Offer to cap monthly overage for pilot at X% if they commit to 12 months.

5) Discounting: For a 12-month minimum commit (anchor across hosted + compliance add-ons), Marketing/Finance okay with a 15% bundle discount. For 3-month pilot keep discount conservative (5-8%).

6) SLA credits: Legal says we can offer an operational SLA credit (one-sided, capped) but avoid financial penalties in pilot SOW. Put escalation path and service credits tied to incident severity.
