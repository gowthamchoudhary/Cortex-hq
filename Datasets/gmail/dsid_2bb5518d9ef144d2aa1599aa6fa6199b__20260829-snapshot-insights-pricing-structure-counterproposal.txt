Hi Emma — great to hear and thanks for the context. High-level answers and a starter commercial we can refine: 

- Pricing structure: Snapshot Insights can be licensed either as a seat-based bundle (per user seat + base storage/retention allotment) or as a usage-first model (GB/month for traces + dashboard units). For pilots we usually recommend the usage-first route to align cost to ingest.

- Retention: Full-fidelity retention at 13 months is materially higher cost. Typical levers we recommend:
  * Sample 40–60% of low-risk traces and keep 100% for high-value traces (error/latency spikes)
  * Use tiered retention (hot storage first 90 days, warm next 275 days) — we can surface the cost delta in the table attached.

I've attached a short pricing packet with two scenarios:
A) Usage-first (recommended for pilot): monthly charge = ingestion GB * $0.28 + dashboard units * $15; includes 90d hot + warm tiering for the remainder.
B) Seat bundle: $1,200/month per admin seat + 13-month retention add-on priced as a per-GB surcharge.
