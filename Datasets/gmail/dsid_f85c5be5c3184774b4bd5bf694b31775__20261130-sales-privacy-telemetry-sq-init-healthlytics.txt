Thanks Marta — got the VSQ. A few clarifying answers inline and next actions: 

Inline answers:
- Request-level telemetry: by default our hosted plan collects request metadata (timestamps, latency, model id, token counts) and retains raw request/response content for 30 days for diagnostics. Customers can configure shorter retention or opt into longer retention for troubleshooting. For Private/Dedicated customers, we can configure zero retention for requests or route logs to customers storage.
- Storage/region: hosted telemetry is stored in the customer-selected region (we currently support us-west-2, us-east-1, eu-west-1). We replicate control-plane logs as needed for durability but do not move raw request content out of the chosen region unless the customer opts in.
- Docs site cookies: default is analytics (GA) and functional cookies. There are no advertising or cross-site ad trackers in our docs cluster unless a customer installs third-party widgets. We can provide a CSV cookie inventory and enable a stricter consent mode for your team.
- HIPAA/BAA: we do provide BAAs for customers with appropriate contracts; typically we recommend Private deployment or Dedicated capacity for PHI to reduce surface area. Hosted can be scoped but requires specific controls and ingestion patterns.
