2. Measurement: Availability is measured as the percentage of successful API responses for production routes as instrumented by Redwood's telemetry, aggregated over the calendar month. Latency p95 is measured server-side from request receipt to completion (excluding customer network egress).
3. Maintenance: Planned maintenance will be scheduled in the default window of 02:00–04:00 local time with >=72 hours' notice; emergency maintenance is allowed with as much notice as possible and excluded from availability calculations only when used for critical security fixes.
4. Remedies: For availability breaches, Redwood will provide a credit schedule: 0.5% credit for a single-month drop below the commitment, increasing to 2% for repeated breaches within a three-month rolling window. For emergency maintenance causing >30 minutes downtime outside the planned window, Redwood will provide a 2x credit and a postmortem within 7 business days.
5. Escalation: Redwood will provide a named escalation contact for on-call response (24/7 phone + email) and a separate operational runbook for postmortem and mitigation steps.
6. Scope: The commitments above apply to traffic within the committed order-form capacity (QPS) and region; cross-region failover behavior is described in Appendix B (attached).
