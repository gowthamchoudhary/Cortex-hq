Hi Sarah — thanks for the note and the usage file. Short answers: yes (we support a mid-cycle top-up) and yes (we can co-term the added capacity). Some details and options below so you can pick the path that matches procurement cadence: 

Option A — Simple top-up (shortest path)
- One-time mid-contract top-up for the 4 months. Billed pro-rata for the remainder of your billing period.
- No price change to base units; uplift billed at standard list price.
- Recommended when speed is priority and procurement prefers a single invoice.

Option B — Phased uplift w/ temporary incentive (proposed)
- Commit to the uplift for 6 months. We can offer a limited-term 12% discount on the uplifted units for that 6-month window.
- After 6 months, uplift either rolls back or converts to permanent increment at list price (your choice).
- We can co-term the uplift to your annual renewal date when converting to permanent.

Option C — Annualize now (co-term immediately)
- Annualize the uplift and move the whole capacity to a new co-term with an annual pricing tier. Requires legal amendment and sign-off.  
- Better for customers who expect sustained higher usage beyond the 6-month window.

Operational notes:
- If you want the 12% promotional incentive, we need internal approval from CRO and Finance (I can start that thread).
- For billing, we can do an invoice split (50/50) or a single invoice with line-item notes — whichever helps MediArc's PO processing.

Next steps: can you confirm (1) desired option (A/B/C), (2) billing preference (single invoice vs split), and (3) whether procurement wants a 6-month or 12-month commitment? I can get a formal proposal and draft amendment ready.

Thanks — happy to hop on 30 mins if that helps.
