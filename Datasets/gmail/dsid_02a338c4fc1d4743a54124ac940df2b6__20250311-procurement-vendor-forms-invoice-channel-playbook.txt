Hi Natalie — great to hear from you. Short answers and next steps below.

Channel: Portal-first. We prefer vendors upload to our supplier portal (or SFTP for high-volume partners) because it provides deterministic parsing and faster AP processing. Email to payments@redwood.com is allowed as a fallback for early invoices; when using email please put the onboarding token in the subject like: ACME-REDWOOD-PO.

CSV / PDF handling: We can accept either, but the most reliable ingestion is a CSV with one line per invoice line-item and a PO column. If you submit a combined PDF only, our ops team will do a manual ingestion which increases reconciliation time and may delay payment. For multi-PO invoices we ask vendors to either: (a) include a PO column in the CSV mapping each line to a PO; or (b) if sending a single PDF, include a cover page that enumerates the PO mappings in a simple table.

Minimal CSV header we prefer (you can follow this exactly):
invoice_number, invoice_date (YYYY-MM-DD), vendor_name, net_amount, tax_amount, currency, po_number, line_description, qty, unit_price, tax_code, remittance_id

Example row (pasteable):
INV-202503-001,2025-03-01,Acme Co,1200.00,0.00,USD,1001;2002,Cloud credits - March,1,1200.00,TX0,ACME-001

Notes:
- For multi-PO line allocations put PO numbers separated by semicolons in po_number and add an extra row per PO if you need line-level splits.
- remittance_id is your internal reference that will flow to GL so please populate consistently.
- Attach vendor_onboarding_form.pdf (fill & return) so we can provision your supplier record and issue the portal credentials.

Thanks Monica — quick procurement note from my side: we require that each invoice references all applicable PO numbers. Preferred pattern is to have each invoice line tagged with a single PO. If a single charge must be split across multiple POs, provide either (A) per-line CSV rows that allocate amounts to each PO (preferred), or (B) a cover sheet with a clear allocation table.

A few operational constraints to be aware of:
- If we receive only a combined PDF without machine-readable PO mapping, the invoice may be routed to manual review and matched at the invoice level (not per-line), which can block automated PO match and delay payment.
- For multi-entity POs we need the billing_entity column in the CSV to map to the correct legal entity. If you're sending invoices that hit multiple legal entities, include billing_entity and remit_to fields.

Happy to review the test CSV once you send it.
