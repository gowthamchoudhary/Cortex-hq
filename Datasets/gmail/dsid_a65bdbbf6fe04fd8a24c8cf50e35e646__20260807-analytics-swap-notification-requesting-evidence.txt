1) Residency: StreamMeter will run regional ingestion endpoints. EU tenant logs will remain in EU processing clusters (Ireland and Frankfurt) and will not be replicated to US clusters by default. We provided the updated dataflow here: https://docs.streammeter.com/dataflow/streammeter-eu-us (see EU region diagram).
2) Keys: StreamMeter currently offers vendor-managed KMS for the multi-tenant pipeline. They have a roadmap for customer KMS binding (estimated Q4 2026). For now they provide AES-256 at rest and TLS in transit; key rotation is automated but keys are managed by StreamMeter.
