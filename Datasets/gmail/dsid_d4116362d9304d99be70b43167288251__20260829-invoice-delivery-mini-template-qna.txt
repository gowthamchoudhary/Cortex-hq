Hi Emma — thanks, and welcome!

Short answer: we prefer the vendor portal for onboarding and invoices (more reliable and provides automatic ACKs). However, we do allow encrypted/email fallback for smaller partners during the first 60 days while portal access is being provisioned. For long-term cost & SLA reasons we ask vendors to migrate to the portal.

Minimum required invoice fields when submitting via email (CSV or in the body):
- Redwood PO number (exact match)
- Invoice number (unique)
- Invoice date (YYYY-MM-DD)
- Line description / SKU (single line per item preferred)
- Line amount (net) and currency
- Total tax amount and tax ID (if applicable)

Optional but helpful: unit qty/unit price, department/team code.

I attached a very small mini-template (jonas_mini_template.csv) you can drop into your export. It is intentionally compact — we find it speeds reconciliation. If you'll be on email fallback, please include a one-line csv header and submit as a .csv attachment; that avoids OCR errors.

Example (first two lines of the CSV file):
"PO,InvoiceNumber,InvoiceDate,LineDesc,Qty,UnitPrice,LineTotal,Currency,TaxID"
"PO-10293,INV-2026-334,2026-08-25,Cloud inference credits,1,12000.00,12000.00,USD,12-3456789"

We'll need the onboarding form signed and a remit-to address. After that Kim will issue a vendor code and final PO enrollment.

This sounds good. A couple of operational clarifications from AP: 
1) We require the Redwood PO number on the invoice (visible near the top) for automated matching — otherwise the invoice goes to exceptions.
2) For vendors billing US entities, please include vendor tax ID (EIN) and remit-to address. For EMEA vendors we need VAT registration number and the billing legal entity.
3) Payment terms: default is Net 45 unless a contract specifies otherwise — please confirm which terms StellarNet expects so we can set payment terms in the portal.

If the vendor will be sending invoices by email during the temporary fallback, please tell them to put "AP-BILLING" in the subject and BCC ap-ops@redwood.ai so our parser can pick it up.
