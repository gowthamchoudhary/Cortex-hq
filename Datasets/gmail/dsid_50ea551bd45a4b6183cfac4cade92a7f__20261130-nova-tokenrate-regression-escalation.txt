On the initial look: we have a kernel-selector policy change that flipped a heuristic for selecting quantized kernels on longer sequences at ~02:58 UTC (server logs: kernel_roll_log.txt attached). The change was intended to prefer newer int8 kernels for better cost/TPS on typical workloads, but the selector is mis-evaluating model sequence-length distribution for this customer's route and ends up using a slower emulation path for some batches.

Preliminary findings:
- Observed tokens/sec drop: ~35-45% for NovaCommerce workload shape (many short messages but frequent context prefix hits).
- p95/p99 increase lines up with batches hitting a fallback path that does extra dequantize/reorder work.
- Error spike tied to an edge-case where a kernel probe returned an unexpected capability flag and triggered a slow fallback; we see increased CPU stalls on the host.

Attached: perf_trace_20261129.json (they sent), and our kernel_roll_log.txt with timestamps. I'll push a hotfix to the selector to short-circuit the problematic probe and revert the heuristic by EOD unless QA objects. Will post diffs.
