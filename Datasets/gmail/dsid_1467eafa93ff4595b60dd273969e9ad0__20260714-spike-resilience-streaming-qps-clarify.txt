Markus — thanks for the clear recap. Attached is a first dump of our 24-hour window that includes per-second QPS and latency buckets; we labeled the three spikes we talked about. A few clarifications inline: 

- Concurrency: long streams are held per-user session (WebSocket) and go through our gateway. We typically see a 1:1 mapping from session to backend connection but our gateway multiplexes short-lived chats into pooled workers for efficiency.
- Peak behavior: the end-of-day sustained peak is tied to our reconciliation jobs that fan out conversational prompts to users; those are often batched in the gateway and can result in many concurrent long-stream sessions starting within ~2 minutes.
- Fallback: we can accept a fallback to a lower-capacity model for up to 3 minutes during extreme events but prefer graceful degradation (reduced token budget) over abrupt rate limiting.
