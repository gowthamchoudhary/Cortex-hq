Great questions, Alex — short answers below and we can cover details on the ops call.

1) Ratecaps: Hosted supports both per-API-key and per-endpoint limits. For multi-tenant simulation you can:
   - Create separate API keys per simulated client and apply per-key caps, or
   - Use per-endpoint caps combined with per-key caps for tighter control.
   (Ben will share a recommended mapping based on your expected concurrency.)

2) KV/prefix caching: warm-up is traffic-driven (we cache frequently seen prefixes and KV entries at inference runtime). There's no manual "push" step for customers — you just need a warm-up window or targeted synthetic traffic to prime the cache. We’ll include recommended warm-up scripts in the quickstart.

3) Logs/retention: default telemetry exports (request metadata, token counts, latency) are retained for 30 days on Hosted; raw request/response bodies are not retained by default unless you opt-in. We can provide a daily export to your S3 bucket for 30 days (or a shorter window) if that helps debugging without long-term retention.
