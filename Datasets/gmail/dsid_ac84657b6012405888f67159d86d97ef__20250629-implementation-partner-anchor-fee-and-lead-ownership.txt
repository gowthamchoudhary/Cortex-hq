Jamie — thanks for the clear note and the attachments. Quick answers up front:
- Anchor fee: Redwood typically treats the anchor as a non-refundable commitment payment but credits it against the first invoice if the customer proceeds within 120 days. We can consider refundable terms for pilot-only engagements but prefer crediting for conversion.
- Success fee timing: we usually scope success payments at a 30-day post-go-live usage milestone (healthy token/usage thresholds). 90 days is common for larger enterprise deals — we can be flexible by tier.
- Lead registration: minimum evidence is the intro email + a preserved SFDC opportunity id and a signed PO or SOW. For priority protection we require registration before demo or before PO (whichever is earlier).
- Rev-share: for combined delivery + referral we typically split implementation margin differently from ARR rev-share. For implementation-only partners we pay a flat implementation fee (project-based). For joint-sell/ISV-style packages the common range is 10–20% of first-year ARR as a referral/rev-share.
