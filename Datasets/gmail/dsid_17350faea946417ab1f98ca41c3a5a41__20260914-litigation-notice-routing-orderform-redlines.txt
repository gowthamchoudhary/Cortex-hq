Elena / Grace — I reviewed NexCorp's redlines and drafted a concise compromise. Summary (see attached Proposed_Indemnity_Redline.docx):

1) Defense control: Redwood will assume sole control of defense for covered third-party IP claims. Redwood will provide NexCorp with written notice of the claim within 5 business days of receiving it and will keep NexCorp reasonably informed.

2) Settlement approvals: Redwood will not settle any claim that (a) admits fault of NexCorp or (b) imposes ongoing obligations on NexCorp without NexCorp's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. For monetary-only settlements, Redwood may settle without NexCorp's consent if Redwood's insurer is paying and the settlement does not exceed $25,000.

3) Scope carveouts: Claims arising solely from (i) customer content, (ii) customer modifications, or (iii) use outside the Documentation are excluded from Redwood's obligation to defend/indemnify.

4) Notice & evidence: NexCorp agrees to provide initial notice reasonably promptly and to preserve relevant evidence for at least 10 business days. Redwood will provide a request list within 7 business days of notice.

5) Cap & remedies: Redwood proposes a liability cap equal to the greater of (i) insurance proceeds actually available and (ii) 12 months of fees paid under the Order Form. This is in lieu of uncapped liability for IP.

6) Escalation path: If Redwood declines defense, Redwood will provide written reasons and designate a senior counsel contact; both parties will escalate to execs (legal + account lead) within 3 business days to attempt a resolution before NexCorp may seek other remedies.

I attached the redline with insertions and version comments for clarity. If you can accept this form in principle, procurement can proceed while legal finalizes bracketed language for the cap and timelines.
