Inventory snapshot (us-west) as of 2026-11-16:
- H100 80GB available: 22 units (slots suitable for inference pods)
- H100 80GB with maintenance windows: 4 units (limited between 12/20-12/23)
- A100 80GB available: 30 units
- Mixed-precision quantized options (4-bit) supported on subset of A100s — reduces footprint by ~30% if acceptable

Suggested approaches:
A) Soft-hold (no immediate fee): reserve 18 inference lanes mapped to 18 H100 slots; not guaranteed against emergency infra rebalancing but first-priority for allocation. Recommended if Vanguard is still finalizing procurement.
- Impact: low internal friction; 1 business-day notice required to release.
- Feasibility: 16/18 lanes map directly to idle H100s, 2 lanes would fall back to A100 or be throttled in peak windows.

B) Provisional reserve (recommended for SLA): reserve 18 lanes via provisional reservation contract — includes a small hold fee (1.5% of projected monthly commit) and an explicit capacity guarantee for the window 2026-12-15 -> 2027-01-31.
- Impact: stronger guarantee for the customer and less operational juggling for us.
- Feasibility: can be fulfilled by combining 14 H100 80GB + 4 A100 80GB with quantization for two lanes.

Risks & mitigations:
- Holiday maintenance spike (12/20-12/27): we'll mark those 4 units as constrained; recommend advising customer to avoid critical ramp during that week or accept temporary fallback.
- If Vanguard later expands beyond 18 lanes, we should trigger a quota re-evaluation and provide a 48-hour provisioning estimate.

- Diego: confirm which option Vanguard prefers; if they want provisional reserve, provide signature timeline.
