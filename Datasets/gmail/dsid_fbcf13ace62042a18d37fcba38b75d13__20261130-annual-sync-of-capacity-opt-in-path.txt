   - The uplift (200k tokens) is billed as part of next year’s commitment and will be priced at your current tier rate.
   - No additional discount; clean accounting.
2) Annualize + tiered uplift discount (recommended for expansion)
   - Move the 200k to next year and apply a one‑time uplift discount based on increment:
     - +25% uplift: 10% discount on uplift portion
     - +50% uplift: 15% discount
     - +100% uplift: 20% discount
   - Proration: we credit the remaining days of the current top‑up and bill the annualized amount on renewal date.
3) Hybrid co‑term path (spread + annualize)
   - We split the uplift: remaining months of current term are credited; the rest is split across the new fiscal year with a small promotional 5% onboarding credit.
   - Useful if you want budget smoothing and immediate capacity without full annual commitment.
