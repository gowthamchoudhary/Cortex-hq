Hi Linda — really appreciate the clear asks. Attaching a redacted sample CSV export and our standard commit schedule PDF to illustrate. A quick summary below; full details are in the attachments and the HubSpot deal record.

TL;DR
- Per-token generation (prompt+completion) is billed at model-specific per-token rates. Embeddings are billed separately under the "embeddings" line item.
