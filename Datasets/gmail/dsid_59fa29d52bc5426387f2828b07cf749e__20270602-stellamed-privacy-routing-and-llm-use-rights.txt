Hi Elena — great to hear from you and thanks for the clear list. Summary answers up front, then I attach a redline proposal for the DPA:

1) Model improvement: Redwood will not use Customer Content to train, fine-tune, or improve any model unless there is an explicit, written agreement that permits model improvement. We can include an opt-in appendix for any future experiments (customer must opt-in and sign an amendment).

2) Subprocessors & notifications: We will provide the current subprocessor registry and notify you 30 days prior to onboarding any new subprocessor that would process StellaMed data. For subprocessors that will access or persist sensitive categories (including PHI), we will route those workloads to onshore-only pools or use a named subprocessor that meets your US-residency requirement.

3) Geo-routing: We support region pinning for inference and storage. We can configure a US-only tenancy for your account with explicit routing policy in the SOW.

4) Prompt/response rights: Proposed clause — Customer retains ownership of prompts and outputs; Redwood only receives a limited license to operate the service. We will not assert derivative rights over generated responses.

I'm attaching a draft redline that reflects the above (see redline_stellamed_dpa_v3.docx). Happy to schedule a short call if counsel wants to walk through language.

Thanks David — this is helpful. Draft language suggestions below (legal redline in Drive & DocuSign link to send after you confirm):

- No-model-improvement clause (contract text suggestion): 
  "Provider shall not use, access, or process Customer Content for the purpose of training, improving, or deriving model parameters for any general-purpose models or shared model artifacts, unless Customer provides an express, written, and separate opt-in amendment. Provider shall document any such opt-in in an amendment and shall provide Customer with the scope of data, processing locations, and subprocessors involved."

- Subprocessor routing clause: 
  "For any Subprocessor that will access, persist, or process Sensitive Customer Content (including PHI), Provider shall route such processing to infrastructure located within the United States and shall provide Customer with a Subprocessor Attestation containing the Subprocessor's name, security certification(s), jurisdiction of processing, and a summary of controls."

- Prompt/Response IP: 
  "Customer retains all right, title and interest in and to Customer Content and Outputs. Provider shall have a limited, non-exclusive license to process Outputs solely to provide the Service and for debugging and support purposes; Provider shall not assert ownership or derivative rights in Outputs."

Operational evidence we can provide: subprocessor registry snapshot (CSV), redacted SOC 2 attestations, region-specific KMS key metadata (region tag), and a network egress IP list for the US-only pool. I attached a working redline (redline_stellamed_dpa_v3.docx) and put a copy in Drive: https://drive.redwood.ai/shared/stellamed/dpa-redline-v3

Sales: offering a 6-month US-only pilot (no model improvement) with monthly checkpoint deliverables (attestation + registry snapshots) is reasonable. Recommend we include that in the SOW as a risk-reduction pilot.
