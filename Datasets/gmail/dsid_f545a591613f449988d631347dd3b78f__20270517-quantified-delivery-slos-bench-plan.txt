Latency targets: we can commit to P95 tail <= 180ms for the standard batching profile (512-token requests, dynamic batching enabled) under the accepted test load. For lower-latency requirements we offer a low-batch profile (reduced batching at higher cost) with P95 <= 120ms — we should scope that separately.

Measurement methodology (short):
1) Define canonical request set: you provide a 2k-sample set representing production payloads (we can redact).
2) Use continuous replay agent: open connections / ramp to target QPS, run 3 independent windows of 30 minutes each during business hours, collect per-token throughput, P50/P95/P99 latency, and errors.
3) Warm KV cache for 20 minutes before measurement window (we'll include cold-start and warmed scenarios in appendix).
4) Report accepted: tokens/sec (sustained average over window), requests/sec (sustained), P95 latency under load, and error rate.

Acceptance and credits: if measured sustained tokens/sec falls below committed band by >10% over the averaged windows, we propose crediting 10% of monthly fee for that month; for >25% shortfall, 25% credit and remediation runway. We are open to tighter credit terms in exchange for larger commits.

Pricing/discount bands (12-month prepay examples):
- 4-GPU commit: list $X/month -> 10% discount for 12mo prepay.
