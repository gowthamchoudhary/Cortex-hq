Hi Olivia — thanks for the note and congrats on the demo outcome. Putting a short, pragmatic answer here and attaching a sample invoice and a one-pager. Looping Monica from billing for invoice-format specifics.

Quick summary (hosted API):
- Baseline (US-hosted) generation: $6.00 per 1M tokens (applies to prompt + completion token usage).
- Baseline (US-hosted) embeddings: $0.90 per 1k vectors.
- EU residency adder: typically +10% on the token/vector charge when you require EU-only processing and storage (applies to hosted API instances running in our EU region and where we pin logs/data to EU-only). This is billed as a separate invoice line labeled "EU Residency Surcharge" (not as egress).
- Currency: We invoice in USD by default; for EUR invoicing we can convert at month-end and show EUR invoice lines.

Example (rounded):
- Hosted API (US region): Generation 1M tokens x $6.00 = $6.00
- EU Residency Surcharge (10% of generation) = $0.60
- Total effective cost per 1M tokens (EU residency) = $6.60

Committed discounts: they apply to the base token/vector rate before region adders. So a 10% commit discount reduces the $6.00 base to $5.40, then the EU adder is applied to the discounted base (10% of $5.40).
