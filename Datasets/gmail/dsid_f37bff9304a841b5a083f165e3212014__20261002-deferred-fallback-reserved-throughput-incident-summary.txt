Adding a quick engineering triage summary (what we see on our side):
- At 08:41 PT we observed a reduction in available GPUs in Finexa's dedicated pool: delta = -18% (3 nodes cycled due to out-of-band host reboots). No planned maintenance shows up for those hosts.
- Queue depth increased from baseline ~40 to ~420; continuous batching was unable to keep up which drove token-generation latency higher and resulted in gateway timeouts as upstream LB retries exhausted.
- Error breakdown: 502 (upstream gateway) + 504 (timeout) increase to ~10% combined between 08:45–09:15. P95 moved to ~3.9s, p99 ~8.6s.

Immediate mitigations executed (09:30):
- Rebalanced active sessions across remaining nodes to avoid hot shards.
- Temporarily allowed controlled burst to Redwood hosted pool for low-priority routes (we excluded critical Finexa models; confirmed with Hannah).
- Increased per-customer concurrency limit for Finexa routes from 20 -> 30 to drain the backlog.
