Attached is the redacted sample we prepared: nova_admin_events_sample_redacted.csv (75 rows). Quick operational notes:

- Schema: event_time_utc (RFC3339), original_timezone, event_type, actor_id_token, target_resource, ip_hmac, session_id, request_id, change_summary
- Redaction: ip_hmac uses HMAC-SHA256 with export-specific 12-byte salt; actor_id_token uses reversible tokenization stored in a secure vault (we include only token IDs in the sample).
- Retention: admin-events raw (per-tenant): 90 days full fidelity. Search indices / aggregates: 365 days. Historical archives (beyond 365) are available via reserved-retention option.
- Exclusions: we do NOT include full prompt/request payloads in admin-event exports; KV-cache keys are not included and are documented in the README.
- Timezones: we keep event_time_utc and original_timezone (ISO offset). When we normalize we do not shift the event semantics — the timestamp_utc is derived from the server-recorded instant and the original_timezone is preserved for audit trace.
