Hi Alex / LedgerPoint team,

Thank you for your patience while we completed the post-incident analysis for the event on Jan 14 that resulted in a short window of duplicated inference requests for a subset of your traffic. We know this impacted throughput accounting and caused a small number of doubled outputs — we apologize for the disruption.

Summary (tl;dr):
- What happened: A race condition in the request deduplication layer allowed duplicate in-flight requests for ~3 minutes (00:14:22–00:17:16 UTC).
- Impact: ~0.08% of transactions in that window for your account saw duplicated token outputs. No tokens were delivered to third parties; data residency and encryption remained intact.
- Root cause: A recent optimization to the async request coalescer introduced a timing window during high request fan-in where cache insertions could be skipped.
- Remediation & verification actions we completed: rollback of the faulty optimization, additional unit+integration tests, deploy of a hotfix with deterministic locking, and an automated replay-based verification job keyed to your account.

Attached: postincident_summary_ledgerpoint.pdf (full RCA), evidence_bundle_ledgerpoint.zip (request traces and sanitized logs), verification_checklist_ledgerpoint.csv (step-by-step checklist we recommend for your verification run).
