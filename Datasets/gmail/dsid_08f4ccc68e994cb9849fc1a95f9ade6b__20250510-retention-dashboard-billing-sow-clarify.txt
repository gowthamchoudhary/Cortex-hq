Anna — hi, Kimberly here (SE). Below is a worked example and a technical note about rollover. Also attaching a draft SOW that maps line items to invoice-friendly labels.

Worked example (approx) for 8 TiB/month ingest
- Ingest band pricing: 0–5 TiB @ $X per GiB-month, 5–20 TiB @ $Y per GiB-month. For modeling, we convert TiB -> GiB (8 TiB = 8192 GiB).
- Consumption this month: 5 TiB billed at band 1 + 3 TiB billed at band 2 -> blended unit price.
- If you take the monthly slab (0–5, 5–20), you'd pay the slab price for the covered volume and overage triggers at the band 2 rate for excess.

Dashboard mapping suggestion
- Power users (25): dashboard seat entitlement required.
- Viewers (100): viewer role can be a free or low-cost seat depending on feature access; we propose grouping viewers into a 10-viewer seat SKU for billing simplicity.

Rollover technical note
- Rollover does not change retention guarantees. It simply credits unused GiB-months against future consumption under the same contract year. It does require a committed annual volume (minimum) and is not available for month-to-month paygo. Rollover does not change sampling or SLA behavior; your retention window remains the same.
