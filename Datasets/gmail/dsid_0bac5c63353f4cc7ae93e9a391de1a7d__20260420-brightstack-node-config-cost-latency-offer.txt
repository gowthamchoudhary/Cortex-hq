Sara —

Great to meet you and Jordan yesterday. High-level answers up front and I’ve attached a small throughput model (throughput_model.xlsx) plus a baseline proposal.

- Short answer on perf/$: H100 gives the best raw throughput for large-LLM workloads but A100 (80GB) often wins on perf/$ for mid-sized models because memory headroom reduces token-level fragmentation; L40S is compelling for chat-heavy, low-latency small-model routing because of lower cost per card but will saturate for the 70B variant.

- Memory impact: models that require >40GB KV/cache memory (70B) push you to 80GB-class cards or require sharding which increases latency. For your 13B opt model, an L40S or A100 40GB can be efficient if we use prefix caching aggressively.

- Commit tiers: we usually show a three-pack: entry (4–8 cards), growth (12–32 cards), and committed (64+ cards). Each step unlocks deeper slab discounts and burst buffers. I put sample perf/$ numbers in the sheet — they assume 70/30 traffic split, the traffic profile you described, and conservative batching.
