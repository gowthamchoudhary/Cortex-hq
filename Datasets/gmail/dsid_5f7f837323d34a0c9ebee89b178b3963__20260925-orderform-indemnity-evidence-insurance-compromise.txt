Proposed compromise (to be inserted into the MSA/Order Form):
1) Trigger & scope: Redwood will indemnify and, at Redwood's option, defend CloudShift against third‑party claims that allege infringement by Redwood‑supplied models/weights as delivered. The indemnity excludes claims arising from (a) Customer modifications to models, (b) Customer‑provided prompts or data that materially change output, and (c) third‑party components incorporated via Customer.
2) Defense control: Redwood will have the right to assume defense with counsel of its choice. If CloudShift reasonably believes the claim would materially affect its rights, CloudShift may (i) assume control of the defense within 30 days of notice; and (ii) Redwood will not unreasonably withhold consent to settlement that binds CloudShift.
3) Cap & calculation: Liability cap is the greater of $1,000,000 or 12 months of Subscription Fees paid by CloudShift under the Order Form in the 12 months preceding the claim. We can agree to revisit the cap in renewal conversations; if CloudShift needs a multiple of 18 months for procurement sign‑off, we can offer a managed escalation to $X for a 12‑month price uplift (procurement to confirm).
4) Insurance & offsets: Redwood will provide a certificate of insurance (commercial general liability and professional liability limits) and insurance recoveries will reduce Redwood's indemnity to the extent of actual payments by the insurer. Settlement control language will require consent of both parties if the settlement would release CloudShift from liability or materially bind its operations.
5) Evidence & retention: Redwood requests an evidence package (sample outputs, timestamps, model id, call traces, and a description of downstream commercialization) to be provided promptly. Redwood can commit to preserving call traces and per‑request metadata for 12 months; aggregated metrics and sampled outputs will be retained for 18 months on request for triage. If CloudShift requires full‑fidelity logs for 18 months that are beyond our standard retention, that can be added as a paid add‑on.

If this looks agreeable, we can produce a clean order form reflecting these bullets and share a DocuSign envelope.
