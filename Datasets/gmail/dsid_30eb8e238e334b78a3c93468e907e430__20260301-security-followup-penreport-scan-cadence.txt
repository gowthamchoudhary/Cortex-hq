Hi all — thanks for the context, Liam. Short answers and attachments below.

Pen-test & scan scope (what we typically provide):
- Annual external penetration test (3rd-party vendor) covering public network perimeter and externally facing application endpoints.
- Application-layer assessments include authenticated web-app testing, common business logic checks, and OWASP Top 10 coverage.
- Internal network tests are performed for Dedicated/Private customers by request (add-on) and included for on-premises installs.

Scanning cadence & toolset:
- Weekly authenticated SCA (software composition analysis) for dependency vulnerabilities.
- Continuous DAST for production endpoints with daily non-intrusive scans and scheduled deeper scans weekly.
- Monthly internal vulnerability scans (Nessus/Qualys) where applicable; quarterly full infrastructure scans.

Vulnerability SLA & disclosure process:
- Severity tiers: Critical (CVSS >=9), High (CVSS 7-8.9), Medium (4-6.9), Low (<4).
- Initial acknowledgment: 24 hours for Critical/High, 72 hours for Medium.
- Target remediation cadence: Critical — mitigation or workaround within 7 days, permanent fix in 30 days; High — fix in 30 days; Medium — plan in 60 days; Low — triage into normal release cadence.
- We provide tracking updates via our ticketing system and an executive summary for major incidents.
- Responsible disclosure: we operate a coordinated disclosure process and will notify impacted customers within 48 hours of confirmation for Critical issues.

Artifacts we can share:
- SOC 2 Type II report (redacted where needed) — attached.
- Executive pen-test summary (we typically share full reports under NDA; otherwise we can provide a 2-page summary and remediation attestation).
- Monthly scan digest (sample PDF) and CVE tracking export.
