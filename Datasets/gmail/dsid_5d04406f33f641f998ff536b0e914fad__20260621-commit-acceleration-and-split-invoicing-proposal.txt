Thanks Elena — great context. Looping in Lauren (pricing) and Ben (finance ops) to validate the invoicing flow. High-level answers up front:

- Pro-rata: we calculate based on the committed token units and the portion of the month remaining. For a 6-month uplift we can either bill the uplift monthly over the 6 months or bill it up-front and credit over the remainder. For this case I recommend monthly capture to align cost to usage unless Procurement prefers advance billing.

- Split invoicing: operationally possible. We can issue an interim "top-up" invoice (monthly or quarterly) tied to the uplift, and keep the annual co-term renewal invoice separate. Finance will need the amendment signed to link the uplift to the existing contract so that accounting treats them as the same deal.

- Uplift discount: as a ballpark, for a 30% short-term uplift we can offer a 5–12% discount on the incremental capacity depending on whether you commit to annualize (higher discount for an annualize commitment). Quick pricing note for D-45321:

Suggested commercial approach (workable operationally and clean for accounting):
- Structure: 30% uplift for 6 months as a separate amendment line item. If RetailCo annualizes after the 6 months, we convert that uplift into an ongoing commit and apply a retroactive credit for the months charged at uplift rate (effectively the annualized rate minus interim paid).
- Discounting: 8% off the incremental units for the 6-month uplift. If they commit to annualize within 45 days of uplift end, discount becomes 12% and remaining months are repriced to the annualized rate.
- Invoicing cadence: monthly uplift invoices (preferred) OR quarterly if procurement requires fewer invoices. Finance notes monthly is heavier operationally but standard; quarterly is doable with a small administrative fee.
- Billing split example (numbers rounded):
  * Current annual commit: 1,000k units at $0.045/unit = $45,000/yr
  * 30% uplift = +300k units over 6 months ~ 50k units/month.
  * Monthly uplift charge at $0.0414/unit (8% off) = ~$2,070/month for uplift.
  * If annualized and 12% applies, blended credit would be issued on conversion.
