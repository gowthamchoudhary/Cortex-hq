Hi team — ran numbers with Finance and Product. Attached is a short term-sheet sketch and a spreadsheet with monthly burn scenarios. Key highlights (Option A vs Option B):

Option A — Auto-top-up with buffer tranche (recommended for low friction):
- Structure: Customer prepays 50% of Y1 committed dollars upfront; remaining Y1 committed amount is invoiced in two equal installments (month 6 and month 12). Years 2–3 invoiced annually at year start.
- Ledger: unused credits within a contract year roll forward to later quarters within the same year. Credits expire at year boundary unless customer opts into an annual carry-forward addendum (subject to fee).
- Auto-top-up: when remaining annual credits < 15%, system will automatically purchase a pre-agreed buffer tranche equal to 10% of annual committed dollars at the discounted committed rate. Customer can opt for a 72-hr grace delay before auto-purchase.
- Discounts: unit price discount tiers tied to total committed ARR: 12% at $1–1.5M/year; 18% at $1.5–2.5M/year; 24% > $2.5M/year. For a 3-year program we can structure year-over-year step-down pricing or lock the year-1 discounted rate for all three years with an annual CPI adjustment.
- Early termination: standard clawback on unearned discount if the customer terminates early (prorated recoupment).

Option B — Notification + manual top-up (conservative, lower revenue certainty):
- Same ledger rules. Top-up is a notification to FP&A and the named billing contact; if not approved within 72 hrs, service continues until buffer exhausted then falls back to standard on-demand rates.
- Pricing: top-up tranche priced at blended committed rate + 3% administrative fee.
