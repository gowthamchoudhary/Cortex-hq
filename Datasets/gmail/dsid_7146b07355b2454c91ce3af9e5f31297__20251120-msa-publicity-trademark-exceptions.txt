Karthik / Marissa,

Thanks - reviewed Acme's markup. Below are recommended redlines and rationale. I have kept the language narrow to preserve our commercial needs while addressing their concerns.

Proposed redlines (summary):
1) Publicity/Press Releases
   - Redwood may list Customer in an online customer list and cite Customer as a customer in sales materials without advance consent.
   - For press releases, case studies, or joint marketing that reference Customer by name or logo, require prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
   - Include a 10-business-day review period. Customer may request removal or edits for trademark accuracy.

2) Trademark use
   - Permit limited logo use for listing purposes and agreed co-branded assets. Any use beyond the list requires Customer's trademark guidelines compliance and prior approval.
   - Add an objective misuse standard: Trademark misuse that materially and demonstrably harms the Customer's brand qualifies as misconduct.

3) Limitation of Liability and Carve-outs
   - Retain overall liability cap at the greater of fees paid in prior 12 months or the Order Form commit, but exclude from the cap: (a) Redwood's willful misconduct or gross negligence, (b) IP indemnity breaches, and (c) narrow, demonstrable reputational harms stemming from unauthorized publicity or trademark misuse.
   - To avoid open-ended exposure, require Customer to provide specific evidence of reputational harm and allow Redwood a 30-day cure period before remedies beyond specific performance are available.

Rationale: we need to avoid a broad reputational carve-out that becomes an effective unlimited liability bucket. The notice/cure requirement and objective standard for misuse limit exposure while being commercially reasonable.

I can draft the exact MSA redline if you want me to send it to Acme. Suggestions for commercial trade-offs if Acme insists on broader language: tie the reputational carve-out cap to a multiple of the Order Form value (e.g., 2x) or restrict such carve-outs to a specific product line.

Sarah,

Thanks for the edits. After internal review, here is Redwood's consolidated counterproposal to address your concerns while keeping a manageable risk profile:

- Customer lists and partner directories: permitted without prior approval.
- Press releases / joint marketing referencing Customer by name or logo: require prior written consent; consent not to be unreasonably withheld; 10 business day review window.
- Trademark/logo use: permitted for listing purposes; any co-branded asset requires Customer's trademark guidelines to be followed and prior approval.
- Liability carve-out: reputational harms from unauthorized publicity or trademark misuse will be excluded from the liability cap only where: (a) Customer demonstrates material and measurable harm, and (b) Redwood is given a 30-day cure period. Remedies beyond specific performance or injunctive relief must be proportionate and, if monetary, capped at 2x the fees paid under the relevant Order Form for the affected product line.

We are keeping standard exclusions from the liability cap for willful misconduct and IP indemnity claims. If Acme needs a higher monetary ceiling for reputational damages, we can consider a negotiated multiplier tied to the Order Form value.
