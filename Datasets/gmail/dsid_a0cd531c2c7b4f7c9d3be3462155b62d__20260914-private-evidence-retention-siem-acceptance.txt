Kimberly / Alex — quick technical clarifications on tracing and telemetry delivery:

- Traces: we'll provide OTEL proto JSON examples showing header propagation and baggage usage. For the acceptance run, set sampling to 100% for a 48-hour window; after acceptance we suggest probabilistic sampling (0.5-2%) depending on qps.
- Spans: examples will include the runtime inference span, network sidecar span, and exporter span with clear start/stop timestamps so you can verify ordering in your SIEM.
- Delivery: 
  * Splunk HEC: we support HEC over TLS with token auth. We'll include a HEC payload example and recommended batch_size and max_events_per_request.
  * Syslog over TLS: RFC5424-compliant messages; we recommend facility=local0 and structured-data fields for service and customer_id.
- Rate limiting & backpressure: the private control plane includes configurable backpressure; for your validation plan, we'll demonstrate burst handling with a dedicated forwarder and a traffic ramp script.
