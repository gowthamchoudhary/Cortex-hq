Hi Audra — thanks for the clear asks. I captured the short answers below and attached our operational playbook summary for Dedicated customers. Happy to jump on a 30-min call if you want to walk through the purge procedure live.

1) Region pinning / locality
- For Dedicated deployments we offer region pinning at the cluster level. Tenant compute and primary storage are provisioned in the pinned region and are not actively replicated to other regions for normal operation.
- Backups (daily snapshots) are stored in an encrypted object store. By default backups are stored in the same region; cross-region replication is an opt-in feature for customers that request multi-region DR (this is a billable add-on and is documented in the Dedicated configuration form).
- Short form: primary data and live KV caches remain in the pinned region; backups are same-region unless explicitly configured for replication.

2) Retention & deletion workflow
- Default retention: request/interaction logs: 90 days; KV cache ephemeral entries: TTL-controlled by customer; system-level hold: 7 days for transient artifacts before archiving.
- Customer-initiated purge (tenant wipe) — high-level timeline:
  * T+0: Customer requests purge via Console or signed support ticket.
  * T+24: Ops kick off tenant suspension and begin KV cache invalidation.
  * T+72: Primary storage deletion complete (logical deletion + verification).
  * T+7 days: Snapshot rotation ensures deleted data is no longer present in retained snapshot set; for customers requiring accelerated snapshot purge we provide a certificate of destruction after manual removal (subject to backup retention policies).
- Note: If cross-region replication was enabled, purge includes replicated copies; additional coordination may extend completion time (we'll enumerate per-region timelines in the full playbook).

Audra — adding a few operational clarifications and the wipe checklist as requested.

- Wipe checklist highlights:
  * Confirm tenant suspension and stop ingestion streams (operator step).
  * KV cache zeroization and invalidation (automated + manual verification).
  * Logical deletion of primary objects, then overwrite/crypto-erase where applicable for ephemeral cache nodes.
  * Snapshot pruning: remove snapshots that include tenant data and rotate snapshot manifest.
  * Backup retention holds: if customer has legal hold, we require documented authorization to bypass standard scrape.
  * Final verification: Ops generates a tenant-erase attestation and a timeline CSV for audit.

- Typical evidence package we deliver: tenant-wipe-checklist.xlsx (detailed activity log), attestation letter (signed by Ops lead), and optional storage-provider removal receipts.
