Quick read of the RFP attachment. A few immediate flags from a Legal/Finance risk perspective:

Folks —

From an engineering/ops perspective, here's the practical read:
- 99.995% translates to ~4.38 minutes downtime per month. That's aggressive for a single-region hosted deployment.
- Achievable approaches:
  * Dedicated reserved capacity + cross-AZ redundancy within a region — reduces instance-level failures but doesn't address zone-level events.
  * Active/active across two regions with automated traffic failover — provides the best route to 99.995% but increases latency for some geo traffic and adds cost.

Constraints and costs:
- We would recommend requiring Dedicated (reserved) capacity for primary traffic and a cold-standby in a second region that can failover within 60–120s. That implies a ~30–50% incremental monthly cost on top of standard hosting.
- Observability: we need SLI definitions (successful completion / 5xx vs timeout vs queued) — current prod SLIs are token-success rate and p95 latency; uptime for long-running streaming requests needs special handling.
- Ops runbook: I sketched a minimal runbook for failover and verification (ops_runbook_stub.txt). It includes escalation, automated traffic cutover steps, and expected RTOs.

Bottom line: Technically feasible with Dedicated + cross-region failover and stricter operational commitments, but we should avoid open-ended liability. Also expect a multi-week onboarding cost and an additional monthly invoice line for reserved capacity.
