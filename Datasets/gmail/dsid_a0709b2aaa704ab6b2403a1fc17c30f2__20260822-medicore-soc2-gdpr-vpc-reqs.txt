Susan / team —

Following up with the initial deployment sketch attached in Drive: https://redwood.gdrive/repo/diagrams/medicore-vpc-deployment.png

High-level notes from our architecture side: 
- VPC peering option: requires a /20 or smaller CIDR on your side to avoid overlap. We will request a single CIDR block for Redwood-managed endpoints and will terminate all outbound flows through a controlled NAT/egress GW in our VPC.
- PrivateLink option: exposes an ENI in your account; recommended when you cannot allow peering. Slightly higher setup time but avoids route table CIDR coordination.
- Air-gapped/on-prem: our on-prem package supports isolated KMS via customer HSM and a hardened appliance. That path requires a site-visit for the initial installation and an SLA addendum; ETA for on-prem packaging is ~6-8 weeks from SOW.
- KMS/HSM: We support customer-managed keys (AWS KMS) for hosted and a separate HSM integration for on-prem. Audit logs are stored for 12 months by default; we can extend retention with add-on.

I’ve included a short port/CIDR summary in the diagram notes. If you confirm VPC peering we’ll generate a formal Network Requirements PDF (one pager) for your network team.
