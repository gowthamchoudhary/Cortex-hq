Alex — thanks for the clear list. High-level answers below; I attached a short spec and two sample export bundles. I will also loop in our infra engineer for a throughput estimate.

1) Retention model: tenant-configurable retention policies enforced at the platform metadata layer. We support per-log-type retention rules (e.g., auth events vs request traces). Policies are declarative (retention window + storage tier) and enforced by automated lifecycle jobs. Hot vs cold tiers are configurable; cold archives are retained immutable for the configured period unless a legal hold is applied.

2) Export slices & integrity: we support time-range, tenant-id, route, and event-type filters. Exports can be produced as NDJSON (line-delimited JSON) or partitioned Parquet files for analytics consumers. Each bundle includes:
  - manifest.json with per-file SHA256 checksums
  - signed manifest using our key (we can provide public key or integrate with customer's KMS for cross-checking)
  - metadata file with ingestion timestamps, server timestamps, and last-modified ORCID-like IDs for traceability

Tamper evidence: bundles are signed at creation; we also produce a ledger entry (append-only) that records the export event and hash pointer. For customers requiring highest assurance, we can produce a notarized timestamp (RFC3161) and deliver the notarization artifact.

3) Delivery & SLA: delivery options — secure S3 transfer (push to customer bucket via temporary credentials), SFTP over managed endpoint, or encrypted HTTPS streaming to a specified endpoint. For a 500GB slice we typically multiplex across 8 concurrent workers and estimate 3–6 hours end-to-end depending on destination throughput and compression. We provide a delivery job id and progress logs. Legal hold: holds override retention delete operations; we persist a hold flag at the tenant and record the hold lifecycle in the audit ledger.

Alex — following up with some concrete SLAs and export knobs, pulled from our internal runbook: 

- Export formats: NDJSON (recommended for simplicity) or Parquet (recommended for analytics). Default compression: gzip.
- Integrity: SHA256 per-file checksums, manifest signing using RSA-2048 (we can switch to ECDSA on request).
- Delivery throughput SLA: small bundles (<50GB) — same-day; medium (50–500GB) — 4–8 hours typical; large (>500GB) — custom schedule, often staged. All estimates dependent on destination write speed.
- Retention enforcement: periodic sweeps run at UTC 02:00; deletions are soft for 24 hours then purged unless a legal hold flag exists.
- Legal hold API: create_hold(tenant_id, reason, expires_at). Holds recorded in audit ledger and surfaced to retention jobs.
