Summary of impact:
- Scope: small subset of sessions from US-East POPs and EU-West POPs; transient APAC tail latency for specific long-running chat sessions.

- APAC customers: no forced region switch required unless you see > 2% 5xx; if so, prefer SIN over SYD.

Operational steps we've completed:
- Opened canary pools in fallback POPs and validated within 30m.
- Increased instance pool and sustained worker threads for the affected tenant pools for 4 hours.
- Deployed an updated routing policy to prefer paths with lower retransmit counts.
