Lena / Kimberly — technical follow-up and constraints from the runtime side: 

1) Export formats & slices
- We support NDJSON (newline-delimited JSON) and Parquet for exports. Parquet is preferable for large multi-day exports (better compression / schema).
- Exports can be partitioned by tenant_id/date/hour; we generate a manifest.csv listing all files, SHA256 checksums, record counts and time ranges.

2) Erasure (shredding) semantics
- Live indexes: we can run targeted deletions against the serving index and purge associated KV cache entries and any cached prefetched fragments. That results in the data no longer being queryable. We then generate a deletion manifest with timestamps and operator IDs.
- Backups & immutable snapshots: many customers have immutable snapshot retention (forensic timeline). By design those snapshots are not modified; for these we provide two options: (A) scheduled retention expiry (set TTL shorter going forward) or (B) redaction at export time — we strip or hash PII in the archived files and issue a redaction attestation. Full retroactive removal from immutable backups is operationally complex and often not feasible without reingesting datasets; we can discuss a one-off remediation plan if required but it is heavier (project scope, cost, longer timeline).

3) Proof & auditability
- We produce: export bundle (NDJSON/Parquet) + manifest.csv (file list + checksums) + signed attestation PDF where our Security lead signs off on the deletion/export event. For higher assurance we can deliver a time-stamped notarized attestation (third-party) — needs procurement.

4) Residency & encryption
- Exports can be staged to an EU region bucket and encrypted with a customer-managed key (KMS) if requested. We can also deliver via SFTP to an EU endpoint.

5) Edge cases
- Legal hold: if there's an active legal hold, we will not execute erasure until revoked.
- Related objects: activity that references the user (e.g., session records in aggregated metrics) may remain as non-identifying aggregates unless specifically requested for removal.
