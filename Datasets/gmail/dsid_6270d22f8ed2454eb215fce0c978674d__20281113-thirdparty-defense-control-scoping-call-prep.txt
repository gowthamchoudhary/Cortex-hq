Team — captured Markus' summary into legal positions. A few points to propose as compromise language for the order form / MSA: 

1) Scope carve: Redwood will accept defend-and-indemnify for third-party IP claims only where the claim arises from (i) Redwood-delivered model weights or (ii) Redwood-provided training dataset that is itself proprietary to Redwood. For customer-provided training data or customer prompts/responses, Novacorp retains primary control of defense.

2) Evidence obligation: Redwood agrees to preserve relevant infra and model telemetry for 30 calendar days upon receipt of a written preservation notice. "Relevant" will be limited to the minimal set required to investigate the claim; we will not provide full PII-laden logs absent a lawful process. See proposed text in paragraph 4.3 of the attached checklist (kms_evidence_checklist.docx).

3) Costs & thresholds: Redwood will fund defense costs up to USD 250k for qualifying claims; above that we propose a shared-cost escalation (50/50) unless insurance applies. This gives Novacorp an immediate funded path and avoids blanket exposure.

4) KMS attestation: we'll provide an operational KMS/HSM attestation and a redacted key-management diagram; we cannot hand over HSM credentials.
