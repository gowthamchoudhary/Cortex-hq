Marissa — thanks for flagging and for the redline. High level assessment: the requested changes are above our standard risk appetite and should be escalated.

Top issues:
• Indemnity: They seek broad customer-side indemnity language that could expose us to third-party claims unrelated to Redwood’s services. We typically limit indemnities to IP infringement and willful misconduct; any expansion should be mutual and narrowly tailored.
• Liability cap: 2x ARR is materially higher than our standard 1x ARR cap. We have precedent to allow 1x ARR in strategic deals but anything above that requires exec approval and a clear commercial offset.
• Consequential damages: Removing the limitation opens us to open-ended exposure and is non-starter.
• Warranty & SLA: They added a 30-day remediation SLA and specific remedies. That’s operationally doable in many cases but needs explicit scope and acceptance criteria.

Recommendation: escalate for executive sign-off to allow 1x ARR cap (not 2x), accept mutual indemnity limited to IP, data breach caused by Redwood negligence, and willful misconduct carveouts. For warranty, propose a limited warranty window (60–90 days) with defined remedy steps and an explicit exclusion for third-party integrations.

If the commercial team is offering a discount as a trade, legal needs the final commercial terms documented so we can quantify residual risk. I attached a short summary for the packet (legal_summary_prismcare.txt).
