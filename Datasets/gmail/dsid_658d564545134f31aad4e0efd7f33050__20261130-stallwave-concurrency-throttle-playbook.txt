Quick summary of what I'm seeing so far (08:45 snapshot):
- A small subset (~0.7%) of pods in the rdx-3b-int8 pool experienced KV-cache miss storms simultaneously due to a cache eviction cascade after a spike of large-context requests from a different tenant.
- In-flight queues on affected hosts surged to 12–16 concurrent requests; our configured safe concurrency is 8 for this machine class — we hit a soft cap which triggered request queueing and eventual timeouts.
- Retries from the client (exponential backoff misconfigured) amplified the pressure.

Immediate mitigation proposal (30–90 min window):
1) Apply a per-tenant concurrency cap of 4 for Odyssey on the rdx-3b-int8 pool to stop amplification.
2) Enable prefix KV caching for Odyssey sessions where applicable (reduce repeated kv misses).
3) Roll temporary fallback: route <=200-token requests to rdx-1.3b-quant (lower cost, faster warm paths) for Odyssey only.
4) Patch the request-retry guidance in our SDK to prevent aggressive client retries — coordinate with Customer.
