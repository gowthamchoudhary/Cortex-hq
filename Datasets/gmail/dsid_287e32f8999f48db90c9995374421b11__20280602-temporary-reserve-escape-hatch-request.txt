Short answer: yes, with caveats.

Notes from infra: 
- We can provision a time-boxed reservation for 8 vGPU-equivalent slices in us-west for 2028-07-01 through 2028-08-15. We'll represent this as a transient reservation that auto-expires at midnight UTC on 2028-08-15.
- Escape-hatch implementation: we can implement a soft-trigger that (a) notifies product & CS at 85% cluster util for 30m, (b) attempts non-disruptive rebalancing for 10m, then (c) programmatically releases the transient reservation if util remains >90% for 15m. Release will send a webhook + email to the listed owners.
- Autoscale exception: we can add policy-level exclusion that prevents eviction for the reservation during 09:00–13:00 PT daily for the pilot window. Outside that window standard preemption applies.

Caveats: this path requires a quick config change on our allocation controller and a small DB migration to tag the transient reservations. Ops can deliver the change, but we'll need a one-hour maintenance window to flip the policy (can be done 2028-06-09 03:00–04:00 UTC).
