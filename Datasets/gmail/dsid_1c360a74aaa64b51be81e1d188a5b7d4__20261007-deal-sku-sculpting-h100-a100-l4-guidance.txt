Folks —

Ran the sample through two scenarios. Attaching the workbook with assumptions. Short summary:

Scenario A (Conservative, no quant):
- 70B at 32k context: ~1.6 H100-equivalent slots to support 120 qps with 30% headroom for bursts (model sharding + KV cache). Equivalent on A100-80GB would require more aggressive tiling and results in ~20-30% higher p95.
- 7B generator: requires ~45 L4 slots to meet 1.2k qps at target latency (p50 ~60ms, p95 ~220ms) assuming moderate batching and prefix caching.

Scenario B (Aggressive, 8-bit quant + prefix cache):
- 70B: quantized 70B can fit onto A100-80 with good perf in batch pipelines; H100 still has lower tail-latency and better headroom — estimate 1.0 H100-equivalent slot or 1.5 A100 slots.
- 7B: can be consolidated into ~30 L4 slots with quantization and caching hopefuls, reducing cost ~30%.

Recommendations:
1) Commit to a mixed pool: baseline 2x H100-equivalents (or 3x A100-80GB equivalents as a conservative procurement) + burstable L4 pool for generator traffic.
2) Hedge: include a clause to convert a portion of A100 capacity into H100 in quarter 2 if p95 latency budget breaches or long-context load increases.
3) Autoscale policy: target conservative headroom on the 70B pool (25–35%) and aggressive bin-packing on L4 with short cool-downs.
