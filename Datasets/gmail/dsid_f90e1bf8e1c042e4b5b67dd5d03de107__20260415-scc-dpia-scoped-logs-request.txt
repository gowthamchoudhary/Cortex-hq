Hi Samir / Alex,

Appreciate the detailed questionnaire — I reviewed it and prepared an evidence plan. Summary below and I've attached two artifacts: a scoped Article 30 extract (artifact-access-sample.csv) and a short policy note on KMS rotations.

1) SCCs / transfers
- For Hosted customers based in the EU we offer SCCs (EU SCCs, module 2/3 as applicable) in our DPA addendum. SCCs cover processing and transfer of logs if they contain personal data. For your planned analytics, the key question is whether Federal Bank will be uploading personal data into the vault/logs; if you submit only pseudonymized identifiers the transfer risk is reduced.

2) Article 30 extract (scoped)
- Attached: artifact-access-sample.csv — this is a sanitized extract showing the fields, categories of data processed, typical recipients (Redwood analytics team, infra subprocessor storage), and retention policy applied to vault logs.
- Format: CSV extract + short narrative we recommend including in your DPIA; we can produce a formal PDF excerpt on request.

3) KMS & rotation
- For Hosted accounts, encryption keys are managed in our cloud KMS. Key rotation is scheduled quarterly; rotation is key-material rotation and does not change logical retention metadata. If you later request key export/bring-your-own-key, rotation behavior differs and we document re-encryption windows. Re-encryption during restores can add a 1–4 hour maintenance window for affected artifacts.

4) Audit & evidence options
- Remote evidence pack: we can compile the scoped Article 30 extract, sanitized artifact sample, KMS certificate excerpt, and subprocessor roster. Typical delivery timeline: 3 business days for a scoped pack, 10 business days for a full production audit packet.
- On-site audits: allowed under our DPA with 30 days' notice and a signed confidentiality addendum. For most DPIA follow-ups we coordinate remote sessions and supplemental Zoom walkthroughs.
