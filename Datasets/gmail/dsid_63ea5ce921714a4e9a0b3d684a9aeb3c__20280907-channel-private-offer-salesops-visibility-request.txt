Adding some AWS/ops detail from our CPPO playbook:

- Required fields for CPPO activation: customer entity ID, reseller org ID (10-digit), offer ID, list of SKUs, pricing profile ID, and a designated contact email for marketplace communications.
- Seller visibility behavior: if Redwood populates the reseller org ID and selects partner visibility, the reseller's sellers will see the offer and can claim leads in Seller Central. If org ID is missing, visibility will be limited to direct Redwood listings.
- Timing: once we submit the CPPO package to AWS, provisioning typically takes 3–5 business days if all fields are valid. Any missing tax/W-9 info or invoice samples can add delay.
