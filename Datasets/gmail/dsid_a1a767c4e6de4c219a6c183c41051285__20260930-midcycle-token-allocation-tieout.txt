Quick update from the metering side. I pulled the per-request ledger for Nimbus Labs for 2026-09-01 -> 2026-09-21 and diffed it against their CSV. Key observations:

- Between 2026-09-18T03:05:12Z and 2026-09-18T04:46:58Z we see a replayed ingestion batch that re-submitted ~420k requests. These requests carry identical request_ids but different request timestamps (retried from an external connector).
- Our current metering pipeline counts each arrival as a charged event because the upstream retry header (X-Redwood-Idempotency) was not present on the second submission. The connector in question (third-party ETL v2.1) added an extra POST attempt during a transient network error.
- Using token-per-request averages from the sample window, the replay accounts for ~588,234 excess tokens (this aligns with the customer's reported delta within reasonable sampling variance).

Proposed remediation:
1) Issue a one-time credit for 588,234 tokens translated at their committed rate = USD 2,352.93 (pre-VAT).
2) Reverse VAT on the credit line and issue an updated invoice for September with net owed adjusted accordingly.
3) Add a guard in the metering pipeline: reject duplicates when request_id matches within a 24-hour window unless a specific idempotency header is present. We'll schedule that change for the next sprint and provide release notes.
