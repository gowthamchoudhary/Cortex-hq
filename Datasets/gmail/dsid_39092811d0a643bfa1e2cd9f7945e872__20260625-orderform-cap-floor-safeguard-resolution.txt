Hi all,

Attached is a proposed legal redline for Exhibit A and the Order Form that implements the commercial points Rafael outlined. Key changes (summary):

1) Minimum Monthly Commitment (MMC):
- Section 1.2 updated to define MMC as the minimum billable spend for each month.
- Quarterly true-up: at the end of each quarter, actual usage is compared to MMCs for the quarter and over/under amounts are invoiced/credited pro rata.

2) Surge cap and rollback:
- New Section 3.4(a): temporary surge cap — per-token surcharge capped at 1.25x of the committed rate during the first 72 hours of any unforecasted burst.
- New Section 3.4(b): automatic rollback — if sustained excess usage > 40% of baseline for a rolling 7-day window, we revert to prior committed tier pricing until baseline normalizes for 7 consecutive days.

3) Safeguard triggers (replaces discretionary language):
- Replaced “may, at its discretion, implement stabilizers” with explicit triggers: (i) sustained CPU/GPU utilization > 85% for 48 hours, (ii) sustained token generation QPS above agreed baseline for 7 days, or (iii) proven abuse or rate-limited activity documented by both parties.
- Included required notice and remediation steps: Redwood provides 48 hours notice, a mitigation plan, and a right for BrightHealth to pause non-critical workloads.

4) Promotional onboarding credit: clarified that any promotional credits are paid forward first and not refundable; credits apply before MMC calculations for the first two billing cycles.
