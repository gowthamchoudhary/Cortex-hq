1) Rate limits / concurrency:
- Baseline tier (committed): 3000 requests/min, per-connection concurrency up to 80 streams.
- Tier 2 (sustained high): 6000 requests/min, concurrency up to 160.
- Burst (within burst pocket): up to 12k requests/min, concurrency up to 320; subject to burst pocket available.
These are conservative caps — we can carve dedicated throughput for Realtime if you want guaranteed p99 latency SLAs (requires Dedicated or an add-on).

2) Sustained bursts beyond 48 hours:
- Primary behavior: we exhaust the burst pocket first, then automatically apply soft-throttling (graceful slowdown) to maintain latency SLOs.
- Fallback: if sustained usage exceeds the burst and commit for >14 days, our routing will route overflow to a pay-as-you-go rate (higher per-token price) unless you elect to convert the overflow into committed capacity mid-term.

3) Project allocations / POs:
- Yes — commit can be logically allocated 70/30 and rebalanced quarterly. The billing line can be split across two POs; we’ll reflect separate cost centers on the invoice.
