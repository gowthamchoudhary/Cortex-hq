Folks — quick triage update from SRE/network:

What we see so far (summary):
- Correlated spike across our ingress for requests from Hightail's announced prefixes (AS64570).
- DNS lookups from our edge to resolver pool show intermittent timeouts (100-300ms spikes on resolver latency) — some sessions fall back to TCP which adds latency.
- NAT/conntrack counters on the peering edge show a sharp increase in ephemeral port churn; one of the NAT nodes briefly hit conntrack ~85% of its capacity around 14:46Z.
- A small subset of requests were dropped behind the proxy due to transient firewall rule evaluation taking longer (policy engine CPU spike on proxy-3).

Attached: edge-trace-20270727.log (edge timings, DNS latencies, conntrack counters).

Immediate mitigations applied (15:00Z):
- Increased conntrack table size by 30% on the impacted NAT host (risk: temporary additional memory).
- Rolled a proxy process restart on proxy-3 to clear the policy-engine CPU spike.
- Pushed a temporary DNS resolver fallback rule (prioritize stable resolver pair) — monitoring.

Proposed next steps (owners):
- Marissa: continue increased telemetry, correlate with BGP/peering metrics, confirm whether packet retransmits match Hightail tcpdump.
- Rishi: prepare a temporary routing change (poc-routing-change.diff) to steer Hightail traffic across a less-utilized peering pair; requires ops coordination.
- Vanessa / Lauren: coordinate a short canary cutover with Hightail and get approval for Dedicated pool flip if cutover isn't successful within 2 hours.

Impact notes: median latencies unchanged; P95 had large improvements after the conntrack bump but P99 still elevated for a portion of clients. We should avoid a full-scale cutover unless the canary shows benefit.
