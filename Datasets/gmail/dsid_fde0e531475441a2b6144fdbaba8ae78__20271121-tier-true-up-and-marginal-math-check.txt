Nicola — thanks for the clear ask. Attaching a simple CSV with the worked example and a short explanation below. High level:

- Prorating: For partial-month usage we'll prorate committed units when the customer has a monthly billing cadence (example: if you commit to 600M tokens/month and use 300M in the first half, we do not move the breakpoint mid-month; commit is evaluated on actual usage at the invoice cycle and true-up is applied at quarter close if agreed).
- Marginal vs blended: Marginal pricing charges each token at the rate for its bracket (you only pay the lower rate for tokens above a breakpoint). Blended takes total spend and divides by total tokens to produce an effective per-token rate. We prefer marginal for predictability and to avoid large retroactive credits, but we can offer blended for simpler procurement math at smaller deals.

Nicola — attaching the CSV with two scenarios (marginal and blended) and a short executive summary inline below. Key points:

Scenario A — Marginal (recommended):
- Tier 1: 0–500M tokens @ $0.0040/token
- Tier 2: 500M–2B tokens @ $0.0030/token
- Tier 3: >2B tokens @ $0.0018/token
Worked example: if you use 2.6B tokens in a month: first 500M at 0.0040, next 1.5B at 0.0030, remaining 600M at 0.0018. The invoice reflects the sum of each bracket. True-up: evaluated quarterly; if usage < committed, credits applied up to 15% quarterly cap.

Scenario B — Blended (procurement-friendly):
- Same breakpoints but we compute an effective blended rate = total cost / total tokens for the period. Blended may result in a higher near-term invoice if their usage is concentrated in lower tiers, but it simplifies forecasting. We can allow blended for Q1 onboarding only (as discussed) and then transition.

Prorating/Partial months: monthly commit is the unit for breakpoints; prorated partial months will not shift the breakpoint inside a month.

Next steps: if you want a blended option for the first two quarters, we can add this to the SOW. Otherwise we will proceed with marginal + 15% credit cap + quarterly true-up. Rafael will prepare the specific contract addendum language.
