Karthik — quick technical response and recommended compensating controls.

Feasibility: yes. We can tag ingestion streams at the tenant+stream level (e.g., tenant=novum-prod-22, stream=ephemeral-debug) and apply separate lifecycle policies. Audit logs are written to a separate index/bucket (immutable-ops-audit) and are never subject to ephemeral TTLs.

Controls we recommend to satisfy SOC 2/CAIQ:
- Encryption: tenant-specific envelope keys stored in the customer's KMS scope for novum-prod-22. We will provide a keystore manifest showing residency and key rotation cadence.
- Logical separation: distinct buckets/indices with RBAC preventing cross-export
- Deletion certificate: retention engine will emit a signed deletion manifest (SHA256) including affected object IDs and timestamps; we keep a non-reversible hash of the manifest in our internal evidence store for audit (30d retention of the manifest hash)
- Forensics snapshot: prior to deletion we offer a one-time export that Novum can request within a 7-day notice window (we will produce an export and mark associated ephemeral traces as expired)

Acceptance criteria to provide Novum (draft):
1) Redwood will apply a 30-day TTL to objects tagged stream=ephemeral-debug for tenant novum-prod-22;
2) Core audit logs (stream=audit) remain retained for 180 days and are not affected;
3) Customer must sign a retention exception addendum acknowledging that reduced retention may limit long-term forensic capability for ephemeral traces;
4) For legal holds: Novum must issue a preservation request via the support-forensics mailbox; upon receipt we will snapshot affected objects (forensic export) and suspend TTL for those items until release;

Artifacts we can provide: keystore_manifest.pdf (shows key residency and rotation), a CAIQ export (caiq_export.csv) with relevant control mappings, and a sample deletion_manifest.txt. I can assemble the artifacts and a short tech note explaining the lifecycle policy.

I'll prepare the bundle and propose language for the addendum. If Novum wants, we can run a 30-minute technical walk-through to show the separation and demo the export.
