Elena / team — a couple of procurement/legal flags from our side: 

- Availability credits: procurement typically wants a simple fallback table (e.g., <99.0% = 30% credit, 99.0-99.5% = 15%, 99.5-99.9% = 5%). I attached a suggested matrix that maps to our billing model.
- Evidence: we can provide uptime telemetry slices and audit logs for incidents, but Redwoods’ standard process is to validate incidents against platform telemetry + incident tickets (we’ll include that in the runbook).
- Burst protection/fallback: if you're running a POC that may hit spiky loads, we should either (a) include burst protection commitment on the order form or (b) scope a max sustained qps for the POC and call out degraded-mode behavior.
- Data retention costs: enabling 12-month retention will add a storage cost; that should be included as an order form line item or captured as an add-on.

I’m adding an attachments spreadsheet with the credit matrix and a short checklist for the evidence we’ll include. Happy to redline the draft SLA if you share changes.
