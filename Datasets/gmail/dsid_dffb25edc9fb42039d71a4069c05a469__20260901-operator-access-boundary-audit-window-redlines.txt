High-level proposal (we'll send redline text attached):
- Operator access: limited, timebound operator actions performed by Redwood support staff via an outbound, customer-initiated support session (no persistent backdoor). All actions logged; customer retains final access control.
- Audit pathway: scheduled read-only snapshot + signed attestation from Redwood that the snapshot was taken from the live environment at the specified window. Alternative: supervised 48-hour read-only audit with mutually agreed scope and an on-site or remote observer.
- Escrow-like option: if MedSys requires a recoverable image or source-level artifact for extreme failover/continuity, propose a "sealed snapshot" held in escrow under narrow release conditions (legal hold + court order / contract default). We expect most buyers to accept the snapshot+attestation path.

Thanks — drafting notes inline and a short set of redline snippets (see attached CSV with options). Quick summary of recommended contract language and safeguards:

1) Operator scope (example clause):
"Redwood Support personnel may, only upon Customer's express, time-limited authorization, access Customer-managed environments for the sole purpose of diagnosing and remediating issues. Such access shall be limited to the minimal necessary privileges; Redwood will not persist credentials or maintain any means of autonomous access after session termination. All access events will be recorded and available to Customer upon request."

2) Audit window (example clause):
"For purposes of verification, Customer may request a scheduled, read-only snapshot of runtime artifacts. Redwood will produce a verifiable snapshot and provide a signed attestation of its provenance within 10 business days of request. Alternatively, the Parties may agree to a supervised audit window not exceeding 72 hours in any rolling 12-month period. Redwood may require reasonable notice (not less than 7 business days) and adherence to mutually agreed confidentiality terms for on-site or remote audits."

3) Escrow-style release (narrow conditions):
"A sealed snapshot held in escrow will only be released upon the occurrence of a material breach of this Agreement that remains uncured after notice and the expiration of a cure period, or upon a final, binding legal process requiring release."

Operational suggestions: ephemeral operator accounts scoped per-session + MFA + customer-controlled OIDC approval; role-based logging with signed audit trails; a monthly summary of access events.

I've attached the proposed addendum (redline) and a CSV listing three evidence-delivery options (snapshot/attestation; supervised audit; escrowed sealed snapshot). Please review and let me know which option MedSys prefers so I can finalize the legal language.
