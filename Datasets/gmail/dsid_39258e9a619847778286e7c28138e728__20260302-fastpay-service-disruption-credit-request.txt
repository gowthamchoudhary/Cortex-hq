Hi all — quick tech update from triage:

Summary (interim):
- Root cause: a combination of FastPay client-side retry policy (default SDK backoff disabled in a config roll last week) and an unfortunate Redis connection flapping on a subset of hosts in our EU pool (we routed some US traffic during capacity reshuffle). The client retries turned transient 503s into sustained load, exhausting asynchronous worker queues and causing 429s as the request admission controller started shedding traffic.
- Impact window: 2026-02-27T22:28:15Z — 2026-02-28T02:08:42Z (we'll validate exact cutover and export full traces).
- Mitigations applied during incident: temporarily increased worker pool and toggled admission thresholds to accept queued requests; rolled a targeted config to stabilize Redis connections; instructed our edge to serve a short retry-safety 429 to encourage clients to back off.

Next steps (48h):
1) Full trace export and token-level error breakdown (48h).
2) Permanent fix: tighten admission control + improve autoscaling and add a circuit-breaker for upstream Redis errors (ETA 7 business days).
3) SDK guidance: we'll publish a recommended retry/backoff configuration and coordinate an advisory with FastPay engineering.

I've attached an internal RCA draft (ransom_root_cause_analysis.pdf) and a short CSV with the per-minute error spikes.
