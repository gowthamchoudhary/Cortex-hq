Hi Lina — quick technical notes from infra/evals perspective.

Short answers to your questions: 
- Quantized variants (8-bit) are great for throughput and cost but we typically do not recommend them as the only safety control for legal/regulatory output. For legal-critical outputs we recommend a two-stage pipeline: generation on a cost-optimized variant, then verification (exact-match/soft semantic match) against authoritative glossaries using a specialist verifier model (we can provide a tuned checker).
- We can configure the verifier to enforce glossary term preferences and surface a confidence score; outputs below threshold are flagged and routed to the more capable (non-quantized) model or to a human-review queue.

Proposed eval plan (detailed):
- Stage A (3 days): Run generation comparison across the three model candidates (Llama-3-70b-qat, Redwood-Delta-16b, Red-8q) on your prompt sets. Metrics: BLEU, ROUGE (for templated generation), hallucination-rate (entity insertion), and legal-term F1 against your glossaries.
- Stage B (2 days): Run verifier pass and estimate false-positive/false-negative rates for glossary enforcement.
- Stage C (1 day): Latency and cost run under your burst profile; we will test autoscaling behavior with a simulated 2x burst.

Deliverables: per-locale CSV with failure categories, a short deck with recommended production config, and a risk log with severity & mitigation (we'll populate the first draft).
