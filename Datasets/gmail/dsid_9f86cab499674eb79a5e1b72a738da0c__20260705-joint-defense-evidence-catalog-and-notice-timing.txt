Laura, Marcus — thanks for the context. Below is a proposed, narrow compromise we think is commercially reasonable and defensible for Redwood. Key principles first: we want to preserve the ability to manage litigation risk while giving Greenshore the operational transparency and evidence access they need.

Proposed changes (high level):
- Notice window: propose a 45-day notice window for third-party IP claims that arise from customer-facing outputs. Rationale: 30 days is tight for certain complex claims (patent assertions, compound source investigations). We will commit to an earlier acknowledgement within 48 hours of receiving written notice and a rolling evidence handoff schedule.
- Joint defense / cooperation: retain a mutual-defense option rather than unilateral control. Specifically: if both parties agree in writing, Redwood and Greenshore will engage in a coordinated defense with reasonable cooperation steps (shared counsel briefing, evidence exchange, mutually agreed settlement parameters). Redwood retains the right to control settlement when settlement would bind Redwood beyond the indemnity or create obligations on Redwood's broader customer base.
- Evidence catalog: attach a concise list of items Redwood can reasonably provide (request/response logs, model version, prompt bundle, timestamps, and redaction-friendly transcripts). See attached evidence_requirements.xlsx. We will accept procurement-level evidence requests under a standard eDiscovery schedule (initial production in 14 calendar days after acceptance of the notice; additional productions on a rolling 14-day cadence for up to 60 days).
- Retention: Redwood can commit to 12 months of request/response retention for account-associated logs by default; for extended retention requests (24+ months) we can include a commercial addendum in the Order Form.

Draft language snippet (for legal redline):
"If a third party asserts a claim resulting from the Customer's use of the Services, the Customer shall provide written notice to Redwood of such claim as soon as reasonably practicable and in any event within forty-five (45) days after the Customer first becomes aware of the claim. Upon receipt of notice, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to exchange relevant evidence in accordance with the Evidence Catalog and Production Schedule attached hereto. If the Parties elect to pursue a coordinated defense, such coordination shall be subject to mutual written agreement and neither Party shall unilaterally settle any claim that would impose obligations on the other Party without prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld."
Open items for Greenshore to confirm:
- Is 45 days acceptable? If not, would Greenshore accept 30 days only for claims based on customer-provided content (rather than Redwood model outputs)?
- Any required specific SLA for the identity of Redwood evidence custodian / point-of-contact? We propose a named tech contact and a legal contact (Irene).
