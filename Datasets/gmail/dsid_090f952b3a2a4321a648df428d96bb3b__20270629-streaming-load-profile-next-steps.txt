Current stack (what you shared)

- Traffic profile: normal load ~50 RPS, burst to ~300 RPS during batch batch jobs and marketing spikes.

Success criteria you listed
- End-to-end p95 generation latency: < 600ms for 90% of traffic, goal < 400ms for interactive flows.
- Streaming chunk latency (TTFB per chunk): < 150ms on steady-state.
- Sustained concurrency: support 250–350 concurrent live sessions without visible UX degradation.
