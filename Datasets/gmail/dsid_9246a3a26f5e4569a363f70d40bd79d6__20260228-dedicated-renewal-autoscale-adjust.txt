Hi — answering the ops questions and adding implementation notes.

1) Committed throughput: we measure token consumption per model endpoint aggregated at the Dedicated pool level (all routes mapped to the pool). We report daily totals and 30-day rolling averages in the Console.

2) Warm pool billing: warm_pool instances are reserved (startup/idle) and counted toward reserved capacity for availability guarantees. For billing: warm instances are billed at the reserved rate (i.e., part of your commit); they do not incur additional on-demand charges while idle.

3) Overage behavior: by default we allow overage up to a configurable burst threshold (e.g., +25% daily) and bill the overage per-token at the on-demand rate. For renewals we can negotiate an overage credit schedule or usage true-up (example in attachment).

Autoscaling specifics we recommend:
- min_replicas: 3 (keeps KV caches warm for baseline tail-latency)
- max_replicas: 18 (supports peak throughput with sharded worker pools)
- warm_pool: 2 (pre-warmed GPUs with model and KV cache warmed)
- target_scale_up_lag: 30s (time from metric trigger to accepting requests; includes instance spin + model load)
- scale_up_cooldown: 45s, scale_down_grace: 600s

Operational notes:
- We'll pre-stage model shards to warm pool during your maintenance window to minimize cold-start.
- For KV cache priming, we recommend a short prewarm script that replays recent active prefixes — we can provide that script.
