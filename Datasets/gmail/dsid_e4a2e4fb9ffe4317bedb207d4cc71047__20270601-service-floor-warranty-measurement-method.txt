Proposal summary (service-floor warranty):
- Service floor definition: sustained baseline of tokens/sec (aggregated) and requests/sec for a pinned model family across the committed pool. Example: 1.2M tokens/sec per 8-GPU pool running Llama2-13B-8k quantized (median observed).
- Latency targets: p95 <= 220ms, p99 <= 520ms for single-request generation of <=512 tokens at steady-state, measured across a 30-minute sampling window after warmup.
- Measurement method: acceptance run is a 2-phase job — calibration (10 minutes, warm caches) + measurement (30 minutes). We run the customer's prompt corpus under representative concurrency; both prefix-caching and KV-cache utilization are considered.
- Remedy: service credits tiered by severity (minor: 5% monthly credit; major: 15% credit + remediation plan) over the affected billing month. Repeated failures (3+ occurrences in 90 days) allow termination rights with prorated refunds for reserved commit.

Discount/commit notes:
- Standard bands: 3/6/12-month commit bands scale discounts by commit size; deeper discounts available if you adopt a 12+ month reserved commit and a 24/7 support SKU.
