Ayesha — thanks for the clear list. Quick answers below; I'll follow up with a short paragraph for Legal and a proposed SLA-friendly retention summary.

1) Replication: For marketplace-listed models we do not replicate customer inference data across regions by default. We pin customer tenancy and KV caches to the selected region (AWS/GCP/Azure) unless the customer explicitly enables cross-region analytics.

2) Deletion timelines (expected):
- Metadata & logs: searchable logs are deleted or anonymized within 30 days in-region; aggregated analytics may persist up to 90 days (aggregated, not PII).
- KV cache / inference caches: Evicted immediately on request; TTLs mean cold-state data may take up to 24 hours to fully flush depending on active sessions.
- Backups/snapshots: Snapshots are on a 30-day rotation; deletions trigger the next retention cycle but we provide an expedited deletion option for enterprise contracts (typically 7–10 days).

3) Central processing: We do route billing/usage aggregation to a central telemetry ingestion pipeline in our US control plane. That pipeline consumes aggregated tokens/usage — no raw prompt text is routed there by default. If you require zero routing we can provide a Dedicated or Private deployment option where control plane telemetry is limited.

I'll draft the one-paragraph annex and circulate. For clarity, looping in Marissa for legal framing and Jordan for marketplace ops nuances.

Ayesha — a couple of technical clarifications from infra so Cloudfolio can update the annex properly:

- Active KV cache eviction: when we receive a deletion request we invalidate session-level KV entries immediately and mark the cache shards for GC. In high-throughput listings there can be delayed eviction windows (up to 24h) if the same session continues to push traffic; we can provide an SLA-backed path to shorten that to <6h on Dedicated.

- Snapshots: periodic snapshots are incremental. If you need complete destruction immediately we do a full snapshot purge but that requires a support ticket and, depending on backup replication, may take the snapshot retention window (we can expedite under enterprise terms).

- Audit log artifacts: admin/audit logs for marketplace onboarding (who requested what) are kept for 365 days by default for compliance; we can scope down to 90 days for Marketplace-only listings if you need.

If Cloudfolio wants concrete wording for each bullet (eviction / snapshot / audit retention) I'll prepare short lines to drop into the contract.
