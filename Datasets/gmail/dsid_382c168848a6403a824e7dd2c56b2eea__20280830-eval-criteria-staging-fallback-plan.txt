Team —

We're getting close to the promptset v2 canary (BetaCorp). I want to lock the acceptance criteria and the staging fallback behavior before we cut the canary next week. High level asks from Product and CS: predictable quality, and a clear fallback so BetaCorp's UX doesn't degrade.

Proposed acceptance gates (staging canary):
- Delta in core-intent accuracy: <= 1.5 percentage points drop vs baseline on the canonical 6-intent set.
- Hallucination rate (measured by our QA rubric): no net increase > 1.0% on the sampled set.
- Latency: p95 token latency increase <= 20ms for 16k context requests.
- Regression on top-1 response quality (human eval): mean rating drop <= 0.2 on 5-point scale.

Fallback policy (if any gate trips in staging or during initial canary):
1) Immediate canary pause and roll forward to safe-model (promptset v1) for traffic > canary fraction.
2) If safety/hallucination spike observed in production, automatic route to fallback by routing policy and notify on-call + BetaCorp.
3) Owner-led rollback if fixes need code changes — target 24h mitigation window.

I attached the initial AB plan and the promptset file for reference. Please do three things by EOD Monday (Sep 1):
- Owners: volunteer for metric ownership and alert thresholds.
- Measurement plan: confirm the exact eval harness and sample size.
- Canary fraction + escalation contacts for BetaCorp.
