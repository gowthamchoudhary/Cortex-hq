Thanks all — quick replies ahead of the call and an updated artifact attached (rdw-otlp-config-snippets.txt).

Answers: 
- Throughput: our default adapter emits ~420 spans per 1k requests with average span size ~1.8KB; metrics and logs add ~0.6 KB per request. We'll send a short CSV sample before the call.
- Keys/certs: BrightLayer supports mTLS to the ingestion endpoint; for the PoC we'll use a customer-provisioned cert stored in their KMS. We do not persist private keys outside the customer's vault.
- Troubleshooting: we can accept timebound jumpbox access and provide session logs; we will not keep snapshots of customer telemetry.

Attachments: rdw-otlp-config-snippets.txt (contains OTLP gRPC listener examples, sampling config, and a recommended statsd -> OTLP mapping).
