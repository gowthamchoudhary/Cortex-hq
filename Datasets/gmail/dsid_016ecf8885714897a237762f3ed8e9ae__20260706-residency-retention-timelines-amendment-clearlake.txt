Hi Emma / Procurement team —

Thanks for the redlines. I'm Redwood Legal Ops; below is a concise explanation of how we implement bespoke retention and deletion, and proposed contract language we can add to the Order Form Amendment (tracked changes to follow):

1) Residency and storage
- We operate customer-dedicated storage namespaces which can be constrained to EU regions (we currently support eu-west-1 and eu-central-1 for EU-only residency).
- By default, backups and snapshots are retained within the same region; we will include a EU-only data-at-rest guarantee for your tenant in the amendment.

2) Retention windows (proposed to mirror your ask, with technical notes)
- Inference logs & model input traces: EU = 180 days; US = 90 days.
- Ephemeral KV/cache TTL: 7 days (best-effort continuous eviction; critical caches may be pinned by application request and would require explicit opt-in).
- Backups/snapshots: maximum retention 120 days unless you elect a longer retention in writing. Note: snapshot purge is asynchronous and subject to replication/GC windows (see deletion timeline below).

3) Deletion pipeline & certificate (how we operationalize the SLA)
- Step A (mark): Customer-initiated purge request marks objects for deletion in our metadata catalog (t=0).
- Step B (active purge): Data is removed from active serving stores and caches within 48 hours of t=0 (subject to no legal hold).
- Step C (asynchronous background GC): Underlying object storage and replicas are queued for background garbage collection; completion depends on snapshot/replication windows but we commit to completing the GC for non-archival objects within 30 calendar days of t=0.
- Step D (backups/snapshots): For snapshots, we will schedule deletion and complete physical removal within 90 calendar days, unless an accelerated snapshot purge is requested (accelerated purge window: 10 business days for an additional operational fee; we can include this as an optional line item).

Certificate timing: we will provide an attestation that includes the purge ticket id, affected object ids, and internal audit ids within 5 business days of the final GC completion for the affected namespace. If you need an interim attestation at Step B, we can provide a preliminary confirmation within 48 hours of the active purge step.

4) Key management and unrecoverability
- We use customer-scoped encryption keys for private deployments; for hosted customers we maintain KMS-managed keys per-tenant. A deletion request triggers key material rotation/revocation for the affected tenant objects where EKM/KMS is applicable; revocation plus completed GC renders data cryptographically inaccessible. We will add language describing key revocation steps and timing.

5) Legal holds and e-discovery
- Documented legal holds will pause automated deletion. Redwood will provide notification and an estimated timeframe for hold release. We suggest adding a clause that legal holds must be accompanied by a named requestor and scope.

6) Proposed order form language (summary):
- Section X: Customer may specify per-region retention windows; Redwood will implement regionally-scoped namespaces and adhere to the retention windows in the amendment.
- Section Y: Deletion certificate: Redwood will provide a deletion attestation within 5 business days of completion of internal GC; interim attestation available within 48 hours of active purge.
- Section Z: Snapshot purge: default 90-day physical removal; accelerated purge within 10 business days available as an optional paid service.
