I pulled the metrics and we have a correlated autoscaler oscillation on the dedicated pool serving BrightWave in AZ2 — it was trying to scale up but new nodes were failing health checks due to a library-level cold-start regression introduced in the 2027-08-17 runtime build. That causes transient 503s and backpressure that pushes latency out.

Immediate actions taken (09:36-09:46):
- Disabled autoscale for the affected pool and pinned to current capacity.
- Rolled back runtime to 2027-08-15 stable build for the pool.
- Activated a temporary router policy to steer 28% of traffic to US-West (usw2) for this customer while we validate rollback metrics.
- Throttled client retry window via edge token TTL to reduce retry amplification.

Next steps:
- Monitor key metrics for 30 minutes; if error rate remains >2.5% we will escalate to full regional evacuation and route 60%+ traffic to alternate regions.
