High-level recommendation (for your peak pattern)
- Deployment: Redwood Dedicated in your AWS VPC (customer-managed subnets) — this gives predictable throughput and private networking.
- Pool sizing: start with 3 x NVIDIA H100-style nodes (for heavy model variants) + 2 x A100-style nodes for cost-effective short-prompt inference. This gives horizontal headroom for 270 TPS peaks with room for KV cache efficiency.
- Autoscale & burst: configure autoscale to add 1 node after sustained 60s above 200 TPS, with a burst pool up to +2 nodes for sudden spikes.

Latency & throughput guidance (anticipated after optimization)
- Short prompts (<128 tokens): expected P95 ~220–340ms depending on model quantization and KV cache hit rate.
- Mid prompts (128–512 tokens): P95 ~500–850ms (streaming recommended for UI).
- Long prompts (>512 tokens): prioritize throughput and streaming; tail latency will vary — route to a lower-cost variant if burst capacity is constrained.

Model & runtime notes
- Model choices: recommend starting with a quantized 13B–34B family for short prompts and keep a 70B-compatible variant for generative long-form tasks.
- KV cache: enable prefix caching for your common enrichment prompts — we saw 30–60% fewer compute tokens in similar workloads.
- Batching strategy: micro-batching at 16–64 requests depending on latency targets; dynamic batching policy per-route is best.
