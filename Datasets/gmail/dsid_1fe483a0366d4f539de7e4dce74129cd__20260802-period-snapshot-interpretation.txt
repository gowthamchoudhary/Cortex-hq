Laura / Marissa — quick technical confirmation below (keeping it tight for auditors):

- Snapshot timing: our daily scheduled point-in-time snapshot for production metadata and KV caches is captured at 00:00:00 UTC (i.e., the first instant of the day). The snapshot file includes all transactions with timestamps < 00:00:00 UTC of that day — which means the snapshot taken at 2026-07-01T00:00:00Z includes events through 2026-06-30T23:59:59Z.
- Logs: application logs are flushed continuously, but we have a short batching window for some workers (up to 30 seconds) before final write to the centralized logging system. That batching is internal and doesn't shift day boundaries beyond the 23:59:59 cut-off.
- Rollforward jobs: no rollforward that truncates the previous day; the only post-midnight jobs are housekeeping tasks that don't modify historical event records.
