Hi Laura — thanks for the clear list. Answered inline below at a high level; happy to schedule a short call if you prefer a walkthrough.

---
1) Data residency
- Default behavior: Hosted API request payloads and associated ephemeral caches are stored in the region selected at account provisioning. For self-serve signups the default is US (us-west1).
- Region selection: Customers can choose a single region (us-west1, us-east1, eu-west1) at onboarding; Redwood pins storage and primary compute to that region unless an explicit multi-region DR opt-in is requested.

2) Retention windows
- Request payloads / temporary request logs: default retention is 30 days for hosted customers. This covers immediate diagnostics and transient debugging traces.
- Conversation persistence: by default, Redwood does not persist customer conversation history beyond transient logs unless the customer enables the optional "conversation retention" feature (used for analytics/eval). If enabled, persisted conversations are retained according to the customer's selected policy (30/90/365 days).
- Audit logs (control plane events): retained for 365 days by default and available as export on request.

3) Deletion workflow & proof
- Erasure request: Customers submit a Data Erasure Request (via Console or Support ticket). For hosted customers, Redwood executes a tenant-scoped purge job that removes persistent storage and marks associated cache entries for eviction.
- SLA: standard hosted purge executes within 72 hours for persistent data; ephemeral caches are usually cleared within minutes.
- Proof: after job completion we issue a Data Erasure Report that includes a purge job id and an immutable audit log entry (audit id) detailing objects deleted. We can provide a redacted sample for your team under NDA.

4) Backups & snapshots
- Backups of platform state (control-plane metadata) are taken periodically. By default, hosted backups do not include customer request payloads unless the customer has enabled long-term conversation retention.
- Replication: backups are region-local by default. Cross-region replication for DR is only enabled for customers who opt into our Multi-Region DR offering (explicit opt-in; not default).

5) Key management & encryption
- Encryption at rest: all customer data is encrypted at rest. Keys are Redwood-managed by default.
- Customer-managed keys: we support integration with customer KMS (AWS KMS, Google Cloud KMS) for hosted customers on enterprise plans. When enabled, encryption keys are controlled by the customer and we store only key identifiers and access logs.

6) Evidence & attestation
- We can provide SOC 2 Type II and a Data Handling Attestation under an NDA. Sample audit extracts and a one-off retention matrix export can be provided; typical turnaround for an attestation package is 5–10 business days.

Hi Laura — chiming in from Redwood Security. Short answers and an offer to provide redacted examples under NDA: 

- Backups & encryption: if a customer enables conversation retention and also opts into encrypted backups, backups of that data are encrypted with either Redwood-managed keys or the customer KMS key (if they configured CMK at onboarding). Cross-region copies are not created without the customer explicitly selecting Multi-Region DR. We never copy retained customer payloads to a different jurisdiction unless the customer has a contractually agreed DR configuration.

- Data Erasure Report sample fields (we can provide a redacted CSV/PDF under NDA):
  • purge_job_id
  • tenant_id (redacted)
  • objects_targeted_count
  • objects_deleted_count
  • deletion_start_time
  • deletion_end_time
  • audit_entry_id
  • operator_id (service account)
  • retention_policy_applied

- Audit logs: control-plane audit events (job submission, job completion, object deletion markers) are retained for 365 days and include timestamps, job ids, and checksums where applicable. Those logs are sufficient to demonstrate the sequence of events for an erasure request.
