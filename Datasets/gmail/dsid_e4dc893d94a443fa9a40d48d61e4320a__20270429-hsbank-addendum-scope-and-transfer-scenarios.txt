Following Marcus' note, a few specifics from an ops and subprocessor perspective (high level; happy to deep-dive on the call):

- Residency lanes we typically recommend:
  * Hosted + onshore logging: Use hosted inference but route logs/telemetry to an onshore logging endpoint and apply subprocessor gating. This is the fastest path (~2–4 weeks).
  * Dedicated (reserved GPU pools in us-east-1): model serving and cache isolated to customer pool; logs can be surfaced only to customer VPC. Typical lead time 6–8 weeks.
  * Private (customer VPC or on-prem appliance): full control, supports customer HSM, longer lead time.

- KMS: For Dedicated we can accept AWS CMKs with cross-account key policy. For Private we can take customer HSM/KMS with dual-control. For Hosted we do envelope encryption and can support a bring-your-KMS model for enterprise plans (there may be additional integration work).

- Logs & telemetry retention: configurable per-account; we can set a 30/90/365 day retention and offer a no-export option for the GLBA-scoped dataset (i.e. never persist raw logs outside customer VPC for the GLBA workload).
