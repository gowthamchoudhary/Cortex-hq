Per-feature math (example 500 clinicians, 6 interactions/mo)

- If we price feature at $1,450/1M tokens: revenue/month = 6 * $1,450 = $8,700? (Better to compute: revenue = (6M/1M)*$1,450 = $8,700) -> gross margin = (8,700 - 4,680) / 8,700 ≈ 46%.

CAC payback (example)

Using mid anchor (margin ~46%), monthly gross contribution = 37.5k * 0.46 ≈ 17.25k -> payback ~0.7 months (fast).
