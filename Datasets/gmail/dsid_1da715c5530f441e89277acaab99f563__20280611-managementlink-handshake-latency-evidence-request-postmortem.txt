Quick triage notes (initial):

What we saw in our early telemetry: between 01:50-03:15 UTC there are intermittent increases in the management-link handshake latency (SYN->TLS handshake completion) localized to nodes that received the private-agent v2.13 rollout. The runtime traces show occasional 200-400ms additional delay in the client hello->server hello exchange for a small percentage of connections (~0.7% of handshakes) that line up with customer-observed 504s.

Root hypothesis so far: a configuration change in the v2.13 private-agent altered the keepalive/handshake retransmit timers. On some kernels this surfaces as delayed ACKs which prolong TLS negotiation and occasionally triggers request timeouts at the load layer. We are reproducing locally and collecting:

Subject: Post-incident update and preliminary RCA — private-agent v2.13 rollout

Hi Susan — thank you again for the pcap and the timestamps. Quick summary of where things stand and the corrective action we took: 

Summary:
- Root cause (prelim): a timing change in private-agent v2.13 adjusted the management-link TCP/TLS keepalive and retransmit timers. On a subset of kernel/network stack combinations used by ClearHealth, this caused delayed ACK behavior that manifests as longer handshake latency and occasionally triggers request timeouts at the proxy/load layer.
- Mitigation performed: at 02:30 UTC we rolled back the private-agent on the affected staging pools to v2.12 and restored the previous keepalive configuration. Post-rollback telemetry shows handshake latency returned to baseline and error rates dropped to normal within ~5 minutes.

Artifacts attached for your audit and records: redwood_prelim_rca_20280612.pdf (redacted), privnet-handshake-trace-20280611.jsonl, tcpdump-clearhealth-20280611.pcap.
