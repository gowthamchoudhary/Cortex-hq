Hi Olivia —

Thanks for the clear list. Below is a concise response we can share with your legal/security teams; I also attached a customer-friendly acceptance checklist and our internal site-lock playbook (redacted for security).

Summary (high level):
- Region pinning: For Dedicated, a tenant is assigned to a single region (region-aware pool). Active compute, the KV cache, model KV stores, and object storage snapshots for that tenant remain within the pinned region by default.
- Backups & snapshots: Automated backups for Dedicated are retained in the same region unless a cross-region backup policy is explicitly enabled by the customer. We do not enable cross-region replication without a written request and customer opt-in (documented via ticket).
- Ephemeral caches: Transient caches are local to the serving cluster; eviction and rehydrate hops do not stage data in other regions.
- Tenant wipe flow: 1) Customer submits formal wipe request via support portal (or signed email). 2) Engineering schedules a controlled wipe job on the pinned cluster; 3) We sweep primary storage, delete relevant snapshots, and then run a verification scrub that enumerates object/store keys and confirms tombstone markers.
- SLAs and timing: Standard wipe completion target is 72 hours for datasets under 5TB; large datasets are on a case-by-case timeline (we state expected completion in the ticket).
- Evidence & validation: Post-wipe we provide: (a) scrub log (listing object keys removed), (b) snapshot deletion audit entries, and (c) a signed attestation. For higher assurance customers we can provide a wipe-run-snapshot file with timestamped hashes.
- Legal holds: If you place a retention/legal hold, we pause scheduled scrubs and archive the hold metadata; a separate authorized release is required to proceed.

Mina / Lauren — quick internal note on artifacts and timings.

- Snapshots/backups: Confirmed — default behavior keeps snapshots in-region. Cross-region replication is off unless the customer explicitly requests it and signs the configuration change (we create a recorded work item that includes the requestor and justification).
- Wipe verification artifacts: We can provide the following after a wipe completes:
  * wipe-run-snapshot-<timestamp>.txt — contains a list of object keys deleted with SHA256 hashes and UTC timestamps (we redact internal hostnames).
  * snapshot-deletion-audit.log — audit entries showing snapshot IDs and deletion timestamps.
  * ticket-links and attestation: We attach the support ticket and a signed attestation from the ops lead.
- Typical timing for a 1.2TB tenant: Plan for 24–36 hours to perform controlled deletion and verification on the pinned cluster, plus 12–36 hours to reconcile snapshot metadata and run the scrub across object storage depending on queueing. Our public guideline of 72 hours covers this window; we will communicate any longer timelines upfront.
