Hi Asha — quick guidance from Security: we treat Hosted and Dedicated pools as within the common control environment and those were included in our 2025 Type II audit. Private/VPC configurations are customer-managed network boundaries; the core Redwood controls still apply (change control, identity, logging), but network-level controls in a customer VPC are out of scope for our auditor.

What we can provide for Private/VPC customers: 
- Draft bridge letter tailored to Finexa describing which controls we assert and which controls require customer responsibility (network, VPC ACLs).
- Control mapping (CSV) that maps Redwood controls to SOC 2 criteria and typical evidence we can produce (logs, patch records, config snapshots).
- Redacted network topology diagram showing connector handoff points (we will remove internal IPs).

We will attach the draft bridge letter and the redacted topology for your review. If Finexa requires the bridge letter to be signed and notarized, let us know—our standard is an executed DocuSign copy.
