Hi team — quick technical summary for Elena:
- The upcoming model variant changes affect tokenizer slightly for a subset of embedding models. Typical delta is +0 to +2 tokens per 1k tokens for plain-text inputs; in pathological whitespace cases it can be +4–6.
- No change to vector dimensionality or embedding surface APIs is planned. Payload size is unchanged.

Implication: most customers will not see measurable billing changes; customers with strict token-based billing should validate with their representative prompts.

For the exemption process: we can pin a model variant (quant) for accounts for up to 90 days. This requires a signed exemption form and an ops ticket. There's a capacity cost; Priyom will confirm cost/backfill.
