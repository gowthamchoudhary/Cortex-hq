Hi Emma — Karthik from Security & Privacy here. Quick technical clarifications to address your follow-ups below; please let me know if you want deeper detail or a short call.

1) BYOK / KMS separation:
- Statement: For BYOK configurations, the customer provisioned key material remains under the customer's KMS/HSM. Redwood stores and uses only ciphertext and wrapped keys; we do not have access to unwrapped customer key material or plaintext. We can provide an HSM-backed key-wrap flow and an operator-level audit trail showing key usage for verification.
- Attachment: KMS-separation-diagram.png (diagram showing key-wrap and envelope encryption flow).

2) EU-only routing availability:
- Hosted self-serve: We provide an EU-region hosted option that ensures inference infrastructure and token processing remain in EU regions for standard workload patterns (subject to compatible model selection). This is generally available with account region pinning and is suitable for many controller requirements.
- Dedicated/Private: For stricter regulatory/regulator evaluation (auditability, isolated subnets, or air-gapped ingest), our Dedicated and Private deployments provide stronger contractual guarantees and support customer-managed VPCs and HSMs. Lead time: hosted EU-routing is immediate on account enablement; Dedicated/Private typically requires a 4–8 week provisioning window depending on region and compliance add-ons.

3) Audit/log routing and retention:
- Admin audit logs can be configured to be retained within EU-only storage endpoints. Default operational telemetry (service health telemetry) may traverse minimal, aggregated US-based endpoints for ops; however, customer-facing audit logs and request/response traces that contain PII can be configured to stay in-region for customers that require it.
- Retention: Token/debug logs default 30 days; we can set down to 0 for customers that require no retention. We also support long-term export to customer-managed S3/GCS compatible targets for archiving.

4) SCCs and execution notes:
- We will provide a DPA annex with SCCs embedded and options for binding corporate rules or standard contractual clauses depending on your preference. Execution typically follows standard legal review; Marissa will supply the annex draft and redline workflow. Attachment: SCCs-execution-note.docx (legal note on SCC selection and implementation).
