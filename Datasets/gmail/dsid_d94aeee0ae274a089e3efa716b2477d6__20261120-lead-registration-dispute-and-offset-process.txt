Hi Caroline — thanks for the clear scenario and for attaching the registration form. I work Partner Ops / Partner Legal at Redwood and can confirm our policy and the exceptions process. TL;DR: we can make NimbleStack whole for this if the evidence meets our thresholds; here are the specifics.

Policy summary:
- Standard registration protection: 90 days from registration acceptance. Extended protection (180 days) requires signed MOU or SOW at time of registration.
- Evidence threshold for retroactive credit: one of the following is required — (a) CRM timestamp + email thread showing first outreach before Redwood opportunity creation; (b) signed SOW/LOI predating Redwood opportunity; (c) mutually acknowledged MMP (mutual marketing plan) with customer signature.
- Lookback window: default 12 months from customer's close date. Exceptions for enterprise (> $500k ARR) can be requested and reviewed by Partner Legal.
- Remedy model: if evidence passes review we generally issue a retroactive commission payment net of any discounts or credits Redwood applied to the invoice. Alternatively, we can apply an agreed offset against future commissions for up to 12 months if the partner prefers cashflow smoothing.
- Caps and limits: we do not make downward adjustments to customer pricing; commission adjustments are strictly between Redwood and the partner. For bundled services, splits must be tied to invoiced line-items and are subject to audit.

Process and contacts:
1) Partner files an Exceptions Request form (link: https://redwood.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/partners/Exceptions_Request_Form.xlsx).
2) Attach supporting evidence (CRM export, email threads, signed docs, registration form).
3) Partner Ops reviews within 10 business days; if accepted, Partner Finance issues payment within 30 days or applies offsets per the partner's election.

I'll attach a redlined Partner Exceptions checklist to this thread. Two quick notes: we cannot honor registrations that were never acknowledged by Redwood Partner Ops (i.e., if a registration was sent to an individual AE but not recorded in our portal before opportunity creation). Also, for co-sell deals where both Redwood and NimbleStack contributed materially to pipeline, we reserve the right to apply a pro-rated split rather than a full reassignment.
