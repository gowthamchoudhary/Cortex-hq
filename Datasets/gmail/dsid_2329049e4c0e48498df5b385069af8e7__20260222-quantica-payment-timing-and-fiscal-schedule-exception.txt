Grace — thanks for the context. I reviewed the attached commercial proposal and the estimated spend. Quick notes / conditions from Finance: 

1) Net-60 + quarterly invoicing is acceptable for a 6-month pilot only, provided we receive an upfront signed purchase order that explicitly references the quarter invoice schedule and payment account.

2) Because quarterly invoices increase our receivable window, we will add a 1.25% invoice processing surcharge to cover working capital costs (applied to each quarterly invoice). If sales prefers to waive the surcharge for strategic reasons we need a written exec approval and acceptance of increased DSO in our forecast.

3) Require ACH or wire as primary payment method; checks will incur an additional 7 business day processing buffer.

4) Credit check: request a simple trade reference and a logo/finance contact. If Quantica cannot provide references, we'll limit the pilot to a $60k cap (first two quarters billed).

If Ben/Laureen (cc) and Revenue Ops are comfortable with the commercial offsets, I'm good to sign off and RFP the terms to legal to add clauses for audit rights and termination if payments fall 60+ days past due.
