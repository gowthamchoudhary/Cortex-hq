Thanks Karthik — quick edits and suggested language below (inline/high-level):

On KMS/BYOK: I agree we should not accept customer-managed KMS for Hosted as a default. Suggested clause we can propose for Hosted: “Customer may request BYOK for Hosted under a separate onboarding schedule; acceptance of BYOK requires completion of Redwood’s technical validation and may be subject to additional fees and a minimum term.” For Dedicated/Private, include standard BYOK commitment.

On SCCs and Transfer-Impact: propose we accept SCCs with an exhibit that lists our data flows and subprocessors and a short transfer-impact addendum (TIA) template. The TIA should be explicitly scoped to the agreement’s initial subprocessor list and state that additional subprocessors are governed by the notification process. This prevents open-ended retroactive obligations.

Suggested audit evidence language: “RNW will provide substantiating evidence in response to reasonable requests within ten (10) business days of receipt; where evidence requires third-party engagement, RNW will use commercially reasonable efforts and provide an expected delivery date.”

I'll draft the counter-DPA and include the TIA template by EOD.
