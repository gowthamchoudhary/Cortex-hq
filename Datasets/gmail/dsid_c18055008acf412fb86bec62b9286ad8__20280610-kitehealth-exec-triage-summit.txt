Quick technical read from infra:
- Correlation shows the latency/error spike coincides with a quantized variant swap for kite-text-v1 at 05:40 PT. The new variant (v3-q8) appears to trigger repeated KV cache invalidation on nodes with older NV cache warm states — this is producing memory pressure and intermittent OOMs, which manifest as 502/504 from the SGW.
- Rollback path: we can revert traffic to v2 (legacy non-quantized) across the affected Dedicated pool. Expected rollback time: ~12 minutes to drain and switch traffic, plus ~10–15 minutes for KV warmup to recover tail latency metrics.
- Mitigations in parallel: enable per-request retry with exponential backoff at the gateway (low-cost), and route latency-sensitive customers to the hot dedicated pool if capacity allows.

I will start the rollback dry-run at 09:05 PT if we green-light. I’ve uploaded a snapshot (sla-metrics.csv) to the drive link for the 04:00–08:30 window.
