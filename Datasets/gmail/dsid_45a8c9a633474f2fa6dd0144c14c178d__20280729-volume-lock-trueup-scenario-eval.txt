Key tradeoffs: predictability (A) vs flexibility and potential lower initial cash outlay (B). For a 2-region rollout we'd recommend a hybrid: modest region-level commits (to secure capacity) plus a company-level true-up and an onboarding credit tied to migration milestones (pilot -> production).
