Emma —

Answers inline:
1) Yes — split-pay (50/50) across fiscal years is fine. We carve the discount off the committed baseline, not the cash timing, so you will still qualify for the multi-year blended discount if you commit to the full dollar amount up-front in the term sheet. We model both cash-flow and accounting treatment in the attached spreadsheet.

2) Service fee / early-exit options:
   - Option A (Time-pro-rated refund): refund = prepaid_balance * (remaining_days / total_term_days) - 6% administrative/service fee. No minimum clawback beyond the fee.
   - Option B (Amortized schedule): we amortize the discount benefit over a 36-month sunset. Refund = prepaid_balance - recognized_amount; recognized_amount is the sum of monthly amortized revenue. We can use an escrow for the unrecognized portion with a fixed release schedule.

3) Anniversary alignment: we can co-align the contract to 1 Jan without impact to pricing, but that will require a partial pro-rating in the first year (we treat the partial period as an initial ramp window).

Worked example: 3-year commit, $1.8M total, proposed commercial compromise: 30% Year-1 discount on the expected consumption bucket with an amortized clawback that phases out linearly over 36 months. If terminated at 18 months, the modeled refund in Option B (escrow approach) is approximately $460k (see line 47 in the CSV). I’ve attached a version with the example highlighted.

If that sounds directionally OK I’ll ask legal to produce a redline of the early-exit language specifying exact amortization math and whether escrow is required.

Vivek / Tessa —

I’ve prepared a redline (attached, Redwood_Term_Redline_Legal.docx). Key legal positions to preserve Redwood’s economics:
- Sunset credits: we will amortize any discount benefit over a defined period (36 months by default). That should be express in SOW Exhibit B with the amortization table; otherwise the customer can treat the whole discount as non-refundable.
- Administrative fee: recommend 8% rather than 6% to cover re-provisioning & contract admin costs.
- No blanket escrow requirement: escrow is optional and to be used only if the customer requests accelerated release terms.
- Anniversary alignment: we can accept alignment requests but must include a first-year pro-rate to lock in the agreed effective price.

I redlined the termination clause to require the customer to provide 30 days’ notice for early termination and added an explicit calculation example. If Clarity objects to the 8% admin fee, we can offer 6% but tie that concession to a shorter refund window (e.g., 24 months amortization).
