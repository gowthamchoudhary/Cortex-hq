Hi Jane / Samir — Dev here from infra. Below are our operational answers and the attachments referenced above (SLA spec + monitoring onboarding sheet). I kept each item aligned to your numbering for easy copy/paste into your questionnaire.

1) Uptime / availability SLOs
- Hosted API (standard): 99.9% monthly availability (measured over calendar month).
- Dedicated (reserved capacity contracts): typical contractual SLOs start at 99.95% monthly; higher tiers available by negotiation.
- Measurement method: availability is computed using 1-minute evaluation windows across three layers: control plane health, serving node reachability, and API data-plane success rate. We synthesize internal checks and also compute per-tenant success-rate by aggregating request-level outcomes (2xx = success, 5xx/timeout = failure). We maintain a 3rd-party probe set for cross-region verification.

2) Latency SLOs
- We publish recommended operational targets rather than hard latency SLAs for Hosted: e.g., p95 < 220ms for short generation requests (<512 tokens); p99 targets are provided per-model. For Dedicated, latency SLAs can be contracted for p95 and p99 with explicit workload profiles.
- Measurement: per-request timings are captured at ingress and at final token emission; latency percentiles are computed on 1-minute rolling windows and aggregated to hourly and daily percentiles for reporting. We record p50/p95/p99 and sample traces for tail analysis.
- Bust handling: when observed SLO breach occurs (see incident window below), we evaluate root cause, calculate customer impact windows, and apply credits per SLA spec or invoke fallback policies (model downgrade or regional failover) per customer config.

3) Metrics and customer access
- Exposed metrics: request rate (RPS), success rate, error breakdown (4xx/5xx), average latency, p95/p99 latencies, active connections, KV cache hit ratio, and per-model token counts.
- Access: customers receive a read-only Grafana dashboard scoped to their tenant and can request a Prometheus metrics endpoint (scrape-proxy with tenant-scoped tokens). Raw metrics retention: 90 days. Aggregated time-series (hourly) retained 365 days for billing and audits.

4) Alerting and on-call
- Hooks: we support PagerDuty, OpsGenie, email, and webhook endpoints. Customers can register escalation policies for notification mirroring.
- On-call & MTTR: our primary SRE on-call target is to acknowledge P1 incidents within 15 minutes and hit a median time-to-repair of 1.5 hours for common infra issues; larger incidents with cross-region impact target an RTO defined in the contract (often 4–8 hours depending on Dedicated tier).

5) SLA credits and exceptions
- Credits: credits are calculated as a percentage of monthly service fees proportional to the fraction of time the SLO was not met (see SLA-Spec-v1.2.pdf, section 4). For example, Hosted 99.9% breach results in graduated credits starting at 10% for a small window up to 100% in extreme prolonged outages per the spec.
- Exceptions: scheduled maintenance windows (regular maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance) are excluded from SLA calculations. Emergency maintenance and force majeure are considered on a case-by-case basis and documented in an exceptions log.
- Exception requests: customers open an exceptions ticket via support portal and include impact evidence; expedited remediation or credit claims are handled by our Support -> CS -> Contracts workflow. Critical exception requests can be escalated to our Head of SRE and Legal for fast-track review.

6) Read-only dashboard & onboarding
- We can provision a tenant-scoped Grafana link within 48 hours of contract signature and provide a Prometheus scrape token during onboarding. We also provide a short onboarding window (2 weeks) where our monitoring engineers assist with queries and alerts.

7) Runbooks and incident response
- Attached: SLA-Spec-v1.2.pdf (SLO definitions, credit calculations, measurement rules) and Monitoring-Onboarding-Guide.xlsx (dashboard samples, metric names, example alert rules).

Notes on measurement nuance (common procurement questions):
- Rolling windows: availability and latency percentiles use rolling 1-minute windows to avoid amplification from bursty traffic. Monthly SLO evaluation aggregates these minute windows into outage windows per the SLA spec.
- Multi-tenant isolation: metrics are computed tenant-scoped after request attribution. We do not expose other customers' identifiers in any dashboard.
- Audit logs: we provide request-level audit logs for Dedicated customers under a separate retention agreement (typically 12 months) and integrate with customer SIEMs via secure log forwarding.
