Thanks for the ping — looked through the sample and correlated with our telemetry. Notes: 

- The spike windows line up with two things: a) HeliumGrid's burst traffic, and b) background nightly batch compaction that sometimes overlaps morning restarts on a subset of EU hosts (rare but measurable).
- The runtime is prioritizing fairness across tenants which can cause your tail to increase if you're hitting burst concurrency limits.

Options and lead times:
1) Quick pilot (recommended): Add 2x reserved GPUs to your EU pool for 09:00–18:00 UTC Mon–Fri. We can enable this as a scheduled override within 24–48 hours and monitor. No code change required.
2) Priority lane: Configure tenant-level soft priority so your in-flight tokens get ahead of batch traffic. Requires a config push and a smoke test; estimate 3–5 business days.
3) Time-sliced reservation: Create a time-sliced reservation window in the dedicated cluster (more formal contract change). Lead time ~2 weeks and legal/ops signoff.

Trade-offs: scheduled extra GPUs are fastest but costlier; priority lane is lower-cost but requires careful fairness tuning to avoid impacting other customers. For a launch-sensitive customer, we usually recommend (1) pilot for immediate mitigation + (2) implement (2) if pilot shows positive results.
