Emma — adding rack and site-prep notes below so facilities can evaluate physical constraints. See inline answers and a proposed checklist at the end.

Inline: 
> maintenance windows limited to weekly 4-hour windows (Sat 02:00–06:00 local)
We can schedule non-disruptive firmware and minor patching during that window. Major upgrades that require reboots of the cluster should target a canary approach to avoid full downtime.

> no outbound internet from the appliance network
We require a secure jump box pattern for the signed USB installer. The jump box must be on a separate maintenance VLAN that can be physically controlled by your team. All installer signing and verification is done offline — we provide checksums and a signature bundle for validation.

> on-site engineer networked to a restricted ops VLAN
Great — that fits our expected operational model. Please confirm whether your engineer will have console access to the appliance (serial/KVM) or only local GUI. That changes the level of pre-staged spares we recommend.

Proposed site checklist (short):
- Physical rack depth: min 1100mm, 42U recommended for scale.
- Power: dual-cord PDUs with redundant feeds; list breakers and tag circuits.
- Cooling: N+1 CRAC suggested for high-util GPU racks.
- Network: at least two isolated 10Gbps links (management + data).
- Access: secure lockable cabinet and a maintenance VLAN for jump-boxing.
