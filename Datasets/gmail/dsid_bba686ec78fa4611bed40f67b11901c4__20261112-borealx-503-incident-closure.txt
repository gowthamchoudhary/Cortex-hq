Hi all — ops update and answers to Priya’s questions below. Copying the incident channel notes.

Short answers
- No evidence of data escaping project boundaries or being persisted externally. Traces indicate failures occured during KV cache management paths, not in the network/persistence layers.
- We will NOT charge for failed/errored tokens during the mitigation window (11/12 08:40–11/12 12:00 PST). We will apply billing adjustments to the affected invoices and follow up with a credit.

Technical summary (ops notes)
- Root cause (preliminary): A runtime change deployed in v2.7 modified the attention-KV garbage collection heuristic. Under very long-context workloads, this produced a growth in per-request memory accounting that could trigger cache eviction races and then cause worker threads to return 5xx when a critical resource was exhausted.
- Impact vector: Requests with >10k tokens processed in a single session (docs ingestion + multi-turn chat) disproportionately hit the codepath.
- Fix applied: Rolled back the v2.7 heuristic to the previous stable behavior on the BorealX serving pool at 09:50 PST. We also increased the KV cache soft limit by 25% for the affected pool to smooth the transition.
- Validation: Post-rollback telemetry shows 5xx rate dropped to baseline (<0.2%) within 12 minutes. We ran synthetic workload samples that approximate Boreal’s traffic and saw stable latency and no eviction-driven errors.

Follow-ups and commitments
1) Engineering will produce a formal RCA document by EOD tomorrow with exact timelines and a patch that hardens the GC heuristic under long-context workloads.
2) We will add a synthetic test that replays your long-session traces against new runtime builds before canarying them.
3) We'll provide you with the raw failure samples we collected (attached) and schedule a 1:1 technical review next week to walk through fixes and any changes you should expect in latency/throughput.

Follow-up as promised — consolidated post-incident note and next steps. Thank you for your patience and for flagging observed retries.

Incident timeline (UTC):
- 2026-11-12T08:40:00Z — first uptick in 5xx observed for BorealX pool servicing boreal-prod.
- 2026-11-12T09:50:00Z — rollback of v2.7 attention-KV heuristic on affected pool initiated.

Root cause (final)
- A memory-accounting regression introduced in runtime v2.7 changed the timing of KV garbage collection under long-context encoding. For certain workloads that maintain long-lived KV state, GC could fail to reclaim memory quickly enough, causing worker resource exhaustion and resulting in 5xx/timeouts.

Customer impact and actions we took
- Affected requests: primarily long-session ingestions and multi-turn chats >10k tokens originating from your project.
- Data exposure: No evidence that any input or output left your project or was written to external systems.
- Billing: We will issue a credit for all errored tokens during the incident window (11/12 08:40–11/12 12:00 PST). Our billing team will follow up with the exact credit amount within 5 business days.

Remediation and prevention plan
1) Engineering patch: A permanent fix that corrects the GC timing and adds conservative back-pressure to the KV path will be deployed behind canary by 11/20/2026.
2) Regression tests: New long-context synthetic tests will be added to the CI pipeline to block builds that regress memory under replayed traces.
3) Canary policy: We’ve updated our canary policy to include a long-context traffic slice for any runtime or kernel changes affecting KV/attention behavior.
4) Operational: We will expose a per-pool KV headroom metric in Console so customers can monitor the metric tied to this issue.
