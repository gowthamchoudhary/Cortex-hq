Data points:
- avg_seq_len (last 90m): 2.7k -> 6.1k median during spike
- batch_size distribution shifted right (p50->p75 increased by ~40%)
- no infra hardware faults; pool headroom exhausted for sustained period

C) Shorten batch window to 20ms to reduce large-sequence aggregation (runtime change) — low-risk, no restart, takes ~10 minutes to propagate.

Longer-term: recommend a model-variant policy change for pinned customers so we can transparently serve quantized equivalents during headroom events, and an autoscale soft-threshold tied to sequence-length distribution.
