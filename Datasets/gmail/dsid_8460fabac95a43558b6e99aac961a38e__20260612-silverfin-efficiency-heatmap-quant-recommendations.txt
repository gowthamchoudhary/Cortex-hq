Hi Elena — Karthik here. I put together a short technical note and conservative assumptions that Naomi/Grace used to build the heatmap (attached as quant-recs-summary.pdf). High level up front:

1) Quantization impact (typical for our verified model variants):
   - FP16 -> INT8: ~35-45% per-token compute cost reduction; P99 typically increases by ~10-20ms but often stays within SLO for 2k/1k profiles when batching is enabled.
   - INT8 -> INT4: additional ~20-30% compute savings on supported kernels; P99 increases more noticeably (~40-70ms) and can push you past 400ms depending on kernel maturity and sequence length. INT4 is best for very high throughput background jobs or when you can accept occasional P99 spikes.

2) Batching vs immediate latency:
   - Small batching windows (5-20ms) that maintain tail latency often provide 20-40% throughput efficiency at minimal P99 impact.
   - Aggressive batching (50-200ms) yields bigger cost savings but drives P99 up; not recommended for strict 400ms SLO unless combined with prefix/KV caching.

3) KV/Prefix caching assumptions used for heatmap:
   - Conservative hit-rate: 20-30% (default scenario) — this assumes moderate reuse in retrieval flows.
   - Optimistic hit-rate: 45-60% — requires pinned prompts or heavy overlap across users/sessions.
   - Cache reduces generation compute proportionally to the cached token count and can dramatically lower P99 when the cached prefix is large (>512 tokens).

4) Operational notes:
   - For bursts up to 180 qps, we recommend a mixed policy: route latency-sensitive requests to lower-batch/FP16 or INT8 nodes and route bulk background/gap-filling traffic to INT4-preferred nodes with relaxed tail SLOs.
   - Because SilverFin cannot use external persistent caches, we recommend an in-cluster KV cache with encrypted memory-only storage; we can document kube-level config and expected memory footprint (see attached heatmap Excel for examples).

I'll highlight two concrete recommendations in the attached PDF (and the XLSX has the full heatmap): 1) Start on INT8 with small batching (10-20ms) + 25% conservative KV hit rate; 2) Evaluate INT4 for non-latency-critical backfills after a 2-week AB test.
