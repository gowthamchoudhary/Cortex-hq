Hi all — quick summary after the baseline validation runs we completed earlier today against staging.df.internal. Short executive summary (full artifacts attached): bench-results-20261201.pdf

Measured baseline (steady-state, warmed caches):
- Peak throughput: 12,000 tokens/sec (sustained for 30m during step ramp)
- Median (P50) latency: 120 ms per token
- P95 token latency: 420 ms
- TTFT (client-observed median): 620 ms; TTFT 95th: 890 ms
- Sustained concurrency: 400 streams for 2 hours without elevated error rates (errors <0.5%)

Observations and notes:
- KV cache warm-up cost affects first 5–10 minutes of a run; recommend a 20–30 minute warm-up period for MAP rubric measurement.
- The 600-concurrency short spike produced increased P95 to ~1.1s and token drops at 3% — acceptable for stress validation but outside our acceptance SLOs.
- Function-heavy prompts increased TTFT by ~150–250 ms due to additional decoding and external call overhead; we recommend scope-limiting function calls during the acceptance run unless DataForge specifically requires them.

Recommended next steps / acceptance checklist for sign-off:
- DataForge confirms final acceptance SLOs (we recommend P95 token latency <=500ms and TTFT 95th <=900ms after warm-up).
