Hi Elena — quick operational notes from AP:

- Portal is our strongly preferred ingestion path because it preserves metadata and triggers immediate acks. Please prioritize portal for high-volume partners.
- Email fallback is allowed for onboarding and edge cases. Requirements for email fallback:
  * Subject line must contain token as Marcus described (INV|PO{PO}|VW{VENDOR}). Missing token will put the invoice into manual triage and delay processing.
  * Attachments: one PDF per invoice preferred; if you must send CSV, include a PDF stub or attach a reconciliation PDF to match.
  * Minimal line-item rule: at least one invoice-level total and per-line quantity/description. We cannot ingest line-less credit memos via email without a form.
  * For multi-file per PO: allowed, but each file must include the PO in both filename and subject token. We will merge by PO on ingest.
- Example ack header we emit on receipt (email): "Received: INV|PO123456|VW9876|RcvdTS=2026-07-20T17:05:00Z". I'll attach a brief sample text you can use in automation.

We'll provision a temporary vendor ID after you return the onboarding PDF; Marcus will coordinate the vendor creation.
