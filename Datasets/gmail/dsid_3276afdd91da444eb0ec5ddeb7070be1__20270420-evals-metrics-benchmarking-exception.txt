Adding the eval details — I ran both A/B baseline and the new v2 set across the canary model variants. Highlights attached in eval_summary_20270420.pdf. TL;DR: semantic_match_f1 drops by ~1.6 pts (acceptable within noise), hallucination_rate increases from 1.2% -> 3.8% on v2, concentrated on a small subset (~3%) of prompts where the prompt injects a numeric comparison that the model misattributes.

Notes / suggestions:
- I agree with Dev's rollback triggers; the 4% absolute threshold maps closely to the empirical spike.
- For canary-only exception: accept temporary relaxation for 48h with the following constraints:
  * Canary must be throttled to <10% production queries.
  * Add a targeted prompt-filter that tags examples matching the known failure pattern and routes them to a 'safe path' (lower-capacity but conservative model) until we patch the model.
  * Add a dedicated eval shard that samples 1000 canary queries every hour and posts results to the dashboard.

I attached the raw CSV heatmap and a short analysis. If we get signoff, I can prepare a quick patch to the canary orchestration and the dashboard queries.

-markus
