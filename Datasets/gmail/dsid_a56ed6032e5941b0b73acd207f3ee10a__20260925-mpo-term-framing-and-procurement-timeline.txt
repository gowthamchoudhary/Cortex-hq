Hi Alicia — great to hear from you and glad the demo was useful. Quick answers below and then a couple options attached for your procurement/CFO.

1) Term vs price: We typically publish three tiers for private offers: 12m (baseline), 24m (5–12% unit discount depending on commit), 36m (12–20% depending on volume). Exact % depends on committed spend and whether you opt for reserved capacity (Dedicated) vs hosted API. See the attached spreadsheet that maps illustrative bands and discount triggers.

2) Offer window: A private offer link is editable for 14 calendar days by default. We can request an extension up to 30 days for enterprise procurement processes, but we need the request before the original window expires. For deals requiring CFO approval beyond 30 days we typically roll a short-term pricing hold and then lock pricing once a PO is received.

Options I recommend (attached as "redwood-mpo-term-options.xlsx"):
- Option A: 12m baseline, expedited activation (no upfront commit) — shortest approval path, standard price.
- Option B: 24m with 8% unit discount, 14-day offer window, procurement to attach PO once CFO sings off; we hold price for an additional 7 business days on request.
- Option C: 36m with blended discount, reserved capacity add-on for predictability; requires legal review and longer lead time.
