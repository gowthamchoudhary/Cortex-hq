Following up with technical detail and a recommended minimal-risk path (quoting Dylan's bullets below):

On regionally pinned buckets and single attestation:
- We will implement per-tenant routing rules in ingest so EU-origin traffic is persisted to eu-immutable buckets and US-origin to us-immutable buckets. Metadata (tenant_id, event_stream, sequence_id, ingest_ts) will be written alongside payloads so the chain of custody is preserved.

On export format and delivery:
- Export output: gzipped newline-delimited JSON (ndjson) files, accompanied by a signed manifest JSON containing per-file SHA-256, byte count, and record count.
- Delivery options:
  * Push to customer S3 (preferred) with temporary presigned URLs for verification.
  * Secure SCP to an Acme-managed endpoint for isolated transfers.
- A custody ledger (audit events: requested_by, approved_by, export_job_id, checksums) will be created and retained for 2 years after export.

Operational impact and cost:
- Retaining selected streams at 24 months vs 12 months increases cold storage and indexing surface. Rough model: +7% on your committed capacity spend for this footprint (subject to exact volume). Exports are billed as 1) export run fee and 2) transfer egress (if pushed).

Compliance notes for SOC 2 team:
- We'll ensure the export runbook and the per-bucket controls are included in the re-attestation test plan. Kimberly, can we add the export validation test to the attestation checklist?
