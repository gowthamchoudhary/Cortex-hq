Sergio, team —

I reviewed Acorn's redlines. Summary and recommended compromises below (I will produce a clean DPA for signature if we agree):

1) Notification timing
- Acorn asks for 48 hours for material incidents. Redwood's standard language is 72 hours.
- Proposed compromise: 48 hours for \"Critical\" incidents (as defined by agreed severity matrix), 72 hours for all other incidents that materially affect customer data or availability. This gives us operational breathing room while meeting Acorn's need for rapid notice on highest-severity events.

2) Evidence / forensic access
- Acorn requests logs and artifacts within 5 business days. Security concerns center on preserving chain-of-custody and third-party obligations.
- Proposed language: Redwood will provide reasonable technical and legal cooperation, including access to non-production forensic artifacts and relevant logs within 7 business days, accelerated to 5 business days for incidents classified as Critical. All disclosures will be subject to mutual NDA/confidentiality and limited to data necessary to investigate the incident.

3) Subprocessors and subprocessors carve-outs
- Acceptable to add a 30-day prior notice for new subprocessors and the right for Acorn to object in writing within 30 days. For approved subprocessors, Redwood will remain liable for subcontractor compliance.

4) Evidence retention and preservation
- Add a section stating Redwood will preserve logs and relevant forensic data for 90 days after notification unless otherwise required by law. For Critical incidents Redwood will preserve for 180 days.

- If you approve the above, I'll prepare a final PDF with tracked-accepted clauses and we can push the envelope. Suggest Sergio be the primary signer on the Redwood side and Monica be notified on completion for onboarding/ops.
