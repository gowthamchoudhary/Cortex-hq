Subject: INTERNAL: Ops update — HyperionV2 kernel load contention (initial RCA and mitigations)

Team,

Summary (TL;DR): We identified a regression in the HyperionV2 rollout that increased cache thrash on high-concurrency workloads when running the new 4-bit kernel path. That caused the runtime to serially reload quant kernels for some shards, producing transient stalls and 5xx responses.

What happened:
- New HyperionV2 variant (v2.3.1) introduced a revised kernel selector that prefers the 4-bit attention kernel for large sequence lengths. Under high concurrency the kernel selector triggers frequent cache evictions.
- Evictions + on-demand kernel loads introduced contention and an I/O wait pattern on the device which can manifest as timeouts or internal 5xxs.

Impact & scope:
- nv-usw2 pool only; observed spike from ~08:40–09:05 PT with peak error rates at 08:51 PT. NovaApps traffic represented ~12% of the affected requests (their routing policy hit the impacted pool).

Immediate mitigations applied (08:55–09:10 PT):
- Rolled back HyperionV2 to v2.3.0 on nv-usw2 nodes that serve NovaApps traffic.
- Enabled a device-side kernel pre-warm path for high-concurrency customers while we tune the selector.
- Applied a short-rate throttle (10–15% reduction of accepted concurrent generations) for routes pinned to HyperionV2; traffic that tolerates alternate quality was routed to HyperionV2-lite.

Observed outcome: visible 5xx rate dropped to baseline within ~12 minutes of mitigations. We continue to monitor for residual errors.

Next steps:
- Laura to complete a full postmortem doc (timeline, root cause, remediation plan) and share by EOD PT Jul 30.
- Platform engineering to deliver a kernel-selector patch and controlled rollout with additional pre-warm heuristics. Estimated fix → canary rollout in 72 hours.
