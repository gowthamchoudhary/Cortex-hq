Alex — quick note from legal/ops POV, attached is a starting draft (proposed-sla-addendum-v2.docx). Highlights:
- Definition: "Customer-Controlled Network Event" — a networking incident wholly attributable to hardware or configuration under the customer's administrative domain (examples enumerated).
- Evidence: required artifacts for an exclusion claim include time-indexed NAT/gateway logs, BGP route-change outputs, and pcap/traceroute samples where available.
- Timelines: Customer must submit an exclusion request within 7 days of the incident; Redwood has up to 10 business days to validate with the customer.
- Partial attribution: If the incident is jointly attributable (e.g., poorly tuned customer LB amplifies a small host interruption), the SLA remediation will be prorated; we include a short arbitration path.

Open items for us to finalize with you:
1) Are the evidence artifacts above typically available from your side?
2) Is the 7-day submission window workable for your ops team?
3) Would you prefer a longer validation window (e.g., 15 business days) if you need to pull vendor logs?

Alex — if that list works, we’ll prepare a short mutual action plan (MAP) for sign-off and route the addendum to legal.
