Sonia / James —

We put the detailed cost model into the attached PDF. Essentials here:  

- Example calc (5 TB ingest / month): ~150 GB/day avg. If 10% of that is 365d retention, that group contributes ~15 GB/day * 365 days in stored bytes. With our dedupe/compression profile we model an effective stored size of ~0.55x of raw. Using the 365d unit rate in the proposal yields an annualized add-on cost that is ~1.9x the 90d plan for the same hot subset because of storage duration.
- Credits: credits expire at month 12 by default. For customers who convert to Dedicated within 18 months we offer credit rollover as a one-time conversion bonus (we'll apply remaining credits against the first dedicated invoice).
- Data deletion certification: we can include a SOC-aligned attestation and an automated deletion report (PDF) exported on request; legal typically wants language that we provide deletion logs within 30 days of request.

If this looks OK we can produce a priced SOW and a redlineable contract. Happy to hop on a short call with your finance/legal to walk through the numbers.
