Great questions — quick answers below and a suggested concrete structure we can send to procurement/legal: 

1) Co-terming and proration: we can align to the 2028-02-15 window. Practical approach: convert your remaining 6 months on the current contract into a prorated credit applied toward the new 24-month term (credit shows up on the first post-amendment invoice). That avoids duplicate full-price payments.

2) Auto-annualize example: if you reserve N tokens for Year 1 and consume C tokens, unused tokens (N - C) convert at a 50% credit rate toward Year 2 committed spend. Example in attachment: Year 1 reserved 10M tokens, consumed 7M, credit = (3M * unit_price) * 0.5 applied against Year 2 invoice. We show two alternative credit rates (50% or 75%) — we can pick one depending on how conservative you want the economics to be.

3) Ramp / start date: yes — amendment will include an explicit effective start date (2027-10-01) for ramped capacity. For billing we can start guaranteed commit billing on that date and keep burst charges separate as usage occurs.
