Karthik / David — looping legal in with our initial positions. I reviewed Acme's redlines and attached a tracked version focused on pointing language we can accept vs items that need escalation. Summary of our position:

1) Cap basis: Redwood can accept a fee-linked cap. Proposed compromise: cap = greater of (i) 12 months' fees for the services under the Order Form, or (ii) $1,500,000 (flat floor for enterprise SLA). We will not accept a cap that is only a single-month fee multiple for an enterprise deployment.

2) Multiplier vs annualized: We prefer annualized (12 month) basis to avoid balloon exposure from usage spikes. If Acme insists on a multiplier, we will consider 3x annual fees but need finance sign-off.

3) Carveouts: standard carveouts for IP indemnity, breaches caused by Redwood's gross negligence or willful misconduct, and obligations to pay third-party claims should remain outside the cap. We propose limiting other carveouts (e.g., loss of business) to direct damages only.

4) Consequential damages: Redwood seeks a general waiver of consequential damages; however, we will allow narrowly tailored consequential carveouts for agreed categories with a monetary subcap (e.g., data restoration costs capped at $200k).

5) Transition/termination exposure: for early termination related to Redwood's breach, remedies should be limited to refund of prepaid fees and costs of reasonable wind-down, not punitive multiples.
