Thanks, Dylan & Nathan — putting some flesh on the bones here. Please see the attached redline-ready BAA (BAA-draft-1.2.pdf) and a lightweight DataFlowMatrix.xlsx that maps which attributes touch inference vs telemetry.

Key points from privacy side: 
- Subprocessors: our payable/subprocessor list is narrow; for inference we primarily use: inference-hosting (Redwood-managed GPU pool), observability (metrics & traces), and a small analytics pipeline (aggregated usage). We can commit to excluding certain analytics for this pilot via addendum clause: "No telemetry export of tokenized PHI to third-party analytics vendors."
- Residency gating: we can implement a route/policy that pins requests with a "pilot.health" prefix to US-only hosts. This is enforced at the routing layer and appears in runtime traces.
- KV/Prefix caching: we will disable prefix caching for any request labeled as PHI for the pilot; this avoids long-lived state outside the model KV.
- Evidence: attaching SOC 2 Type II start/end dates and our HSM/KMS attestation excerpt. (We'll provide the full artifacts under NDA or via a controlled folder link.)
