Quick internal thoughts:
- Credit: 8% is reasonable as a starting point but I’d push to tie the credit trigger to last-quarter spend bands. Example: 6% credit if last q < $120k, 8% if $120–250k, 10% above. That helps protect Redwood on high outliers while giving Photon upside.
- Price-hold: 6 months is fine. Recommend we add a mid-term review at month 4 to confirm usage trajectory.
- Burst pool: Offer the +50k tokens/day as a capped burst credit pool (we'd only bill for what’s used beyond the daily cap). Consider charging a blended surcharge (e.g., 0.75x of standard overage) and absorbing the rest into the credit.
- Commit structure: propose a modest uplift to the annual committed tokens if they sign 24 months (e.g., +15% commit) and make the first 3 months true-up free (no shortfall charges). That keeps headline AR up but is easier to sell to Procurement.
