Hi Avery — thanks for the clear outline. High level recommendations below, followed by a suggested 6‑step MAP (mutual action plan). I attached a short pilot SOW and our pricing grid to make the math transparent (attachment stubs below).

Quick recommendations
- If latency/cost is your primary constraint and you expect modest steady-state throughput: start Compact + feature bundle. This minimizes upfront commit and lets you enable extended KV caching as needed.
- If you expect unpredictable spikes or need predictable p50/p95 latency at scale: run the 3‑month Throughput pilot. We'll size a reserved pool and enable burst capacity. Pilot results are the cleanest data to choose a Dedicated vs. hosted commit.
- Pilot months can be applied toward a future annual commit if agreed up front in the SOW. We can credit pilot consumption up to the committed baseline (not to exceed the committed units).

Feature-bundle mechanics (short)
- Bundles (e.g., extended KV retention, quant exec profile) are add-on SKUs billed monthly. During a pilot -> commit conversion we can:
  * Roll the bundle pricing into the committed SKU with a one-time reconciliation credit for pilot overages, or
  * Keep bundles as separate line items but provide a migration credit (typically 50–100% of pilot-billed bundle fees rolled into the first quarter of commitment).

Step-up/stepdown and grace
- Upgrade (Compact -> Throughput): immediate technical upgrade; billing reconciled at month end with prorated charges for the days on the higher tier.
- Stepdown protection: if you downgrade within 90 days of upgrade we offer a one-time grace credit to smooth the first billing cycle (applies to annual commits only).

Suggested MAP (6 items)
1) Week 0: Sign pilot SOW (3 months) — attach pilot SLA and conversion clause.
2) Week 0–1: Technical onboarding + baseline perf tests (Redwood + MediSage run joint eval).
3) Month 1: Mid-pilot checkpoint — telemetry review and feature-tiling recommendations.
4) Month 2: Cost/quality readout + proposal for target commit band.
5) Month 3: Final conversion decision; sign annual commit if converting.
6) Post-conversion: migration runbook + 30‑day rollback window.
