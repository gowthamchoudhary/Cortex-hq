Thanks for the ping. I reviewed Lexa's redlines and have flagged the following issues: 

- MFN carveout: an unbounded or broad carveout is high risk. If we allow carveouts that aren't time-boxed or narrowly scoped to named partners/SKUs, it undermines our pricing governance. Recommend a) explicit list of partner SKUs, b) carveout sunsets after 12 months, and c) no retroactive price parity for past invoices.

- Benchmarking clause: acceptable only if: (i) metrics are objectively measurable and defined in the SOW (latency percentiles, sample workload), (ii) we have a remediation period of 30 days and one cure right, (iii) remedies are capped (e.g., service credit up to X% of monthly invoice) rather than automatic price reductions or MFN triggers.

- Acceptance criteria & audit: avoid open-ended audit rights. If they want performance sampling, limit to quarterly synthetic tests agreed in advance (shared test harness).

I can propose redline language for both items. If Deal Desk wants to accept with concessions (discount/cap), we should document a strict matrix in the SOW and have commercial sign-off from Sales + Finance.

Attaching short guidance doc with suggested language.
