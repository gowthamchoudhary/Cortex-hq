Neha — quick notes from pricing/legal:
- EU adder: 10% is our standard. For large, multi-year commitments we can convert that to a fixed EUR-per-1M-token discount instead of a percentage (example: EUR 550 per 1M tokens in lieu of 10% when committed spend > €3.5M/year). That helps customers hedge FX and simplifies local procurement.
- Discount sweeteners: we can move from 18% -> 20% for a 24-month firm commitment and a minimum monthly floor of $150k. Any further increases (25%+) require VP approval and must be tied to ARR or professional services add-ons.
- Model parity: all production-curated model variants are available in EU regions; for very large instance types we sometimes run on dedicated clusters with slightly different exact throughput. Add a note in the SOW about a 30-day capacity ramp and a performance validation run.
