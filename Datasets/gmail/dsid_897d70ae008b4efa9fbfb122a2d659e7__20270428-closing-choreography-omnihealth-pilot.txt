Olga / Daniel — quick note from legal on sequencing and common friction points.

On Wed, Apr 28, 2027 at 10:03 AM Olga Petrov <olga_petrov@redwood.com> wrote:
> Proposed sequencing (weeks, calendar):
> - Week 1–2: Legal review cycle 1 (MSA redlines) — typical turnaround 3–5 business days; we aim to review within 48 hours and return comments

Reality checklist (our side):
- Initial MSA review: we will aim for 48 hours, but complex commercial or indemnity edits can add one full review cycle (7–10 business days total).
- Privacy/DPA: if the DPA requests non-standard data residency or subprocessors, expect an extra legal/privacy review (additional 3–5 business days).
- Order form: pricing sign-off from finance is usually quick once template is complete — 1–3 business days.
- SOC 2: we can share the latest SOC 2 Type II report immediately; security questionnaire responses typically take 2–4 business days depending on scope.

Can we agree to the following constraints to keep the timeline tight:
1) OmniHealth provides procurement and legal point people and a single consolidated set of redlines (no parallel split reviews).
2) Any commercial deviations (term length, indemnity caps, escrow) are flagged at contract kickoff so we can triage.
3) Order form SKU/pricing finalized before legal final sign stage to avoid rework.

If we hold to those constraints, the end-to-end close for a standard pilot MSA/DPA/orderform can be ~3–5 calendar weeks. Otherwise plan for 6–8+ weeks.
