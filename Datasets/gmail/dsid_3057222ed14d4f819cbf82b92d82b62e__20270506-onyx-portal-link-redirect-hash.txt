Thanks for the heads-up, Camila. I dug into the provisioning logs and saw a URL-rewrite race on our edge cache for Onyx's tenant. The first invite was issued before the tenant alias propagation completed, which produced a hash computed against the old base URL. When the vendor clicked the link, the rewritten URL didn't match the signed hash -> token mismatch.

What I did:
- Invalidated the previous invite token for samir.patel@onyxmfg.com (server-side)
- Regenerated a new invite from the supplier portal and forced token-signing against the canonical tenant URL
- Cleared the short-lived cache entry for Onyx's alias routing (propagation should take effect across edges in ~2 min)

New invite (test link): https://supplier.redwood.ai/accept-invite?invite=eyJ0ZXN0LW5ldw.. (expires in 24h)
