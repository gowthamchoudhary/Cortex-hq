Initial notes from our side: we rolled a conductor config change (increasing the concurrent orchestration window) as part of the 00:30 release candidate to improve rollout throughput. Early signs are that the new concurrency setting combined with a burst of puppet-driven deployments caused a short resource pressure on the conductor, which triggered our internal circuit-breaker to prevent K/V cache divergence across nodes. That protection prevents split-brain but does block new handshakes until the cluster stabilizes.
