Sofia / team — quick technical summary and constraints: 

1) Reserved pools: We can provision regional reserved pools exactly as requested. For EU-WEST multi-AZ, we recommend spreading the 8 GPUs across 2 AZs (4/4) to reduce single-AZ impact.

2) Warm-standby & failover timings: Our warm-standby design keeps model parameters and KV caches resident; failover to warm standby is typically sub-90s for routing + cache rehydration for most models, assuming cross-region bandwidth is stable. 3 minutes (180s) warm-standby SLA is conservative and acceptable. Cold-start (spinning new long-tail instances) is variable — 8–12 minutes depending on image pull and model weight sizes; 12 minutes is achievable but should be scoped in the SLA with a caveat for unusually large models.

3) Cross-region latency: intra-region tail_latency (p95) target we can support is <60ms for EU regional traffic. Cross-region failover will add network RTT — ~80–150ms depending on APAC exit points. If Polylink needs sub-100ms global p95 for all users, we should recommend active-active with geo-routing and additional APAC capacity (higher spend).

4) Autoscale & burst: The +20% burst window is fine; we recommend a queue-based admission with graceful degradation when burst limits are hit. Overage billing can be set to a discounted per-token rate.

5) Compliance: APAC deployments require customer-managed KMS for certain APAC-regulated accounts; confirm with Polylink if they'll need that. No other regional blocks.

I'll sync with Jordan on pricing numbers — early estimate: the 15% uplift seems in range if we amortize warm-standby costs across the term.
