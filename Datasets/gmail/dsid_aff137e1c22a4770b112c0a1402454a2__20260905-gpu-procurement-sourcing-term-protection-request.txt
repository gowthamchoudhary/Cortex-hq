Elena — great to hear, thanks for the clear ask. I put together three pragmatic options and attached a short PDF with SKU-level examples (redwood-reserve-options-20260905.pdf). High level:

Option A (conservative): 12-month commit, fixed per-token price, small onboarding discount (5%). Minimal flexibility but simplest for forecasting.

Option B (balanced): 18-month commit, tiered discounts (8% base, step-up incentives tied to usage milestones), and a 6-month review window to adjust SKU mix. Add a capped GPU-index clause: if GPU market index moves >±15% vs. baseline, both parties enter a 30-day renegotiation window with a pre-agreed arbitration path.

Option C (flexible): 24-month commit with lower baseline discount (6%) + quarterly SKU rebalancing and a built-in cost-pass-through band (+/−20% before price adjustment). This reduces Redwood's exposure but gives Stellaris predictability within a band.

A few notes about approvals: discounts up to 10% are finance-approved in most cases; anything >10% needs VP Sales + Finance sign-off. Special indexation constructs require legal review and a short SOW addendum.
