Quick legal note on the invoice entity / tax ask:

1) We can allow "LumenHealth Global Services (US)" as the invoice recipient BUT we need a signed statement or a line in the Order Form that confirms the billing entity and the payment remittance entity to avoid double-liability exposure. Wording we typically use: "Invoices may list a Billing Entity and a Payment Entity; Redwood's acceptance of an invoice is conditioned on the Billing Entity representing that all taxes have been properly accounted for and the Payment Entity will make remittance in accordance with the payment instructions."

2) VAT / reverse charge — because Redwood is supplying hosted inference services, VAT treatment depends on the buyer's jurisdiction. If Lumen's tax team requires the invoice to show a specific entity to apply reverse charge, we need them to provide a completed VAT/residency certificate (or equivalent). Otherwise Redwood may need to collect VAT where applicable.

3) Regarding Net 45: if Finance (Ben) approves an upfront payment (25%) we can put Net 45 into the Order Form as an exception. Legal will add a clause that ties the term to receipt (e.g., "Net 45 where an initial payment of 25% of the first-year fees is received within 14 days of execution; otherwise Net 30 applies").
