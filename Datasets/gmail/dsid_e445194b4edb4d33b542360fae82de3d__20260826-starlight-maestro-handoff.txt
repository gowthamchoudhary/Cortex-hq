Jonas / Elena — quick tech note ahead of the kickoff so the team can prepare.

Top networking & install items (summary):
- Ports: outbound HTTPS (TCP 443) mandatory; control plane mTLS on TCP 8443 (mutual TLS certs required).
- IPs: we will consume a /28 for the private endpoint in your VPC; please reserve 10.240.16.0/28 (or an equivalent private /28 not in use).

- Certificates: provide a cert signing request or indicate if you9ll use an internal CA. We require a PEM bundle for mTLS.
