Team —

Summary (brief):
Root cause: A recently deployed runtime optimization (continuous-batching heuristic change, rollout id=rb-20261109) caused pathological batch formation for a subset of short-sequence checkout requests. The pathological batches elevated per-request queuing, which in turn increased tail latency and triggered transient OOMs on a narrow class of Dedicated nodes. Combined with a rate shift from a partner spike, the system hit degraded throughput and higher error rates.

Immediate mitigations performed (11:00–11:25 PST):
- Roll back rb-20261109 on the affected pool (Karthik) — removed heuristic; observed 60% reduction in 95th percentile within 6m.
- Scoped model variant canary to 3.1 for checkout route (Marissa) — eliminated the KV-cache mismatch and avoided OOMs.
- Emergency capacity burst to 4 extra Dedicated slots in us-east-1 (Ben) — provided headroom during warmup.
