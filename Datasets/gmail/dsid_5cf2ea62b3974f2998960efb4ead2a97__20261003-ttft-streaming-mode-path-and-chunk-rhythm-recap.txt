3) Chunk cadence policy (pilot): emit first partial token as soon as available (to satisfy TTFT), then stream subsequent chunks on whichever comes first: 250ms elapsed OR 64 tokens emitted. That balance gives snappy UX while keeping throughput-efficient batching.
4) Ops guardrails: initial capacity reservation for the pilot (reserved pool for BrightCart), 5% headroom, and health checks to trigger graceful fallback to a cached, shorter-response mode if concurrency spikes.
