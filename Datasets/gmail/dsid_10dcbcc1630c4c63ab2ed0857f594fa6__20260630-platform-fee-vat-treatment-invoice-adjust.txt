Hi Lars,

Thanks — appreciate the sample. Short answer: the tax treatment depends on whether Redwood is acting as a marketplace operator supplying a platform service to the seller (place of supply rules) or as an agent/fulfilment provider. For most of our marketplace customers we treat platform fees as a supply of services to the seller, and when the seller is established in the EU and the buyer is outside the EU the reverse-charge may apply for B2B sales — but we need concrete evidence of the seller's VAT registration and a breakdown by buyer jurisdiction before we can finalize retrospective treatment.

Alex / Grace / Marissa,

Summary of position after quick review:
- Redwood treats marketplace platform fees as a supply of services to the seller (not the buyer).
- For EU-established sellers providing B2B supplies, we generally invoice net of VAT and rely on reverse-charge if the buyer is VAT-registered and the place of supply is the buyer's country. However, when the buyer is non-EU, place of supply often falls outside EU and supplier should not charge EU VAT. Edge cases: cross-border B2C or where seller is unverified (no valid VAT ID) — then local VAT may apply.

Key risks for Juno:
- Seller VAT not validated: we cannot apply reverse-charge without evidence; tax authorities may assert VAT due.
- Aggregated invoices lacking per-transaction buyer country data: prevents correct place-of-supply determination.

Proposed action items (for Alex to offer to customer):
1) Request seller VAT IDs and attach our VAT evidence request form (see VAT_evidence_request.pdf).
2) Ask Juno for buyer-country breakdown for fee-generating transactions. Junomarket should be able to export this from their settlement report.
3) If provided, Redwood can issue an adjusted credit/debit note for platform fees where reverse-charge should have applied. We limit retrospective adjustments to a single prior month unless there's an SLA exception.
4) Collections note: Grace to confirm whether adjusted invoice triggers a new payment run or netting against future settlements.

Marissa — can you draft a short external-facing reply that includes the evidence request and a template CSV we can accept? Keep it tight — customer's accounting team will forward to sellers.
