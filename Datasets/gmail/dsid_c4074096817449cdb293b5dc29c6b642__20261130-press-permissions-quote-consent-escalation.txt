Neha — thanks for the ping and Samantha/Ben — thanks for the redlines. High-level legal position below and suggested language to start negotiating. I'm keeping this narrowly focused on publicity/reference/logo clauses as requested.

1) Publicity / Press Releases - We can accept an approval requirement with a 30-calendar-day review window for any press release that mentions the customer or co-marketed announcements. Proposed: "Neither party shall issue any press release or public announcement regarding this Agreement that uses the other party's name, logo, or trademarks without the prior written consent of the other party, not to be unreasonably withheld; consent shall not be withheld for routine announcements. Receiving party shall provide the other party with a draft of the press release at least thirty (30) days prior to publication."

2) Quotes and Testimonials - We propose: "Customer grants Redwood the right to use non-confidential customer quotes or testimonials provided in writing and approved in advance by Customer for use on Redwood's website, case studies, and marketing collateral. Approval shall not be unreasonably withheld and Customer may revoke consent on reasonable grounds with 30 days' notice. Quotes do not grant perpetual rights beyond the term of the Agreement unless expressly stated."

3) Logo Use and Branding - Limit to a single line-item placement on Redwood's customer logos page and in joint marketing materials only with prior approval. Proposed: "Customer grants Redwood a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to display Customer's name and logo on Redwood's website and marketing materials solely to identify Customer as a Redwood customer, subject to Customer's standard logo usage guidelines and prior written approval for any use beyond a single logo listing."

4) Reference Rights and Duration - Orbit's ask for no perpetual rights is reasonable. We recommend a default reference period of the term of the Agreement plus 12 months. Proposed: "Reference rights granted hereunder shall expire twelve (12) months after the termination or expiration of this Agreement unless otherwise agreed in writing."

5) Confidentiality carve-out for press - We will not require overly broad carve-outs. Proposed: "Notwithstanding the foregoing, Confidential Information shall not include information that is publicly known without breach of this Agreement or independently developed. Neither party may disclose Confidential Information in a press release absent written consent."

I'll integrate these clauses into a clean MSA redline and send back to Orbit. Two open items for your input Neha: (a) do we want to offer Orbit a brief exceptions process for enterprise case studies beyond the default 12-month window (i.e., via a separate written consent) and (b) clarify whether a joint announcement will be co-authored (we usually propose Redwood drafts and share).
