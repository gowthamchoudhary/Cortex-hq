Notes from the 16:45 bridge and immediate triage (short):
- Symptom window: 15:43–17:22 PST, peak at ~16:02.
- Root symptom: a sudden workload burst (approx 5x baseline QPS) combined with a KV-cache invalidation storm on the model host group; that produced re-warms and KV lookups which serialized on a hot lock in the runtime.
- Observable effects: token latencies spiking 5x, queue depth increase, and a growth in local GPU memory churn causing transient 5xx from OOM/retry guards.

Immediate mitigations applied (by 16:58):
1) Traffic split: shifted 35% of HarborWorks traffic to the lower-latency quantized variant (v2-qtz) to relieve hot-path pressure.
2) Autoscale tweak: reduced scale-up threshold to be more aggressive for this customer pool.
3) Cache tuning: increased KV cache retention and disabled one aggressive eviction rule.
