Elena — thanks for the clear ask and congrats on the pilot traction.

Summary: two pragmatic paths we typically use for customers in this situation. I attached a draft SOW for the expansion and two quote options below.

Option A — On-demand burst (fastest):
- Turn on additional nodes to support ~4x current traffic immediately.
- Pricing: list-rate add-on metered at $0.012 per inference-unit, billed monthly.
- No minimum commitment.
- Pros: Immediate capacity; no contract changes. Cons: higher unit cost and exposure to variable monthly spend.

Option B — Structured ramp + renewal alignment (recommended):
- Commit to an incremental monthly minimum for 3 quarters (scaled buckets: Q1 +50%, Q2 +100%, Q3 +100%).
- Pricing: blended discount tiers applied to incremental capacity (10% discount on Q1 incremental, 15% on Q2, 18% on Q3) with an annualization step to shift the renewal date to March (we convert the remaining committed spend into an annual commitment at the end of Q3).
- Pros: lower unit economics, predictable invoicing, ability to lock price for 90 days while procurement reviews.
- Cons: minimums through the committed ramp.

Numbers (illustrative) — baseline monthly token-equivalent = 10M inference-units: incremental monthly cost estimate for Q1 (50% increase):
- Q1 incremental (5M units) @ $0.0108 (10% discount) = $54,000
- Q2 incremental (10M units) @ $0.0102 (15% discount) = $102,000
- Q3 incremental (10M units) @ $0.00984 (18% discount) = $98,400
