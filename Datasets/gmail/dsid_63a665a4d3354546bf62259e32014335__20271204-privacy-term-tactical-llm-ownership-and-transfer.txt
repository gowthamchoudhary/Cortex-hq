I reviewed Novamed’s redline and Kimberly’s note. Proposed legal-friendly wording that keeps the ask tight:

--- proposed clause ---
Provider commitments: Provider will not use Customer Content (prompts, messages, files, or metadata) to train, fine-tune, or otherwise improve Provider models or model weights without the Customer’s explicit, documented opt-in for the specified environment or project. 

Operational evidence: Upon Customer request, Provider will deliver an evidence pack including: (a) a description of the technical configuration preventing Customer Content from entering model-training pipelines, (b) relevant audit logs for the most recent 90 days, and (c) a Security Team attestation of non-use for the requested period.
--- end ---

Notes: this keeps scope to the standard "no-use-unless-opted-in" commitment and ties the evidence deliverable to a 90-day log snapshot (we can change window if Novamed needs longer).
