Following up with the draft postmortem and the immediate corrective actions we completed overnight. Attached is a short postmortem (redwood_postmortem_2026-02-17.pdf) that includes a timeline, root-cause analysis, impact assessment, and the remediation plan. Executive summary below: 

• Incident window: 2026-02-17 08:44–09:04 PST.
• Impact: elevated 5xx responses for affected webhook paths; retry storms caused delayed deliveries for ~1.2% of requests to Acme (no PII observed in the affected telemetry).
• Root cause: an autoscaling policy change combined with a routing table mismatch in the ingestion tier led to transient token validation failures for a small subset of routed requests.
• Mitigation: reverted the autoscaling policy at 08:58 PST; applied a hotfix to routing rules and increased circuit-breaker thresholds to prevent retry amplification.
• Evidence available: load balancer logs (hashed request ids), KV cache traces, audit trail of the deployment, and API gateway logs. Links are in the Drive folder.

- 30d: harden autoscaling policy templates and add canary evaluation for routing changes.
