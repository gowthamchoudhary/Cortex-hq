Hi Aisha — thanks for outlining the request. Below is what Redwood can commit to for the pilot and the evidence we will provide to procurement. This is written for operational/legal review and we can convert to contractual language in the addendum.

Commitments (pilot scope):
- Rerank-only: Inputs and model candidates submitted to Redwood for reranking will not be used to train, fine-tune, or improve any Redwood models (no adaptive training). That includes: exclusion from our model-training datasets, no contribution to embeddings corpora for training, and no parameter updates resulting from pilot data.
- Ephemeral caching: Any prefix/KV cache used to speed rerank inference will be transient and resident only on the serving nodes for a maximum of 72 hours. Caches are not backed up to durable storage; snapshots are disabled for nodes participating in the pilot unless explicitly requested.
- Telemetry/metrics: Aggregate telemetry (e.g., latency, error rates) may be retained for product monitoring but request and candidate payloads will be redacted at the point of collection and will not be stored in telemetry datasets used for model training.
- Subprocessor trace: We will provide a CSV inventory (attached) listing subprocessors, the services they provide (e.g., metrics, logging, monitoring), and country locations. For the pilot, we will gate any changes to that list and notify Ragnar 30 days in advance.

Evidence artifacts we will share by Thu (7/23):
- Draft DPA addendum with explicit rerank-only clause (attached: dpa_rerank_addendum_draft_v1.pdf).
- Subprocessor inventory CSV with hosting/region columns (attached: subprocessor_inventory_20260721.csv).
- Ops diagram showing KV/cache retention and backup configuration (attached: kv_cache_retention_diagram.png).
- Short ops note describing retention policy and a pointer to our internal runbook for ephemeral caches.

Suggested clause (for legal):
"Redwood shall not use Customer Data submitted for the limited purpose of reranking as training data for model development, machine learning, or any model improvement process. Temporary in-memory caches used to serve reranking requests will be ephemeral, limited to a maximum retention period of seventy-two (72) hours, and will not be included in any backup or dataset used for model training or evaluation."

If Ragnar needs a stronger contractual box (e.g., a warranty + audit right), we can discuss adding an auditor clause or an onshore-residency requirement for the pilot.
