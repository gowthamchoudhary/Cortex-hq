Quick findings from infra side (initial):
- Peering metrics show a latency jump and intermittent packet loss between our us-west peering fabric and CloudBelt ASN around 13:46 PDT. See exported counters (peering_metrics.csv).
- DNS logs show increased NXDOMAIN/timeout responses for the resolver chain used by Acorn's flow starting 13:50 — correlates with trace hops.
- Firewall/proxy service on our edge nodes is returning a higher fraction of 502s; early look at firewall snippet attached (firewall_logs_snippet.txt).

Hypothesis: transient congestion at the CloudBelt peering edge causing packet drops -> DNS retries -> application proxies timing out and surfacing 502/504 to clients.

Immediate mitigations in progress:
1) We started a canary reroute for Acorn to alternate peering path (low-risk traffic only) — roll started at 15:01 PDT.
2) Increased proxy thread pool and temporarily extended HTTP timeouts for impacted routes.
3) Reached out to CloudBelt NOC to investigate peering queueing and BGP flap indicators.

Next steps we will complete in the next 60 minutes: full traceroute sweep from our edge nodes, cross-validate packet loss with CloudBelt, and hold rollback if reroute increases error rate.
