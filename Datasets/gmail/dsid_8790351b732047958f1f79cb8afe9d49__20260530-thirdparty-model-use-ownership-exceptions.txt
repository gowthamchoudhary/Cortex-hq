Hi Nina — thanks for the clear list. I'm looping in Ben (sales lead on the account) and Marissa (legal ops / contract counsel) so we can give proposed language and explain operational constraints.

High level response:
- Output ownership: Redwood's standard approach is customers retain rights to customer-provided content and to outputs derived solely from customer prompts. Where outputs incorporate Redwood proprietary model content or third-party licensed model outputs, we typically grant the customer a broad, sublicensable right to use output for their business but cannot assign our or third-party IP. There is a middle path: explicit "customer output ownership" for user-generated outputs, with carveouts for underlying model weights/derivative model IP.
- Training/feedback: We can offer an opt-out for using customer data for model training. By default, anonymized telemetry helps product quality; for enterprise customers we support an explicit opt-out and separate feedback license limited to improvement of Redwood services (non-attributable and aggregated).
- Third-party model use: We can restrict routing for a customer's tenancy to Redwood-verified model catalog and disable any experimental partner routing. We can also add a clause preventing routing to models that introduce additional third-party license obligations without prior consent.

Hi Nina / Elena / team — attaching a short draft with three options for each area so your counsel can pick the right risk posture. Summaries below:

1) Output ownership (three options)
- Option A (Customer Ownership): "Customer shall own all right, title, and interest in and to Output generated solely from Customer Input." Carveout: "This does not include Redwood's underlying model weights, training data, or third-party models; Redwood grants Customer a perpetual, sublicensable license to use Outputs."
- Option B (License-Forward): Customer has full usage rights (perpetual, sublicensable) but assignment of Redwood/third-party IP is excluded.
- Option C (Narrow Right): Customer has use rights but Redwood retains ownership; good for high-risk regulated data where supplier must control model artifacts.

2) Training / Feedback (two options)
- Default: Redwood may use anonymized, aggregated telemetry to improve services, with standard privacy controls.
- Enterprise Opt-out: "Customer Data and Outputs will not be used to train or improve Redwood models absent Customer's explicit written consent." If Customer elects opt-out, Redwood will offer a commercial offset (increased per-token rates or a dedicated processing fee).

3) Third-party / partner model routing
- Add clause: "Redwood will not route Customer Requests to third-party models that impose third-party licensing obligations on Customer or place outputs under additional restrictions, without Customer's prior written consent." Also add a process for requesting temporary partner routing (change control + notification).
