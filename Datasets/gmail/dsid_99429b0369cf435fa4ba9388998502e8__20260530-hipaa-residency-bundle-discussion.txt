Appreciate the checklist. Quick answers up front, then I attached a one-page mock pricing summary. High-level:

- Audit logs: priced two ways — pay-as-you-go at $0.10/GB/month (30-day retention) or a bundled audit package (flat $3,000/month for up to 50GB daily ingest with 12mo retention).

Discounting: we can provide a term discount — 12mo commit: 15% off hosted list for the add-ons; 24mo commit: 20% off. For Dedicated/Private contracts there are separate reserved-capacity credits.
