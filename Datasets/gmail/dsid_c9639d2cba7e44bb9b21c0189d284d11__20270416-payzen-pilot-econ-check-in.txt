Summary of what we heard (pain & goals)
- Pain: production costs ballooning on current LLM experiments (esp. chat/assistant flows) when sessions hit 1k+ tokens.
- Goal: test a 6-week pilot to validate 1) user latency under 250ms p50, 2) achievable per-conversation cost <= $0.02, and 3) no regression in top-3 user intents.

Token economics & suggested levers (high level)
- Baseline: your dev estimate was ~1.8M tokens/day for the 50k MAU scenario; raw hosted cost (no batching/cache/quant) ~ $5.6K/day.
- Recommended: 3 core levers to hit target unit cost:
  1) Batching: batch window 20–40ms on low-latency routes to increase throughput — estimated 22–30% token cost reduction for generation-heavy flows.
  2) Prefix & KV cache: cache common prompt prefixes and KV cache across sessions for repetitive system messages — adds another ~18% savings for conversational flows with repeated context.
  3) Quantization & model variant policy: run an 8-bit quantized variant for background/low-importance rerank/embeddings and keep FP16 for high-sensitivity generation; overall model mix can shave ~15% of cost while holding quality.

Pilot proposal (what we’ll run)
- Duration: 6 weeks (1 week sandbox, 4 weeks pilot, 1 week wrap and handoff).
- Scope: 50k DAU simulation (staggered), 2 main routes (chat-assist + billing Q&A), measure latency p50/p95, token/unit economics, and quality on 200 eval prompts.
- Success criteria: p50 < 250ms, cost <= $0.02/conversation, quality regressions <= 5% on our eval.

- Pilot: committed capacity sizing + burst ($12K–$18K/mo depending on model mix). Exact numbers in payzen_pilot_estimate_v1.xlsx.
