Elliot / Logan — thanks for the details and the DPA redline. See proposed legal language below and a note on operational feasibility. Happy to hop on a short call to finalize.

Key proposed clauses (draft text excerpts):

1) Definition — Customer Content:
"Customer Content" means all data, prompts, files, attachments, prompts + responses, and other material provided by or generated on behalf of Customer via the Services. Excludes aggregated/anonymized telemetry not attributable to a specific customer.

2) No-Training Commitment:
"Provider will not use Customer Content to train, fine-tune, or otherwise adapt any machine learning model in a manner that could reproduce or reveal Customer Content to any third party. Provider may use aggregated, de-identified telemetry and system performance metrics for product improvement, provided such data cannot be reconstructed to re-identify Customer Content."

3) Subprocessor Change Notification & Objection:
"Provider shall provide Customer with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any engagement of a new Subprocessor or material change in processing by an existing Subprocessor. Customer may object to the engagement of a Subprocessor within thirty (30) days following receipt of notice on reasonable security or privacy grounds; upon objection, Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to address Customer's concerns, including offering alternative subprocessors or contractual mitigations. If Provider cannot reasonably address the concerns, Customer may (i) suspend use of the impacted service or (ii) terminate the affected Services for convenience as to that Subprocessor with pro rata refund for unused prepaid fees. For Subprocessors designated by Customer as \"high-risk\", Provider shall provide an expedited fourteen (14) day review process."

4) Escrow & Preservation of Logs:
"Provider will preserve request/response logs and associated metadata in encrypted escrow for a period of twelve (12) months by default. Access to escrowed data will be granted to Customer only upon a validated dispute showing and subject to joint audit and access controls that protect other customers' data. Provider will offer a contractual option to shorten or lengthen the preservation period upon mutual agreement."
