Thanks for the clarifications — super helpful. I updated the attached model (novahealth_hitrate_model_v1.xlsx) and included a concise pricing anchor (redwood_pricing_anchor_v2.pdf). Key assumptions I used so we have a shared baseline:

Assumptions (baseline):
- Median monthly tokens (from your numbers): 140,400,000 tokens/month
- Base list price (uncached) we’re using for this example: $1,200 per 1M billed tokens
- Prefix cache operational overhead: we apply a small storage/serve uplift ~5% on top of billed compute to cover cache management and ephemeral storage (this shows as a small uplift vs pure linear math)

How we compute effective $/1M original tokens:
- Billed tokens = original_tokens * (1 - hit_rate)
- Total cost = (billed_tokens / 1,000,000) * base_price + cache overhead (modeled as 5% uplift on cost)
- Effective $/1M original tokens = Total cost / (original_tokens/1,000,000)

Scenario outputs (rounded):
- Hit rate = 0% -> Effective ~$1,260 per 1M original tokens (1200 * 1.05)
- Hit rate = 25% -> Effective ~$945 per 1M original tokens (1200 * 0.75 * 1.05)
- Hit rate = 50% -> Effective ~$630 per 1M original tokens (1200 * 0.5 * 1.05)
- Hit rate = 70% -> Effective ~$378 per 1M original tokens
- Hit rate = 90% -> Effective ~$126 per 1M original tokens

Interpretation / recommendation:
- Your target band ($500–$900 /1M original tokens) maps to a required hit rate roughly between 40%–60% (given our 5% cache overhead and the $1,200 baseline).
- Given your stated ~60% prefix reuse, a realistic pilot hit rate (prefix + session repeat) of 50% is achievable with:
  - Aggressive short TTLs on the prefix cache for intake templates
  - A warm-start strategy where we pre-fill hot prefixes for the pilot cohort

Pilot proposal (next steps):
1) 30-day pilot with a capped spend of $40k/month (you mentioned the cap) — we’ll scope traffic to validate hit rate.
2) Redwood will run a one-week warm-fill: pre-populate prefix cache for top 200 intake flows and monitor hit coverage.
3) Weekly check-ins to update the model and adjust TTLs.
