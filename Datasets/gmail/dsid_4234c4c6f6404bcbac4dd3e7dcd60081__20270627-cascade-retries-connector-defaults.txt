Sam / Tara — after initial triage on our side I want to call out two observable conditions that look like a classic client-driven retry cascade: 

- High request fan-in from BrightGrid’s primary ingestion host between 08:38–08:52 (concurrency ~60).
- Client-side retries showing deterministic, fixed backoff of ~200ms and max_retries=10 (no jitter). These retries line up exactly with repeated 429/5xx responses from our edge.

Preliminary root hypothesis: the BrightGrid connector is configured (or using SDK defaults) that aggressively retries without exponential backoff or jitter; that turns transient 429s into sustained load spikes that push our pod pools into higher queueing and 5xx tails. In short: retry logic + high concurrency => cascading failures.

Immediate mitigations we recommend while we stabilize:
- Disable automatic retries in the connector (set retries=0) or reduce to max_retries=2.
- If disabling isn't possible, switch to exponential backoff with jitter (base 200ms, factor 2, jitter 0.5) and cap concurrency to 10–15.
- Respect Retry-After headers from Redwood responses.
- As a short-term safeguard we can apply a per-account rate limit and route BrightGrid to a higher-capacity pool (we can do this now if you authorize).

Attached is a screenshot of the 429 spike and a snippet of the request trace. We'll continue watching and can roll a temporary rate-limiter if you like.
