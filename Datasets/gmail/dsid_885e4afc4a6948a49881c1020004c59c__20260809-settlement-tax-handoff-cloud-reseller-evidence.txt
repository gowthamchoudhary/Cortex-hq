- For US tax withholding cases: W-9/W-8BEN-E as applicable (we'll ask only if the payout is routed through a US tax entity).

Laura / Julie — quick ops notes from Marketplace side: we've seen two common patterns across AWS/Azure/GCP co-sell flows:

1) Marketplace invoices the end-customer and pays the reseller (CloudReseller) net of marketplace fees. The reseller must provide VAT evidence for the receiving entity. Marketplace then applies reverse-charge if end-customer is EU VAT-registered and the reseller's invoice flags reverse-charge.

2) Marketplace lists Redwood as the Seller-of-Record for listing metadata but routes billing through the reseller. In this model, marketplaces sometimes require the vendor (Redwood) to accept a pass-through invoice template that references the reseller as the payer — which triggers additional GL mapping.
