Hi Samantha — thanks for the clear ask and the heads up. We can support a provisional SLA posture for the alpha ramp, but we want to lock in operational mitigations so the customer experience and safety nets are explicit.

Proposed approach (initial suggestion):
- Temporary uptime floor: 95% for a 14-calendar-day alpha stabilization window.
- Latency guardrail: exempt P99 up to 1.2s during the window; require P50 <= 250ms where possible.
- Automatic fallback: traffic routed to a stable fallback model variant (no experimental quantization) if P99 exceeds 1.2s for 6 consecutive minutes.
- Daily status: 15-minute sync M-F; on-call paging if >3% traffic error rate in any hour.
- Telemetry: full request/response logs retained for 90 days; redacted KV traces shared with DeltaHealth for audit.

I'm attaching a short proposal that lays the timeline and rollback criteria out for legal review. If the proposed floors are too permissive we can tighten them — looking for internal input below.
