Hi Vivek — answers below inline: 

> 1) Is any raw patient data exported to third-party analytics vendors outside the US?

We do send de-identified telemetry to two analytics partners (BrightMetrics, based in Ireland; Medlytics, based in Canada). The exports are limited to event-level telemetry and pseudonymized IDs, not raw PHI.

> 2) For Dedicated deployments: will Optima host any logs or KV cache snapshots that we might retain for debugging?

No — for Dedicated we expect logs to remain in the customer's VPC. For the hosted option only, we may retain short-lived KV cache fragments for performance; those are stored in our US region by default.

> 3) Do you require SCCs for processor-to-processor relationships or only for transfers originating from Optima to non-EEA hosts?

We'd like SCCs documented for any transfer to EEA or to processors outside the US where the data could be re-identified. We will mark specific vendor names during procurement.

Note: I've uploaded Optima's initial DPA redline to the contracts inbox (filename: optima_initial_dpa_comments.pdf).
