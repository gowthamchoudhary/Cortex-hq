Thanks for the call today — quick technical notes and follow-ups from my side.

Summary of what I heard:
- Models: 7B (primary) + 13B (occasional) — quantization planned; ~8k sequence average, 4k tokens prompt/response on bursts
- Traffic pattern: baseline steady 40k tpm, 4–5 short daily spikes to ~250k tpm for ~10–20 minutes, occasional longer weekend batch runs
- Latency target: p50 <= 120ms for single-turn generation on 7B quantized, p95 <= 400ms acceptable for larger 13B calls

Initial pool proposal (draft):
- Primary pool (us-west): 6x A100 equivalent reserved; autoscale threshold policy: scale up when 95th percentile GPU utilization > 70% for 3 consecutive minutes; scale step +2 GPUs; max autoscale to 14 reserved GPUs.
- Burst layer: pre-warmed shared burst capacity that can be assigned at peak up to +8 GPUs for short windows (burst pools have ~5–10 minute warm-up if not pre-warmed). We can reserve pre-warm slots for recurring spike windows (savings vs immediate burst).
- EU fallback: lightweight pool 2x A100 equivalent, cold standby with KV cache replication disabled initially to avoid cross-region data movement.

Questions for Ops/Procurement:
- Do you have hard constraints on burst locations (must stay in us-west) or can bursts come from cross-region shared capacity?
- What is your target SLA/uptime and acceptable RTO for region failover?
- Any compliance checklist items beyond SOC2 (e.g., data residency, egress controls)?
