Proposed language below (keeps marketing flexibility but limits legal exposure). Suggest we include this as an Appendix to the Order Form rather than the MSA core to keep the MSA minimal.

--- begin proposed clause ---
Logo and Publicity. Customer may use Redwood Inferences trademarks and logos solely to identify Customers use of the Services for marketing and promotional purposes, subject to Redwood Inferences trademark usage guidelines and prior written approval of the final press release or marketing collateral. Approval will not be unreasonably withheld; Redwood Inference will provide any requested approval or comments within two (2) business days of receipt of the final text and assets. Customer agrees to cease use of Redwood Inference trademarks upon termination of the Order Form or upon written notice if used in a manner inconsistent with Redwood Inferences guidelines.

Quotes. Any quote attributed to a Redwood employee or officer must be pre-approved in writing by Redwood Inference. Redwood will use commercially reasonable efforts to provide approval within two (2) business days.

No endorsement. No use of Redwood Inferences logo or quotes shall imply endorsement, sponsorship, or joint product offering without a separate written agreement.
--- end proposed clause ---
