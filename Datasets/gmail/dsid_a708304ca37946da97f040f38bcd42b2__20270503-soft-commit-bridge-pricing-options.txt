Sofia — thanks for the clear asks. Short answer: yes. I sketched three options below that fit a conservative ramp with controlled burst economics. Attaching the one-pager and a draft term sheet.

Option A — Soft Commit (recommended)
- Minimum: 30M tokens/month for a 6-month bridge (creditable toward commitment)
- Effective per-token discount vs on-demand: ~18% at minimum
- Burst / overage: on-demand rate + 20% premium (capped monthly at 3x baseline for 90 days)
- Monthly true-up: any shortfall credited against final invoice for the bridge period

Option B — Pay-as-you-go with Volume Tiers
- No minimum; tiered per-token discounts at 50M, 100M thresholds
- Bursts billed at on-demand; no premium but surge throttling rules apply after sustained 2x baseline

Option C — Anchored Commit with Soft Exit
- 100M token commit w/ early-exit credit schedule
- Lower unit price, but higher penalty if canceled before 3 months

Notes: the soft-commit gives you predictable base capacity while keeping Q4 campaign bursts affordable. Burst premium in A is designed to prioritize committed traffic and give you predictable marginal cost during spikes.
