Hi Olivia — great to meet you and thanks for the clear breakdown. Short answers up top, details below:

1) Yes — we can do a single committed pool and bill a blended per-token rate.
2) Blended calculation options (we recommend #2 or #3 depending on volatility):
   • Option A — Regional commits (separate buckets): commit is allocated per-region; unused credits do not automatically transfer. Best for customers that want strict regional spend boundaries.
   • Option B — Global commit, blended billing at invoice time: we compute a weighted average of list regional rates based on actual usage each invoice period, then apply a single committed discount to that blended list price. This is simplest operationally and is our standard for multi-region customers.
   • Option C — Global commit + surcharge cap: same as B but with an explicit APAC/EEA surcharge cap (we absorb excess to avoid unpredictable month spikes). Good when risk tolerance is low.

3) Region surcharges / taxes: list per-token list prices are denominated in USD but we do VAT/GST passthrough for EU customers — invoiced VAT is shown separately and paid by you to local tax authorities. APAC may include a small regional surcharge (typical 2–6%) to reflect local pricing and egress. We will include any VAT line items on the invoice — this does not change the blended discount calculation (which is applied pre-tax).

4) Overages and burst handling: for global commit (Option B), overages are billed at the overage per-token rate defined in the SOW. We can add cross-region smoothing: if one region goes over and another is under, the pool is reconciled at invoice time (so long as you select the Global commit flow). If you need hard regional caps, choose Option A.

5) Sample tiers & commercials (high-level):
   • List per-region sample (list prices): US $0.00060 / token, EU $0.00070 / token, APAC $0.00085 / token.
   • 100M tokens/month, 12-month commit: Standard committed discount = 10% off blended list; we can offer a stepped discount up to 17% for multiyear or higher commit levels.
   • Example blended: using your distribution (60/30/10) -> blended list = $0.000645/token; with 10% commit discount -> $0.0005805/token.

I've attached a short pricing workbook that shows alternative scenarios and a recommended clause for cross-region smoothing. Key callouts in the workbook: surcharge caps, VAT handling, and an overage ladder (0–10% overage = list+10%, >10% = list+25%).
