Hi all,

Initial findings from a quick pass on INF-9931: the v2.1 build includes a quantization kernel change and a new attentional compilation that reduces average token latency on typical sequences, but introduces higher variance on infrequently-seen prefix shapes. We're seeing increased cold-KV miss rates for certain multi-turn prefixes (matches the Stride traces).

Short-term options (pros/cons):
- Full rollback to psi-9 v1.9: fastest path to restore baseline p95; requires a coordinated revert and cache warm-up (expected full stabilization ~20–30 minutes after traffic pin).
- Traffic pin to v1.9 for 80% of StridePay traffic + 20% to v2.1: preserves telemetry on v2.1 while limiting customer impact. Requires routing rule change and will keep a small sample for post-fix validation.
- Apply a configuration change to serving runtime to prefer older kernel path for the specific prefix patterns: higher engineering risk, longer time to validate.

I've attached a short perf diff (perf-diff-psi9-v2.1-v1.9.csv) and a sampling of traces showing KV-cache misses. For urgency, rollback is lowest operational risk. If Stride requests a carve-in, we can authorize a 48-hour grace window while we push a targeted fix to the compilation path.
