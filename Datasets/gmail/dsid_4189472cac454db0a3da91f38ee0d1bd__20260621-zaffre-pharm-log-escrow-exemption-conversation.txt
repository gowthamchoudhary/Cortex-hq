Devon — hi, I'm Marissa from Redwood Security & Compliance. Short answer: yes, we can support a scoped, time-boxed retention exception for a customer tenant, but it requires an operating pattern and contract addendum. Below is what we typically do and the deliverables you'll receive.

What Redwood provides for a scoped retention exception (typical engagement):
- Scoped retention: tenant-scoped retention policy applied at ingestion (not org-wide). We can retain the indicated log types for an agreed window (30–180 days) depending on retention medium.
- Evidence pack: time-stamped manifests, SHA256 digests for exported artifacts, chain-of-custody notes, and a signed attestation of deletion once the hold expires. We produce a CAIQ/SIG crosswalk showing which evidentiary artifacts map to questionnaire items.
- Forensic escrow: optional immutable archive (encrypted, customer-keyed) for sensitive debug payloads. Access is limited and we provide an audit trail of retrieval.
- Controls/constraints: retention exceptions increase storage and egress; debug payloads that include PII must be scoped and redacted/pseudonymized prior to archival unless your contract permits otherwise.

What we need from Zaffre to complete a feasibility and quote:
1) Exact log surface: a short list (CSV) of event types and fields (e.g., request_id, kv_cache_snapshots, full_request_payload).
2) Expected cardinality and retention window (example: 1.2M events/month for 90 days).
3) Legal contact for contract addendum and whether you'll accept a Redwood attestation vs third-party escrow provider.
4) Any compliance constraints (e.g., EU data residency, patient data flags).

Operational notes and costs:
- For short-term holds (<= 90 days) we often use our escrows + attestation workflow; for longer windows or very high cardinality we recommend Dedicated pools or a Private deployment with a configured retention policy. Pricing is usage + fixed onboarding/attestation fee; I'll include ranges on the follow-up SOW.
