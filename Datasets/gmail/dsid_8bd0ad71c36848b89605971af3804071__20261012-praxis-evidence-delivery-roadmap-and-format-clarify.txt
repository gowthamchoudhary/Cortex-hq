Immediate answers to your bullets: 
- Event types captured: auth_success, auth_failure, token_refresh, inference_request.start, inference_request.complete, inference_request.error, model.deploy, model.rollback, config.change, routing.fallback, billing.usage_summary (we will provide full event dictionary in the zip).
- Retention tiers:
  * Raw trace payloads (full request/response metadata) — default hosted 90 days, configurable to 1/3/5 years on Dedicated/Private with contract addendum.
  * Derived telemetry (token counts, latency histograms, aggregate error rates) — retained 24 months.
  * Immutable evidence exports (archival handoffs) — retained per customer escrow policy; Redwood can store up to 7 years on request.
- Export formats & examples: NDJSON (recommended for line-based event processing), Parquet (columnar for analytics), gzipped CSV for gated legacy tooling. The NDJSON sample includes a canonical field set: event_id, ts, customer_id_hash, event_type, model, request_id, user_ip_masked, payload_meta (length-limited), checksum.

Caveats & controls:
- PII handling: IPs and user identifiers are either pseudonymized (sha256 salt per-customer) or redacted on export depending on your legal needs. We document the redaction settings in the zip.
- Schema drift: model-specific fields may vary; we include a versioned schema and recommend pulling the schema manifest with each export (manifest included in our signed archive).
- Tamper evidence: each archive contains a signed manifest (PKCS7) and block-level SHA256 checksums. We also provide an optional notarized timestamping step via a third-party timestamping authority for high-assurance use cases.

Delivery timelines (examples):
- T+1 (expedited) bulk NDJSON export — 1 business day from signed request (available for accounts with pre-authorized support plan; expedited fee applies).
- Standard bulk harvest — 7–10 business days including verification and manifest signing.
- Scheduled periodic exports (monthly/quarterly) — configurable via contract; lead time 2 weeks to enable automation.
