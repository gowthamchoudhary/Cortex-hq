Hi all — I reviewed Everwell's MSA redlines. High-level: most edits are reasonable operational clarifications, but there are two legal points we should escalate before the call:

1) Indemnity scope: Everwell's proposed mutual indemnity limited to data handling is acceptable, but their draft narrows Redwood's ability to seek indemnity for infringements tied to customer content. We should push to preserve Redwood's right to indemnity for IP claims originating from vendor-provided components.

2) Limitation of liability / consequential damages carve-out: they've expanded the carve-out to exclude punitive/consequential damages entirely. Suggest we propose a capped consequential damages clause tied to order form value for the pilot.
