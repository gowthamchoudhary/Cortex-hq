Hi Maya — thanks for the forecast and the context. High-level thoughts below; I'll follow up with a short proposal PDF by Oct 15 and we can adjust based on procurement timing.

1) Phased commit patterns (examples we see work well):
  - Fast-onboard (3-step): 40% commit at T0, 75% at month 6, 100% at month 12. Good when you want rapid performance gains but keep flexibility.
  - Conservative ramp (4-step): 25% / 45% / 70% / 100% every 3 months — lowers initial cash requirement.
  - Windowed prepay (annual): Year 1 upfront 60% commitment, Year 2 & 3 settle with quarterly true-ups (best for blended discounts).

2) Discounts (ballpark until we size):
  - 1-year prepay: standard term discount 8–12% depending on capacity size.
  - 2-year prepay: 12–18% (higher if you commit corridor in Y1).
  - 3-year: 18–25% with front-loaded prepay + predictable ramp milestones.

3) Overage and SLA handling:
  - Overage: on-demand rate applies by token beyond committed capacity; we can include a soft cap alerting and auto-burst with spend guardrails.
  - SLA: uptime SLA and latency SLOs are included for Dedicated; credits are pro-rated against monthly charges — can provide exact language in SOW.

Next steps: I'll prepare a staged onboarding proposal (three schedules, blended pricing, and a sample milestone invoice schedule) and attach it to this thread. If Oct 20 is a hard deadline for procurement, we should schedule a 30-min call this week to confirm assumptions.
