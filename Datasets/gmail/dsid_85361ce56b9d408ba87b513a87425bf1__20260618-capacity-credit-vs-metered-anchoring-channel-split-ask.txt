Claire — great questions. Short version up top: we can anchor metered overages to an agreed list-price equivalent of the committed bucket and apply distributor uplift on the invoiced amount (post-discount) as a separate pass-through line — but there are two practical ways to present this to procurement depending on how you want invoice clarity vs. rebate mechanics.

Option A — Invoice clarity (recommend for procurement):
- Commit: prepaid node-hour bucket at a negotiated committed discount off list.
- Overages: charged at a metered rate equal to the list-price per token/hour, but the contract defines a capped anchored overage at X% above the committed list equivalent for first 30 days, then standard metered thereafter.
- Distributor uplift (12%): appears as a separate line on invoice, calculated on the net charged usage (post any committed discount), so finance sees both net Redwood charge and distributor pass-through.

Option B — Simpler waterfall (recommend for partner ops):
- Commit: same as above.
- Overages: charged at a blended metered rate (committed-equivalent blended into overage for the first bucket) to avoid surprise spikes.
- Distributor uplift: applied to the gross list-price before discounts (this preserves partner margin but requires reconciliation).

Soojin — good summary. Operational notes from billing/partner side:

- We implemented Option A for a few recent customers (healthcare & fintech) and it reduced invoice disputes. Key point: distributor uplift as a separate pass-through line requires us to collect partner tax/VAT info up front so their invoicing matches.

- If DataSilo wants distributor uplift applied to net post-discount amounts, our billing engine will show: 
  1) Gross usage at list rates
  2) Committed discount line (negative)
  3) Net payable to Redwood
  4) Distributor uplift (positive) calculated on line 3
  5) Total due

- Example (1M node-hour commit, 20% overage, 12% distributor):
  - List usage: $1,000,000
  - Committed discount (25%): -$250,000
  - Net Redwood charge: $750,000
  - Distributor uplift (12% of $750k): $90,000
  - Total due: $840,000

- For auditability we also include a reconciliation CSV with per-invoice usage rows that partners can ingest. That avoids manual waterfall disputes.
