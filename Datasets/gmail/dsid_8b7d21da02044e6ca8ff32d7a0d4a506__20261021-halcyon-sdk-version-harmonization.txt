Hi Alex — thanks for the early heads up and the matrix. Helpful.

Short answers up front:
- Hosted API: we can support a 90-day dual-accept window, but it will require a short rollout playbook per model/route (we'll need to gate traffic and monitor token-level regressions).
- Dedicated: supported in principle, but depends on customer pinning and autoscale config; we'll need the Dedicated accounts to opt into the dual-accept policy.
- Streaming signature: our runtime can accept the wire changes, but there are some kernel-selection implications for long-lived streams. Marcus, can you estimate if the new signature will add any median latency for 1–2k token streams?
