Looping in some performance takeaways and a recommended fleet split for Acme (draft):
- Observations from quick-run: H100 consistently reduces TTFT on long-context workloads (larger SRAM and HBM access patterns), while L40S offers better cost/token for high-throughput embedding batches but with increased latency tail for chat-style workloads.
- KV cache: set KV prefetch threshold for long-context to avoid stalls on A100; H100 execution path benefits from tile-level streaming which lowered TTFT in our tests.

Draft recommended fleet mix (for an initial committed capacity purchase):
- 40% H100 nodes (low-latency routes / long-context production)
- 35% L40S nodes (batch embeddings & background throughput-heavy jobs)
- 25% A100 nodes used with 1/2 MIG slices for dev/test and low-cost warm pools

Rationale: H100 buys low-latency headroom and better long-context TTFT; L40S lowers unit cost for throughput; A100 MIG provides flexible density for staging and low-priority workloads.
