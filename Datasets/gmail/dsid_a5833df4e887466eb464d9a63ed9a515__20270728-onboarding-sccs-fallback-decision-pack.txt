Emma / team — quick legal read and proposed DPA language. Forwarding highlights from MedSync's redline for context.

1) Trigger scope: avoid open-ended "law or policy change" triggers that require immediate repatriation without runway. Proposed text: "If applicable compliance obligations require a change in hosting region or transfer mechanism, Redwood will implement the mutually agreed fallback within 30 calendar days, except where emergency legal restrictions prevent such action."

2) Exhibit approach: create an Order Form Exhibit entitled "SCCs & Fallback Procedures" that lists three fallback tiers and ties the selected tier to the Order Form. This keeps the MSA DPA concise while giving procurement an exhibit to sign.

3) Liability and transition: limit any damages for performance degradation during transition to service credits defined in the Order Form; no open-ended indemnity. Where customer requests a non-standard archival or immediate deletion workflow, that will be a separately priced professional services item.

If MedSync accepts the tiered exhibit approach, we will produce a redline of your Word doc and a short change-log for procurement review.

Emma / team — quick technical feasibility note from infra/ops: 

- Tier A (regional-fallback): feasible but requires data movement and testing; estimated operational window 14–30 days depending on dataset size and routing. Network egress cost may apply; we'll surface estimated cost delta in the exhibit.
- Tier B (model-variant-fallback): generally the fastest to implement (no data repatriation). Servicing model variant in an alternate region is supported; expect an average latency delta of ~20–80ms depending on region pair. Cost delta is moderate (compute + inter-region transfer).
- Tier C (temporary-queueing): can be enabled immediately as a protective mode for short incidents. Queueing acceptable up to 72 hours before it materially impacts application integrity.

We'll attach an ops runbook and a one-page checklist for each tier. For any Tier A movement, we need a 72-hour maintenance window to run consistency checks.
