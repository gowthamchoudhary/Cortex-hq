Quick technical summary from runtime:

- Load profiling shows a ~2.8x increase in attention kernel time for the hot model shards after applying the qv2b quant profile.
- KV-cache hit rate dropped on several clients with long-prefix sessions; the runtime is falling back to slower decode paths for ~12% of requests.
- The 502/504 errors correlate with decode stalls on nodes where GPU fallback occurred while waiting for async IO (we've captured the stall stacks).

Initial hypotheses: quant rounding in qv2b changed kernel selection heuristics and increased register pressure on certain hardware combos; under high-concurrency workloads this surfaces as p99 spikes and occasional decode failures.

Mitigation options:
- Immediate: rollback to qv2a (previous quant profile) and re-route traffic — expected to restore p99 within minutes for most routes.
- Short-term: enable non-quantized v4.1 fallback for the highest-volume route while we instrument further.
- Medium-term: produce a micro-fix to kernel selection + a tuned qv2b-1 profile with reduced quant aggressiveness.

We’re attaching the trace snapshot and profiling summary (trace-snapshot-10-32.json, profiling-summary.txt). I recommend the rollback now; I don’t see data blocking rollback safety. Karthik — runtime can execute rollback if you greenlight.
