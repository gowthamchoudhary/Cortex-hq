Hi Kate — quick bullets to close out the two items: 

- Escrow vault jurisdiction: For EU split-key escrow we use an EU-only escrow vault operated in our Frankfurt region (DE) — shards are stored encrypted at rest and are tagged with tenant ID and escrow-event id. Access to escrow vaults requires a dual-approval workflow (ops + legal) and every access generates a separate audit entry in our custody log. The custody log is included in SOC 2 audits and is available to customers under NDA and on request for escrow events.

- Auditing separation: Redwood maintains separate audit pipelines for normal keystore operations and for escrow access events. Escrow access events are written to a write-once audit ledger and to a restricted-access SIEM stream; both are part of the evidence package we referenced.

- Privatelink checklist (high level):
  * Required: static CIDR for Helix on-prem, TLS-termination at Helix edge, BGP ASN for Direct Connect (if used).
  * Ports: typically 443 for API, 22 for bastion (ops only) — full port list and firewall rules attached in the Privatelink prep doc (see eu_network_topology_nodes.png and the CAIQ spreadsheet row links).
  * Timeline: peering prep typically takes 5–7 business days once network info is exchanged.
