Lauren / Marcus — looping you in; Elena at Cogent wants a PO by mid-August and is asking for both a price-hold and flexible credits. My proposed commercial structure (aiming to be conservative on downside while giving Cogent predictability):

- Term: 12 months initial renewal, with option to extend.
- Commit: 2x current baseline (target 500k tokens/day average). We'll build the quote on three tranches: Base Commit (250k/day), Upsell Commit (150k/day), and Retainer (100k/day).
- Retainer Toggle: customer prepays retainer credits equal to 100k/day * 30 days (monthly equivalent). These credits buy the lowest tier unit price. If the customer misses ramp milestones (defined at 3 and 6 months), up to 50% of the retainer is refundable as a credit against future invoices or convertible to a longer commit at a reduced rate.
- Price-hold: apply only to retainer-backed volume for 9 months; non-retainer commit receives a smaller discount and standard escalation clauses.
- Overage Protection: cap monthly overage exposure to 20% above forecast for months 1–6; above that we charge standard overage rates.
- Discount band (internal guidance):
  - Retainer-backed volume: 28–32% off list (depending on length of extension).
  - Upsell commit: 14–18% off list.
  - Base commit renewal: 8–12% off list.

Key risks: procurement may push back on refundable retainer language (legal will need to keep refund as a credit vs. cash refund to avoid revenue complications). Also, the 9-month hold is longer than our usual 6-month standard — need leadership sign-off if we want to go to 9 months on the retainer-backed volume.

Requesting: approval to present Option A (retainer toggle) with the discount band above. If leadership prefers a firmer commitment, we can present Option B (6-month price-hold + lower retainer benefit).
