Hi Sanjay —

Thanks — quick answers below and I attached a short runbook (temporary-upgrade-runbook.pdf) plus a portability checklist. TL;DR: we can do a 90-day trial with a built-in rollback safety-net and a prorated credit model.

Rollback & credits
- Upgrade starts on effective date; charges are prorated to the day.
- If you opt to downgrade within 90 days we issue a service credit equal to the overage (calculated on actual usage vs. committed uplift) applied to the following billing cycle.
- Major configuration changes (custom routing or paid add-ons) that were explicitly purchased for the trial are eligible for partial credit based on usage.

Feature portability
- Configs: prompt templates, system messages, and route definitions are fully exportable and reusable.
- Live-only features: long-lived stream hooks and dedicated KV cache snapshots are tied to running capacity and need small migration steps (we provide scripts).
- Add-ons (e.g., advanced structured-output parser) are toggled by entitlement; during downgrade toggles revert, but templates remain intact.

Risk minimization playbook (high level)
1) Mirror 10% of production traffic to the upgraded pool for 48–72 hours.
2) Run end-to-end prompt/regression tests we include in the checklist.
3) Enable automatic fallback to quantized-compatible model variant if latency exceeds threshold.
