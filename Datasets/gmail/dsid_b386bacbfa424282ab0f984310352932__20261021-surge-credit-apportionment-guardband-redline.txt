Jordan / Laura / Sarah / Ben —

I reviewed Acme's redline and Sarah's questions. High-level position and suggested wording options below. I put the alternatives in plain language to make the tradeoffs clear.

1) Surge credit apportionment — position: prefer account-level (aggregate) application, with an operational carveout to support per-SKU adjustments where systemically required and mutually agreed in the band mapping. Rationale: account-level reconciliation reduces billing churn and legal ambiguity.

Suggested contract text (Option A — aggregate):
"Surge Credits. In the event a Surge Credit is triggered, such credit shall be applied at the Customer Account level and reflected on the invoice as an account-level reconciliation item. The Parties may agree in writing to specific per-SKU apportionment rules for identified SKUs listed in the Band Mapping Schedule; such agreed apportionment shall be expressly documented in the Band Mapping Schedule and incorporated by reference."

Suggested contract text (Option B — per-SKU):
"Surge Credits. In the event a Surge Credit is triggered, such credit shall be allocated to the SKU(s) to which the triggering usage is attributed. The Parties acknowledge that per-SKU allocation may require additional operational reconciliation steps, and agree to cooperate in good faith to implement necessary billing adjustments as set forth in the Band Mapping Schedule."

2) Guardband (cap/floor) precedence — position: make the default bundle-level (simpler) but permit per-SKU override if expressly specified in Band Mapping Schedule. Also include an explicit tie-breaker if both clauses could apply.

Suggested text (precedence):
"For avoidance of doubt, where the Pricing Exhibit contains both Bundle-Level Caps/Floors and SKU-Level True-Up mechanics, the Bundle-Level provisions shall govern unless a SKU is expressly listed in the Band Mapping Schedule as subject to SKU-Level True-Up, in which case the SKU-Level provision shall govern for that SKU only. In the event of a conflict, the Parties shall apply the provision that is more protective of the Customer's billing rights."

Operational notes / risks:
- Option A reduces billing complexity and dispute volume.
- Option B is customer-favorable but increases operational overhead; require the Band Mapping Schedule to be complete and signed off.
- If Acme's system cannot support cross-SKU negative allocations, we must include a non-negotiable operational clause that prevents negative reallocation across unrelated SKUs.

I will prepare a marked PDF with the two alternative paragraphs inserted (one for each option) and track changes. Jordan — let me know which direction you prefer commercially.
