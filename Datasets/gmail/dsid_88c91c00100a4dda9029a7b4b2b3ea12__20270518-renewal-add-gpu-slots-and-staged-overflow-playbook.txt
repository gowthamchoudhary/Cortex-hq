Tom / procurement team — attaching the draft amendment and a short staged overflow playbook for Acme’s SOW. Key points (summary):

1) Capacity add
- +24 reserved GPU slots (A100-equivalent), 12-month term beginning 2027-08-01.
- Pro-rated invoice for remainder of current month if mid-cycle start.

2) Throughput guarantees
- Reserved pool provides dedicated throughput capacity targeted at sustained 1.2M tokens/min aggregate (approx 50k tokens/min per 4-GPU carve).
- P99 latency targets per model family are provided as an appendix.

3) Staged overflow policy (playbook highlights)
- Stage A (prefix caching / local KV warm): Automatic. No billing change.
- Stage B (soft overflow to Redwood on-demand): Up to 25% above reserved throughput for short bursts (3–10 minutes). Requests routed to on-demand instances, billed at on-demand rates. No throttling while on-demand capacity is available.
- Stage C (extended spike handling): If bursts exceed Stage B windows or on-demand capacity is constrained, platform will apply priority queuing (service tiers) and rate-limit lowest-priority tokens to protect overall SLOs. Customer can opt-in for guaranteed soft-credits or higher priority at increased commit.
- Stage D (hard cap): Only enforced if account exceeds contractual safety thresholds (e.g., sustained 200% over reserved for >1 hour) or at customer's explicit hard cap setting.

4) Controls & guardrails
- Admin-level hard cap toggle in Console.
- Burst-alerting integration to PagerDuty/Slack.
- Monthly usage report and cost-estimate dashboard with token and GPU breakdown.

5) Billing & credits
- Pro-rated GPU add. On-demand usage billed at published on-demand rates.
- Optional burst credit pool: pre-purchased credits that amortize on-demand spend to smooth bills.
