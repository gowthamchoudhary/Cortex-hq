Samantha — thanks for the clear ask. I pulled together a concise cross‑walk and attached our public ratecard to this thread (Redwood_ratecard_Q2_2026.pdf). Below is a short executive summary and a worked example for the volumes you called out.

Executive summary (short):
- Hosted API (baseline): pay‑as‑you‑go per token and per‑embedding. No committed minimum. Fastest time‑to‑value.
- Dedicated: reserved GPU capacity; lower per‑token unit cost in exchange for a capacity commitment (monthly/annual). Predictable throughput and data isolation.
- Private (VPC / on‑prem): usually a subscription or license + infra costs. Lower variable unit cost possible, but higher one‑time onboarding and ongoing infra/ops costs.

Representative pricing (examples, see attached ratecard for full SKUs):
- Hosted (baseline):
  • Generative prompt tokens: $0.40 per 1K tokens
  • Generative completion tokens: $0.75 per 1K tokens
  • Embeddings: $0.012 per embedding (small model) / $0.025 per embedding (high‑quality)
- Dedicated (reserved pool, 12m commit):
  • Effective per‑token: $0.22 — $0.45 per 1K tokens depending on committed throughput and batch/latency profile
  • Embeddings: $0.006 — $0.015 per embedding (volume + caching)
  • Commitment tiers: example discounts at $100k, $250k, $500k ARR with stepped unit pricing
- Private (VPC or on‑prem):
  • Typical structure: one‑time onboarding $15k–$40k, platform fee $5k–$20k/month (depending on retention/trace retention), plus infra costs (instances / GPUs).
  • Variable unit cost after infra: $0.15 — $0.35 per 1K tokens (model, quantization, utilization dependent)

Examples for EdgeApps (100M tokens/month):
- Hosted: ~100M tokens ≈ 100,000 * $0.75 / 1K = $75k / month (completion cost; plus prompt costs) — rough estimate $90k–$110k/month all‑in depending on prompt/completion split and embeddings usage.
- Dedicated (12m commit, mid tier): ~30–40% lower unit cost; estimate $55k–$70k/month with committed throughput and caching optimizations.
- Private: higher initial cost (onboarding + infra) but potential steady state $40k–$65k/month depending on hardware choices and ops model.

Commercial levers we typically apply:
- Volume commitments (reduces per‑token cost)
- Committed throughput windows (smoother autoscaling, lower tail costs)
- Caching/prefix reuse & batching guidance (we can model expected savings vs your usage patterns)
- Migration credits: we offer limited migration credit for committed pilots (negotiable, typical $10k–$40k on deals of this size)
- SLA / support: Hosted has standard SLA; Dedicated gets SLA uplift and named CSM options; Private can include premium support (with addtl fees)
