Thanks — reviewed Irene's draft and made three legal edits that tighten the trigger and preserve Redwood's ability to rely on our incident handling procedures: 

1) Trigger refinement: replace "Confirmed Unauthorized Access or Exfiltration of Customer Data causing Actual Harm" with "A Data Security Incident that results in: (a) confirmed unauthorized access to or exfiltration of Customer Data, and (b) documented material adverse impact to Customer, such as regulatory notice obligations, fraud, or system unavailability exceeding 72 hours."
