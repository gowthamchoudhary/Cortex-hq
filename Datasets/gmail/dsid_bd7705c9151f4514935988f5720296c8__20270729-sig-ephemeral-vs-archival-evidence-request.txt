Hi Laura — thanks for the clear ask. Short answers up front, full artifacts attached: evidence_pack_v2.zip includes policy excerpts and a sample audit export.

1) Prompt & output retention: By default, request and response logs are retained for 30 days in the hosted API logs. Prompts are stored in raw form in transient request logs used for debugging and billing, but long-term archives only contain aggregated telemetry (no full prompts) unless the customer enables log archival.

2) KV / prefix cache behavior: KV cache (per-session) is ephemeral and tied to the serving instance; typical TTL is 6 hours of inactivity before eviction, and explicit GC runs reduce persistence. We do not surface KV caches to long-term storage; they are not included in training datasets. See attached ephemeral-kv-ttl-diagram.png for lifecycle.

3) Training opt-out: Customers can opt out at the org-level via the Console setting or by contract for Dedicated/Private tiers. Opt-out is enforced by a flag on ingest pipelines which prevents entries from entering our training pipeline. Proofs we can provide: policy excerpt (in pack), sample ingestion audit log (redacted), and an attestation statement signed by our Security Lead.
