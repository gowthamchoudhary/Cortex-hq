Alice — quick technical answers to the bullets you asked about. I’ll keep it high level and flag where we’ll need a short workshop to walk architecture diagrams.

(a) Private peering for Dedicated: yes — Dedicated capacity can be deployed behind a VPC peering connection (or PrivateLink) so traffic between MedSync and Redwood compute is kept on the cloud provider's network. That still implies our control plane (by default) is hosted unless we ship a Private control plane deployment into your VPC (separate offering).

(b) Per-tenant GPU pools: Dedicated offers tenant isolation at the compute level (separate GPU instances/VMs per tenant). We do not colocate tenant workloads in the same process but some orchestration components may be shared; for the strictest separation we recommend Private where the control plane and orchestration run in your environment.

(c) Audit stream: we can publish an audit log feed to your S3/GCS bucket (immutable lifecycle rules can be applied on your side). We also support syslog/CloudWatch export for integrations. Retention policies are configurable; default retention in our hosted control plane is 90 days but we can configure 12+ months for Dedicated/Private via contract (we noted the SLA ask).

Other notes:
- BYOK & HSM: supported. We integrate with customer KMS (AWS KMS CloudHSM, Azure KeyVault HSM). Key operations remain visible to the customer; we can provide signed attestations.
- Egress of artifacts: we can block outbound copying of model checkpoints by policy; any exception would require explicit signed approval.
