Hi Sanjay — thanks for the clear list. High-level answers first, then I'll attach the artifacts.

Short answers:
- Typical propagation window: we aim to propagate tombstone markers to regional backups within 24–72 hours, and final expunge from replicated cold stores within 7–14 days, depending on ongoing DR snapshot cycles and offsite replication windows.
- Audit traces: we can provide an expunge trace that ties the original delete event (primary write with deletion flag) to the snapshot/tombstone events in backup manifests and the final expunge job. Those are timestamped and verifiable.
- Restore guarantees: our restore logic consults the retention/erasure index before materializing any historical snapshot; if a record is flagged as "erased" and the retention rule applies, restores will skip resurrecting that record. For additional assurance we also produce a post-restore validation report that lists skipped record IDs.

I'll attach a sanitized sample expunge trace and a diagram that maps the propagation path (primary -> hot cache -> regional snapshot -> offsite archive). One caveat: exact timelines can vary for customers with custom retention overrides or legal holds.
