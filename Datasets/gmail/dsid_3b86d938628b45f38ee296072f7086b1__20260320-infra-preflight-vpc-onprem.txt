High-level preflight (please confirm):
- Network: public IPs for outbound egress, allowed CIDR blocks for inbound control-plane/API traffic, and BGP adjacency if you plan to run cross-site routing.
- Firewall: ports 443 (TLS), 8443 (control plane, if applicable), and ephemeral ports documented in the checklist; allow outbound to console.redwood.com and our telemetry endpoints.
- DNS: CNAMEs for api.solstice.redwood.example and wildcard cert plan for internal services.
- TLS & PKI: cert chain and rotation plan; will you provide certificates or do you want Redwood to provision with an ACME flow?
- Identity & access: service account for deployment with least-privilege roles, SAML/IdP metadata if using SSO, and KMS/HSM integration for model keys.
- Storage & volumes: NFS/S3-compatible endpoints, throughput and IO requirements, and snapshot/retention policy.
- OS/Drivers: target AMI/OS image, required NVIDIA driver versions, CUDA/cuDNN compat matrix.
- Time & monitoring: NTP sync, Prometheus endpoint or pushgateway, and access for log-collector agents.
- Audit & compliance: where audit logs should be shipped (SIEM), SOC2 evidence packet requirements, and retention windows.
- Capacity & placement: expected GPU types, node counts, and AZ/regional placement constraints.
