Quick technical notes after a sweep of Jordan's SOW:

- Site readiness: we'll need site IP ranges, firewall allowlists, and a maintenance window for initial sync (48–72 hours for first data sync).
- KMS/HSM: MedFlux's ask for tenant-specific KMS keys is fine; if they require an HSM-backed key we need to confirm HSM vendor and certs (lead time + procurement).
