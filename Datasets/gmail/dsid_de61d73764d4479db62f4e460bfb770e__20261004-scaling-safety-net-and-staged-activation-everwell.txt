Grace — quick internal coordination. A couple of operational points before we finalize the packet: 

- Warm lanes vs cold start: For Option A, guaranteeing 40 GPUs as warm is reasonable, but we should call out a 6–8 hour warmup window for KV cache priming in heavy-tail workloads. Payments/procurement often asks for availability SLAs — suggest we offer a 99.5% availability SLA on the warm lane and a 99% SLA on full cluster availability.
- Burst lanes: We can commit burst capacity up to an extra 16 GPUs regionally for 8 hours/day on an autopurchase style credit; better to keep it opt-in in the SOW rather than automatic.
- Ramp triggers: Use usage thresholds (70% of guaranteed for 7 days) and a calendar trigger as a fallback. Procurement likes deterministic triggers.
