Thanks for the context — I sketched a pragmatic ops plan below.

Summary (proposed):
- Ingestion methods: Accept both. Small artifacts (<50MB) via pre-signed HTTPS links into a secured S3 staging bucket; large artifacts via SFTP to a per-partner folder.
- Manifest: evidence_manifest_v1.csv (attached) with fields: filename, sha256, size_bytes, artifact_type, scan_date_utc, scope_id, uploader, retention_days. Manifest must be uploaded alongside artifacts (same timestamp).
- Encryption: Partners may encrypt with our PGP public key (we will publish this to the secure drive) OR use server-side encryption on the S3 bucket.
- Access & retention: Default staging retention 90 days. To request >90 days, partner submits a retention request form (linked) and Legal approves.
- Handoff & processing: Once a manifest is ingested, Ops will run a validation job (verify hashes, check schema). Valid batches trigger an entry in our GRC via API. Failed batches open a ticket to the partner with failure reasons.
