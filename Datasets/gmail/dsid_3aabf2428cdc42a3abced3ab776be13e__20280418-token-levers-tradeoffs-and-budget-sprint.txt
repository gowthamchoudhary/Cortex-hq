Elena / Julian — thanks again for taking the time today. Quick recap from our discovery and recommended next steps so we have a shared view. I tried to keep this concise so procurement can forward as needed.

TL;DR
- Primary pain: token spend predictability and unacceptable per-request cost for short, high-QPS inference (NLP enrichment and intent classification across customer chat).
- Desired outcome: get inference cost under a fixed per-engagement threshold while maintaining <200ms 95th perc latency for most requests.
- Immediate ask: agree pilot scope (technical spike + short production pilot) and a conservative budget guardrail so your finance team can sign off.

What we heard (pain points)
- High token velocity from many short requests (avg 60 tokens/request) driving unit cost.
- Current vendor spends unpredictable due to no batching, limited caching, and occasional burst pricing spikes.
- Need for strict audit logs and residency (we discussed Dedicated vs Private briefly).
- Target cost band: procurement indicated an internal threshold ~ $0.60–0.85 per 1k effective requests (not per token) — we clarified this in the call.

How Redwood helps (short summary)
- Batching + prefix/KV caching: can reduce per-request compute by combining short requests into micro-batches and reusing KV cache for repeated contexts. Typical savings we see in similar customers: 35–65% depending on request distribution.
- Quantization-friendly model variants + routing: switch to lower-precision variants for lower-cost routes while keeping higher-precision models for edge cases.
- Cost-aware routing: route traffic by policy (latency-sensitive vs cost-sensitive) to meet SLOs while lowering average unit cost.

Proposed pilot (recommended, flexible)
1) 2-week technical spike: integrate SDK, run a live traffic replay/approx sample (no prod traffic) to quantify batching/caching potential and baseline token velocity.
2) 6-week controlled pilot: small portion of real traffic (5–10% of customer chat) with Canary routing + metrics (cost per 1k requests, latency p95, error rate).
3) Deliverables: measured savings scenario (best/likely/conservative), suggested batching config, staging playbook, and a short decision one-pager for procurement.

Budget and asks
- Ballpark pilot budget: $30k–$45k total (covers dedicated reserved capacity for the pilot footprint + engineering support). We’ll scope down if procurement needs a lower cap — the technical spike will give a tighter estimate.
- Data/ops asks: a sample traffic export (anonymized) and an IAM contact to set up limited log access for latency and token profiling.
