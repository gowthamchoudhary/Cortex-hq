Hi Olivia — thanks for the usage export, that made things clear. Short answer: yes, we can convert a portion of burst to reserved capacity and we have two pragmatic paths depending on how predictable HorizonAI's traffic looks.

Option A — conservative reserved tranche (recommended short-term)
- Convert 40% of the last-quarter burst consumption into a reserved tranche for 6 months.
- Expected provisioning: 2–3 business days to allocate dedicated pool and route traffic.
- Cost delta: ~+18% to current committed monthly spend but reduces burst overage risk and lowers per-token unit price compared to on-demand bursts.

Option B — aggressive reserved tranche (smoother scaling, commit-focused)
- Convert 80% of burst consumption into a 12-month reserved tranche with seasonal buffer.
- Expected provisioning: 5–7 business days if additional hardware needs to be reserved in the region.
- Cost delta: ~+8% on average (amortized monthly) versus current spend due to larger commit discounts.

Operational notes:
- We will enable prefix KV caching and adjust batching defaults for HorizonAI's profile; that typically reduces effective token consumption by ~6–10% which I included in the cost math.
- If you want a hybrid approach we can keep a small burst buffer on top of the reserved tranche for spikes (recommended).
