Quick triage notes (observed at 08:50 UTC):
- Symptom: concentrated 502/503/504 and elevated 429s across the eu-* regional edge. Error density tracks to a handful of NLBs that front our EU ingress fleet. 429s suggest retry amplification from upstream clients increasing connection concurrency.
- Probable cause: intermittent connection drops between edge proxy and pooled backend hosts combined with aggressive client retry logic -> retry storm amplifies backend load -> sporadic 5xx responses.

Immediate mitigations we've applied (08:58 UTC):
- Enabled stricter circuit-breaking on affected NLBs (reduce max concurrent upstream to 8 per connection).
- Increased idle timeout on the proxy->backend to 10s to reduce premature 502s from short-lived connections.
- Throttled aggressive healthcheck flapping by pushing a short backoff window.

Short-term plan (next 60-180 mins):
- If errors persist, we'll BGP-shift a slice of Finova's traffic to an alternative EU PoP with spare capacity and warm backends (requires Finova sign-off due to data residency).
- Implement a deployable L4 rate-limit at the ingress to dampen retry storms for problematic client IP prefixes.
- Run a rapid canary for config changes and monitor error-rate, p95 latency, 429/5xx ratios.

I've attached the raw metrics snapshot (eu-ingress-metrics.csv). Please confirm whether Finova can accept temporary traffic shift or if we must keep all traffic in EU PoPs. If they can't shift, we'll proceed with L4 throttling and the circuit-breakers as the primary mitigation.
