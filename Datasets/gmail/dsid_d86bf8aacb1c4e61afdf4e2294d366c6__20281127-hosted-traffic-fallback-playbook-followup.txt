Hi Andi — quick technical notes on the burst patterns and fallback options.

1) Temporary burst allowance: we can open a controlled burst window for the quickstart sandbox — recommended approach is a pre-warmed burst token bucket for the duration of the campaign tests. That gives you short-lived headroom without changing baseline quotas.

2) Fallback strategies when rate-limited: 
- Prefer: client-side exponential backoff with jitter + small retry budget (3 retries) for idempotent requests.
- Alternative: auto-fallback to a lower-cost compatible model we pre-stage (e.g., a smaller decoder-only variant) — this preserves throughput but will change output characteristics.
- For very latency-sensitive flows, consider prefix/KV caching for repeated prompts (chat prompts with shared context) — we can instrument cache-hit metrics in the quickstart.

3) Endpoint mapping suggestion (will follow with a CSV): 
- chat-worker endpoint: provisioned with baseline 20 QPS, burst token up to 150 QPS for 2m windows; target 95th latency < 240ms.
