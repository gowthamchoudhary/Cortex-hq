Thanks for the details. A few things from AWS Marketplace operations:
- AWS Private Offer mechanics: Marketplace will accept an annual (or multi-year) committed payment that is charged according to the private-offer terms. For annual prepay we typically require full payment up front.
- Billing/true-up: Marketplace does not perform automated per-quarter refunds against the private offer. Where customers want quarterly reconciliation, the standard practice is for the seller (Redwood) to provide quarterly usage statements and then issue settlement invoices/credits off-market (netting the prepaid and actual usage). This is what Monica described and is an accepted pattern.
- DocuSign & PO: I have created a private-offer draft in our system (POID: AWS-PO-99821). I can share a DocuSign link for Redwood's commercial terms so Stratus can sign and AWS can associate the private offer to the buyer account. DocuSign: https://docusign.aws.marketplace/PO/AWS-PO-99821
- Marketplace note on ramps: Structure the commercial as a single annual committed amount on the private-offer; any internal ramp or phased allocation should be documented in the seller-customer SOW/LOI and not as separate marketplace SKUs.
