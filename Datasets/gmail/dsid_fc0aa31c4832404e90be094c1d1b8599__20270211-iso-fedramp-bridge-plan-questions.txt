Thanks for looping us in, Markus — appreciate the mapping. High-level observations from a FedRAMP intake perspective: 

- ISO artifacts are very useful; many policy/procedure docs and risk assessments map well to NIST families.
- Primary shortfalls we typically see: evidence of continuous monitoring pipelines (SIEM retention + alerting runbooks), formalized POA&M tracking for open issues, and explicit cryptographic key lifecycle proofs (KMS/HSM configs + attestation).
- SIG/CAIQ will likely flag a few operational items (e.g., privileged access reviews, ephemeral key rotation cadence) that ISO policies reference but may lack dated artifacts.
