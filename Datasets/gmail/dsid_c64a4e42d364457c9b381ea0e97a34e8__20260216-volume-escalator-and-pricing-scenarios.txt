Hi Lila — great questions. Below are three concise scenarios plus a short worked invoice example for each. I attached the spreadsheet (Redwood-pricing-scenarios-Calc.xlsx) so you can flip the volumes.

TL;DR options
- Scenario 1 — Pay-as-you-go: no commit. Baseline list price = $0.00060 / token. Simple but unpredictable.
- Scenario 2 — Blended commit: you commit to X tokens/month; we give a single blended per-token rate for all usage that month. Good for finance planning.
- Scenario 3 — Stair-step escalator (breakpoint): tiered marginal discounts; each tier price applies only to tokens consumed in that tier. Better for upside sharing.

Worked examples (monthly): assume 6B expected tokens; actual usage = 7.8B (30% over).

Scenario A — Pay-as-you-go- List price: $0.00060 / token- Usage 7.8B -> Invoice = 7,800,000,000 * $0.00060 = $4,680,000

Scenario B — Blended commit (6B commit, blended rate 0.00046)- Commit: 6,000,000,000 tokens at committed blended rate $0.00046 -> $2,760,000- Overage: 1,800,000,000 tokens billed at list price $0.00060 -> $1,080,000- Total invoice = $3,840,000- Effective blended rate (invoice/usage) = $3,840,000 / 7.8B ≈ $0.000492 per token

Scenario C — Stair-step escalator (marginal tiers)- Tiers: 0–2B @ $0.00060; 2–5B @ $0.00048; 5–10B @ $0.00040- Invoice: (2B * 0.00060) + (3B * 0.00048) + (2.8B * 0.00040) = $1,200,000 + $1,440,000 + $1,120,000 = $3,760,000- Effective blended rate = $3,760,000 / 7.8B ≈ $0.000482 per token

Reconciliation / credits note (example approaches)
- Blended guarantee + true-up: commit gives you the blended floor; if you exceed commit by sustained months we can offer retroactive blended uplift or a renewal-based true-up. Example: if >20% over commit for two consecutive months we convert to stair-step marginal for the remainder of the quarter, with a one-time credit to offset the initial months (cap apply).
- Stair-step + monthly true-up: charges billed monthly based on marginal tiers; we provide a quarterly credit if aggregate monthly effective rate is worse than an agreed blended threshold.

Looping in a couple of operational points to make any proposal implementable:

- Billing model: Redwood invoices monthly. For blended commits we amortize any one-time commit discount across the month (or term) and show a "Commit Amortization" line on the invoice.
- True-up mechanics: recommend quarterly true-up (helps smoothing for both sides). We can do month-to-month, but that creates noisy credits.
- Reporting: need to provide a usage CSV with these fields per invoice period: timestamp, API route, token_in, token_out, model, environment tag. That lets finance map to tiers.
- Credits cap: propose cap credits at 15% of monthly invoice to limit over-indexed adjustments.
- Revenue recognition note: our finance team prefers that credits are applied as contra-revenue on the month they are issued; procurement teams sometimes prefer a future invoice credit. Both are possible but we should surface the accounting preference now.

Example invoice lines (scenario: blended commit converted to stair-step after two months >20% over)
- Line 1: Commit amortization (6B @ blended $0.00046) -> $2,760,000

- Line 3: Quarterly true-up credit (retroactive adjustment) -> -$100,000
- Net payable -> $3,780,000
