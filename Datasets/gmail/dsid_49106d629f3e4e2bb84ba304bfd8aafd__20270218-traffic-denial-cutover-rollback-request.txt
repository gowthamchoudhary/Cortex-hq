Investigating — preliminary triage below.

What I see:
- At 08:37 PST there was an automated tier reassignment job that ran as part of the scheduled "org-tier cutover" for a set of customers. The job touchlist included org cw-9812 (ClearWave) inadvertently due to a filter mismatch.
- The job applied a lower throughput tier to one of ClearWave's projects (project cw-prod-main) rather than their prior reserved tier. The mapping table shows project -> tier_id = tier-standard (should have stayed tier-premium).
- When the client retries kicked in, the rate limiter started returning 429s and our autoscaling did not catch up because quota was incorrectly scoped at org-level rather than project-level.

Suggested immediate fix (emergency path):
1) Rollback the cutover patch for ClearWave's org mapping (re-assign cw-prod-main -> tier-premium).
2) Temporarily raise the project cap by 2x while we validate.
3) Monitor for 15 minutes for 429/5xx reduction, then proceed with controlled canary if needed.

I can apply (1) now if we have sign-off. I will also tag the change so it's reversible.
