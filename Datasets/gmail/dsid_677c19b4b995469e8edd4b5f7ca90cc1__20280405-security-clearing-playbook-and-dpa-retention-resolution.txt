All — confirming feasibility and a few ops clarifications:

- Transaction logs: we support 5 years by default. For customers requiring 7 years, we can enable a per-customer retention profile (WORM + HSM-backed key rotation) — will require a scope/fee for long-term storage.
- Audit / auth logs: we keep full auth logs for 3 years by default; can extend to 7 years for regulated customers with an escrow or add-on.
- Metrics/telemetry: 12 months (rollup) is fine.
- Embeddings: default 30 days ephemeral cache; if customer purchases retention add-on, we will store encrypted embeddings for up to 24 months with strict access controls and explicit TOS.

Operational dependencies:
- KMS: we support AWS KMS and customer-managed keys (Bring Your Own Key). HSM integration is available for Dedicated/Private customers.
- Escrow: we can set up encrypted escrow to a certified custodian (we have partners) but need a short SOW.
