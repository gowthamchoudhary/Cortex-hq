Hi Sarah —

Short answer: yes, we can convert runway credit into reserved units mid-contract, and we have a couple of ways to do it depending on Econify's priorities. High level options: 

1) Conversion to short-term reserved pool (30–90 days)
   - Convert runway amount into reserved GPU-hours at a small conversion fee (2–4%)
   - Pricing: reserved units priced at a 12% discount to on-demand token rates for 30d; 16% for 90d
   - Converted units can be set to co-term with Oct ’27 renewal but will be prorated for remaining term

2) Apply runway as an opportunity credit toward upgrade + annualized reserved commitment
   - Apply runway as a one-time credit against the first invoice of a bumped commitment; additional discount bands possible (tiered at 250k/500k units)

3) Hybrid: split conversion and credit to keep some runway for burst while locking some as reserved.

Adding the numeric bands so procurement has concrete options. All figures are illustrative and net of standard volume discounts for Econify's existing commitment.

Option A — 30-day reserved conversion (conversion fee 3%)
- Convert 50k runway => reserved rate = $0.0064/unit (≈10% off on-demand)
- Convert 100k runway => reserved rate = $0.0059/unit (≈13% off)
- Convert 150k runway => reserved rate = $0.0054/unit (≈16% off)

Option B — 90-day reserved conversion (conversion fee 2%)
- Convert 50k => $0.0059/unit
- 100k => $0.0054/unit
- 150k => $0.0049/unit

Option C — Apply runway as upfront credit toward a +250k committed uplift
- If Econify upgrades by ≥250k total commitment, we can offer blended uplift credit equivalent to 20% of converted runway value spread over first 3 invoices.

Billing notes:
- Converted reserved units can be co-termed to Oct 1, 2027; final invoice will true-up prorated unused time.
- We can spread the converted value across 2 invoices for smoothing on request (backed by SOW amendment).
