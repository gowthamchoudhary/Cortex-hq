Thanks, Steph — quick technical notes (high level):

- Hardware profile: for their p95 latency target at 100 qps and medium-length sequences (512 tokens) we should recommend H100 80GB (or H100s in multi-instance) if they want headroom. A100 40GB is possible with aggressive quantization but increases engineering lift and may worsen tail latency.
- Storage: NVMe local SSDs for KV cache; recommend at least 4TB usable for model artifacts + cache.
- Networking: 25GbE minimum; dedicated NICs preferred. If they want cross-site failover we need WAN replication plan.
- Security/keys: KMS integration acceptable; for air‑gapped installs offline key provisioning is doable but requires an HSM appliance on their side and server-side tooling changes (extra ~2 sprints work).
- Operational gap: we don't currently have a turnkey air‑gapped installer for customers — it's a 6–8 week eng project for first customer (includes packaging, tests, and compliance checklist).

Open questions for procurement/CISO we should ask Acme now:
1) Expected concurrent models/size (Llama2-70B/Redwood-optim?);
