Hi Sarah — following up with the consolidated answers and clarifications you requested. See attachments and the short Q&A below. We can jump on a call if you prefer.

Q: Who holds the primary keys across environments?
A: Hosted: Redwood-managed KMS by default; Dedicated: customer-managed KMS supported (customer supplies CMK; Redwood can also proxy on request); Private: customer fully owns KMS/HSM (no Redwood access). See 'kms_ownership_matrix_v1.xlsx'.

Q: Are identifiers retained in logs or cache, and when are they redacted?
A: Ephemeral tensors (including raw tokens) are held only in GPU memory during inference and are not persisted. KV cache contents are encrypted at rest; for Hosted tenants, we provide configurable redaction that hashes or strips identifiers before writing to persistent cache/logs. For Dedicated/Private customers, redaction is configurable; we recommend enabling redaction for any PII-heavy workloads. Karthik confirmed the runtime performs hashing at the cache-write boundary (before commit to persistent storage).

Q: Telemetry and retention windows?
A: Default telemetry (latency, error rates, aggregated usage) is retained for 90 days in the Console. Request-level diagnostic traces (stack dumps, payload samples) are retained for 7 days unless a longer retention is contracted. We can export immutable audit logs to your SIEM on request.

Q: Access controls & auditability?
A: RBAC is supported via Console; roles include Viewer, Engineer, SRE, and Platform Admin. Production log access is restricted to SRE and Platform Admin by default. Audit logs are immutable and contain identity + action + timestamp for each access event.
