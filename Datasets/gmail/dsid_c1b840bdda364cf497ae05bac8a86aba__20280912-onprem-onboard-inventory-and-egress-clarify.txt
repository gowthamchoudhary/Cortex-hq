Alex/Team — thanks for the fast turnaround and the CIDRs/IPs. Short answers to Alex's question and some follow-ups: 

1) Sidecar/proxy support: yes — our private deployment agent can be configured to route all control-plane egress through a local HTTP(s) proxy or a sidecar listening on 127.0.0.1:8443. We can also bind to a node-level mTLS proxy if you require mutual TLS between the agent and your egress gateway.

2) Whitelisting: if you prefer not to open public IPs at the firewall level, the sidecar approach is the recommended path. If you do whitelist, the minimal set of outbound endpoints is in egress-allowlist-example.txt; those are control-plane endpoints (management telemetry, billing ping, and model catalog).
