Hi Jane — thanks for the clear list. Short version up front: for Redwood Private deployed into a customer-managed VPC we operate with customer-controlled storage and do not copy customer data into Redwood-managed buckets; residency is therefore fixed to the region you deploy in; deletion is customer-initiated with configurable retention windows and documented irrecoverability procedures. I attached a concise runbook and a SOC2 extract we can share with auditors.

Below are the specifics and a proposed timeline:

- Residency:
  - When deployed in your VPC, all runtime artifacts (request logs, KV cache dumps that we persist for warm starts, and user-provided files) are stored in your S3 buckets (or equivalent) that you provision. Redwood’s control plane (separate, optional) communicates over TLS but does not host your data.
  - We do not replicate your S3 buckets to other regions unless you explicitly configure cross-region replication on your side.

- Encryption and keys:
  - We support customer-managed keys (KMS/HSM). You can provide the KMS key in your cloud account; Redwood’s processes use that key for any optional server-side encryption operations. Redwood employees do not have access to the plaintext without keys you control.

- Deletion / Erasure process:
  - Customer-initiated deletion is executed by running our deletion job (we provide a runbook or an automation token). That job deletes primary objects and triggers background cleanup of auxiliary state (index shards, ephemeral caches).
  - Backups/snapshots: Redwood Private does not take independent long-term backups of your customer data. Any short-lived snapshots for in-cluster upgrades are retained only per the retention window you set (default 48–72 hours) and are stored in your bucket namespace unless you configure otherwise.
  - SLA & timeline: Primary deletion completes within 24 hours; garbage collection across ephemeral caches and short-lived snapshots completes within 7 days by default. We can contractually shorten that window for the pilot in a Dedicated SLA.

- Evidence & audit:
  - We can provide deletion logs, KMS key rotation records (redacted to show process), and a signed statement for your SOC2 binder.
