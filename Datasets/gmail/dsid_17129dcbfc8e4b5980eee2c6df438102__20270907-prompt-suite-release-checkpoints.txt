Evelyn / team — putting a concise proposal here (short bullets for Acme):

Proposed acceptance criteria (pilot):
- Primary metric: Corrected-Response Accuracy (CRA) on 1k held-out prompts from Acme — baseline = 0.842.
  - Gate: no more than 1.5% absolute drop vs baseline (CRA >= 0.827) during canary window.
- Secondary metric: Anchored user satisfaction (surveyed NPS-like) — no statistically significant decline (p>0.05) vs baseline.
- Error/abuse rate: automated classifier false-positive rate < 0.5% and no safety violations flagged by manual review > 0.1% of sampled responses.
- Latency: 95th percentile inference latency must not exceed baseline + 60ms overhead.

A/B plan (suggested):
- Round 0 (internal staging): 1000 synthetic prompts, run stability evals + coherence/verbosity checks.
- Canary 1 (1% traffic) for 24 hours — collect CRA and latency, sample 200 responses for manual safety review.
- Canary 2 (5% traffic) for 48 hours — automated metrics + expanded manual sampling.
- Ramp to 20% for 72 hours if metrics hold, then to 50% and monitor for 7 days before full roll.

Rollback triggers (automated):
- CRA drop > 1.5% sustained for three 10-minute windows OR
- Latency P95 increase > 60ms sustained for 30 minutes OR
- Safety violation sample > 0.5% in manual checks.

We can make thresholds looser/tighter per Acme risk tolerance — recommend we document these with explicit measurement windows. I'll attach a short MD with the metric calculations.
