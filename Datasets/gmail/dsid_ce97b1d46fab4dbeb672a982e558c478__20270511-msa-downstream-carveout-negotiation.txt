Karthik / Marissa —

Thanks, looping legal counsel. High-level position: Redwood generally accepts a mutual exclusion of consequential/special/incidental/punitive damages, with the following carve-outs that we consider non-negotiable from a risk perspective:

1) Breach of confidentiality / unauthorized data access resulting in third-party claims (we cannot limit or exclude this where regulated data is involved).
2) IP indemnity claims where Redwood's infringement causes downstream customer losses (we need to preserve indemnity obligations).
3) Willful misconduct and gross negligence results (we carve these out of exclusions).

Proposed compromise language (for Section 8):
"Except for liability arising from (i) breach of confidentiality or privacy obligations; (ii) infringement of a third party's intellectual property that Redwood is required to indemnify; (iii) Redwood's willful misconduct or gross negligence; and (iv) amounts payable under applicable indemnities, neither party shall be liable to the other for consequential, special, incidental or punitive damages, including loss of profits, loss of business, or loss of customers. Notwithstanding the foregoing, claims for downstream customer indemnities or regulatory fines shall be subject to an express monetary cap of 12 months of fees paid under the applicable Order Form or $1,000,000, whichever is greater."

Why this works: it preserves a mutual exclusion while protecting Redwood against open-ended downstream exposure. It also gives Acme a defined recovery path for truly catastrophic downstream impacts.

Olga — can you run this against the redline and highlight any matching language in Acme's text?
