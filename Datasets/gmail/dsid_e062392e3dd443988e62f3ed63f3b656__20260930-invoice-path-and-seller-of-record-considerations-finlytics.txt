Hi Amelia — great to hear from you. Short answers up front, details below:  

- Seller-of-record: For AWS Marketplace private offers, Redwood can be configured as seller-of-record in most cases (we can be SOR on the private offer), which simplifies approvals. If we’re listed as SOR, AWS will still process the transaction but the invoice comes from Redwood.
- Timing: Marketplace private offer + PO typically gets you to first invoice within ~7–14 business days after accepting the offer (faster for smaller deals). Direct purchase (MSA + PO + invoice) tends to be 2–4 weeks depending on legal review.

Amelia — adding a bit more color from the sales side.

- Co-sell credits/partner credit: If you accept a private offer on AWS and we pursue a co-sell, the timing on co-sell rebate/credit to Redwood does not affect your billing; it's a back-office settlement between AWS and Redwood. However, for internal Finlytics rebate tracking we can provide invoice-level metadata (SKU mapping) so your finance team can reconcile credits.
- Pricing/discounting: We can put the agreed discount on the private offer so the PO amount equals what you expect on the Redwood invoice if we’re SOR.
- SKU & itemization: Marketplace SKUs are available and helpful for intra-org chargebacks; we can mirror those on a direct invoice if you prefer.

If the priority is speed to provision and SOC 2 review, private offer + SOR is usually the least friction path. If you need extended payment terms or custom MSA redlines, the direct route gives more flexibility but takes longer.
