Hi Taylor — great questions. Short answers up front then details below:

1) Yes — we can do an "upfront commitment with split invoicing" (single SOW / single prepay commitment) and issue invoices across fiscal years per an agreed split (your 40/30/30 example is workable). We treat the full prepay as customer consideration; Redwood invoices per the split and our finance team records deferred revenue and amortizes performance obligations monthly over the service period.

2) If Ventra prefers 3 separate annual invoices (one at the start of each year), we can price it either as: a) identical effective list rates (no multi-year discount), or b) preserve a multi-year commitment discount but require a signed multi-year SOW that references the commitment and provides the discount schedule — the difference is how credit risk and collections are handled.

3) For ASC 606, here's the short note you can share with your controller: 
- Performance obligation: continuous access to Redwood inference capacity and platform features; revenue recognized over time as services are delivered.
- Consideration: total committed amount (the prepay) is allocated to the performance obligation and recognized ratably (monthly) over the term; when invoiced upfront but split for cash collection, revenue recognition follows the contract's service delivery schedule, not the cash receipt, and the excess collected is recorded as deferred revenue.

Attached: a simple invoice phasing worksheet (Ventra_3yr_PhaseSplit_v1.xlsx) showing the 40/30/30 example and effective monthly recognition per fiscal year.

Notes on discounts: our standard multi-year discount tiers are: 10% for 2-year, 18% for 3-year; for your $1.8M band we can offer a 20% effective discount if Ventra agrees to the split-invoice with a signed SOW and DocuSign for the full term. We typically require that the SOW references the invoice schedule and the allocation table so both finance teams can reconcile.

If it's helpful I'll pull in our accounting lead for a quick call with your controller.

Aditya / Marissa — looping in a couple of finance ops notes that should help Ventra's controller. I'm attaching a short memo (ASC606_mapping_notes.docx) and a draft SOW clause. Key points: 

- If we invoice 40/30/30 across FY boundaries but it's a single signed commitment, Redwood treats the full consideration as contract consideration and recognizes revenue monthly over the 36-month service window. The invoicing split is strictly a cash collection schedule and does not change revenue recognition timing.
- We will provide a monthly schedule showing deferred revenue balance and recognized revenue for audit purposes.
- For the accounting clause in the SOW we recommend: 
  "Customer and Redwood agree that the total committed consideration is for a single series of performance obligations representing access to Redwood's inference platform. Redwood will recognize revenue ratably over the term and provide schedules on request for audit."
