For immutability/tamper-evidence we can provide SHA-256 manifest files per export and timestamped object metadata. We do not provide a write-once filesystem but can sign export bundles with a key and provide checksums for verification.
- Retention: configurable per-account; default 90 days for most telemetry, configurable up to 3 years for Dedicated/Private deployments.
