TL;DR — root cause appears to be transient headroom exhaustion in the MeridianLedger pool due to two concurrent large batched jobs from an internal partner region plus a misapplied autoscale knob. Immediate mitigations below.

What we see:
- Several hosts in the MeridianReservedPool entered queueing at ~07:55 PT. CPU/GPU metrics show 92–98% utilization on compute bound kernels.
- Autoscaler policy was set to gradual scale (30s cooldown); a burst of three large batch jobs pushed the pool past its headroom before new nodes came up.
- A small fraction of requests hit the fallback routing path and returned 429s while others experienced higher latency. Errors correlate with token throttle events.

Immediate actions (in progress):
1) Temporarily increase pool burst capacity +1 node (evicting lower-priority burst reservations) — ETA 8 min.
2) Bypass gradual cooldown for this pool (force immediate scale) — ETA 6 min.
3) Apply targeted prefix caching for high-frequency prompts from Meridian (hot key cache) — ETA 30–45 min.
4) Route low-latency critical routes to the compatible hosted model variant in another region as a temporary fallback — requires ops approval for cross-region burst (Irene: approval?)
