Summary (short answers)
- Hosting matrix (see attached PDF):
  * Hosted (Redwood-managed): inference + KV cache execute in the selected cloud region (US-EAST, EU-WEST). Persistent artifacts (logs, usage analytics) are stored in the region unless customer opts for cross-region export.
  * Private (VPC / Dedicated): compute and any persistent stores remain in the customer's VPC or dedicated tenant region. We do not replicate inference payloads outside the chosen region for Private deployments unless configured for explicit failover.
- Customer-managed keys / BYO-HSM: Supported in Private deployments. Import flow: secure manifest + encrypted key import via SFTP with HSM vendor-signed CSR exchange (see section 3 of hsm-rotation-policy.pdf). We also support AWS KMS and Azure KeyVault integrations for BYOK.
- Rotation cadence and procedures: default crypto rotation policy is annual for workload encryption keys and 3 years for root HSM keys. Operational keys (used for session/token signing) rotate on a 90-day cadence. Emergency rotation runbook included — verification steps, automated smoke tests, and customer verification checklist.
- Emergency rotations & validation: runbook includes a staged roll (canary → regional → global), live traffic cutover tests, and an HMAC-based verification step to confirm key continuity across caches.
