Alex — great questions, thanks for the notes and the clarity on traffic. Short answers up front, details below and attached checklist + diag. Attaching: redwood-migration-checklist-v1.pdf and auth-flow-diagram.png.

1) Vector dims: you can map 1:1 iff you continue using the same model family — but our recommended reranker works best with 1024-dim dense vectors for the pretrained rerank weights we ship. If you keep 768, reranker quality may drop ~3-6% depending on dataset; we can either:
  - Re-embed historical corpus to 1024 (best quality), or
  - Train a lightweight projection layer (768->1024) during ingest to avoid full re-embed (faster, lower cost but slightly lower precision).

Storage impact: ~33% increase for 1024 vs 768 (rough estimate; depends on quantization). With 8-bit quantization + compression, unit storage growth is smaller.

2) Batching: for 300 qps peak we typically recommend micro-batching at 16-32 tokens per request and scheduling batches of 32-64 vectors for embedding calls on Dedicated. That keeps P95 <200ms on our 2xA10 pools; in Hosted, we recommend autoscale to a 2-3 node pool with batching window 25ms. We'll include concrete perf numbers in the checklist.

3) Auth & Private: yes — Private supports customer-managed KMS and HSM-backed keys, and we support mTLS for Private deployments. Dedicated offers VPC peering + KMS integration. For Hosted API, we support rotating API keys and can enable IP allowlists.

SDK + rerank quickstart: see step 4 in the attached checklist. Short pattern: 1) fetch top-K via approximate neighbor search on embeddings, 2) call Redwood reranker endpoint with structured prompt/tool-call, 3) stream final results. We can drop a sample SDK snippet for your stack (are you on Node or Python?).
