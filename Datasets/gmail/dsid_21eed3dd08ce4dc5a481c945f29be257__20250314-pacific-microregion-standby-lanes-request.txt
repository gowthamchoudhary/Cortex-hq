Proposed dedicated pools (initial commit, month 1):

- EU (Frankfurt fallback): 6x A100 80GB (3 nodes) configured for GDPR workloads and isolated logging
- US (Ashburn standby): 4x A100 (2 nodes) for burst and cross-region failover

Operational safety buffers and policies:
- Buffer: 20% capacity headroom (hot spare on APAC) to absorb demand spikes; additional 10% cold reserve that can be warmed within 30–60 min
- Routing: primary by region, automatic failover to nearest fallback region based on latency + compliance tags
- Prefix caching: enable KV/prefix cache on APAC primary to reduce token compute by ~12–18% for repeated session patterns

Triggers & rollout cadence (deal-linked):
- T0 (SOW signed + 25% prepay): provision APAC primary pool (16 GPUs) + baseline EU/US reserved nodes (total 26 GPUs)
