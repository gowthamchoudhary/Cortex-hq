Hi Luc / Ananya —

Thanks for the clarifications. Quick technical plan and recommendations so we align on evaluation scope:

Model recommendations (to evaluate A/B):
- redwood-multilang-16k-v2 (high-capacity, better long-context FR/DE/JA/KO handling)
- redwood-lite-7b-quant (4-bit quantized, lower cost, faster warm-starts)

Why these two: the 16k gives stronger context retention and fewer hallucinations on long instructions (useful for FR/DE legal phrasing). The 7b quant is a cost-effective candidate that often meets latency targets for short queries.

Benchmarks / estimates (EU region, single-GPU serving profile):
- redwood-multilang-16k-v2: P50 ~ 420ms (seq len 128), cost est ~ $0.023/token for heavy tails (preliminary)
- redwood-lite-7b-quant: P50 ~ 160ms (seq len 64), cost est ~ $0.004/token

Evaluation plan:
1) Run batch eval (offline) across your FR/DE prompt set + human annotations (accuracy + usefulness). We’ll use the following metrics: exactness (binary), BLEU/chrF for short responses, and a human-judged relevance score (1–5).
2) Locale checks: we’ll add locale-specific checks for formality and legal term handling (FR: vous/tu, formal register; DE: separable verbs and compound nouns; JA/KO: honorifics and respectful phrasing).
3) Regression guardrails: we’ll include a prompt-based canary set (50 golden prompts per locale). Any candidate that drops > 8% on canary accuracy will be flagged for manual review.
