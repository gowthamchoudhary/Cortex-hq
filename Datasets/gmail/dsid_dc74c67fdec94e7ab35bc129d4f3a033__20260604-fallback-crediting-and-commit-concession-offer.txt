2) Credits (worked examples):
- Single-region 30-min incident: credit = 30 / (30*24*60) * region-MRR * 1.0 = 0.00208 * region-MRR. Example: if region-MRR = $50,000, credit = $104.
- Cross-region failover 120-min incident: credit = 120 / (30*24*60) * sum(regions-MRR) * 1.5 (cross-region multiplier) = 0.00833 * sum(regions-MRR) * 1.5. Example: two regions at $50,000 each -> credit ≈ $1,250.
