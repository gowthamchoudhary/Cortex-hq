Update from infra/serving: we correlated the spike with an autoscaler mis-evaluation that caused a burst of connection churn on three us-west instances; churn increased KV-cache misses for longer sessions and made per-token kernel selection spike. Root cause: noisy latency signals from a networking BLIP node that temporarily reduced our batching headroom.

Short-term fixes already applied by infra (10:00 PT):
- Isolated the noisy BLIP node and drained it from the routing pool.
- Turned on a conservative autoscale threshold for the affected ring.
- Deployed a targeted kernel fallback that prioritizes low-latency execution for streaming (this will be live for FastBank in ~5 minutes).

Customer-facing mitigations we recommend now (we included config snippets):
1) Client: enable smaller chunking and 150ms initial retry backoff (see attached).
2) Route: pin FastBank to the low-latency kernel variant for the next 6 hours (we can do this for you).
