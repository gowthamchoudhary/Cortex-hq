Thanks for the heads up — we've pulled relevant traces and logs. Preliminary findings: starting 08:38 PST we see a 3x increase in concurrent token requests from Acme's API key and a correlated rise in 429 responses from our per-tenant rate limiter. Around the same time several clients retry aggressively (exponential backoff with low jitter and max attempts 8), producing a feedback loop where 429s trigger retries that amplify load and push shared dispatch queues past latency SLOs.

What we've done so far (in-flight):
- Scoped: isolated Acme traffic to a separate burst bucket to prevent cross-tenant amplification.
- Throttled: applied a temporary elevated burst window to allow their inflight operations to drain (30 min window).
- Enabled: server-side additional jitter on retry guidance headers (Retry-After semantics) and increased visibility metrics for their key.

Immediate ask for Acme: reduce client-side retry attempts to 3 with randomized jitter; if you can roll that change we expect p95/p99 to improve within 10-20 minutes of drain. If they cannot change client behavior quickly we can route them to a warmed dedicated worker (manual) — that will buy ~5x throughput and isolate tail effects.

We will keep the temporary burst open until traffic normalizes and then revert with a postmortem. I’ll attach the log snippet and traces to the Jira ticket.
