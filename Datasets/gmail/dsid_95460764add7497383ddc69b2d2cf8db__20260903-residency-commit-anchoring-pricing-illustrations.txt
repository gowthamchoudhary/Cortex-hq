Illustration A — 12M commit (monthly billing)
- HSM: 2 seats, vendor HSM + Redwood orchestration. Seat list price $45k / seat / year. With a 12M commit we typically amortize over 24 months for quoting optics; expected concession: 10% credit applied as monthly bill credit.
- Audit logs: 90-day retention tier: $0.02 / GB / month. With your projected Q1 traffic (~5 TB/month) that equates to ~$100/month for storage (90d).
- Residency: EU region add-on surcharge: $0.06 / token for EU-hosted tokens beyond baseline. No residency-specific commit credit at 12M level.

Illustration B — 36M commit (prepay or monthly with committed spend)
- HSM: same seats, amortized over 36 months; concession: 25% effective credit on seat cost if seats are contractually committed for 36M and billed annual upfront or via agreed milestone.
- Audit logs: 365-day retention: $0.015 / GB / month (volume discount). For 5 TB/month average, ~ $75/month.
- Residency: We can offer a 15% residency surcharge discount applied to token surcharge if the commit covers >60% of EU traffic across the contract life.

Illustration C — Hybrid approach (EU cold‑storage + US hot; mixed commit)
- HSM: one seat dedicated in EU (for key residency), one shared in US (for throughput). We propose a blended seat rate and credits split by region by traffic weight.
- Audit logs: tiered retention (90d for hot, 365d for cold) — blended storage price.

What typically requires Finance approval:
- Any concession >20% on line items (HSM, residency surcharge)
- Upfront amortization beyond the standard maximum of 36 months
- Credit structures that apply to usage lines (tokens/embeddings) rather than one‑time seats or add‑ons

Adding quick technical + costing context from infra: 

- HSM seats: hardware vendors invoice annually; our typical approach is to pass-through vendor invoicing for capital purchases and layer our managed service fee on top. If Stitch prefers Redwood to absorb vendor capital and amortize internally, we need a 36M minimum and a committed revenue profile to underwrite it.
- KMS vs HSM: if they accept KMS with BYOK in EU, we can reduce effective cost by ~40% vs dedicated HSM seats but it does not meet all attestation profiles some customers require for HIPAA enclaves. We should present both as options.
- Audit logs: retention cost scales with ingestion and retention window. We can include a simple formula in the worksheet: cost = ingestion_GB * retention_months * storage_rate. For retention >365 days we typically require an S3‑Glacier style path which lowers cost further but increases retrieval latency and gets a separate line item.
