Ran first pass on the Dedicated-like cluster (4x A100 equiv). Summary highlights:
- Test payload: mixed token sizes per InfraOps profile.
- Observed aggregate token throughput: ~1,800 tokens/sec at steady 750 RPS.
- Median TTFT (warmed KV-cache): 82ms. Cold runs added ~120ms to TTFT.
- Latency at steady 750 RPS: P50=95ms, P95=320ms, P99=580ms.
- Burst 2.5k RPS (10s): queue growth observed ~120 requests after 3s; P95 spiked to ~1.4s and ~3% of requests experienced queue timeout/dropped by LB under default rate limits.
- Soak 2-hour nominal: slight tail drift — P95 increased ~18% over 2 hours (likely GC or mem-pressure related on smaller worker shards).

Immediate takeaways:
1) TTFT targets are achievable for warmed caches; recommend a light pre-warm step for interactive sessions.
2) The current default rate-limiter causes abrupt drops under very short bursts; rate limiter should use a token-bucket with short refill bursts (i.e., allow 3x burst for 5–10s) instead of hard RPS cap.
3) Per-instance concurrency cap of 64 appears reasonable; however, we should scale pool sizes proactively when queued requests exceed 30% of active capacity.
