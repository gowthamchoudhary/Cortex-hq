Thomas / team,

Technical confirmation and delivery options below. TL;DR: we can support the Order Form addendum with a 120-day optional extension, and we can provide both PDF attestation and machine-readable evidence. Important operational caveats are noted.

Operational details:
- Deletion flow (hosted):
  1) Customer triggers purge (or Redwood processes scheduled purge) -> objects marked for deletion and removed from active storage.
  2) Object lifecycle removes replicas and index entries; background job removes from cold storage according to configured backup retention cycle (typical backup cycle is weekly; backup purge may lag by up to 7-14 days depending on retention tier).
  3) Final irreversible metadata removal occurs after backup purge completes; we publish a purge manifest when the final removal job runs.

- Certification artifact we will deliver:
  * Signed PDF attestation (Redwood security contact) stating dataset IDs, purge date, and scope.
  * CSV manifest: dataset_id, object_id, deletion_timestamp_utc, storage_tier, sha256_hash, purge_operation_id.
  * Audit log extract: entries listing the purge_operation_id and timestamps for each lifecycle step.

- Backup/escrow options:
  * We can retain the purge manifest in an audit escrow for up to 6 months (customer option) — Redwood will store the manifest and provide a time-limited signed link.
  * We do not retain actual deleted object content in escrow; only the manifest and audit evidence.

- SLA wording considerations: we can commit to artifact delivery within 7 business days for bespoke windows (standard plan), or 10 business days if a grace hold was active and purge requires additional reconciliation. We can make the 120-day extension an Order Form addendum (opt-in).
