Vanessa — copied a few quick notes inline.

> - Latency: p50 = 38ms (stable), p95 = 240ms (up from 160ms on 2027-07-18 spike)
The 07/18 spike correlates with the 00:45 deploy to prod of model-serving v2.3. Two things we observed: a) a KV-cache invalidation path triggered for long-tail prefixes which amplified immediate token generation cost; b) a kernel selection fallback on older GPU instances when sequence length distribution hit the long tail.

Planned immediate actions (to include in 1-slide incident summary):
- I will extract the deploy timeline + trace snippets and attach to the slide (doing now).
- Re-roll the canary policy to restrict v2.3 to 10% traffic and enable rolling rollback automation if p95 > 200ms for 10 minutes (owner: Markus; ETA: Aug 3 EOD).
- Short-term mitigation: increase KV cache TTL to avoid invalidations for repeated short prefixes (Grace — can you validate perf impact in the benchmark?).

I'll push a draft 1-slide summary to the Drive by EOD tomorrow.
