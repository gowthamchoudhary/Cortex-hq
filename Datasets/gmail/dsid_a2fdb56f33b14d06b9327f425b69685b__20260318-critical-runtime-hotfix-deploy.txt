Hi all —

Quick heads up from Redwood Inference. We’ve identified a memory regression in the serving runtime introduced by a recent kernel change that is causing elevated tail latency for a small subset of long-sequence requests. We will deploy an emergency hotfix (hotfix_1.0.3) to address an off-by-one cache growth issue and reclaim leaked KV cache memory.

What to expect:
- Planned rollout start: 2026-03-18 09:30 PDT (rolling canary -> ramp).
- Brief rolling restarts per host; each host restart is expected to take ~30–60 seconds.
- During restarts you may see short latency spikes (50–300ms) for affected requests.
- We do not expect request loss; retries will be served.

Immediate asks:
1) If you have a live-critical window in the next hour, reply here ASAP and we’ll delay the start for that window.
2) Please avoid mass-batching client-side retries during the rollout to reduce amplification.

Attachments: hotfix_1.0.3.patch (diff), heap_dump_20260318.tar.gz (sample logs).

We’ll post live updates to status.redwood.ai and follow with a postmortem within 48 hours. I’m the internal incident lead — looping in Dylan (rollout), Marcus (runtime), and Hannah (customer comms).
