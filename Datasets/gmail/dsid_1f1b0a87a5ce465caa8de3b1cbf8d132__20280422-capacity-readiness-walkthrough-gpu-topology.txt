I reviewed the AtlasMed export. Quick technical notes to prepare options for Thursday:

1) Topology options I'll pack into the deck:
   - Option A (Cost-optimized): Single-region us-east pool, autoscale to baseline + 3x burst with soft-preemption (best for predictable averages).
   - Option B (Latency/high-availability): us-east primary + us-west warm-standby with active-passive routing; warm-standby keeps KV cache snapshots for ~20 mins. Better p95 but ~18% higher monthly cost.

2) Autoscale/burst policy defaults and tuning knobs:
   - Continuous batching window: 20–60ms (workload dependent)
   - Burst policy: pre-warmed slots + ephemeral burst VMs. We recommend: pre-warm 10% of peak, ephemeral for remaining.
   - Fallback behavior: automatically route to smaller/quantized variant if tail latency > SLO for >2min.

3) Warm-up times: model-dependent. For a ~6–8 token average request, warm-up to 90% throughput typically ~90s. If AtlasMed wants <30s warm-up, we'll need sustained pre-warm capacity.
