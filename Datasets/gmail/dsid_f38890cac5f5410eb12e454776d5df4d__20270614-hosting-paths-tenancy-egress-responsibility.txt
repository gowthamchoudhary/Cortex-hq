Thanks — quick notes from infra: 

- PrivateLink endpoint addresses: AWS PrivateLink endpoints are elastic and use the VPC’s private IPs assigned when we create the endpoint in the customer VPC. Those addresses are not guaranteed to be globally stable if the endpoint is re-created. For a customer allowlist, two common patterns work: 1) we provide a short list of IPs and commit to notification on rotation + a 48–72 hour freeze window; or 2) FinEdge allowlists the service DNS names plus our maintenance window SLA.

- Static egress pool: we can front the PrivateLink with a small customer-facing ALB/NAT in the FinEdge VPC under the PrivateLink configuration and assign stable private IPs in agreed subnets — this requires coordination and a small infra config step in customer staging/prod. It’s feasible but we should call it out in the appendix and SOW as a configuration item (approx 2–3 engineer-days).

- Tenancy separation: separating by subnet per BU is straightforward. We’ll produce recommended CIDR splits and port lists once Rahul shares allowed CIDR ranges and any port blocking policies.
