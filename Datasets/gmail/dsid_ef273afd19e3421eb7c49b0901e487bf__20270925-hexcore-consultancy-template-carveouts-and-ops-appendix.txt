Team — reviewed HexCore's redlines and Monica's summary. Key legal positions and suggested counters below (high level):

1) Indemnity / Liability Cap
- We should insist on mutual IP indemnity (Redwood will indemnify for IP infringement of our Deliverables; HexCore will indemnify for their content/use).
- Cap: propose the lesser of (i) 12 months of fees paid under the applicable SOW or (ii) $750,000. This protects both parties and is consistent with comparable ISV deals.
- Carveouts: personal data breach indemnity and willful misconduct remain uncapped (standard).

2) Audit Rights
- Agree to provide SOC 2 report annually and a redacted subprocessor list.
- For audits: offer remote/desk audit rights annually and one on-site per 24 months with prior notice and cost-limited to HexCore unless a material compliance failure is found.

3) Data Residency / DPA
- We can offer region pinning in the SOW. DPA: add a clause that for EU customer accounts we will process data within EU regions where required; however, we cannot guarantee third-party subprocessor locations beyond our control — offer to use EU-based subprocessors where necessary and sign a DPA appendix.

4) SLA / Acceptance & Financials
- 99.5% SLA OK for production tier in SOW; include service credits (not direct refunds) capped at a % of monthly fees.
- Acceptance: staged signoff with 5% holdback until final acceptance for integration work; milestone acceptance criteria must be objective and testable.

5) Procurement & Payment Terms
- Net-30 standard. Invoice schedule should align to milestones (30/40/30 or similar).

I'll draft counters and mark the clauses with rationale. If Procurement pushes for uncapped liability or unlimited audit scope, escalate to execs (Vanessa/Monica) for concession decisions.
