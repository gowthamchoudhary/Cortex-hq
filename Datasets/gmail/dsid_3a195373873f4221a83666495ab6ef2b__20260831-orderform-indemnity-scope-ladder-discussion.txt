Soojin / Sara — thanks. I reviewed GreenPath's annotated language and mapped it against our standard MSA. Below are legal's recommendations and a suggested compromise redline.

Key points from counsel:
- Redwood can accept a defend-and-indemnify obligation for third-party IP claims that arise solely from (i) outputs generated by Redwood's hosted models and (ii) Customer uses that are within the documented API/SDK (i.e., not modified by Customer post-processing that changes the output substantially).
- We cannot accept an unqualified obligation where Customer fine-tunes models on their own infra or where Customer injects training data that would cause the output to infringe. Those should be carveouts.
- Notice, cooperation, and control of defence: Redwood asks for the right to assume control of defence where Redwood agrees to defend, subject to Customer's reasonable participation.
- Cap: propose a per-claim cap equal to the total amount paid under the order form in the prior 12 months OR USD 1,000,000, whichever is higher. This balances exposure and commercial reality.
- Insurance offset and escrow: we can allow (i) insurance proceeds to be offset against Redwood's obligation and (ii) an escrow trigger for remediations for cases involving training data (but escrow terms need separate SOW).

Suggested redline language (drafted as high-level bullet points here; I'll produce clean redline PDF if you want):
- Redwood will defend and indemnify Customer from third-party claims alleging that Customer's use of model outputs (as delivered by Redwood's hosted API) directly infringes a third party's intellectual property rights, provided that such infringement is not caused by Customer-supplied training data, Customer-driven model fine-tuning on Customer infrastructure, or Customer post-processing that materially alters the output.
- Customer shall provide notice within 30 days of receiving a claim, and Redwood shall have the right to assume sole control of the defence, provided Redwood does not settle any claim that admits fault without Customer's written consent for settlements that materially affect Customer rights.
- Redwood's indemnity obligation for any single claim shall not exceed the greater of (a) the fees paid by Customer under the applicable order form in the twelve months preceding the claim and (b) USD 1,000,000.
- Insurance proceeds recoverable by Redwood may be applied against Redwood's indemnity obligations; an escrow mechanism may be agreed for issues relating to Customer-supplied training data.

Risks/asks:
- If GreenPath requires defence for derivative outputs created by Customer fine-tuning on Redwood-managed fine-tuning service, we will need to add additional safeguards (review of training data or a separate indemnity carveout).
- Kimberly (legal) asks that procurement confirm whether GreenPath will accept the per-claim cap language vs. an absolute monetary cap equal to the order value.

If helpful I'll push a redline PDF tonight and propose the language for the order form that references the MSA bucket and the per-claim cap.
