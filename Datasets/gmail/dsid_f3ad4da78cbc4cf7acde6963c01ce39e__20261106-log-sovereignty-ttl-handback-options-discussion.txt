Morgan — adding the infra specifics: 

1) Region and copies
- Primary storage is tenant-region; ephemeral processing copies are wiped within 24 hours and never included in retention counts. For EU tenants we keep everything inside EU regions (no US egress) unless customer enables cross-region exports.

2) Retention levels
- Hosted: 90 days default (rolling window), snapshots available on request for 6–12 months for compliance reviews.
- Dedicated: configurable, commonly 1y/3y options; bespoke terms for >3y.

3) Export mechanics
- Formats: JSONL (indexable), CSV (for summary reports), Parquet (columnar, compressed).
- Delivery: S3 presigned, SFTP with key exchange, or multipart transfer to your provided bucket. For live streaming we support rsyslog/CEF over TLS.
- Encryption: AES-256 envelope encryption with customer-provided KMS for Dedicated; hosted uses Redwood KMS but we can use customer KMS for Private deployments.

4) Legal hold & deletion
- If a legal hold is active, we will not purge data; exports will reflect legal hold status.
- Post-export deletion: we provide a manifest (object ids + checksums) and a tombstone file. For Dedicated/private we can provide a signed deletion attestation (PKI-signed) stating the erase completed on X timestamp. Hosted customers can request an attestation; delivery depends on the support tier.

5) Timeline & throughput
- Small slices (<50GB): typical turnaround 2 business days.
- Medium (50GB–5TB): 3–10 business days depending on slice complexity and retention range.
- Large (>5TB): custom schedule.
