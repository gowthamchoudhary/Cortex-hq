Team —

I put together a short proposal for introducing two small changes to our comp band methodology that we discussed in sync last week:

1) Experience Adjusters — a set of +/- percentage adjustments to base bands that capture mid-career vs early-career value for the same leveling title (to reduce offer friction for candidates whose scope trends slightly off the canonical band). Proposed buckets: Early (0–3 yrs): -3%, Core (4–8 yrs): 0%, Senior (9+ yrs): +6%.

2) Country-level uplift model — a lightweight multiplier applied to the anchor pay by country, calibrated to local 50th/75th percentile benchmarks and cost-of-living index. I've attached the model and a percentile analysis from PeopleBench we licensed. The goal is to minimize manual per-offer adjustments while preserving local competitiveness.

Key asks: please review assumptions (sheet: geo_uplift_model.xlsx) and the percentile tables (salary_percentile_analysis.pdf). If we agree, I want to pilot this on SDE IC offers for EMEA and LATAM hires for the next quarter (hiring ramp: +12 headcount).

Notes on design decisions in the spreadsheet:
- Anchors stay at current level midpoint; experience adjusters modify offer target before finalizing cash comps.
- Uplift multipliers are capped at +/- 18% from US anchor to avoid outliers.
- We preserve final SLAs for exec approval on any offer > 15% over target.

Would like sign-off by end of day Friday so Talent ops can bake into offer templates.
