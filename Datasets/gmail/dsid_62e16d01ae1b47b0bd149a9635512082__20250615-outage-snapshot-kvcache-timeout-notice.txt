Alice / NovaProd support team,

I wanted to share a quick incident snapshot: starting ~08:45 PT we observed intermittent 503 responses and elevated p99 generation latency for the hosted inference endpoints backed by the us-west GPU pool. Impact is concentrated on models that keep large KV caches (conversational sessions with long histories) and appears as timeouts during KV lookups.

What we've done so far:
- Fault isolation: routed a portion of traffic to a fallback model variant without KV prefetching (reduced error rate for affected customers).
- Short-term mitigation: disabled aggressive KV prefetch on the us-west routing tier to reduce cache churn.
- Monitoring: enabled additional debug logs for KV manager to capture eviction sequences (attached a small sample for context).

Immediate customer-facing actions:
- Status page: incident "US-WEST: intermittent 503s and elevated p99" is live (link below).
- We are providing a rolling update to affected tenants and have prioritized NovaProd per the SLA.

Next steps (we'll keep this thread updated):
- Ops will continue to collect traces and determine root cause (KV index corruption vs. orchestration race).
- We'll follow with a more detailed timeline and a postmortem doc within 72 hours.

Apologies for the disruption — I'll be monitoring and will update as we learn more.

Short ops update and next-capture: we ran a read-only KV index integrity sweep on the affected nodes at 12:30 PT and found several index entries with inconsistent offsets after a scheduled compaction job — that appears to have caused stale pointers and transient KV lookup timeouts.

What we did:
- Disabled compaction window for the KV tier to prevent additional index rewrites (immediate stabilization).
- Deployed a targeted patch that skips in-flight compaction when active sessions are reading the same prefix.
- Continued traffic routing to fallback variants while we validate.

Impact for NovaProd: we have their workspace in the affected cohort but the fallback route preserved availability. Quality impact should be minimal; we'll run a short validation test and report back to Alice in the next 90 minutes.

Follow-up items:
- Postmortem doc (internal draft) will be available under the incident link; public-facing postmortem planned once we finish validation and confirm mitigations.
- Recommend offering NovaProd a brief credits/SL concession depending on the window of degraded throughput — happy to coordinate with sales/legal if you want to make the call.
