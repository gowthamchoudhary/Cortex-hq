Hi Sam — great questions, short answers below and an attached onboarding form.

Summary (preferred order):
- Preferred: Vendor Portal upload (SFTP/secure web UI) — it guarantees faster ACKs, better PO matching, and structured ingestion.
- Fallback: email to billing@redwood-invoices.com — acceptable for early-stage vendors while portal access is being set up. Flagging emails with a subject token helps automation (see below).

Multi-PO invoices:
- If you can, provide a CSV export with one row per PO line plus one combined PDF with supporting attachments. Our ingest prefers the CSV as the canonical data for line-level matching. Example CSV headers we require: vendor_id, invoice_number, invoice_date, po_number, item_description, qty, unit_price, line_total, tax_amount, currency, redwood_entity
- If a multi-PO PDF is all you can send immediately, include a short manifest CSV (even two columns: invoice_number, po_numbers_comma_separated) in the same upload.

Tax / VAT handling:
- Include vendor_tax_id and buyer_tax_id fields when applicable. For EU vendors, please populate VAT_rate and VAT_amount per line. We will not accept ambiguous tax fields — please use the headers above.

Filename & subject conventions (important for email fallback):
- Email subject: Redwood Invoices — [VendorID] [InvoiceNumber] [PrimaryPO] (e.g. "Redwood Invoices - APEX123 2028-0045 PO-7890")
- Attachment names: invoice_{invoice_number}.pdf, lines_{invoice_number}.csv

Attachments: vendor_onboarding_form.pdf (attached; please sign and return), sample_invoice.csv (attached sample row).
