Hi Ava — thanks for the questionnaire and the clear asks. Looping in Priya (compliance) and Irene (forensics/ops) to ensure complete responses.

Quick summary to start (detailed items and attachments follow):
- Default retention: ephemeral inference/debug traces are retained for 30 days by default. This covers per-request debug payloads and ephemeral KV cache snapshots unless a customer has a pinned retention config.
- Backups/snapshots: system backups and longer-term telemetry are retained up to 90 days internally, with sanitized summaries preserved for 365 days for service analytics only.
- Proof-of-delete: we can provide a deletion log export which contains metadata rows for deleted objects (object id, deletion timestamp, deletion reason) and an HMAC-SHA256 integrity signature covering the export. See attached initial-data-retention-summary.pdf for format details and a redacted example.
- Exceptions: customer-specific hold requests are processed via our security intake and legal review. If an exception is approved we create a named legal hold that prevents purge, store audit evidence in our secure attestations bucket, and provide a signed attestation and forensic export on request.
