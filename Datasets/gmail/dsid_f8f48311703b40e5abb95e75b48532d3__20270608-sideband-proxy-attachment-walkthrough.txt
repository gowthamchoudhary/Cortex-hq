Alex / team —

Quick technical notes ahead of the call: 

1) ASN plan: our control plane currently uses ASN 64512 (private) for staging and 65010 for production announced space. For the attachment, we can peer with a temporary private ASN and then handover to 65010 when you confirm transit advertising. Sample neighbor config is attached (sideband-bgp-sample.txt).

2) Health checks: we can change the probe to TCP 443/8443 and expose a lightweight /health endpoint on the proxy. I will add a flag to the runbook to toggle UDP probing if you open UDP 33434 later.

3) Firewall matrix: we intend to limit inbound-only control-plane connections from your uplink CIDR; egress for the proxies will be TLS to the hosted telemetry endpoints. I included a short matrix in the diagram notes.

4) Rollback: our playbook includes an immediate withdrawal path to static routes + BGP session flap if needed — documented in step 9 of the checklist.

Please confirm if you need MTU adjustments on the control-plane VLANs (we typically use 1500, but can support jumbo frames if the colo requires 9000).
