Adding a quick technical readout to support decisioning. TL;DR: Dedicated (us-east) for initial rollout; Private for EU PHI as follow-on.

Key tradeoffs (short):
- Hosted: fastest onramp but noisy neighbors and regional guarantees (best for open data/non-PHI pilot).
- Dedicated: single-tenant GPU pool, predictable p99 latencies, supports autoscale/bursting; recommended for US production where throughput matters.
- Private (VPC / on-prem): required if EU clients insist on customer-managed network/KMS and air-gapped controls; higher cost and longer lead time (~6–8 weeks to provision depending on infra).

Technical asks for Acuity to confirm:
1) Expected steady-state qps and 95/99 latency targets.
2) Whether EU traffic must be segmented at time-zero or can be phased (hybrid routing).

Operational note: If we commit to Dedicated, we can provide a temporary routing policy to send EU traffic to hosted EU region until Private is ready; that reduces go-live friction.
