- We see a pattern where client-side retries (3x immediate retry, no jitter) amplify the load 2–3x during the burst window.

3) Apply a quick server-side circuit-breaker rule that rejects repeated rapid retries (after 3 attempts in 30s) with 429 + Retry-After to prevent amplification.
