Appreciate the clear list. Quick recap and our proposed next steps:

1) Artifacts we will share by EOD 4/21 (so you have time before 4/22):
   - Latest SOC 2 Type II report + scope summary (redacted where necessary)
   - Our standard BAA template (Grace will attach a version we typically sign)
   - Network diagram for Hosted, Dedicated, and Private VPC options
   - KMS/HSM integration doc and encryption at-rest/in-transit summary
   - Audit log schema + retention policy (we support configurable retention; default 3 years, can extend to 6 on Dedicated/Private)
   - Penetration test summary and our cadence

2) Hosted vs Dedicated vs Private
   - Hosted + BAA: often sufficient when customers want fast time-to-value and agree to data residency + BAA restrictions. Hosted can be pinned to US regions and supports per-tenant data partitioning, but default retention and some admin controls are limited vs Dedicated.
   - Dedicated (reserved GPU pool): gives stronger isolation and longer retention options; recommended for high-volume PHI workloads. Onboarding estimate: 6–8 weeks after contract execution.
   - Private VPC/on‑prem: full network control and ideal for regulated workloads that require customer-managed keys or air-gapped deployments. Onboarding depends on customer infra readiness; typical 8–14 weeks.
