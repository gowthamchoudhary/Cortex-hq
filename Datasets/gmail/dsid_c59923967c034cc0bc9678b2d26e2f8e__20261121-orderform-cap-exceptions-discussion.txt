Team — draft language and negotiation points below. I kept the clauses short so sales can present them without heavy legalese. Also attached a short Word doc with redline-ready language (Draft_Liability_Clauses_Examples_20261119.docx).

A) Core principle: direct damages = amounts paid to restore service or reasonably necessary remediation costs; indirect damages = lost profits, loss of data, loss of goodwill, and third-party claims for those forms of loss. Keep indirect damages excluded, with narrow carve-outs.

B) Two negotiation options to propose to Acme:
1) Option 1 — Narrow carve-outs (recommended):
  - Limitation of Liability: Except for liability arising from (i) willful misconduct or gross negligence, (ii) breach of the Parties' confidentiality obligations resulting in unauthorized disclosure of Customer Data, (iii) infringement of a third party's intellectual property, or (iv) payments due hereunder, Service Provider's aggregate liability for all claims arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of (A) $500,000 or (B) 6 months of fees paid under the applicable Order Form.
  - Consequential/Indirect Damages: Neither party will be liable to the other for indirect, special, incidental or consequential damages, including lost profits, loss of data or loss of goodwill, except that lost profits may be recoverable only where such losses are the direct result of Provider's willful breach of the SLA and are proven with reasonable particularity.
  - Data-loss carve-out: In the event of a Service Provider data loss caused by provider negligence resulting in material customer losses, the parties agree that the cap for such claim shall be increased to the greater of $1,000,000 or 12 months of fees (limited to claims directly attributable to data restoration and third-party notification costs).
2) Option 2 — Graded caps (if Acme pushes):
  - Aggregate cap remains 1x fees for most claims, but for breaches involving (i) unauthorized access to Customer Data or (ii) IP injunctions, the cap increases to 2x fees with a $1,000,000 floor.
  - Lost profits remain excluded except where (a) Provider's conduct is willful and (b) lost profits are demonstrably tied to a discrete outage event of >= 24 hours.

C) Negotiation tips for Vanessa:
  - Lead with Option 1. It is easier to concede a limited data-loss uplift than to create a general exception for lost profits.
  - If Acme insists on lost profits as a broader exception, require a narrow definition (willful breach + proximate causation + proof of quantum) and push for a separate sub-cap (e.g., $250k / 6 months fees).
  - Avoid per-claim caps that exceed aggregate caps — prefer aggregate with narrow higher-capped categories.

D) Example short clause you can paste into an email to Acme:
  "Notwithstanding the foregoing, Provider's liability for claims directly arising from unauthorized access to Customer Data resulting from Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct shall not exceed the greater of (i) $1,000,000 or (ii) twelve (12) months of fees paid under the applicable Order Form; however, all other claims will be subject to the general limitation of liability set forth above."

Let me know if you want the language tightened to dollar-only or fee-multiple only.
