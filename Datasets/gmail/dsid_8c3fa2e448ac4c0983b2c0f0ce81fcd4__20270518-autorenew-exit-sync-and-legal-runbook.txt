Priya / Karthik — I reviewed Apex's redline and sketched language that should address their commercial concerns while protecting Redwood's exposure. Key points below, attached as MSA_term_clause_suggestion.docx.

A) Termination for Convenience (TFC) — suggested approach:
- Keep TFC but narrow its scope: apply only to pre-production pilots or contracts < $50k TCV. For enterprise deals, replace TFC with a "Termination for Convenience — Limited" clause: 60 days' prior written notice, Redwood to provide 30 days' transition support, and Customer receives a prorated refund for any pre-paid unused service fees.
- Add explicit limitations on consequential damages tied to termination events and a cap equal to fees paid in the 12 months preceding termination.

B) Auto-Renewal / Notice Window:
- Accept 60-day prior written notice to opt-out of auto-renew. To operationalize, require that notice be sent to billing@redwood.com and include Order Form ID. Redwood will feed this into billing so effective cancellation dates align with billing cycle to avoid mid-cycle prorations where possible.

C) Order Form timing mismatch:
- Commercial should confirm whether the Term start date is the date of signature or the service activation date. We prefer activation date. Karthik to adjust Order Form so Term aligns with activation to avoid accidental short initial terms.

D) Compliance / Data Escape:
- Add a short schedule obligating Redwood to provide data export in standard formats and a 30-day retention window for retrieval post-termination. Any additional assisted export work to be scoped as professional services.
