Emma / team — taking the technical lead on this. Below is a suggested credit calculus and fallback policy phrased for inclusion in an MSA/SOW exhibit or order form annex. This is intentionally conservative to simplify procurement review.

Suggested credit schedule (example):
- Measurement window: rolling 30-day window; availability measured at the regional ingress point.
- Degraded-mode definition: any period where p95 latency > 3x baseline p95 or error rate increases > 1% above baseline.

Credit tiers (per billing period):
- Tier A: 0–2 hours cumulative degraded-mode in window: 0% credit (operational advisory)
- Tier B: >2–24 hours cumulative: 10% of monthly service fees for affected SKU(s)
- Tier C: >24–72 hours cumulative: 25% of monthly service fees for affected SKU(s)
- Tier D: >72 hours cumulative: 50% of monthly service fees for affected SKU(s)

Notes on % affected traffic:
- Credits prorated by percent of affected traffic (e.g., if only 30% of customer requests were impacted, credit = tier credit * 0.3).

Caps and exclusions:
- Maximum credit cap: 50% of monthly service fee for a single billing period.
- Exclusions: force majeure, customer misconfiguration, or incidents caused by customer-provided models-in-kernel (if applicable).
- Cascading incidents: credits calculated on a per-incident basis; multiple incidents in a single billing period are additive up to the maximum cap.

Regional fallback language (short form for order form):
"Customer acknowledges Redwood may, in the event of a regional service disruption, route requests to an alternate region to maintain availability. Requests routed to alternate regions will be subject to the data handling and residency terms set forth in Exhibit X. Customer may elect to opt out of cross-region routing for specific SKUs by identifying such SKUs on the Order Form. In the event Customer opts out, Redwood will provide commercially reasonable efforts to restore regional capacity; such opt-out may affect SLA eligibility for the affected SKUs."

Data residency option flow:
- Order Form checkbox to opt out of cross-region fallback for defined SKUs
- If opted-out, reduced credit eligibility for cross-region incidents unless explicit written consent is provided to route traffic

I can draft more formal MSA language if you want.
