Hi Sarah — thanks for the clear summary and the PO template, very helpful. High level answers below; I put a short proposed approach at the end to keep procurement moving.

1) PO number and line mapping: Yes — we can include the PO number in the invoice header and also emit line-level references (we call them PO_line_ref) that map back to your SKU/line ids. That will satisfy three-way match processes.

2) Monthly consolidated invoices: We can produce one consolidated invoice per month that includes a breakdown of each campus/project as discrete line items. Each line can include your project code and PO_line_ref.

3) Net terms: Our standard commercial terms are net45. For EduCo we are prepared to accept net60 under these conditions: (a) the order form references the committed annual spend tier that triggers the negotiated discount, (b) we include an early-payment credit clause (1% credit if paid within 15 days) to offset extended terms, and (c) the first two invoices will be subject to a short onboarding verification step to ensure mapping correctness.

4) Tax-exempt handling: We will mark the account as tax-exempt once we receive your certificate; invoices will then show zero-tax lines for eligible items.
