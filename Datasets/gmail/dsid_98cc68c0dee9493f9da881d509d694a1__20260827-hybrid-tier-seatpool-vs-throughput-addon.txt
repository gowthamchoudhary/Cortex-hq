Hi Simon — thanks for the usage file and the clear framing. Quick summary below plus two concrete pricing pathways; happy to jump on a 30-min call to walk through.

Executive summary:
- Seat pooling (hybrid tier): better for organizations that need predictable concurrency and internal seat reallocation. It buys administrator-level control over who consumes seats but usually has a higher base monthly commitment.
- Throughput add-on: cheaper for purely bursty token volume where concurrency per-user is low; good if bursts are short and you can tolerate per-token billing variance.

Packaging scenarios (high level):
1) Hybrid Tier + Seat Pooling
   - Commit: 12 months = list $14k/mo; 12-mo discount 15% => $11.9k/mo; 24 months => additional 5% (20% total) => $11.2k/mo
   - Includes: 24/7 infra SLAs at reserved capacity, prefix KV caching enabled by default, dedicated quota for pooled seats.
   - Upgrade/downgrade: can scale pool up/down once per quarter; mid-term step-down allowed with 60 days notice and prorated credit.
   - Pilot: 6-week pilot at 50% of list for first 2 seats (applies to orgs new to seat pooling) — available once.

2) Throughput Tier + Burstable Add-on
   - Commit: base throughput list $9k/mo; add-on blocks of 1M tokens = $400/block.
   - Flex discount: 12 months 12% (negotiable), 24 months 18%
   - Upgrade/downgrade: instant scaling, billed on usage. No step-down credits for consumed add-ons.

Key operational notes:
- Rollback/fallback: we can configure automated fallback to a compatible smaller model variant if you hit capacity; for seat pooling we recommend a 2-week canary before full rollout.
- Billing cadence: seat pools are invoiced monthly on committed rate; add-ons are invoiced monthly on consumption.
- Legal: pricing above 15% off list requires approval from Sales Leadership (looping Ben on this thread).
