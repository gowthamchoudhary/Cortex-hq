Proposal (July 1–31, 2027):

This is an opt-in amendment; if you prefer to keep 99.9 we can discuss dedicated capacity options (pricing change).

Credit ladder (monthly invoice credit if monthly availability for the calendar month falls below the stated threshold):
- Monthly availability < 99.0% : credit = 10% of that month’s Redwood invoice
- 99.0% ≤ Monthly availability < 99.5% : credit = 5% of that month’s Redwood invoice
- 99.5% ≤ Monthly availability < 99.9% : credit = 2% of that month’s Redwood invoice
- Monthly availability ≥ 99.9% : no credit

Additional terms:
- Credit cap for the amendment: 50% of one month’s invoice total across all lines (we can discuss lowering this depending on procurement needs).
- Maintenance windows and scheduled platform upgrades remain carveouts and will be excluded from availability calculations (we’ll provide a 72-hr notice window).
