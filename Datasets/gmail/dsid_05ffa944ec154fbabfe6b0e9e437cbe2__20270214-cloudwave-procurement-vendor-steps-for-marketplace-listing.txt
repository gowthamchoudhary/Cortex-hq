Hi Mary / Mina — thanks for the context. From Finance / Procurement side here's the checklist we follow and approximate SLAs: 

Checklist (what CloudWave should provide):
- Completed Redwood_Vendor_Intake_Form_US.pdf (signed)
- Signed PO (matching legal entity & billing address) — send to procurement@redwood.com
- W-9 or equivalent tax document (if US entity) or W-8BEN-E if non-US
- Bank wiring instructions (for initial set-up) or remit-to address on invoice
- If there is a marketplace commission arrangement, include the revenue share schedule in the SOW or PO notes

SLA & process notes:
- PO receipt -> AP validation: 1–3 business days (depends on completeness).
- AP validation -> PO acknowledgement (if required): same day to 2 business days.
- PO acknowledged -> MarketplaceOps schedules listing activation: typically 5–10 business days.

DocuSign: We prefer a DocuSign for agreements that include signature blocks. For pure PO-only flows the signed PO plus vendor intake form is sufficient. If CloudWave prefers DocuSign, we can send a DocuSign packet after intake form review (let us know).
