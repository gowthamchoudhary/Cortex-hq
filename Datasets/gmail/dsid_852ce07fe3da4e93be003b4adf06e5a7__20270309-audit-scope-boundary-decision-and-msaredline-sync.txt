Thanks Ben — reviewed HealthCo's redlines. Drafted a counterproposal for the security exhibit that keeps their intent while protecting Redwood's operational constraints. Key points in the counterproposal (security_exhibit_counterproposal.docx attached):

- SOC 2 / attestations: we will provide an annual SOC 2 Type II report and a quarterly summary attestation that confirms (a) no material changes to SOC 2 controls and (b) no unresolved critical findings. Language avoids guaranteeing third-party audited reports quarterly and states the summary is an internal management attestation.

- Pen testing: accept 2 external tests per 12-month period subject to a mutually agreed notice window (we propose 30 days) and staging environment access only. We propose an obligation to remediate critical findings within 30 days and retest within the next window; for findings that require code-level fixes, timeline will be measured from patch availability. We added a clause that prevents external exploit of live production systems without prior coordination.

- Audit rights & evidence: propose remote-first evidence collection and sample-based inspection. Add a 20 business day standard SLA for routine requests, with a process to escalate to 10 business days where there is an agreed business need. We included a short decision-log template to capture any deviations (so legal can approve exceptions).
