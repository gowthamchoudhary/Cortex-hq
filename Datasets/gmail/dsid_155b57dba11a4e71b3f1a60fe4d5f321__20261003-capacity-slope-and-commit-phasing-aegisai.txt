   - Cluster: 16 x A100-equivalent GPUs (single region, cross-availability failover)
   - Min guarantee: 12 GPUs reserved
   - Expected steady throughput: ~380 tokens/s
   - Ramp: 2 months to 80%, 4 months to 100% (month-by-month activation: 40% / 40% then 20% after capacity validation)
   - Notes: KV/prefix caching enabled, recommended 6-hour daily maintenance warmup window to refresh caches after release cycles.

3) Aggressive Pathway — optimized for growth and burst protection
   - Cluster: 32 x A100-equivalent GPUs (multi-az)
   - Min guarantee: 24 GPUs reserved
   - Expected steady throughput: ~820 tokens/s
   - Ramp: 1 month to 70%, 3 months to 100% (30% / 40% / 30%)
   - Notes: Dedicated warm pool and priority routing, includes SLA credits for sustained latency breaches beyond 3 outages per quarter.

Common items across pathways:
- Model variants: recommend starting on a quantized 4-bit variant for balanced and aggressive paths to lower unit cost; lean pathway starts on 8-bit mixed precision.
- Caching: enable automatic prefix/KV caching for user-session reuse — we estimate a 15–25% token cost reduction.
- Autoscale guardrails: set burst caps per-minute to protect regional capacity during bot sweeps.
- Onboarding milestones: integration test (week 1), traffic validation (weeks 2–4), scale validation (weeks 5–12).

Leah — quick technical clarifications to map expected latency and activation windows:

- Warm pool sizing: for the Balanced pathway we recommend a 20% warm pool (i.e., keep ~3 GPUs ready per 16-GPU cluster to keep KV caches hot); warm pool can be scheduled to spin up 1 hour before expected daily traffic peaks.
- Cold-start penalty: worst-case p95 cold-cache penalty measured on similar workloads is ~300–400ms additional tail; with warm pool and prefix caching we push p95 under 180ms in our tests.
- Prewarming approach: staggered prewarm across AZs to avoid simultaneous capacity spikes; prewarm scripts are run via the deployment playbook — we can provide automation hooks to Aegis's CI so prewarm occurs after a model rollout.
- Quantization risk: 4-bit quantization reduces token cost ~30% but some prompt-sensitive evals saw small regressions on hallucination; recommend a phased roll where Balanced starts on 8-bit and migrates to 4-bit after evaluation pass.
