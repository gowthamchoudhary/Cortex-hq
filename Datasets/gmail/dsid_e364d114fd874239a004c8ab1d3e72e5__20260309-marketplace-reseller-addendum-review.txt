Marissa — quick legal read below (inline/high level).

1) Pass-through billing: we can accept language that allows CloudMart to collect marketplace fees and pass them through, provided: (a) Redwood is not liable for CloudMart collection failures; (b) invoices to end customers remain subject to Redwood's billing disputes/credits; and (c) Marketplace fee is clearly identified as a separate line item. I propose adding an express hold-harmless for Redwood on collection and a mechanism for disputed charges.

2) Indemnity: CloudMart's draft expands indemnity to third-party marketplace claims and removes caps. Not acceptable. We should: keep Redwood's existing indemnity tied to Redwood's acts/omissions, exclude marketplace platform fees, and retain our monetary cap (e.g., liability capped to fees paid in prior 12 months). If they insist on additional marketplace carveouts, we can offer a narrowly tailored indemnity for direct marketplace integration bugs with a small incremental cap.

3) Audit clause: their draft allows broad, frequent audits. Counter: limit to 1 audit per 12 months, reasonable notice (30 days), and scoped to billing/fees related to the marketplace flow. No right to remote access to production systems.

4) Data/security: ensure the addendum references our standard DPA and that CloudMart's marketplace obligations do not expand data access beyond the DPA.

I will prepare a redline of their doc with suggested language and a short memo explaining each change. Target to you by Tue EOD.

Jordan / CloudMart team — thanks for the redline. Attached is Redwood's proposed counter with track changes: Redwood_Proposed_Marketplace_Addendum_redline.docx. High level summary of our positions:

1) Marketplace fee / pass-through: we accept a pass-through model if the marketplace fee is shown as a separate invoice line item and CloudMart accepts collection responsibility. Redwood must be held harmless for CloudMart's failures to collect; disputes over passed-through charges will be resolved between CloudMart and the end customer with an agreed dispute remediation process and a 30-day reconciliation window.

2) Indemnity & liability: we cannot accept their expanded indemnity. Redwood will keep standard indemnity tied to Redwood's acts/omissions and retain our existing liability cap (fees paid in prior 12 months). We are willing to negotiate a narrowly scoped indemnity for direct marketplace integration defects with a small incremental cap, but not a blanket pass-through for third-party fees.

3) Audit rights: limited to once per 12 months, reasonable notice (30 days), scope limited to billing and settlement records related to marketplace pass-through. No production system access and no customer-impacting on-site activities without mutual agreement.

4) Effective date / onboarding: propose signature -> 60 days to go-live.
