Hi Oliver — thanks for the clear list. Quick summary up front, and I'll attach artifacts and loop in engineering for any follow-ups: 

- Residency: Redwood supports region-specific hosting for hosted API customers; for EU we operate in eu-west-1 (Ireland) and eu-central-1 (Frankfurt). When you select EU, inference and primary storage remain in those regions.

Hi Oliver / Lina,

Thanks again for the questions and for sending the SQ. Below is a consolidated set of answers you can attach to your questionnaire; I've also attached the documents again (SOC2 summary and a regional dataflow diagram). If you need a signed attestation we can produce a short one for procurement — let me know the preferred recipient and format.

1) Data residency (hosted API):
- Regions: EU customers may elect eu-west-1 (Ireland) or eu-central-1 (Frankfurt). When EU is selected, customer inference requests, model execution, and all primary customer storage (request/response payloads, reconstructions, embeddings) are processed and stored only in the selected EU region.
- Cross-region routing: control plane metadata (non-customer-identifiable, e.g., availability signals) may traverse our control plane which is logically separated; no customer payloads are sent to non-EU regions unless specifically configured.

2) Default retention & configurable options:
- Default: request/response payloads and inference logs: 30 days. Embeddings: 90 days. Access logs and audit trails: 90 days by default.
- Configurable: Enterprise customers can request shorter retention windows (e.g., 7 or 14 days) via contractual controls. Retention profiles are configurable at account-level or per-route via Dedicated/Private product tiers for stricter controls.

3) Deletion flow and timelines:
- Initiation: customer submits a deletion request via the Console or the Security/Support contact.
- Primary storage: records are deleted/marked for purge within 72 hours and are no longer accessible.
- Caches/KV: any in-memory caches or ephemeral KV caches are invalidated immediately; materialized caches are purged on next rotation (generally within hours).
- Backups/DR: backups are on a 90-day rotation window; deleted items are made unrecoverable after the next backup window and the standard overwrite/rotation period (up to 90 days).
- Audit & evidence: we provide an operational confirmation once deletion completes and include the deletion job id and timestamps.

4) Backups & disaster recovery:
- Backups are encrypted at rest with customer data keys (KMS) and retained for up to 90 days per standard DR policy in EU regions. We can include backup retention exclusions as a contractual item for enterprise SLAs.
