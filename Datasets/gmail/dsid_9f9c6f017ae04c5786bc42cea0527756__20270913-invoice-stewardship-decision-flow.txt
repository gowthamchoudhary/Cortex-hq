Two practical options we typically offer:
1) API/XML push: Redwood pushes an invoice XML per legal entity to your collector URL/SFTP. This preserves per-entity accounting and includes VAT fields. We can deliver an aggregated monthly summary PDF to a single AP inbox as a convenience.
2) Email PDF fallback: If API onboarding will take >2 weeks, we send per-entity PDFs to ap-team@verdanthealth.com. We use invoicing@redwood.ai as the sender and sign with our remit and VAT fields.

Markus / Lauren — quick technical note from the integrations side:

- If Verdant can provide an HTTPS POST endpoint with basic auth and accept our v1 XML schema, we can deliver per-entity invoice XMLs within 3 business days of receiving creds/sample.
- SFTP is also supported, but onboarding to SFTP usually adds 2-3 business days for key exchange and directory mapping.
- For VAT: include taxId and countryCode in the XML element <taxId>. We will populate reverseChargeIndicator when we detect intra-EU reverse charge rules, but Verdant must confirm entity jurisdictions.
- Sender address will be invoices@redwood.ai. We recommend whitelisting invoices@redwood.ai and billing-ops@redwood.ai as notification copies.

Operational recommendation: configure the API push as the primary channel and set up an automated fallback that sends the PDF to ap-team@verdanthealth.com if delivery fails (retry window 24h). For the consolidated summary, we can produce a single CSV with invoice numbers, amounts, and bill-to entity — that should meet AP's reconciliation needs without changing legal invoice issuance.

I'll prepare the technical onboarding checklist and sample payload. Tagging items for Lauren: can you capture Verdant's entity list + VAT numbers and confirm which documents they require on the PDF?
