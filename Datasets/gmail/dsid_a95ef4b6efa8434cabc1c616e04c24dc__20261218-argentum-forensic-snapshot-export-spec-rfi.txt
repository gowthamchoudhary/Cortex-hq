Thanks for the clear ask. I copied Marissa (our solutions lead) to confirm throughput/timeline estimates and Laura (security) for details on tamper-evidence and chain-of-custody. Below is a concise response we can formalize for your packet; happy to expand on any item.

Export formats and content:
- Available formats: NDJSON (newline-delimited JSON), Parquet (columnar), and gzipped CSV for smaller slices.
- Exportable records: per-request metadata (request_id, timestamp, model_name+version, prompt+response hashes), token counts, response latency, route/pool used, client API key (hashed), tenant_id. We do not export unredacted user PII by default unless requested under a preservation order; redaction tooling/LLM-based redaction is available as an add-on.

Retention windows:
- Default hosted retention: 90 days for request/response event payloads; 365 days for billing/usage aggregates.
- Dedicated/Private customers can request custom retention tiers (30d/90d/1y/7y) via contract.
- For regulated preservation we support immutable snapshots (see section below) and long-term archival exports to customer S3 or our secure transfer with SSE-KMS.

Forensic snapshot and tamper-evidence:
- Point-in-time snapshots: we can produce an immutable export representing a defined time range, terminated with a manifest file containing per-file SHA256 sums and an overall signed manifest.
- Signing: manifests are signed with Redwood's HSM-backed key; customers can request concurrent customer-side signing when handed off.
- Chain-of-custody package: export includes (1) manifest, (2) per-file hashes, (3) service logs of the export job (who initiated, job id, start/end times), and (4) a short statement of export integrity signed by our security team.

Timing and SLAs:
- Typical ad-hoc extraction (<= 100GB): 3–5 business days.
- Large-scale forensic exports (multi-TB): we provide project plan and estimate; 7–21 business days depending on scope and redaction needs.
- Rush/expedited: we can commit to 48–72 hours for certified customers under an escalation clause.

Attachments: I attached our export schema CSV and a sample manifest for your review.

Marissa/Ben,

Throughput/operational notes:
- 25GB/day compressed: our export job pipeline comfortably handles this as a background job. We typically observe ~2.5–3x compression on JSON audit events; network egress and multipart upload are the gating steps.
- Packaging: we chunk exports by day (YYYYMMDD) into gzipped NDJSON shards (~500MB compressed target) and produce a signed manifest per-day. This helps with resume and rehydration.
- Redaction: automated PII redaction (configurable) is an extra pipeline step; for preservation under legal hold we typically do a copy-without-redaction and then provide a separate redacted bundle on request. Redaction costs are size+CPU dependent — estimate 1–2 business days additional per 100GB.
- Delivery options: S3 push (preferred), rsync over TLS to customer appliance, or encrypted hard-drive courier for TB+ volumes. Estimated export job run times: 25GB/day -> 24–36 hours end-to-end including packaging and signing.
