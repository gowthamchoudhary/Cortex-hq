- Commitment: Redwood will anchor customer data for processing and primary storage within the EU region you select (EU-west-1 or equivalent) for the duration of the contract.
- Permitted transient windows: in the exhibit we propose a 72-hour maximum transient cross-region window for failover that requires customer notification within 1 hour of activation and a post-event report within 72 hours of restoration.

- KV cache snapshots/replication: our default behavior is ephemeral in-region KV caching. For burst scenarios we may instantiate short-lived replicas in a nearby region; these replicas are encrypted-at-rest and in-transit and are covered by the transient-window language above.
