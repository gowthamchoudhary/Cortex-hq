Looping in a concrete proposal and worked examples. Pedro — thanks for the clear asks. Below is a measurement methodology and a sample calculation. I kept the mechanics intentionally simple to make it auditable by both sides.

1) Measurement cadence and sustained definition
- Sample granularity: 60s (per-minute) tokens/sec as the primary metric (aligns with procurement preference). We record per-minute tokens/sec for every pool node and aggregate at pool-level.
- Sustained throughput (committed): defined as the 95th percentile of per-minute tokens/sec across all minutes in the 30-day measurement window. 

2) Spike credits (how they work)
- Monthly spike credit bucket is sized as: committed_tokens_per_second * 60 * allowance_minutes_per_month * spike_multiplier_factor
  - allowance_minutes_per_month default: 150 minutes (i.e., ~5 minutes per day)
  - spike_multiplier_factor default: 2.5 (allows short bursts up to 2.5x without immediate billing penalty)
- Consumption rule: when per-minute tokens/sec exceeds committed_tokens_per_second, the overage for that minute (minute_tokens - committed_tokens_per_second) * 1 minute is deducted from the bucket.
- Example: committed 250,000 tokens/sec -> bucket = 250,000 * 60 * 150 * 2.5 = 5,625,000,000 tokens for the month. If you have two 10-minute spikes to 600,000 tokens/sec, overage per minute = 350,000 tokens/sec -> per-minute overage = 350,000 * 60 = 21,000,000 tokens; for 20 minutes total -> 420,000,000 tokens consumed (7.5% of bucket).

3) Latency buckets and targets
- Buckets by prompt length: short (0–64 tokens), medium (65–512), long (>512).
- Targets we recommend for contract: short p95 <= 300ms, medium p95 <= 450ms, long p95 <= 800ms. Latency measured at application ingress after KV cache warm start.

4) Exclusions and noise handling
- Exclude measurement minutes from the sustained calculation for: documented deploy windows (up to 20 minutes/day), provider-initiated maintenance with 2-hour notice, and verified major incidents.
- Synthetic internal load (synthetic test traffic) will be marked and excluded; Redwood will provide a signed CSV of synthetic test windows for audit.

5) Sampling & auditability
- All raw per-minute samples retained in an immutable store for 90 days for both parties to audit. We’ll provide a monthly CSV export with: timestamp, pool_tokens/sec, aggregated_pool_minute, latency_p50, latency_p95, flags (deploy, synthetic, incident).

6) Billing and credits reconciliation
- If monthly sustained 95th percentile remains at or above committed_tokens_per_second -> no billing adjustment.
- If the 95th percentile falls below committed -> understand as surplus capacity (no negative credit).
- If bucket is exhausted and overages continue, standard overage rates (TBD in commercial sheet) apply per-million tokens.
