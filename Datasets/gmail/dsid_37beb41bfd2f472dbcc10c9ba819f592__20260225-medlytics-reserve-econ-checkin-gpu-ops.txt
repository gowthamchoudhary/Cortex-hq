Hi Amelia / Raj / Procurement —

Thanks for the note. Attached are two indicative reserved configurations (PDF + spec sheet attached). High-level numbers below — these are intentionally conservative (we assume 20% headroom for spikes).

Config A (H100-focused) — baseline to meet 95th pct without autoscale
- GPUs: 3 x H100 80GB (reserved pool)
- Estimated steady throughput: ~1.1B tokens/day per H100 (mixed-precision, quantized weights, batching for chat workloads)
- Effective steady-state coverage: ~3.3B tokens/day total
- Latency SLO: 95th < 380ms at <512 tokens
- Indicative list price: $9,750 / GPU / month (reserved) -> Total monthly: $29,250
- Indicative unit cost: ~$73 / 1M tokens (assuming 3.3B tokens/day * 30d = 99B tokens/month; amortized and includes infra + support uplift)

Config B (A100-focused) — cost-optimized band with more GPUs
- GPUs: 5 x A100 80GB (reserved pool)
- Estimated steady throughput: ~700M tokens/day per A100
- Effective steady-state coverage: ~3.5B tokens/day total
- Latency SLO: 95th ~420ms at <512 tokens
- Indicative list price: $5,900 / GPU / month -> Total monthly: $29,500
- Indicative unit cost: ~$84 / 1M tokens (higher due to lower per-GPU throughput and increased instance count, but more headroom for small concurrency spikes)

Notes and levers to tune unit economics:
- Autoscaling/burst: adding a small burst pool (2 GPUs) reduces reserve size but increases overage exposure.
- Model quantization: moving to 4-bit paths can raise throughput per GPU by ~20–30% and lower effective $/1M. We can pilot with your eval prompts to quantify.
- Longer term 24-mo commitments reduce per-GPU list prices by ~10–12% — we can show a 12 vs 24 mo price matrix if helpful.

- Confirm preference for H100 vs A100 (or a mixed pool).
- Procurement: preferred invoice cadence and term (net-30 / net-45).
- Can you share a short prompt sample set so engineering can run perf on our dev rig and validate the tokens/sec numbers?
