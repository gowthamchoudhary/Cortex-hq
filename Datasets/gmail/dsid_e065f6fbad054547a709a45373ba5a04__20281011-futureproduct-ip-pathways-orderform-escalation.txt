Suggested clause (for insertion into Order Form):
"Customer Content and Outputs: Customer retains all right, title and interest in and to Customer Content and Outputs. Redwood may collect usage data and aggregate or de-identify Customer Content for internal analytics and Service improvement. Redwood will not use Customer Content or Outputs to train models for commercial resale or third-party productization without the Customer's prior written consent, which may be provided on a per-program or one-time basis as agreed in writing.

Limited Feedback License: Customer grants Redwood a non-exclusive, royalty-free, revocable license to use feedback, anonymized logs, and aggregated telemetry solely to operate, maintain and improve the Redwood Service. This license expressly excludes the right to use such materials to create models intended for sale or distribution to third parties.

Practical notes: 
- We propose the opt-in be implemented as a signed Addendum or checkbox during onboarding for customers that want training-enabled programs; that avoids ad-hoc approvals while giving customers clear control.
