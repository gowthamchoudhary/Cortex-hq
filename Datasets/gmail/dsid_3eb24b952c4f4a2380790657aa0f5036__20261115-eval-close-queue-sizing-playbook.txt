Option A — Conservative (lower up-front spend, faster canary):

- Lead time (Dedicated): 12 weeks from signed PO to reserved tranche activation (region permitting).
- Private eventual target: 24x H100-equivalent (for production headroom), hardware + integration 20–24 weeks after order; recommend we plan the Private activation for 8–10 weeks after canary (allows acceptance gating).
- Fallback if tranche 2 delayed >8 weeks: enable cross-region Dedicated burst pool (additional 6x H100-e) + temporary adaptive rate-limiting and conservative model quantization (q8) to reduce tokens/sec cost. This option preserves p95 for core traffic but will raise p99 during extreme bursts.

Option B — Aggressive (reduce risk of capacity shortfall):
- Canary tranche (Dedicated Reserved): 16x H100-e; handles bursts up to ~400–450 qps with conservative batching.
- Lead time (Dedicated): 14 weeks to guarantee earliest tranche.
- Private eventual target: 32x H100-e; hardware + integration 22–26 weeks.
- Fallback: keep a small hot standby (8x H100-e in a second region) billed as short-term burst capacity; legal / procurement should note the extra commit.

- Quantization: start evals with bf16 / q8 to be conservative; for production we recommend q4f16 for most models once quality checks pass — that reduces cost-per-token materially but requires a validation gate in the MAP.
