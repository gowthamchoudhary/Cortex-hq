Kim / team — posting a short technical follow-up here so we have a single thread for Novacorp.

Summary (TL;DR): temporary token-queue pressure in the US-Central serving lane caused elevated p95. Root cause appears to be a small spike of short, high-concurrency requests that bypassed prefix cache hits (edge pattern), resulting in sustained KV cache misses and transient backlog. The serving layer then routed a fraction of traffic to US-West as our failover policy triggered.

Why it happened:
- A burst of short (<200ms) requests with high parallel fanout hit a common prefix set, causing the KV cache to experience higher miss rates; this amplified token decoding cost per request.
- Autoscale reacted but with a short lag; during that lag p95 rose and a small number of requests were rate-limited returning 503s.

Mitigations applied (already):
- Immediate fallback routing to US-West reduced the user-visible tail.
- Warmed hot prefixes and adjusted the prefetch window for the affected shard.
- Tuned the local request-queue admission threshold to prioritize shorter requests for a short period.

Planned follow-ups (we will share these in the post-incident note):
1) Permanent prefetch window increase for US-Central hot shards to reduce miss amplification.
2) Batching policy tweak to avoid admission storms for very short requests.
3) Updated runbook step to proactively route low-latency traffic during sustained miss spikes.

Relevant links: runbook excerpt (attached), internal runbook: https://redwood-internal.atlassian.net/l/cp/US-CENTRAL-QUEUE-RUNBOOK

Response to Alex: the fallback preserves request semantics — there is no change to session tokens or model variant assignment. You should not see client-visible context loss. If you use any strict geo-affinity assumptions we can discuss a pin/exception. On guardrails: short-term we recommend conservative client retry/backoff (exponential jitter starting at 200ms) and a short client-side circuit-breaker if error rate >2% sustained for >1 minute. We’ll include these in the advisory.

I’ll publish a concise post-incident note to status and share the link once ready (target: within 4 hours).
