Thanks — answered inline below and I pasted a few sample rows into your CSV. High level: we are in active pilot now and expect sustained growth but want to validate committed capacity first.

- Peak concurrent sessions: p50 ~320, p95 ~1,200 (short spikes up to ~1.6k during big campaigns)
- Typical prompt length: 40 tokens; typical response: 220 tokens (median responses are ~180, heavy responses up to 800 tokens for long-form summaries)

- Daily volume: currently ~1.2M requests/day, expected 2.5–3x in 3 months as pilot expands. Burst windows: concentrated 08:30–10:30 PST and Friday 14:00–16:00 PST during promos.
- Heavy batch jobs: nightly rerank and embedding refresh (00:00–03:00 PST), approx 100k batched items/night that reuse context.

Jonas / team / Claire —

Quick preliminary sizing notes (fast pass):

Summary hypothesis: for current baseline (1.2M req/day, p95 conc 1.2k) with mixed chat + stateless we recommend a pilot of ~8 x H100-equivalent GPUs with an initial oversubscription of 3.5x for stateless and 1.25x reserved capacity for chat-heavy nodes (KV/cache pinned). That yields headroom for p95 latency targets at 350ms for interactive and ~200ms for premium for short responses assuming model is a 7B-13B open variant with 8-bit quantization.

Phase plan:
- Phase 1 (pilot, months 0–2): 8 GPUs (split 6 stateless oversubscribed, 2 stateful pinned), autoscale burst to 12 GPUs for marketing spikes; include prefix cache for common prompts to reduce token compute.
- Phase 2 (month 3 growth): increase to 20 GPUs (re-evaluate oversub ratios), or shift to dedicated 16-bit fleet if accuracy vs quantization shows regression.

Notes & assumptions:
- Average request token compute: 260 tokens per request -> estimated token-rate compute used for throughput.
- We assume batching windows of ~50ms for stateless to improve throughput without hurting 200–350ms p95.
- Nightly batch jobs should be scheduled on separate queue to avoid queuing impact on interactive traffic. They can reuse cached KV to lower replication cost.
- Estimated cost: rough committed (N. California): pilot ~$28k/month (reserved + baseline infra), scale ~$72k/month (depends on SKUs & network). Will refine with exact SKUs.

Attached: preliminary sizer workbook (zelos_prelim_sizer.xlsx) with per-GPU throughput numbers and latency sims.
