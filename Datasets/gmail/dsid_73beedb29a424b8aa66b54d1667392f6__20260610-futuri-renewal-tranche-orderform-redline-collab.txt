Karthik / team — reviewed Futuri's markup. High-level legal position and suggested language: 

1) Term / break: We can agree to a 24-month term only if the performance break is narrow. Recommend: 24 months with a 12-month review right tied to an objective SLA metric (e.g., P99 latency and error-rate thresholds). The right to terminate at 12 months should require (a) 2 consecutive months of SLA breaches and (b) a 30-day cure period.

2) SLA credits: Do not accept uncapped credits. Recommend cap at 30% of the monthly fee over a rolling 3-month window. We will propose incremental escalation (first breach: credits only; repeated breaches: credits + remediation plan).

3) Data retention / audit logs: We cannot expand retention to 3 years by default. Offer: we will export and deliver full audit logs on request; for on-platform retention beyond 12 months, offer a paid archiving service or limited export agreement that meets regulatory needs. Provide clause and runbook appendix.

4) Assignment: reasonable restriction with carve for mergers/acquisitions where assignment is allowed with notice.
