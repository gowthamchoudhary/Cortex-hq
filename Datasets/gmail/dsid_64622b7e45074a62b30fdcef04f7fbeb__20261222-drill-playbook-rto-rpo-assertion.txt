- Long-term artifact restore (S3 snapshot -> index rebuild): RPO = 24h; full index rebuild varies 00:45:00–02:00:00 depending on index size (notes present in the CSV).

- KV cache warm-up: automated pre-warm available and recommended for enterprise tenants; if not enabled, on-demand warm-up is used (which lengthens verification).
