Thanks Emma — that's super helpful. Quick answers to your streaming vs batching question, plus what I'll include in the proposal: 

Streaming (low-latency chat):
- UX: best for token-by-token user feedback (typing experience).
- Cost: higher per-token cost when holding long-lived streams, and less opportunity to batch across users.
- SLO: easier to meet tight p95 (200ms) for short responses when routed to low-latency tier.

Short-poll + micro-batching (chat with lightweight batching):
- UX: small added latency (50–150ms) if we batch short windows (e.g., 50–200ms micro-batches).
- Cost: significantly lower per-token cost because requests can be batched across users and KV caching helps for repeated prefixes.
- SLO: can still meet effective p95 targets if configured carefully (micro-batch size and frequency tuned to traffic patterns).

Recommendation for pilot given your budget target ($15k/mo pilot, goal $4k–$6k/1M at commit):
- Option 1 (fast path): Hosted streaming for the chat endpoint with aggressive prefix caching and a 200ms p95 routing policy for critical flows. Good for validating UX quickly; expect higher pilot unit cost.
- Option 2 (cost path): Dedicated small pod + micro-batching (50–150ms windows) for chat + KV cache for common prompts. Slightly more engineering lift but better long-run unit economics and easier to reach $4k–$6k/1M with committed volume.

I'll draft both options in the proposal and include a projected unit-econ table for the 200–400M tokens band. Can we lock Mar 25, 10:00 AM PT for the review?
