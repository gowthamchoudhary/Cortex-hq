Adding a couple of technical constraints for the playbook before the meeting:
- KMS/HSM: if BrightLine requires customer-managed keys for token encryption at rest, we need the KMS key ARN and an AM role to allow Redwood to encrypt/decrypt. This affects the deployment timeline by ~3 business days.
- SCIM: recommend incremental sync first (users created by HR) and a separate group-sync job for entitlement groups. Please confirm if you want full replace-mode for groups.
- Service accounts: we need a contact owner per SA and a rotation schedule for credentials. If you want us to provision SAs in your VPC (private deployment) vs on-hosted, call that out in advance.

Also: for PCI/HIPAA customers, we ensure audit logging for provisioning events — we'll show the logs path during the kickoff.
