For a rough estimate (subject to exact traffic): pilot reserved pool of 4 H100s → estimated 14M tokens/day capacity headroom (burstable) — we'll send a precise committed token worksheet once we get the 24h trace.

GPU tradeoffs (summary)
- H100: best for high concurrency + long contexts. Lower p95 at high batch sizes and with fp8 optimizations; best candidate to hit 150 ms p95 at 512 tokens under 60 concurrency.
- A100-80GB: steady performer, slightly higher p95 at the same concurrency but more predictable across model variants. Good fallback pair with H100.
- L4: significantly cheaper but optimized for smaller models/shorter contexts; not recommended if you must hit <150 ms p95 for 512 tokens at 60 concurrency.

Our recommended starting config for the pilot
- Reserve: 4×H100 in your dedicated pool (we'll also run an A100 comparison run).
- Objective: measure end-to-end p50/95/99 for 512-token exchanges, streaming latency (first-byte and tail), and sustained token/sec at 140 req/sec burst.
- Fallback/autoscale policy: configure an A100-backed burst group if we see sustained queueing > 200ms per token.
