Sofia — quick answers from the finance/solutions side: 

1) Invoice naming & PO matching: AWS Marketplace invoices are issued by AWS and show the marketplace vendor identifier; many customers still automate with a second-step mapping in their AR system. If ClearAudit's AR automation cannot map AWS-issued invoices, then choose direct invoicing. We can also provide a short reconciliation file each billing month to help match POs to usage.

2) Line-item descriptions: For Private Offers, line-item text is relatively fixed at creation, but we can append an explanatory reconciliation file or include metadata in the invoice attachments. Direct contracts allow us to include customized line items and cost centers on the invoice itself.

3) Payment terms: For an initial 12-month commit we can offer Net45 on direct invoicing for deals in the RDW-2000 series; Net30 is standard for hosted API consumption without precommit. If you need Net60 we can route to RevOps and tie it to a signed SOW + credit approval.

Operational note: If you plan to use AWS co-sell benefits, list acceptance can be a gating step. We can submit the Private Offer for co-sell enablement concurrently but partner registration + acceptance may take an additional 7–10 business days.
