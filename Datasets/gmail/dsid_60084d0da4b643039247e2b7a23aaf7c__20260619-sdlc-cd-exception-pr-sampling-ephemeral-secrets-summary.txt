Daniel / team,

On ephemeral secrets and rotation: high-level summary plus a redacted excerpt is below. Full automation runs in our secrets platform and integrates with our KMS provider; HSM-backed keys are used for signing where required.

Key points: 
- Ephemeral token TTL: default 1 hour for CI runners, 15 minutes for build-agent ephemeral credentials.
- KMS rotation: asymmetric keys for signing rotate annually; symmetric data-encryption keys rotate quarterly. Rotation events are recorded with timestamp, key-id, rotate_method, and actor.
- Automated rotations are orchestrated by the ops-rotation service; manual rotations require two approvers in the oncall roster.

Excerpt (redacted):

"2026-05-06T14:07:09Z, token-ephem-PR, automated, ci-token-issuer, ephemeral-ttl=3600s"

Retention and verification: rotation logs are hashed and the hash is stored in an append-only ledger for 365 days. We can provide a hash verification step as part of the assessor checklist.
