Hi Lisa — thanks, and great to hear the pilot went well. Quick answers below and I attached a lightweight breakout spreadsheet (platform-fee-breakdown.xlsx). Happy to walk through on a 30‑minute call if helpful.

1) License buckets (short):
- Staging: 1 operator license + 1 node license (small footprint)
- Prod‑1 (primary): 2 operator licenses + 3 node licenses (resiliency and KV caching)
- Prod‑2 (DR/replica): 1 operator license + 1 node license
Notes: operator licenses cover SRE/ops access and management console seats; node licenses map to GPU groups and are what we meter for throughput. We can provide a detailed SKU export if you want specific node counts by GPU type.

2) Platform fee split (amortization examples in attached sheet):
- Conservative scenario: platform fee amortized monthly across sites; operator licenses billed up front, node usage metered monthly. Example: $90k platform fee for 12 months + $36k operator licenses (one-time) + metered node usage.
- Aggressive (discounted anchor): 20% platform fee credit for a 12‑month pilot-to-production commit; we rebate % of platform fee as consumption credits during year 1.

3) SLA uplift bands (standard):
- Bronze: 8x5 support, 4‑hour response (no uplift)
- Silver: 24x7 triage, 1‑hour response for P1 (uplift = +12% of platform fee)
- Gold: 24x7 with 15‑minute paging, oncall rotation included (uplift = +28% of platform fee or fixed $12k/mo for smaller deployments)

Key asks: confirm whether you want fixed uplift (easier for procurement) or % uplift (easier for finance forecasting).

Callouts: attached platform-fee-breakdown.xlsx shows amortization and a sample concession path (pilot credits).

If agreeable, I can draft a short mutual action plan and a DocuSign SOW with the two scenarios.
