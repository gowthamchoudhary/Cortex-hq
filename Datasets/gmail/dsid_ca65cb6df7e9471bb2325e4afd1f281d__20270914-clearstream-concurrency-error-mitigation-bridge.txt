What happened (short):
- A combination of an unexpected client-side burst and a model-variant kernel hot path caused KV miss amplification and increased synchronous calls to the primary model replica pool.
- Result: p95 increased to ~1.8s, error rate peaked ~3.8%.

- Scoped fallback routing to model-v2-small for heavy checkout prefixes (reduced token pressure)
- Temporarily scaled dedicated pool +3 instances and adjusted autoscale overrides
- Applied aggressive KV prefix caching for stable prefixes and short rate shaping for noisy app-ids

Outcome (verified by midnight run):
- Error rate reduced by ~72% vs peak (to ~1.1%) within first 30 minutes
- p95 latency improved from 1.8s -> 630ms for the checkout route
