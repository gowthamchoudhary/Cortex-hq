Priya / team — quick summary and mitigation. Root cause (preliminary): during the Mar 6 rollout we updated a per-customer policy that governs token-bucket refill windows for the Lumina routing key. The window value was accidentally set to 1s (intended for microburst handling on other workloads) instead of 10s. That caused the bucket to be drained rapidly under their heavy short-lived spikes and the policy’s pacing logic to trigger aggressive suppression, producing sustained 429s and elevated tail latency under the morning QPS pattern.

What I’m doing now (in-flight):
- Reverted their routing policy to the previous stable policy (policy-id: rwp-lumina-03). Command run at 16:37 PT:
  `redwoodctl policy apply --id rwp-lumina-03 --env prod --confirm`
- Disabled dynamic pacing for the Lumina route while we monitor:
  `redwoodctl route update --route lumina-prod --disable-dynamic-pacing`
- Created a hotfix patch (rate_policy_diff.patch) and pushed KPI snapshot to the shared drive (KPI-snapshot-morning-peak.csv).
- Opened JIRA INF-4821 to track postmortem and configuration tests.

Immediate observable impact: 429 rate dropped from ~7.3% to ~0.2% within 8 minutes of the revert; p95 latency returned to baseline after ~12 minutes. I’m leaving the revert in place and will run a controlled A/B test tomorrow morning with the corrected window=10s value in a canary.

Next steps & ETA:
- Monitoring for full business-hour stability: 30–45 minutes.
- Canary for corrected window + automated test coverage: by EOD tomorrow.
- Postmortem draft for execs with root cause, timeline, and mitigations: T+48h.
