Jordan/Kim — quick feasibility notes from infra: 

- Primary snapshots/backups: automated purge pipeline can mark snapshots for deletion and remove them from all active regions within 24–72 hours after the retention job runs. The 45-day window is achievable.
- Replicated backups (cross-region DR): most replicas are retained by our cloud DR partner for a maximum of 90 days in their default configuration. We can set customer-specific retention to 45 days, but it requires: (a) a change request to the DR partner on a per-tenant basis; (b) addition to our subprocessor matrix and contract with the partner to honor the retention on our behalf.
- Emergency purge: we have a workflow that triggers a scoped purge and generates a signed verification package (time-stamped logs + hash manifest). Producing the verification report typically takes 2 business days after the purge completes. We need access to account-scoped audit indexes to compile the manifest.

Team — I put together a short draft amendment that maps to Orbis's 45-day purge ask and preserves the standard legal-hold carve-out. Key points: 

- Addendum A (Backup Purge): Redwood will purge customer backups and replicated snapshots containing Customer Data within forty-five (45) days of deletion instructions, unless an applicable legal or regulatory hold is identified.
- Verification: Redwood will provide a signed verification report outlining the purge actions, including snapshot identifiers and hashes, within two (2) business days of purge completion.
- Subprocessors: Redwood will list the DR partner (NexusDR) and cloud provider (ArcCloud EU) in the subprocessor matrix and confirm contractual obligation to honor the 45-day retention for OrbisHealth's tenancy.
- Exceptions & Escalation: In the event of a cross-border copy hosted in an adequacy-country alternative location, Redwood will notify Customer and either (i) initiate transfer mechanisms that comply with the DPA, or (ii) perform targeted purge if transfer is not required.
