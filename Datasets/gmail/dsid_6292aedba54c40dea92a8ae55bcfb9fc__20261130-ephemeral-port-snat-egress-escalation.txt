- Trace samples attached: redwood-connection-traces-20261129.json (traces include conn-id and timestamp).

Preliminary hypothesis:
- Probable SNAT / egress port exhaustion on FinTract's transit path causing TCP resets on initial handshake or during keepalive. That triggers client retries which amplify traffic, exhausting worker queue capacity and producing 429s on our side.
- We also see a small subset of asymmetric TCP sessions where return path appears via a different node (packet-level TTL differences). That can increase perceived connection failure.

Immediate mitigations we can apply from our side (low-friction, quick):
- Short-term rate-limiter rules for FinTract to cap concurrent active requests (can be enabled in ~5 minutes for a soft window).
- Failover routing: divert 15% of traffic to an alternate region/pool with warmed workers to absorb retry load.
- Increase per-connection timeouts on workers temporarily to tolerate transient resets.

Suggested asks of FinTract network team:
- Increase SNAT port pool or add additional NAT IPs.
- Check transit GW connection tracking table and NAT idle timeouts (reduce short idle timeouts that cause port churn).

Subject: Post-bridge notes & follow-ups — FinTract incident (INF-2142)

Follow-up after bridge and mitigation window (summary):

What happened (high level):
- Root cause: FinTract SNAT ephemeral port exhaustion + aggressive NAT idle timeout led to midhandshake resets and asymmetric returns. That caused a retry amplification pattern from their app tier which overloaded a portion of our warmed worker pool and produced elevated 5xx/429.

Actions taken (what we executed):
- Enabled a 15% diversion of FinTract traffic to an alternate warmed pool (reduced hot-path load by ~40%).
- Enabled a conservative per-tenant concurrency cap and a short-term rate limiter (5 minutes warm window).
- Increased worker-side initial handshake timeout from 3s -> 6s to reduce false-positive timeouts.
- Created Jira: INF-2142 to track end-to-end fixes and postmortem. Link: https://redwood.atlassian.net/browse/INF-2142

Actions for FinTract (requested):
- Add additional NAT IPs / enlarge SNAT port capacity (they confirmed in- flight).
- Increase NAT connection-tracking table size and raise idle timeout to avoid unnecessary churn.
- Audit firewall/IPS for any midstream TCP resets.

- FinTract infra: confirm NAT capacity changes and share metrics (alex@fintract).
