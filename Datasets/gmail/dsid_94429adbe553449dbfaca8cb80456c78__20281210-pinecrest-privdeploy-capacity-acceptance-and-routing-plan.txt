Caroline / team —

I ran a quick simulation using your load profile and the model latency curves Karthik provided. Highlights:
- For sustained 1200 qps (avg seq 64) with 25% headroom we see median GPU utilization ~72% across 12 A100-80GB equiv nodes using batch size targeting ~64 requests. p95 latency projected at ~110-125ms depending on exact kernel mix and KV cache hit-rate.
- If the KV cache cold-start rate is high (e.g., first-minute storm), p95 spikes to ~220ms; recommend a short warmup window (60–120s) and a staged ramp.
- Burst policy suggestion: allow a 2x burst for up to 3 minutes with automated ephemeral scaling to +4 nodes, with a throttling policy that degrades to a quantized 4-bit model at 2.5x sustained qps.

- Pre-warm KV cache with prefix subset (we can provide warmup probes).
