Tom — quick procurement answers from the pricing desk:

- Prorated credits: only applied to the billing month in which the Dedicated commit is activated. We don’t backdate reservation pricing to prior billing cycles, but we will credit any excess billed at burst rates in the same invoice month. If you want a retroactive adjustment, we can discuss a one-time rebate as part of the commercial terms (usually tied to an annual commit).

- Smoothing: burst tiers evaluate at the monthly granularity but our billing system supports optional per-hour smoothing windows (configurable). If you expect short spikes from autoscaling jobs, we recommend enabling the 1-hour smoothing window to avoid band churn.

- Multipliers: multipliers apply to the net price after any evaluation discount or promotional credits. In plain terms: billed_burst_rate = (post-discount unit rate) * multiplier. If you want multipliers keyed to the list rate instead, we can annotate that in the SOW.
