Thanks for the context. Technical constraints and proposal below:

Constraints:
- The embed-legacy endpoint uses an older tokenization path; keeping it live at scale adds operational risk for memory fragmentation on our pool. A global long-term exemption isn't safe.
- We can, however, support a short, customer-scoped pinned variant (v1-compat) routed only for Acme traffic for up to 6 weeks with monitoring and an escape hatch.

- Pinned routing to v1-compat for acme@customer-id for 4 weeks (extendable to 6 weeks with review).
- A daily availability/latency digest and token-level error logs (retained for 30 days).
- A rollback hatch: we will automatically route traffic to the closest compatible runtime if we detect >1.5x error rate or CPU pressure above threshold.
