Summary (concise):

1) Unit pricing (list / reference):
- Text generation (prompt tokens): $0.40 per 1K tokens (list)
- Text generation (completion tokens): $0.65 per 1K tokens (list)
- Embeddings: $3.00 per 1K vectors (per 1K embedding requests)
- Note: all list prices are for hosted API (shared capacity). Dedicated / Reserved pricing below.

2) Commit tiers & discount schedule (monthly commit basis unless annual committed):
- Starter: 5M tokens/mo (approx) — 5% discount off list
- Standard: 50M tokens/mo — 12% discount off list
- Enterprise: 500M tokens/mo — 22% discount off list
- Custom: >1B tokens/mo — negotiated (typical 25–35% off list depending on term/commit)
- Discounts apply to billable token usage up to the committed amount.

3) Overage and burst handling:
- Burst buffer: customers receive a built-in 30% monthly burst allowance above committed tokens where overages billed at 1.10x committed blended rate (not full list).
- True overage (beyond burst): billed at 1.25x list price for that token class.
- For Dedicated customers we offer higher burst (50%) by policy and lower overage multiplier (1.10x).

4) Consumption smoothing & rollover:
- Unused committed tokens can roll forward up to 3 months (FIFO) to smooth seasonal consumption.
- Optional smoothing: for customers that choose it, final monthly invoiced consumption = max(monthly actual, trailing-3-month average) for the purpose of avoiding sharp spikes; this must be elected up front and evaluated quarterly.

5) True-up cadence and credits:
- Standard true-up: annual true-up (on anniversary) where actual usage vs committed is reconciled.
- If usage < committed: credit issued against future invoices pro rata for the remainder of the contract year.
- If usage > committed: overage billed on next invoice; if >20% over the committed run-rate we recommend moving to next tier for remainder of term (sales/CS will outreach for amendment).

6) Reserved / Dedicated capacity:
- Dedicated capacity priced as a fixed monthly fee + per-token usage at a reduced rate. Typical effective discount of additional 10–25% on top of listed commit discounts depending on GPU footprint and term.
- Reserved contracts include SLA (see below) and ability to pin model versions.

7) Billing cadence & payment terms:
- Billing: monthly in arrears by default; annual prepay available for additional 3–5% discount.
- Standard payment terms: Net 30; enterprise Net 45 available upon credit approval.
- Accepted payment: bank transfer (ACH / wire), corporate card (up to agreed limit).

8) SLA credits (summary):
- Uptime target (Hosted API): 99.9% monthly for model endpoints.
- Credits: For outage windows beyond threshold, credits applied as a percentage of monthly invoice: 5% credit for 99.0–99.9%, 15% credit for 97.0–98.9%, 30% credit for <97.0%.
- Credits capped at 100% of the monthly fee attributable to the affected product.

9) Contractual and operational notes:
- Data residency and logging: Private deployments (VPC or on-prem) available for regulated workloads; pricing for Private includes setup fee and ongoing support fee.
- Cancellation: 60-day notice for annual committed contracts; early termination fees apply if commitment not met (pro rata of remaining committed value minus reasonable mitigation).

Thanks for the follow-ups. Below are the three requested concrete examples — we’ve also added them into the attached spreadsheet (RFI_Response_Table.xlsx) for direct paste into your packet.

Example 1 — Burst invoice math (50M commit, 70M actual, hosted pricing):
- Commit tier: 50M tokens (Standard) — effective committed blended rate for this example = $0.50 per 1K tokens (weighted prompt/completion).
- Burst allowance: 30% of 50M = 15M tokens treated as burst at 1.10x committed blended rate.
- Calculation:
  - Committed usage billed: 50M tokens at committed rate = (50,000 * $0.50) = $25,000
  - Burst usage billed: 15M tokens at 1.10x committed rate = (15,000 * $0.55) = $8,250
  - Remaining overage (actual 70M - commit 50M - burst 15M = 5M) billed at 1.25x list price. If list price is $0.65 per 1K for completion-heavy usage, 1.25x list = $0.8125 per 1K: (5,000 * $0.8125) = $4,062.50
  - Total month invoice = $25,000 + $8,250 + $4,062.50 = $37,312.50

Notes: the committed blended rate used above is illustrative; the appendix contains token-class breakout so we can produce exact composites per your expected prompt/completion mix.

Example 2 — Rollover detail:
- Rollover applies to billing months. Unused committed tokens from a given billing month can be applied to consumption in the following up-to-3 billing months (i.e., month N unused -> can be used in N+1, N+2, N+3). After 3 billing months unused units expire.

Example 3 — Smoothing election:
- Smoothing is an optional contract add-on (no one-time fee). Election requires a 3-month minimum observation period to build the trailing window; smoothing remains in effect for at least one quarter and is re-evaluated at each renewal. If smoothing materially increases cost predictability for Redwood (e.g., sustained >40% variance), we may propose an adjustment at renewal, but historically we’ve kept the feature complimentary for enterprise customers.
