Option A — Committed baseline + tiered overage (recommended for predictable spend)
- Committed: 900M tokens/mo at $850 /1M tokens (applies to committed units)
- Tiered overage: 0–25% over committed: +40% unit price; 25–100% over: +80% unit price; >100% over: negotiated emergency rate

Option B — Committed baseline + automatic soft-burst allowance (pilot-friendly)
- Committed: 900M tokens/mo at $900 /1M tokens
- Soft-burst buffer: automatic +25% for up to 72 continuous hours (no charge beyond buffer unless exceeded)
- If bursts exceed buffer, overage billed at +50% unit price for the exceeded tokens
- Grace run: one free soft-burst per calendar month for pilot period
