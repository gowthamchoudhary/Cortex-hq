Hi Neha / Elena — thanks for the clear recap. A few details from the Marketplace side and a sample mapping to help the teams calibrate.

Quick notes
- Marketplace standard fee for private offers varies based on offer type; for software/service private offers like this we typically see an ~10-15% fee/commission. For planning, use 12% as an approximate placeholder unless you opt into a special program.
- Marketplace supports both flat unit pricing and tiered pricing (volume breakpoints within the offer). We can encode price-per-1M-token equivalently as an itemized SKU.

Sample mappings (examples only)
1) Flat example
  - Marketplace list price: $685 / 1M tokens
  - Marketplace fee (12%): $82.20
  - Net payout to Redwood: $602.80 / 1M tokens
  - Note: if Acme needs the customer-facing invoice to be exactly $600 / 1M, we can set list price to $681.82 (rounded) — let us know if you need exact-match precision.

2) Tiered example (breakpoints at 25M / 100M monthly)
  - 0–25M: list $720 -> net ~ $633.6
  - 25M–100M: list $640 -> net ~ $563.2
  - This approach lets you protect the low-volume unit economics while rewarding scale.

Process & timeline
- If you provide final list price(s) and minimum commitment term, we can provision the private offer draft in the Marketplace quoting console within 3 business days and route for internal AWS approvals (total ~5–7 business days typical to publish).
- Required fields to generate offer: Customer legal entity, billing address, AWS Account ID (if they want automatic entitlement), PO/contract contact name + email. DocuSign is supported — if Acme prefers that route we can include the DocuSign envelope.

Attachments: attached an AWS PO / private-offer template for reference (aws_po_template.pdf).

Next steps (per your ask list)
- Neha: send 2 pricing options (flat & tiered) you’d like to test; we’ll draft private offer accordingly.
- Elena: confirm procurement contact details and whether you require the list price to equal $600 exactly on the customer invoice or if a small rounding is acceptable.
