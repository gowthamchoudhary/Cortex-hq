Alice —

Thanks for the clear brief and the worksheet. We can support a few constructs that balance Aurora's exposure while keeping unit economics predictable for both sides. High level options:

1) Tiered credit ladder (non-linear): small GPU index moves (0–7%) -> token rebate; medium (7–15%) -> partial invoice credit; >15% -> exec review for additional concession.

2) Phased activation + SKU swap: allow Aurora to phase activation across months and permit swapping to a compatible model family with a smaller performance delta. This reduces immediate GPU consumption risk.

3) Burst carve-outs: reserved pool plus a limited burst allowance priced at market for emergency throughput.

We need to align on approval thresholds. If the requested credit > 10% of the committed revenue in any quarter, that requires Finance + Sales Ops sign-off. >20% requires VP Sales + CFO ping.
