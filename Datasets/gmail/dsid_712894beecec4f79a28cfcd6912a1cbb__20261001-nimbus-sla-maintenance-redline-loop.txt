Team — quick legal/ops notes to incorporate into the counter: 

1) Maintenance definition: we should split maintenance into (a) \"Scheduled Maintenance\" (customer notified >=72 hours) and (b) \"Emergency Maintenance\" (apply only to critical fixes). Scheduled Maintenance should be excluded from uptime calculations; Emergency Maintenance may be excluded only if we can show necessity and an ops post-mortem.

2) Rolling 30-day credit model: Ops flags that rolling windows increase bookkeeping complexity and may lead to temporary negative balances on billing. If the customer insists, propose the rolling model with a cap (max credit per month = 100% of monthly service fee) and a clear reconciliation process.

3) Response times: define P1 as system unavailable for end users with workarounds for critical paths; P1 target = 1 hour for acknowledgement, P1 mitigation target = 4 hours for initial mitigation steps; P2 = 4 hour ack, 48 hour mitigation target. These are reasonable but we should mark them as \"business hours vs 24x7\" depending on their support tier.

I'll add redline snippets and send the consolidated PDF to Karthik.
