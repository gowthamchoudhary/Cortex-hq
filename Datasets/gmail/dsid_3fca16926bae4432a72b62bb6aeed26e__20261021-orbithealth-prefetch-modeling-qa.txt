Jordan / team —

Quick technical assumptions we used for the prefetched simulation (attached): prefetch-sim-results.csv

1) Caching model
- Prefix cache hit expected: 40% (per your note). We modeled low/med/high at 20/40/60%.
- When cache hit: KV lookup only, no full decode (reduces per-request tokens consumed by ~70% on avg).

2) Batching and latency tradeoffs
- Continuous batching window: 10–15ms at peak; we modeled 3 batching strategies: none, fixed 10ms, adaptive workload-aware batching.
- With adaptive batching and prefix cache, p95 latency simulated < 160ms for your payload distribution. We included a worst-case tail run.

3) Tokens per logical request (median/95th)
- Expected-case: request 180 tokens; response 620 tokens => 800 tokens total logical request.
- With prefix reuse (hit): incremental token issuance ~ 240 tokens (warm response reuse + short tail decoding).

4) Throughput assumptions for cost calc
- Peak QPS modeled: 220 sessions/min -> ~3.7 QPS sustained for peak minute; our sim scales traffic to 10k concurrent to stress test possibility and derive tokens/day.

Notes: the sim includes KV caching efficiencies and a conservative 15% headroom for rerenders/auxiliary calls. See attached CSV for three scenario curves.

Happy to walk through the CSV on the call.

Attached is the econ workbook: orbit_map_econ_v1.xlsx. Below is the 1-page summary you can paste into the MAP and procurement:

Summary (two scenarios):
- Expected MAP (prefetch + adaptive batching + 40% prefix hit):
  - Tokens/day (modeled): ~20.8M tokens/day (aggregated requests + responses after caching reductions)
  - Effective cost: $0.0115 per 1k tokens (after pilot discount, reserved commit guidance)
  - Monthly estimate (30d): ~$7,200

- Conservative MAP (20% prefix hit, minimal batching):
  - Tokens/day: ~32M tokens/day
  - Effective cost: $0.018 per 1k tokens
  - Monthly estimate (30d): ~$17,280

Key levers that move the needle:
- Prefix cache hit rate (most impactful): improving from 20% -> 60% reduces monthly spend by ~55% in our model.
- Batching strategy (adaptive vs none) reduces per-token run inefficiency—savings up to ~12% on token compute at peak.
- Reserved capacity commit (3–6 month) yields additional effective unit cost reduction (~15–25%) vs on-demand.

Notes & assumptions (in workbook): tokenization modeled on GPT-like tokenizer; average prompt/response sizes per Karthik's sim; infra overprovision of 20% to protect SLO during peaks. Workbook tabs: Inputs, Scenario-Curves, Sensitivity (cache/batch/discount), Pilot-Budget.

If procurement wants a line-item SOW for a 6-week pilot (including sim runs, instrumentation, and two rollback windows), we can draft the SOW this week.
