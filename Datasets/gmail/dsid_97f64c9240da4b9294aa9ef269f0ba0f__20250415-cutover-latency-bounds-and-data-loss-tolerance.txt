Jasmine — notes from infra perspective. TL;DR: we can offer a differentiated commitment with conditional exceptions and optional paid warm-standby for APAC.

Technical proposal (warm-standby architecture):
- Recommended RTO (planned cutover, warm standby with active replication): 15 minutes (time to detect + route convergence + model load/kv-priming). We can commit to 15m for US/EU if customer accepts the warm-standby configuration and health checks.
- Recommended RPO: 1–2 minutes for standard streaming workloads when prefix/KV caching is enabled; 0–30s for requests with explicit flush semantics (customers can call a flush endpoint during cutover to minimize exposure).
- Latency delta during cutover: expect a median (p50) increase of 30–80ms and p95 up to 300ms for user requests that are cross-region routed immediately after switchover. That assumes network pathing is healthy; if cross-region egress is congested, p95 could spike (we should call that out as a carveout).
- Observability commitments: we can provide route-level tracing that shows when traffic shifted and a per-route token-rate timeline. For acceptance tests we can provide a signed timeline (timestamped traces) and token accounting for the cutover window.

Carveouts and exceptions we should include in the contract language:
1) Network partitions between regions or major cloud provider outages — Redwood will provide best-effort failover but cannot commit to RTO/RPO bounds in full region-wide network partitions.
2) Sustained regional capacity exhaustion caused by third-party spike (DDoS) — we can commit to fallbacks but need a quota/fair-usage carveout.
3) Maintenance windows (scheduled) — standard SLAs do not apply during mutually agreed maintenance windows unless otherwise negotiated.

Commercial option: APAC warm-standby (hot replicants in APAC region) available as an add-on; adds roughly 12–18% to the monthly reserved capacity cost (see attached warm-standby-cost-estimate.pdf).

If procurement pushes for 30m/5m firm across all regions without carveouts, we should counter with a narrowed commitment and offer credits scaled to severity for exceptions we can reasonably guarantee. Happy to draft short contract clauses.
