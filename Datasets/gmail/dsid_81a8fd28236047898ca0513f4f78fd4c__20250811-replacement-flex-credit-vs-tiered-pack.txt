Hi Olivia — thanks for the clear ask and for the RFP excerpt. Quick summary of two distinct commercial approaches we commonly use for replace-and-onboard scenarios (tailored to the Aquarell profile in the attachment):

Option A — Price-match (simple, low-friction):
- We can match the incumbent's per-token band for a 6-month window, conditional on a 12-month minimum commitment (to keep unit economics sustainable).
- No up-front prepay required.
- We provide a one-time onboarding credit equal to 10% of estimated pilot spend to offset migration engineering effort.
- SLA: standard Dedicated SLA with 99.9% uptime for prod routes, pilot runs on reserved shared capacity.

Option B — Staged prepay + performance credits (deeper discount, higher commitment):
- 3-tier prepay: 3-month pilot prepay (credited), 6-month discounted prepay at 15% off list, followed by optional conversion to run-rate.
- Includes 20% onboarding professional services credit (applied to SOW for initial migration activities).
- Enhanced SLA for critical routes (99.95%) and a 48-hour rollback/fallback playbook included.

Technical notes / constraints:
- KV cache warm-up: for large embeddings/long sequences, we typically see a 12–48 hour warm window before steady per-token cost improves. If Aquarell needs immediate steady-state latency, we recommend a short prefetching plan as part of the pilot SOW.
- Egress: we do not charge separate egress for inference tokens but large artifact transfers (snapshots, model weights) during on-prem migrations can be billed under engineering time.
