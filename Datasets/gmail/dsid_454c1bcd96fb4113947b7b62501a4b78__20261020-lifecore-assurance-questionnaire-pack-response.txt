- Encryption & Network Controls: TLS 1.2+ (TLS 1.3 preferred) in transit. At rest, AES‑256 (GCM) or equivalent. Private deployments include VPC peering and isolated networks; Dedicated hosts provide tenant isolation and optional physically separated GPU pools.

Extended retention and real‑time auditing available as an add‑on. Audit logs include request trace IDs, request timestamps, routing decisions, and model variant IDs.

- Data residency/on‑prem: For customers with strict residency, Redwood Private (VPC or on‑prem) is available; we provide packaging and an operational runbook for onboarding.
