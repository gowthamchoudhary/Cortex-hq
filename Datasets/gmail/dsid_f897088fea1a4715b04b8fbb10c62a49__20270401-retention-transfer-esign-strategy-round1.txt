Priya / Alex —

I've reviewed BlueWave's redlines (thanks Alex). Below are counsel-level responses and proposed compromise language where applicable. I pasted short excerpts with suggested replacements to make review quicker.

1) Retention (Section 3.2) - BlueWave ask: conversational logs retention capped at 12 months.
- Our stance: Redwood can commit to a 12-month maximum retention for "Customer Content" where the customer elects that retention setting in account configuration for the enterprise plan. We cannot universally limit retention across all accounts because some customers pay for extended retention for compliance reasons.
- Proposed language: "Customer may configure retention for Customer Content up to a maximum of 12 months under this DPA. For customers who require longer retention, the parties may agree in writing to a separate retention schedule."

2) International transfers / SCCs - BlueWave ask: explicit reliance model and SCC wording.
- Our stance: Redwood operates under standard SCC module two (processor-to-processor) for EU transfers where applicable and will execute the EU-US Addendum where required. We will accept BlueWave's proposed SCC insert with minor edits to reflect our subprocessors list reference.

3) Subprocessor notice period - BlueWave ask: 30 days' prior notice and right to object for high-risk subprocessors.
- Our stance: Standard is 14 days. We can offer 30 days for this deal provided we retain an exception for emergency onboarding for security or critical operational patches, with notice to be provided no later than 72 hours after such onboarding.

4) Audit rights - BlueWave ask: external auditor, once per 18 months.
- Our stance: Acceptable if scoped, with Redwood's obligations limited to producing compliance documentation and accommodating a single on-site or remote assessment per 18-month period, subject to NDA and reasonable notice.

Operational question (from Alex thread): KV cache TTL
- Answer: KV cache is encrypted at rest and by default inherits retention from the model session. Customers can configure session retention via our console/APIs. We'll document the specific parameters for BlueWave.

Next steps:
- I'll prepare a redline response document with the suggested compromise language and highlight items requiring escalation.
- Priya/Monica: if we agree to the 30-day subproc notice with emergency carve, I'll include that as the counter in the response doc.
