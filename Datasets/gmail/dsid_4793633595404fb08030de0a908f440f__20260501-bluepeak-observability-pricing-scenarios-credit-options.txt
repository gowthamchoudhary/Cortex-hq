Appreciate the clear ask and the forecast spreadsheet. I put together a compact cost matrix and a couple of example invoice scenarios (attached): bluepeak_observability_cost_model_v1.xlsx and a short SOW draft for the pilot (pilot_sow_bluepeak.pdf). High-level summary below so procurement has something concise to review:

1) Retention tiers (hot/nearline split assumed 60/40):
   - 30 days: $0.022/GB-month (storage) + $0.005/GB egress/query estimate
   - 90 days: $0.017/GB-month + $0.004/GB egress
   - 365 days: $0.012/GB-month + $0.0035/GB egress
   Example: 200k GB/month at 90d ≈ $3,400/month storage (approx, see sheet for breakdown).

2) Tracing (we price on ingested spans + optional archival storage):
   - Base ingest: $0.00007 per span (first 5M spans/month at standard rate)
   - Volume discounts: 10% off at ≥10M spans, 20% off at ≥30M spans
   - Archive storage (90d) billed as per retention tiers above; query costs per-1000-queries shown in sheet.

3) Advanced dashboards:
   - Seat model: $65/user/month (manager seat = $95/user/month for advanced features)
   - Project flat: $1,200/project/month for up to 5 dashboards + shared templates
   Note: seats include per-seat query allowance; excess query egress billed per above.

4) Pilot credits and controls:
   - Standard pilot: $12k onboarding credit applied over first 3 months (pro rata against usage).
   - Alternative: 100% credit on retention storage up to 150k GB for month 1, then 50% month 2, 25% month 3.
   - Budget cap: we can set an invoice cap + alerting and automatic throttle to prevent overages; cap terms can be included in SOW.

5) Proration and scale-ups:
   - Usage increases are billed in the month observed; we can include mid-pilot true-up with a one-time adjustment to keep invoices predictable.
