Hi all —

Thanks. A few legal points we need to be explicit about so NeuWave's counsel and our risk team have a common frame:

1) "Right to defend" vs "indemnity payment trigger": we can accept a right-to-defend with language that limits Redwood's payment obligation to court-ordered damages or settlements that Redwood approves (we prefer a requirement that Redwood controls settlement decisions where Redwood is the defending party). NeuWave's ask for "no payment unless adjudicated" is close to acceptable, but we should add a short-circuit for material injunctive relief that is not reasonably avoidable.

2) Scope carveouts: we will propose explicit carveouts for (a) claims arising from customer-modified models, (b) claims alleging training-data provenance where the customer trained their own model, and (c) claims based solely on third-party datasets where neither party can show use in the deployed model.

3) Evidence custody: operationally, Redwood can store hot-tier logs for 180 days and export chain-of-custody bundles on request. We will require an inbound evidence request process tied to the notice procedure in the MSA (48-hour acknowledgement, 7-day staging window to produce initial artifacts). If NeuWave wants total operational ownership of custody we can support a handback model (Redwood stages, NeuWave exports to their environment).

4) Insurance/offset: propose language allowing Redwood to seek insurance offsets or contribution from third parties where applicable; also propose a cap consistent with the order form (we recommend $3M per claim for this customer profile).

I've included proposed redline excerpts (inline) in the attached v3. Ben — please confirm ops SLA and the technical artifact list we can produce (KV cache snapshots, prompt logs, generated outputs, request metadata).
