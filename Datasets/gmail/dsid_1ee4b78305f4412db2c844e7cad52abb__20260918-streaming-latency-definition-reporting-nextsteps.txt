Thanks — helpful numbers, Oliver. A few notes on how Redwood operationalizes measurement and a proposed set of SLA clauses you can expect in the draft: 

Measurement definitions (concrete):
- Ingress timestamp: when the Redwood edge (TLS terminated) acknowledges the full request headers.
- First-token (streaming): delta between ingress timestamp and time first chunk of model output leaves Redwood’s egress (first-byte).
- Full-response: delta between ingress timestamp and final-byte egress for that request.
- Percentiles computed on successful requests only (2xx application-level responses). Failed and timed-out requests are counted separately and factored into availability.

Common exclusions (we recommend):
- Scheduled maintenance windows with >=72 hours notice; maintenance windows are excluded from monthly SLA calculations.
- Client-induced delays (e.g., request retries that arrive after initial connection closed).
- Network blackholes or third-party CDN outages upstream of Redwood.
- Traffic anomalies attributable to customer misconfiguration or malicious activity.

Reporting cadence options (we can support all):
- Live dashboard (real-time) for engineers
- Weekly rolling digest (auto-email) with p50/p95/p99 and top latency contributors
- Monthly formal report (calendar month) used for credit calculations and procurement review

Suggested remedy language (draft excerpt we will expand):
- Monthly Service Credit = min(MaxCreditCap, base_credit_rate * number_of_breached_days), where breached day = day where calendar-month p95 > agreed threshold.
- Cap the credit at 10% of monthly invoice unless otherwise agreed.
- Credits applied after a coordination window (we require a 5-business-day validation window to review instrumentation vs customer-supplied traces).
