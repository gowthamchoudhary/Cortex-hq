Marissa — on retrieval / reranker details: initial recommendations below, with quick rationale. I'll include runnable configs as attachments.

Retrieval settings:
- Chunking: 1,000 token overlap / 3x chunk-per-doc target (to balance context coverage).
- Embedding model: use Redwood-embed-v2 (open checkpoint) for candidate generation.
- Candidate depth: run experiments at k=5, 10, 20. We should log grounding delta per k.

Reranker candidates:
- Option A (light): distilled-cross-encoder (small) — low latency, filters noisy candidates effectively for short factual queries.
- Option B (strong): cross-encoder-simcse (medium) — higher accuracy on long-context matching but adds ~80-120ms.
- Fallback policy: if latency > 700ms, use Option A and increase candidate depth to k=10.

Grounding measurement plan (quick):
- Auto-label: exact-match of cited span to golden answer (fast but brittle).
- Human review: sample 200 queries across high-priority buckets for hallucination labeling (we'll provide annotation template).
