3) Transfer impact addendum / Hosting fallback
- Position: We cannot commit to an onshore-only architecture across all workloads without a deployment commitment (Dedicated or Private) and associated fees.

- Position: We can accommodate KMS integrations (Bring Your Own Key) for Dedicated/Private customers; hosted API customers can use provider-managed KMS. We will add an exhibit describing the encryption options and evidence/attestation timelines.

- Position: Standard caps apply. Pacemed asked for a higher cap tied to severity of export incidents; we are open to a carveout discussion but require procurement approval (Ben).
