Quick technical summary (initial findings):
- Scope: requests routed to the hosted inference pool servicing Fleetly saw a sudden rise in KV-cache misses for long-prefix checkout prompts (related to recent prompt expansion on Fleetly side).
- Root cause (prelim): a pattern of longer-than-usual prefixes caused more frequent cache invalidations across instances, which amplified cross-instance KV-sync operations and increased per-token tail latency. That, combined with a collector bug that delayed garbage collection of per-request caches, resulted in request queues backing up and higher 5xx from timeouts.

Mitigations applied (09:05 PDT):
1) Enabled prefix-level cache warming for Fleetly routes (warm cached keys for hot prefixes).
2) Temporarily increased concurrency on the dedicated shard serving Fleetly (burst capacity + wavier routing).
3) Reduced batch timeout from 25ms -> 10ms for the checkout route to favor lower latency over throughput.

Initial impact: error-rate down from 6.2% -> 1.8% over 15m, p95 improved from >3s to ~450ms; throughput recovering but still ~10% below baseline.
