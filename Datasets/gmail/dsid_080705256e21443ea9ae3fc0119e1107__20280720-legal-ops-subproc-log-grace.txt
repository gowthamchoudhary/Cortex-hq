Thanks — a few operational notes from infra/security to make the proposal concrete:

- Backups: we distinguish between (a) nightly full snapshots (archival), (b) incremental backups (short-lived delta stores), and (c) immutable disaster-recovery copies. For EU customers we can ensure (a)+(b) are encrypted with EU-managed keys and purged automatically after 180 days. Immutable DR copies we propose to keep for up to 365 days but gated behind the exception process Kimberly described; those copies are stored in our EU region unless a customer explicitly requests cross-border replication.

- Logs: telemetry and debug logs contain varying PII density. Default retention is 90 days; for any forensic retention we will create a scoped retention record (ticket) that:
   * enumerates log buckets and fields retained,
   * lists retention start/end,
   * names the approval authority (Head of Security), and
   * ties to the MediMind request id.

- Subprocessors: the current list includes two analytics vendors and one backup vendor that host encrypted copies in our EU region. If MediMind requires that backups not leave the EU region at all, we can offer an EU-only subprocessor set (may affect DR SLAs).

- Evidence: we can provide the latest SOC2-type2 summary and audit artifacts under an NDA.
