Sergio — I recommend Option B with the following specifics (finance + CS + legal okayed the framework):

- Temporary burst allowance: Up to 2x committed capacity (measured as peak requests/sec aggregated over 5-minute windows) for the first 30 calendar days after pilot start.
- Billing for burst: First 30 days: flat onboarding overage rate of $0.0075 per token-equivalent for burst traffic (rather than standard overage rate). After day 30, any usage beyond committed capacity billed at standard overage rates according to the attached Redwood_Overage_Schedule.pdf.
- Throttling: We will not throttle automatically within the 2x allowance. If a burst exceeds 2x capacity for more than 10 consecutive minutes, infra will apply soft throttling and CS will notify the customer.
- Contract language (SOW clause suggestion):
  "During the onboarding period (first 30 days post-pilot start), Customer is permitted burst usage up to 2x the agreed committed capacity. Burst usage within this allowance will be billed at the onboarding overage rate of $0.0075 per token-equivalent. Usage beyond the 2x allowance may be subject to throttling and will be billed at standard overage rates per the Redwood Overages schedule."

- Rationale: provides procurement budget certainty, lets engineering ramp without immediate throttles, and protects our unit economics.
