Hi Emma — thanks for the note and glad to hear the demo landed well. Short answers up front, details below.

Options we commonly propose for pilots about to expire:
- Time-box extension (+14 days): flat fee covering reserved capacity for the extra window. No change to current per-token rates.
- Credit top-up: add a fixed credit bucket (e.g., $5k) you draw from; useful if you expect bursty test traffic.
- Short retainer + discounted rate: a 30-day retainer that guarantees capacity and a 10–15% discount on token pricing for the add-on period. Best if you need predictable throughput and plan to convert.
