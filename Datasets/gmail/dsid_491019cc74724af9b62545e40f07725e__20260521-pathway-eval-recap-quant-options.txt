Hi Sarah —

Thanks for the questions. Quick technical clarifications and an actionable plan below.

1) About the p95 numbers
- The AWQ p95 (380ms) came from a mixed run that included deliberately long/edge-case prompts in the adversarial slice (we wanted to stress quantized kernels). For typical production-length prompts (median 320 tokens in your sample) AWQ p95 tightened to ~340ms. So AWQ can meet sub-350ms p95 in many cases but has less headroom on outliers.

2) Golden set format
- CSV with columns: id, prompt_text, expected_triage_label, comment. De-identification is fine (remove names/PHI but keep phrasing). We’ll use this for regression and precision/recall calculations.

3) Private pilot + PHI
- We can run the pilot in your VPC using our Private deployment flow (customer-managed subnet + KMS). For pilots we often use a short-term Private environment or a dedicated Hosted environment with customer-supplied keys — we’ll surface exact options in a proposal. SOC 2 evidence and audit logs are standard; I’ll attach the SOC2 checklist link in the drive.

4) Quantization tradeoffs (AWQ vs GPTQ vs FP8) — practical notes from our recent tests:
- AWQ (int4-ish): strongest wins on nominal cost (2.5x token cost reduction vs FP16). Great for high-volume non-edge slices. Quality: small but measurable drop on brittle phrasing; hallucination delta ~ +1.8 per 1k prompts on our clinical slice. Latency: generally improved p50, p95 depends on kernel maturity.
- GPTQ (4-bit/8-bit variants): intermediate cost reduction (~1.6-2x), better fidelity than AWQ on edge cases (hallucination delta ~ +0.6 per 1k). Latency: slightly higher than AWQ but often still under FP16 p95 if optimized with grouped attention kernels.
- FP8 mixed-precision: near-FP16 fidelity, moderate cost savings (1.2-1.4x). Good compromise when small regressions are unacceptable.

Our recommendation for Pathway:
- Default serving: FP16 baseline for the critical triage path.
- High-volume, low-risk path (summarization non-decision flows): AWQ quantized to capture cost savings.
- For any prompts that hit your critical triage labels, route to FP16 or GPTQ depending on A/B results. Redwood can do prefix-based routing so that identified prompts are executed on a higher-fidelity model.

5) Suggested pilot configuration (2-week):
- Run Llama-2-13B FP16 as baseline + AWQ variant in parallel on identical traffic slices.
- Traffic routing: 80% baseline, 20% AWQ for initial safety window. Increase AWQ % if no regression detected after 48h.
- Metrics: per-prompt triage precision/recall, hallucination rate, token cost, p50/p95 latency, and throughput. Daily automated reports + a mid-pilot health call.

6) Cost/throughput example (rough numbers):
