Raj / Sean — attaching the quick pass analysis from our bench runs. High-level observations: 

- Workload: streaming summarization + reranker p99 latency target ~120ms, 1k qps steady (peaks 2.5k). Context windows mostly 1k tokens, occasional 6k.
- H100 (80GB): best headroom for >4k context with mixed precision; strong for concurrency and long-context caching.
- A100 (40GB): solid latency for 1k contexts, cheaper per-hour, but memory-bound when context > 4k; we see OOMs without KV-tiling.
- L4 (48GB total, optimized for inference shards): lowest cost per token for short contexts (<2k) but higher p99 tail under bursty concurrency.

Recommendation draft: Dedicated mix with majority A100 for baseline throughput, small H100 pool for long-context and heavy-concurrency fallthrough, and L4 as a burst spot for batch embedding-like workloads.

Raj / Maya — quick answers and proposed next steps below. I've also attached a compact spreadsheet with three capacity scenarios (Baseline / Hedged / Performance).

Answers: 
- Token cost vs latency: relative to A100 baseline, H100 reduces per-token CPU time on long-context workloads by ~18-25% for your 6k windows due to larger KV cache and higher memory bandwidth; cost delta is ~+30% per GPU-hour for H100 vs A100 but tokens/hour increases so net tokens/$ improves in long-context-heavy runs. L4 is ~40-55% cheaper per GPU-hour vs A100 for short-context inference but shows higher p99 tail under burst.
- Model swap fallback: supported. Our routing policy can transparently fall back from H100->A100->L4 when capacity constrained; fallbacks with quantized variants may increase hallucination risk slightly — we recommend canarying outputs and keeping eval hooks (we'll surface quality regression alerts). 
- Procurement table: see attachment. Baseline scenario = 60% A100, 25% H100, 15% L4 (reserve + burst mix). Hedged = 40% A100, 40% H100, 20% L4. Performance = 30% A100, 60% H100, 10% L4.

Ops canary plan (6 weeks): 
1) Week 0: reserve baseline mix, enable prefix caching for frequent prompt patterns.
2) Week 1-2: route 10% of requests to hedged H100 pool (metric: p99 latency, token cost, quality via eval set).
3) Week 3-4: expand to 30% if quality stable; keep automated rollback on quality regression >2% of eval failures.
4) Week 5-6: readiness review, adjust reserve commitments.
