Alice — thanks, this is very clear. High level recommendation up front: for your constraints I suggest starting with PrivateLink (interface endpoints) for the pilot, with a site-to-site VPN as a complementary administrative/backchannel for management traffic and as a fallback.

Why PrivateLink first (short):
- Non‑transitive, cross-account endpoint: avoids CIDR overlap issues because traffic is encapsulated to the endpoint and routed via ENIs in your VPC rather than relying on route-table peering across overlapping IP ranges.
- Strong isolation: we can provision customer-specific endpoints, apply per-endpoint security groups, and minimize blast radius.
- Easier to lock down egress and audit: CloudTrail + VPC Flow Logs give you fine-grained visibility; we can map connections to specific endpoints for retention requirements.

Operational caveats and trade-offs:
- Throughput/scale: interface endpoints have quota limits and per‑ENI bandwidth. For bursty ML inference you must size endpoints and consider multi-AZ endpoints. We’ll provide recommended ENI counts and a batching plan to smooth peaks.
- Cost: PrivateLink adds per-hour endpoint + data processing charges — for steady high throughput peering or Transit Gateway can be cheaper. We’ll produce an estimate spreadsheet (attached).
- Regions & multi-account: if you want cross-region low-latency, pair PrivateLink with regional endpoints + our routing policy; Transit Gateway is an alternative for many accounts but reintroduces CIDR and transitive routing complexity.

When to prefer Peering or S2S instead:
- VPC Peering is attractive only when there is no IP overlap and you control both VPCs (direct L3 routing is lower cost and lower latency). However it is transitive-unfriendly: if you later need to route via a hub, peering becomes unmanageable.
- Site-to-site VPN is useful for low-volume administrative access, or when you must traverse on-premises firewalls/NAT. VPNs have higher and more variable latency — not great for p95-sensitive inference, but OK as a control plane/backchannel.

Security and compliance specifics we can support:
- mTLS termination: we can require client certificates at the endpoint and integrate with your KMS for cert rotation.
- Audit trails: CloudTrail + endpoint logging + our request-level tracing (we can ship logs to your S3 or forward to your SIEM).
- Key management: support for customer-managed KMS keys (bring-your-own) and HSM-backed KMS if required.

2) Run a two-week PrivateLink pilot in one region with synthetic load to validate p95 and throughput.
3) Stand up a low-bandwidth S2S VPN for admin access and log transfer.
