Quick triage notes (initial):

- Pulled the two request IDs Alex provided; correlating trace shows a pattern of "chunk interval elongation" on the proxy to model worker hop: token chunks emitted at ~40–100ms then a gap of 6–18s, then resume. No upstream 5xx returned to the client — the proxy kept the connection open and buffered.
- Within our runtime logs we see repeated transient EPIPE / connection reset to a subset of the model workers at the same time window (09:01–09:12 PST). Those resets were retried, but our streaming stitcher doesn't re-send the missing tokens into the open client stream — resulting in the silent segment the client observed.
- Attached a filtered log (chunk-intervals-20280730.log) with the proxy timestamps and worker error markers.

Immediate mitigation options (short timeline):
A) Route Cognia trial users to the non-streaming generate endpoint for the next 4 hours (recommended for the demo). This avoids relying on continuous chunk stitching.
B) Apply a conservative chunk-emit timeout on the proxy (force a short heartbeat chunk at 2s if no tokens) — low risk but requires a rolling restart of the proxy fleet (30–45m).
C) Roll back a recent kernel change on a small subset of workers that we suspect correlate with increased EPIPEs (requires SRE window).

I recommend option A now to guarantee demo continuity, and option B as a 1–2 hour follow-up if the instability persists.
