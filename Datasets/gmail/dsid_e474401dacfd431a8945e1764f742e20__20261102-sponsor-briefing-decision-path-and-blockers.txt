Sanjay — quick responses to the technical items you raised, and a proposal for the deep-dive agenda.

1) Trial support for a production slice: yes — we can support a controlled 10–20% redirected traffic experiment. We recommend an initial 7–14 day window behind feature flags with circuit-breakers and traffic shaping.

2) Data residency options: two feasible paths
   - Hosted EU region: we can route inference and storage to our EU-hosted clusters (Redwood region EU-West) and provide data-at-rest residency guarantees; easier for short trials.
   - Private (VPC) in your Azure EU subscription: supported for customers who require full network control — will require a private deployment engagement and estimated lead time ~6–8 weeks for provisioning and validation.

3) Performance approach: to hit p99 < 200ms for short summaries we’ll prototype with: model variant with 8-bit quantization, KV prefix caching for repeated prompts, and continuous batching tuned for your request size distribution. We’ll instrument p50/p95/p99 and token-cost.

4) Escalation & SLAs during trial: primary on-call is Solutions (me) with secondary escalation to Reliability (Marissa will coordinate). If we observe regression beyond agreed thresholds, we’ll: (a) automatic fallback to a lower-cost variant with higher latency tolerance, (b) enable a dedicated lightweight pool for the trial if needed, (c) weekly runbook call for first two weeks.
