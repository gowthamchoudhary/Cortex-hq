Hi all — reviewed ACME's redlines and drafted proposed language. High-level position: we can accept written-approval requirements for publicity and logo use, but need to preserve a limited reference program for sales and case studies with an expedited approval process (48 business hours). On confidentiality survivor period, we prefer 5 years standard with an option to extend to 7 years for regulated data categories.

I attached a short summary and a tracked-change MSA with suggested edits. Key proposed clauses (excerpt):
- Confidentiality survivor: "Except as otherwise required by applicable law, confidentiality obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years; for Confidential Information that is regulated (e.g., PHI, PCI) the parties may mutually agree to a longer survivor period not to exceed seven (7) years.)"
- Publicity and References: "Customer grants Redwood the non-exclusive right to identify Customer as a Redwood customer in sales materials and on Redwood's website, subject to Customer's prior written approval for any public press release or case study, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld and shall be provided within 48 business hours of request. Logo use requires a standard logo usage guide and Customer's sign-off for each placement outside of the Redwood customer page."
