Summary of what we see (initial):
- At 08:27:45 the autoscaler added 4 slots to the tenant pool. The prefetch coordinator then redistributed prefix ownership to rebalance memory pressure.
- During the reshuffle there was a short window where KV-lookups for high-volume prefixes routed to cold nodes; that caused synchronous prefetches to pile up on the same hot-shard (observed HOL blocking).
- Batching layer also rebounded: a subset of requests bypassed batching (small sequences) while others accumulated behind KV blocking — this created large variance in lane latencies and increased p99.

Immediate hypothesis: the combination of (a) aggressive prefetch reshuffle during scaling, and (b) our current KV eviction threshold allowed hot prefixes to be temporarily evicted, produced a resonance that amplifies HOL blocking.

Immediate mitigations we’re starting now:
1) Pause automatic prefetch reshuffles for the affected tenant namespace.
2) Increase KV pin threshold for high-frequency prefixes for the next 10 minutes.
3) Temporarily relax batching packetization so small requests join existing batches instead of creating micro-batches.

I’ve attached a metrics snapshot (kv-metrics-snapshot-20280720.json) and a short diagnostic trace. Will update once we validate effects.

- Change prefetch reshuffle policy to be quiescent during scale events (implemented temporarily on 07/20).
