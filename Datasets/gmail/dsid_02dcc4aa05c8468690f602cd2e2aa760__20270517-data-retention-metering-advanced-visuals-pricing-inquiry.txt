Megan —

Great to hear from you and thanks for the clear ask. I pulled together a concise options matrix (attached) but high-level answers below to help procurement model quickly: 

1) Retention pricing models (options):
   - Metered (consumption): billed on GB ingested per month. Overage billed at a per-GB rate. Best if usage is spiky.
   - Slab tiers (recommended for predictable spend): monthly buckets (e.g., 250GB / 1TB / 5TB) with a unit price per bucket. Includes basic retention (30 days) by default; extended retention is add-on.
   - Committed retention: annual committed GB/day with credits and a lower per-GB effective rate (savings scale with commitment).

2) Tracing:
   - Two components: ingestion (spans) and stored trace retention. Ingestion is priced per million spans ingested; sampled traces are cheaper if you route through our sampling pipeline. Retained traces beyond the included retention window count toward retention billing.

3) Advanced dashboards:
   - We offer a hybrid model: seat-based admin licenses + a per-dashboard runtime cost for heavy query workloads. For multi-team usage we can do a seat pool model and a bumped bulk discount for >200 dashboards.

4) Discounts / packaging:
   - Pilot pricing: 10% off consumption rate for a 3-month paid pilot (minimum usage commitment).
   - Annual commit: typical starting discount 15% for retention+tracing+dashboards when bundled. Deeper discounts possible for 12+ month, higher-volume commitments.

5) Compliance & deletion SLA:
   - Deletion: soft-delete occurs immediately; physical deletion from object store by default occurs within 90 days but we can offer a 30-day expedited physical deletion SLA as part of an enterprise addendum. All retention policy changes are audited and surfaced in the console.

Hi Megan —

Looping in a few technical details that often affect pricing and packaging decisions: 

- Per-project retention: yes — retention is configurable at the workspace/project level. Each workspace can set a default retention window; we track usage per workspace and can invoice either aggregated or by-workspace line items.

- Deletion SLA and auditability: soft-delete is immediate and reversible for up to 30 days. Physical removal from object storage follows the configured retention lifecycle and, by default, completes within 90 days. For enterprise customers we can include a 30-day physical deletion SLA and provide tamper-evident audit logs of retention policy changes (exportable).

- Tracing nuance: billing is most accurate when you report pre-sampling spans. If you sample at edge and send only sampled spans, ingestion costs drop proportionally. We also support server-side tail-sampling to reduce ingest while preserving key traces for debug.

Worked estimate (based on your attached forecast):
  - Total ingest: ~320GB/month across three teams (monthly peaks factored)
  - Tracing ingestion: ~1.2M spans/day pre-sampling (projected after peak events)
  - Dashboards: ~1,200 daily queries, ~150 active dashboards

Estimated monthly pricing (ballpark):
  - Retention (slab 1TB tier, including 30-day retention): $2,400/month
  - Tracing ingestion (1.2M spans/day ~36M/month) at $0.50 per million spans: ~$18/month (note: example unit price; actual depends on negotiated rate)
  - Dashboards (seat pool + query runtime): $1,000/month
  - Subtotal: ~$3,418/month
