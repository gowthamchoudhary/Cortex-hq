Appreciate the clear summary. Below is a short post-call econ recap plus the gating we'd recommend for a 6–8 week pilot to validate performance and unit economics. I'm attaching a simple token estimate spreadsheet and our pilot SOW template for procurement. (Attachment stubs below).

1) What we heard (pain & goals)
- Goal: scale fraud scoring from 10k -> 200k calls/day while hitting finance target (~40% lower unit cost) and latency SLOs (95th < 350ms).
- Pain: bursty traffic, cost unpredictability, and current provider opaque batching/parallelism.

2) Cost / token economics quick model (high level)
- Baseline: hosted open-model variant (float32-ish) estimated ~ $2,200 per 1M tokens generated at current runs (includes per-call overhead). For your scoring workload (~120 tokens per call) that maps to ~$0.26 per 1k calls (baseline).
- Typical savings levers (applied incrementally):
  • Continuous batching (request coalescing) — 20–35% savings at 200k/day depending on traffic burstiness.
  • Prefix/KV caching (scoring reuse across short-context requests) — 10–30% for repetitive prefixes (we saw 18% on similar customers).
  • Model quantization (FP16/8-bit) — 25–40% at small accuracy tradeoffs; best for isolated scoring models.
  • Dedicated capacity (reserved GPU pools) — lowers marginal token cost and stabilizes latency; expected 30–50% unit cost reduction vs hosted for sustained volume with a commit.

Illustrative TCO scenarios (monthly, very approximate):
- Discovery/hosted pilot (no commit): 200k/day -> ~6M calls/month -> ~720M tokens -> est $1,584 / month (post basic batching/caching assumptions).
- Dedicated pilot (4–8 week reserve, partial commit): same traffic -> est $900–1,100 / month effective unit cost (depending on reserved capacity share + quantization).

Notes: these numbers are model- and workload-sensitive; we'll validate within the pilot using your real traces.

3) Pilot gating and hypotheses (recommended 6–8 weeks)
- Week 0–1: data & infra prep: provide 3 days of representative traces, confirm token distribution, and allow us to run baseline host tests.
- Week 2–4: run hosted pilot with aggressive batching and KV cache enabled; measure latency SLOs and savings vs baseline.
- Week 5–6: optional dedicated cutover for a subset of routes; test quantized model variant and measure quality delta (A/B).
- Success criteria to move to commit: verified 35%+ unit cost reduction OR latency SLOs met with <10% accuracy regression on scoring metric.
