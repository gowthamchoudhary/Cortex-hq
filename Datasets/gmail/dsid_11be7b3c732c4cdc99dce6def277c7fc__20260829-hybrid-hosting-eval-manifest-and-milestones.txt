Hi Kelly — thanks for the quick response and the stakeholder list. Short answers to your questions below, plus a refined prep checklist.

Q1: Network requirements for Private/VPC path
- Typical minimum: a dedicated /24 (customer-facing subnets) or ability to peer to a staging VPC. We require a few outbound rules for control plane (TLS to our control plane endpoints) and a private route for model artifact pulls if you need on-prem model caching. Exact CIDRs and ports are in the POC worksheet.

Q2: Pilot cadence for Private validation
- We recommend VPC-only pilot in staging first (2–3 days of traffic simulation) to validate KMS and audit log ingestion. If on-prem is required, that adds another week for ops access and firewall exceptions. Overall minimum to validate logs/KMS: ~5 business days if everything is provisioned ahead of time.
