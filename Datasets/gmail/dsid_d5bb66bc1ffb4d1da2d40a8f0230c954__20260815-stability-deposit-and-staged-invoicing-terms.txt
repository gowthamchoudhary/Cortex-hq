Hi James — thanks for the clear ask and context. We can support a staged invoicing structure plus a one-time stability deposit. High-level proposal below:

- Stability deposit: 10% of committed first-year spend, due 14 days after contract execution; refundable and netted against the final invoice.
- Invoice tranches:
  1) Pilot acceptance — 30% invoiced on acceptance (Net30 from invoice date)
  2) Integration signoff — 40% invoiced on signoff (Net45)
  3) Go-live — 30% invoiced on go-live (Net60)
- VAT handling: Redwood will issue invoices from Redwood EU (NL VAT id on invoice) to AstraMed EU entity; we will provide an evidence packet (invoices + VAT registration proof + supply statement) with each tranche.
- Credit limit: with a deposit we can keep a conservative credit hold equal to outstanding balance minus deposit. If AstraMed prefers a separate escrow, we can route to a short-term escrow arrangement (adds ~2–3 business days delay to cash application).

I'll loop in our finance lead (Ben) and revenue ops (Marissa) to finalize the wording and confirm operational steps (payment application, PO matching, and required supporting docs). Would AstraMed be comfortable with the milestone definitions above?

If yes, I can produce a short amendment to the SOW laying out the deposit and tranche schedule.
