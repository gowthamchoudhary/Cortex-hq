Sean/Markus —

See answers below for the RFP subset; attaching the on-call roster and the customer-facing incident templates for Novus to review. I've kept the language conservative for procurement.

Named TAM (enterprise):
- Assignment: Named TAM provided at contract execution. Primary TAM is 8x5 business hours for day-to-day ops and cadence.
- Responsibilities: onboarding/runbook development, monthly operational reviews, proactive risk reviews, coordination of upgrades and maintenance windows, escalation triage and liaison to engineering.
- Optional 24x7 TAM: available as a paid add-on; for mission-critical customers we combine named TAM with 24x7 SRE escalation and an agreed on-call overlap.

Support hours & escalation:
- Standard: 8x5 (Mon-Fri, 9am–5pm local customer timezone) via support@redwood.com (ticket).
- Critical (S1): 24x7 SRE on-call via PagerDuty. Phone bridge and SMS escalation available.
- Escalation matrix: Tier 1 (Support) -> Tier 2 (TAM / Senior Support) -> Tier 3 (Engineering / SRE) -> Exec on-call. Contact methods and SLAs included in the attached CSV.

Severity & SLA targets (enterprise):
- Severity 1 (Service down / critical production impact): initial ack ≤ 15 minutes; continuous updates every 30 minutes; target mitigation/workaround within 4 hours; target remediation timeline depends on root cause but we provide initial remediation plan within 2 hours.
- Severity 2 (Partial outage / degraded functionality): initial ack ≤ 1 hour; regular updates every 2–4 hours.
- Severity 3 (Non-critical / requests): initial ack ≤ 1 business day.

Incident communications & postmortems:
- Templates included: Initial ack, customer update, resolution notice, and 72-hour follow-up post-incident summary.
- Post-incident: we produce a customer-facing postmortem within 5–10 business days with RCA and remediation actions; extended technical RCA for signed customers as needed.

Audit/logs & encryption:
- SOC 2 Type II: available under NDA (Jordan will share link + cover letter).
- Audit logs: API access for log export and a log retention control with defaults at 90 days; enterprise plans configurable to 365 days.
- KMS: customer-managed keys supported for Private deployments; hosted customers can use Redwood-managed KMS with options for customer KMS integration in Dedicated/Private.
