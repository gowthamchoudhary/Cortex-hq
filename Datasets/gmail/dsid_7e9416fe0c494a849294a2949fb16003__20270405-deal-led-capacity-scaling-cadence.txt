Vanessa — thanks for the note and attachments. Quick reactions after looking at the worksheet:

- The 50k token/day steady state with 3x spikes maps to ~150k tokens on peak days. If Orbital's requests skew short (<128 tokens) we can get decent throughput with 12 reserved slots; longer sequences (512+) push us toward 18–20.
- Latency targets matter: if Orbital needs sub-250ms 95th percentile at peak, we should plan for 16 reserved slots with a 50% burst runway rather than 40%. The incremental cost to go from 40%->50% burst is mostly transient and buys headroom.
- If we compress sequences (prefix caching + KV reuse) we can reduce effective tokens by ~10–15% per workload — we should plan an eval run.
