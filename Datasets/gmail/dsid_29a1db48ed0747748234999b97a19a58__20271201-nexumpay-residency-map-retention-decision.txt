Samira — thanks, and great to hear the team is progressing. High level response below and then a MAP draft attached (MAP_Timeline_v1.3.xlsx).

Short answers up front:
- Residency: Yes — we can restrict processing to US regions for your production workspace. We'll document the region pin in the MAP and in the DPA SOW.
- Retention: We propose a tiered retention model:
  * Audit logs & access events: retained 7 years (archived, encrypted) — matches your ask.
  * Request logs (non-PII operational telemetry): retained 2 years by default with options to shorten.
  * Prompt content and model inputs: configurable; default 90 days for ephemeral mode. We will add a flag on your workspace to lock to 90 days.

Security gate we recommend inside the MAP (in order):
 1) SOC 2 Type II report review and bridge call (we provide report + controls matrix) — target by 2027-12-03.
 2) SIG/CAIQ complete spreadsheet review — target by 2027-12-06.
 3) DPA final redline agreement — target by 2027-12-08.
 4) Staging evidence confirmation (audit log access, KMS config screenshot, retention policy set) — target by 2027-12-12.

Given your 12/15 close target, that gives a short buffer but is achievable if SIG + DPA reviewers are responsive. I attached the MAP with milestones and owners.
