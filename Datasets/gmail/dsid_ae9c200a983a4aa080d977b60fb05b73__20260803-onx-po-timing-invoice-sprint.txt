Hi Sara — thanks for the clear list and for sending the template. Forwarding what procurement usually needs from our side and a few options that often work for mid-market healthcare customers.

Quick answers up front:
- PO approach: we can accept either a single annual master PO referencing committed spend or monthly POs. For committed discounts we do prefer an annual (or quarterly) PO that references the commitment.
- Invoice cadence: monthly invoices by default (end-of-month billing) is our standard; we can also do milestone invoicing for projects with clearly defined deliverables (e.g., onboarding, production enablement, first 90 days).
- Net terms: standard is Net30. Net45 or Net60 can be offered on approved credit with a procurement-led request; for Net60 we typically require a purchase order and a signed SOW plus credit approval. Early-payment discounts (1-2%) can be negotiated for Net30.

What we need from procurement to process invoices quickly:
- PO number (free-text field) to appear on every invoice
- Billing remit: Redwood Inference, Attn: AR, 100 Market St, Suite 200, San Francisco, CA 94105 (or the electronic ACH/WIRE details below)
- Tax/VAT info: please include Onnex tax ID on PO if applicable
- If you request Net60, please indicate that on the PO so our Billing team tracks it for credit approval.

Discount/packaging notes:
- Volume tiers: 10k+ embeddings/month or sustained 20M tokens/month over a 12-month window qualifies for tiered discounting (we can share a rate card).
- Committed spend gives the best effective unit price — we can build a stepped discount tied to quarterly spend buckets if that helps procurement forecasting.

Sara — looping in pricing detail so finance has visibility. A few pragmatic options based on what you outlined: 

Option A — Standard monthly billing (recommended for pilots):
- Monthly invoice, Net30
- No long-term commit; list prices apply
- Good for 3-month pilot with low onboarding friction

Option B — Short committed pilot (3 months trial with credit):
- 3-month minimum committed spend (we will credit overage/underage on final invoice)
- Monthly invoices, Net30; we’ll provide a one-time $5k onboarding credit applied to month 1 or month 2

Option C — Annual commitment (best unit economics):
- Annual PO referencing committed $X spend per quarter
- Monthly invoices against the commitment, with quarterly true-up
- Tiered discounts applied based on realized usage thresholds (examples: 5% @ $250k/yr, 10% @ $500k/yr)
- Net30 by default; Net45/60 requires credit approval paperwork

A few operational notes:
- If Onnex wants Net60, we’ll need PO + signed SOW + AP contact for reference checks. Credit approvals typically take 5–7 business days.
- For customers that require milestone invoicing, our Finance team will need milestone definitions in the SOW and the PO must reference the milestone IDs that match invoice line items.

If you want to move fast, Option B gets you into production the quickest and gives Onnex a small onboarding credit. Option C will deliver best pricing but requires a bit more paperwork and lead time.

Happy to join a call with James to walk options and estimated timelines for credit approvals.
