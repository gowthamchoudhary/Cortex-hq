- Uptime: We typically publish 99.9% as the baseline for hosted customers. 99.95% is achievable in many Dedicated deployments but is tied to reserved capacity/committed pool sizing. We can offer 99.95% for a Dedicated plan with a modest commit uplift; if Acme prefers to stay on Hosted the credit model would differ.

- Response times: 15‑min ack for P1 is aggressive but reasonable for enterprise customers on a premium support tier; the key is defining P1 precisely (impact to production traffic vs degraded performance). Time-to-restore (4 hours) is scope-dependent — for multi-region failover it could be faster; for data-plane fixes that require rollforward/rollback it may vary.
