2) Cache hit-rate assumptions: we see 35–65% hit rates for prompt-heavy productization (higher when prompts have stable prefixes). Model changes can require lightweight cache warming; our ops hooks include automated warmup and soft TTLs — in practice customers tolerate a 24–72 hour warmup window with minimal manual intervention. The ops burden is usually low if model variant rollouts are staged (canary first).
