Hi Emily — thanks for the clear ask and for sending the usage file.

Short answer: yes — we can put together a limited price-hold while you ramp, and there are a few ways to structure it depending on your priorities:
- Temporary price-hold (timeboxed) + commit reshuffle: keep current unit pricing for N months while you add capacity, then resume tiered pricing against a revised commit schedule.
- Bridge credit (one-time credit to offset overage while you normalize usage).
- Short-term blended rate for a fixed term.

I'll draft a pragmatic proposal for procurement (commit numbers, bridge credit, and a DocuSign-ready amendment). Can you confirm target ramp month (Oct vs Nov) and whether the +20% is conservative? Also looping in Contracts in case they want an alternate signature flow.
