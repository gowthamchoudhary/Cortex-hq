Hi Lena — thanks for the heads up and timeline. Below are three pragmatic paths we typically recommend; I've attached a quick spreadsheet with scenario math and a redline of the proposed MAA.

1) Burst credits (fastest): one-off capacity credits for the 3-month peak. No change to contract term. No SLA change. Estimate: 45–55% premium on per-token unit pricing for the booked credits. Good if you want minimal procurement overhead.

2) Reserved uplift (recommended if you expect sustained higher usage): add reserved units mid-term with pro-rated pricing plus an uplift discount for the committed months. Example: +50% reserved for 3 months with 12% uplift to the effective per-unit rate vs pay-as-you-go. Requires amendment; legal turnaround ~3–5 business days.

3) Annualize & co-term (best unit economics): convert the uplift into a 12-month annualized commit, co-term with your current agreement, and apply a co-term discount (example: 18% on incremental committed units). Requires exec signoff for discounting (Ben/Revenue Ops) and legal amendment.
