Tom — thanks for the redlines and the clear asks. We ran this by our legal team and propose the following compromise to keep execution on track: 

- Governing law: Delaware law to govern the agreement (Redwood’s standard). If Elysium is uncomfortable, we will consider New York law as a neutral alternative.

- Dispute resolution: binding arbitration for most disputes (monetary and contractual claims) with seat in New York (American Arbitration Association rules).

- Provisional relief (narrow carveout): either party may seek provisional equitable relief (including injunctive relief and temporary restraining orders) in a U.S. federal court or a state court located in either (a) the State of Delaware or (b) the State where the defendant has its principal place of business (e.g., California for Elysium). Such relief is limited to preserving the status quo and protecting intellectual property, customer data, or other confidential information pending the outcome of arbitration. Any request for provisional relief must: 
  - identify the specific irreparable harm;
  - include a proposed expedited briefing schedule (no more than 7 calendar days to responsive briefing unless court orders otherwise); and
  - include good-faith notice to the other party prior to filing, except where imminent harm would render notice impracticable.

- Enforcement: final remedies remain subject to arbitration award enforcement where appropriate; provisional relief decisions are immediately enforceable to the extent permitted by law.
