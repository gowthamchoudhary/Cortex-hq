Olga / Monica / Alan —

Thanks for the clear ask, Alan. Below is a concise proposal that balances Civica's controls with Redwood's operational needs. I included practical notes and links to evidence.

Proposed DPA amendment language (suggestion for insertion under "Use of Customer Data / Model Training"):
"Redwood agrees that Customer Data (as defined in the Agreement) will not be used to train, improve, or otherwise modify any production models or weights used by Redwood for general model improvement, model tuning, or model retraining. Redwood will restrict any use of Customer Data for service improvement or internal analytics to aggregated and de-identified telemetry that cannot be linked to a specific customer or individual. Any retention of Customer Data for debugging or incident response will be limited, access-controlled, and logged."

Operational controls we can offer today:
- Contractual no-train commitment as above.
- Account-level training flag: we can set a customer account flag that excludes all request/response content from training pipelines and improvement telemetry. This flag can be enabled immediately for production accounts (we have an internal SLA for flag propagation within 2 business days).
- Storage & keys: customer data for Civica will be stored in our US region (us-west-2) unless otherwise requested; we support customer-managed keys (KMS integration) and can provide HSM-backed key options for Private deployments.
- Subprocessors: our up-to-date list is published here: https://redwood.example.com/subprocessors?account=civica (this includes hosting, logging, observability vendors).
- Evidence: we will provide a current SOC 2 Type II report under NDA and a redacted audit summary. For logs and incident response playbooks, we can provide a documented runbook and example logs upon a legitimate request process.

Notes on ownership and retention: outputs generated by the service are made available to the customer and by default Redwood will retain request/response artifacts for troubleshooting and analytics. With the account-level training flag enabled, those artifacts will not be used by any model training pipelines and will be treated as retention-for-support only (access-controlled). We can add a retention cap (e.g., 30 days) contractually if Civica requires it.

Next steps I propose:
1) Civica to confirm if the proposed clause language is acceptable or provide redline.
2) If acceptable, we enable the account-level training flag (ETA: 48 hours) and provide SOC 2 under NDA.
3) Execute DPA amendment via DocuSign: https://docusign.example.com/ENVELOPE12345
