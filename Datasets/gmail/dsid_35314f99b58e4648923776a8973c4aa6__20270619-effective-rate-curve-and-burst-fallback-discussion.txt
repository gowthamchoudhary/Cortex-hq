Thanks again for the conversation this morning. Attaching a concise pricing summary; below is a clear description of how our usage-based discounts and on-demand (over-commit) burst rates would work for Aureon. Goal: simple economics you can model into procurement and engineering forecasts.

1) Commit tiers (monthly effective tokens) — sliding discount applies to matched usage within month:
   - Tier A: 0 – 100M tokens: list price (no discount)
   - Tier B: 100M – 500M tokens: 10% discount to list
   - Tier C: 500M+ tokens: 20% discount to list

2) Minimum spend (commit floor) — recommended approach:
   - We typically require a minimum billed commit equal to 40% of the contracted monthly commit; shortfalls convert to credits that apply to future months within the 12-month term (soft floor).
   - Alternatively, we can set a hard minimum equal to 60% of the monthly commit if procurement prefers guaranteed spend.

3) On-demand over-commit / burst behavior:
   - If you exceed the committed monthly quantity, overage is billed at the on-demand per-token rate scaled by a multiplier. Example: 1.6x list per-token for bursts beyond commit within that month.
   - We can put a monthly burst cap (e.g., up to 2x committed tokens) after which we provide capacity-based throttling or negotiated emergency credits.

4) Example (simple math):
   - Committed: 200M tokens / month at tier B pricing (10% discount).
   - If in Month X you use 260M tokens, 200M billed at tier B, 60M billed at on-demand*1.6x.

5) Promotional concession (optional): 5% additional discount on year one for a signed 12‑month commit.
