Hi Emma — thanks for the context and the file, that helps.

Short answers up front: yes, we can increase reserved capacity at renewal and provide SLOs tied to reserved throughput. For spike handling we offer two approaches:

Option 1 (soft overflow): Reserved pool + on-demand spill. When reserved utilization passes threshold, traffic flows to on-demand nodes; you keep latency SLOs for priority routes, and we bill spill tokens (per-token). We can include a bursting credit buffer to smooth cost.

Option 2 (hard cap): Strict limit at reserved pool. Excess requests return 429s or failover to a lower-cost model variant. Lower risk of bill surprises, but possible availability impact during spikes.
