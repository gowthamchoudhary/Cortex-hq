Quick clarifying note — I like option 2 (tiered commission on net collection) for a few reasons:
- Keeps marketplace list-price reporting clean (we avoid lifting list price for pass-through)
- Easier to reconcile retainer/overage credits in our billing system
- We should explicitly call out VAT/GST handling for EU/UK customers if CloudNova ever expands there (procurement often asks).

Suggested margin waterfall for the pilot:
- Base band (<= $30k/mo): reseller commission 12%
- Growth band ($30k–$60k/mo): reseller commission 14%
- Peak band (> $60k/mo): reseller commission 16%
