Hi Alex / Sydney —

Following up with technical details on the audit/export capabilities we discussed. TL;DR: yes to on-demand exports to customer S3, and we support retention policies and append-only activity storage for admin logs. Details below.

1) Storage & immutability
- Admin activity and API logs are written to an append-only ledger in the serving runtime with block-level checksums. For dedicated/private deployments we can enable WORM (write-once-read-many) backed by immutable object tagging in your S3.

2) Exports
- On-demand export to customer S3 buckets (encrypted at rest, SSE-KMS). Exports include a manifest (JSON) and a CSV payload; we provide a signed checksum for tamper-evidence.
- Export scheduling: ad hoc, daily, weekly. Retention export ranges supported: 30, 90, 365, custom up to 7 years (configurable).

3) Fields & format
- Default columns: timestamp, actor_id, action, resource_type, resource_id, request_id, model_version, status_code, lat_ms, source_ip, user_agent.
- We can include full request/response ids but redact prompt/response content by default; content-level exports require DPA/TOS addendum and scoped approvals.
