Hi Sophie — quick ops notes from the ramp perspective (copied from the throughput plan):

- Stage 0 (prep): 7 days before pilot we’ll provision a dedicated capacity slice for UrbanCart in the us-west region, pre-warm KV caches, and set observability hooks (token-level breakouts, per-route p50/p95/p99).
- Pilot (30d, 25% commit): we’ll enable continuous batching and 2ms extra headroom for network retries; collect prompt-set quality metrics.
- Stage 1 (3 weeks, 50%): add automatic prefix caching for the top 10 most common prompts, enable conservative quantization for model v2q.
- Stage 2 (full commit): remove conservative throttles, enable burst pocket.

Throttling and fallback behavior:
- Route-scoped limits will return 429s with a Retry-After header; we recommend client retry with jitter and an exponential backoff.
- If p99 latency increases >1.5x baseline for more than 10 minutes, our control plane will shift traffic to the quantized fallback variant automatically; we will notify both Slack and email.
