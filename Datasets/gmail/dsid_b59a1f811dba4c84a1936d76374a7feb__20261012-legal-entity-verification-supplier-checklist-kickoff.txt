Hi Elena — thanks for the quick reply and for the incorporation doc.

To answer your questions:
- W-8BEN-E: please complete the standard form and include the chapter/part that supports the entity status you are claiming (most EU operating companies complete the chapter affirming foreign status and provide the relevant FATCA classification). If you indicate treaty benefits, please include the treaty article(s) and the US TIN if available. If in doubt, fill out the form and we will have Finance/Tax confirm — do not leave the form unsigned.
- VAT: a clear PDF of your VAT registration certificate showing your legal name and VAT number is sufficient for our records. Please include a translation if the certificate language is not English.
- Bank proof: a recent bank letter or an official bank statement that includes the IBAN and company name is fine — a voided check is less common for EU banks.

Upload: portal is preferred for security (https://portal.redwood.ai/vendor/upload/NT-9987). If you prefer to reply to this thread with attachments we will accept that too. If you want DocuSign, I can generate an envelope for the supplier intake form and the bank authorization.

FYI I have looped in Laura (Finance) and Marcus (Legal) so they can flag any unusual items or additional checks. Once we receive the completed intake form + W-8BEN-E + VAT cert + bank proof, Finance will open the vendor in the system and raise a PO.

On timing: target PO issuance 3 business days after Finance receives complete docs. If Legal needs to review entity docs or there are withholding questions, that could add 2–4 business days.
