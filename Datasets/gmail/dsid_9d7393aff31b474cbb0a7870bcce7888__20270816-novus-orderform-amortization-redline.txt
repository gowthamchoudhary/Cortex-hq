Adding the financial impact modeling I mentioned. Key points: 

- Option A (one-time invoice credit): immediate reduction in Novus's month-1 invoice by the credit amount. Redwood recognizes the credit against billed revenue in month 1; cashflow for Novus benefits earlier.
- Option B (12-month amortization): credit spread evenly across 12 invoices as a monthly discount line-item. From Redwood's perspective this reduces recognized revenue over 12 months; the net ARR impact is the same but monthly GAAP recognition differs.
- Recommendation: offer amortization as default for deals longer than 12 months; offer one-time credit for shorter deals or where Novus requests immediate billing relief.

Attached: Novus_pricing_matrix_2027-08.xlsx (includes a month-by-month cashflow for both options and the effect of the rolling 90-day floor on true-up timing). Also proposed language for Exhibit B below: 

"If Customer elects amortization, the onboarding credit of $XX,XXX shall be applied as an equal monthly reduction to Customer invoices for twelve (12) consecutive months beginning with the first invoice date following Effective Date. If Customer terminates the Agreement prior to completion of the amortization period, the remaining unapplied credit will be recouped on the final invoice."

If legal is okay with the termination recoupment language, I think this is clean. Also suggests we keep a short definition of "sandbox" in Exhibit A so there's no ambiguity.
