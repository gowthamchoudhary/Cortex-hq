Hi Maria — attaching the two scenario workbooks and a support SKU matrix. Summary here so you don't have to open files immediately: 

Scenario A (amortized platform fee, 20% support discount):
- Platform fee: $240k amortized monthly = $20k/mo
- Migration credit: up to 30% (max $72k) applied against PS invoices in first 6 months
- Support (Premium): list price $15k/mo -> discounted to $12k/mo
- Year 1 cashflow (platform + support): $20k + $12k = $32k/mo average; migration PS billed separately but offset by credits

Scenario B (platform fee upfront, no support discount):
- Platform fee: $240k upfront
- Migration credit: none
- Support (Premium): $15k/mo
- Year 1 cashflow: month 1 = $240k + $15k, months 2–12 = $15k/mo; effective cash burden front-loaded

Key tradeoffs to call out:
- Amortize improves monthly OpEx predictability for HealthLytics and smooths budgeting; it increases Redwood invoice volume and defers revenue recognition (we can handle internal accounting).
- Converting platform fee to PS credits reduces our professional services cash receipts but accelerates deployment (less friction). We can set a cap and tie credits to a signed SOW with milestones.
- Renewal uplift: amortized deals have a precedent of +8–12% renewal increase; upfront-paid deals see lower renewal pressure in year 2 but higher initial cost.
