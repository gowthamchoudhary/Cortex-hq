Appreciate the clear ask. Quick note from Cumulus's side on reservation mechanics: 

- Minimum reservation: production-level SLA-backed reserved clusters require a 6-month minimum commitment for the A100-equivalent family; 12-month is preferred for volume discounts.
- Burst capacity: can be provisioned but is governed by AZ quotas and cross-AZ bandwidth; sustained cross-AZ autoscaling typically requires additional networking setup and a higher tier of support.
- For a trial: we can offer a 30-day sandbox with pre-emptible nodes (no SLA) or a time-limited reserved test (6-week minimum) if you want SLA mimicry; the latter requires procurement paperwork and a deposit.

If you want, Cumulus can run our own benchmark on the SKU we recommend (8xA100 equivalent) using your workload profile — we can supply raw telemetry and a config file.
