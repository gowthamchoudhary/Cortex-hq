Hi all — quick legal guidance after review:

1) Module selection: Northbridge is acting as a Controller and Redwood will act as a Processor under the relationship described. Under the EU 2021 SCCs, the appropriate module is Module 2 (Controller to Processor). That is the baseline we should propose.

2) UK transfers: for data flows involving UK-resident data subjects / UK controllers, we should execute the EU SCCs (Module 2) plus the UK Addendum (the UK-specific appendix) or execute the UK version if the customer prefers separate instruments. Practically, Redwood's standard playbook is: offer the EU 2021 SCCs (Module 2) for EU controllers and append the UK Addendum when the data will be processed or accessed from the UK (or the controller is UK-based). This keeps the operative clauses aligned and minimizes negotiation.

3) Common redlines: we should not accept open-ended liability carveouts, broad deletion obligations that conflict with our retention policies, or clauses that grant the controller unilateral audit rights without scoped procedures. We can accept: scoped audit rights (third-party reports + notice and remediation window), standard subprocessors clause with notice and listing, and limited liability tied to breach-related direct damages.

4) Evidence packet: attach a redlined SCCs draft (pre-filled with company details), short SOC 2 summary, and a compact DPIA note addressing the high-risk processing categories they called out in the demo. Those are attached for the outreach.
