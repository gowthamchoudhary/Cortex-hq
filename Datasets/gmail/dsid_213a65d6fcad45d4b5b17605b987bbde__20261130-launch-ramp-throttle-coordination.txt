Aditya / team — quick triage notes and actions taken at 10:27 UTC:

What we saw (quick):
- Tail latency on long-response model routes spiked (P95->P99).
- Evidence of KV-cache thrashing: cache hit-rate dropped when the ramp hit sustained sequences.
- GPU kernel queueing increased CPU-side scheduling latencies — some requests timed out and surfaced as 500/503.
- Client retries caused bursty replays -> retry storm pattern (many 429s follow).

Immediate mitigations applied (10:27–10:35):
- Reduced max_concurrency for Orbit route to 40 (soft throttle).
- Temporarily routed the account to a dedicated small pool with warmed embeddings/kv caches.
- Adjusted LB to map transient 5xx to 429 for a 2-minute window to force client backoff.
- Added a transient 200ms server-side delay to spread out retries (experimental but helped).

Status: error rate dropped to baseline over ~6 minutes after mitigations. We collected heap traces and attached the error dump.

Next steps (short):
- Schedule kernel parameter tuning and KV prewarm before next ramp. Owner: infra (Mina).
- Recommend progressive ramp with 10-minute holds and prewarm step. Owner: Marcus + CS.
