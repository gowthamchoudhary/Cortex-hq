Laura/Markus/Sanjay/Maya — I reviewed Lumos' redlines and drafted targeted language that I think preserves both parties' operational needs while keeping Redwood's arbitration-first posture. Key points and proposed compromise (see inline below):

1) Equitable relief carveout (compromise): Lumos may seek emergency equitable relief in a court of competent jurisdiction solely for: (a) alleged misappropriation of the other party's Confidential Information or Intellectual Property; and (b) claims seeking urgent injunctive relief to prevent imminent, material loss of customer data or risk to data subjects. All other disputes remain subject to the Agreement's arbitration provisions.

Proposed redline (single sentence for MSA): "Notwithstanding the parties' agreement to resolve disputes through arbitration, either party may seek temporary injunctive relief or other provisional remedies in a court of competent jurisdiction solely to prevent the threatened misappropriation of Confidential Information or Intellectual Property or to address imminent, material data-security incidents; any such request for emergency relief shall be limited in scope and duration and shall not be deemed a waiver of the parties' agreement to arbitrate remaining claims."

2) Venue / choice of law: Redwood's standard is California law and CA state/federal courts where equitable relief is sought; we will accept Delaware for the substantive law governing the Agreement only if Lumos confirms (a) the arrangement of arbitration seat and (b) that emergency relief locations are mutually agreed (we would accept Delaware or California for emergency relief requests). Suggest adding a short sentence: "This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California without regard to conflict-of-law principles; notwithstanding the foregoing, for customer-specific commercial preferences, the parties may elect Delaware law in the Order Form, provided that emergency provisional remedies may be sought in California or Delaware as specified in the Order Form."

3) Jury trial: simple preservation clause: "To the extent a party is permitted to proceed in court under the terms of this Agreement, the parties reserve any right to jury trial that is not subject to mandatory arbitration under applicable law."

4) Practical: we should add a two-sentence carveout process to the Order Form: (i) identify whether emergency relief is anticipated, and (ii) where it should be brought (CA or DE). This avoids ambiguity during an incident. I'll attach a short .docx with the exact redline for insertion.
