Grace — I reviewed the redline and the proposed latency-credit matrix. High-level comments (legal):

- We can agree to per-region P99 SLOs, but legal needs a precise definition of measurement methodology (rolling window, sample size, retention of telemetry) to avoid fuzzy disputes. I recommend we codify: "P99 latency shall be calculated on a daily rolling window for requests originating in the specified region, provided the sample size for that day exceeds N requests; otherwise the day is excluded from calculation." We should not set N in the MSA — we can reference an operational appendix (Order Form attachment A) where the numeric threshold is configurable by mutual written agreement.

- Credits for latency breaches: we should make the credits mutually exclusive with downtime credits to avoid double-dipping. If the service is measured as "down" under the availability metric, downtime credits apply; otherwise latency-credit ladder applies.

- Carve-outs: scheduled maintenance, force majeure, customer misconfiguration (e.g., sending >5x baseline traffic without notice) should be explicit carve-outs. Also add a one-time "stabilization period" of 72 hours post-cutover where latency buckets won't trigger credits while routing and cache warm-up complete.

Action items for operationalization:
  1) Grace/Aditya: decide whether Horizon wants daily vs. weekly aggregation.
  2) Grace: propose language for the order form appendix that describes the latency measurement method (we can draft boilerplate).
  3) Aditya: confirm procurement timeline and whether we want to offer a 30-day trial with reduced credit liability.

I'll draft a short paragraph for the MSA and a matching appendix template for the order form.
