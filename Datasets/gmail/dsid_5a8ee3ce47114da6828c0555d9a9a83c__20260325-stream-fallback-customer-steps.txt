Thank you again for flagging this morning's issue and for the session IDs. Below is a concise summary of what happened, what we did, and recommended immediate steps for BrightLayer. Full internal postmortem (draft) is attached and linked.

What happened (summary)
- Impact window: approx 08:14–08:42 PDT on 2026-03-25.
- Symptom: elevated tail latency on streaming chat; intermittent dropped frames and delayed partial responses for a subset of sessions (you reported 10–25s stalls).
- Root cause (initial findings): a routing weight change pushed to a streaming proxy pool at 08:12 accidentally concentrated traffic on a small set of hosts. That concentration, combined with two hosts experiencing kernel scheduling pauses during the peak, produced token emission delays and partial stream flushes.

What we changed / immediate remediation
- Rolled back the routing change at 08:26 and shifted 35% of traffic to warmed standby hosts by 08:30.
- Increased prefix cache reuse and enabled an aggressive cache window for active sessions to reduce compute per-token for the affected flows.
- Monitored continuously; error rates and tail latency returned to baseline by ~08:42.

Customer mitigation steps (recommended, ordered by quickness / impact)
1) Short term (minutes): Switch affected flows to synchronous (non-streaming) generation — this avoids partial-frame stalls. Expect slightly higher median latency but more consistent responses.
2) Short term (minutes): Increase stream connect timeout to 15s and enable a single retry on connect failures (500ms backoff).
3) Short term (minutes): If you can pin model variant per-route, route to redwood-2-small for the next hour.
4) Next hours: Confirm through your SLO dashboards if client-side reconnect logic would benefit from exponential backoff across restarts.
