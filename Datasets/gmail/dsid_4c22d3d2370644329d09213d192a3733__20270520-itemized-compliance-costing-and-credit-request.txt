PATH A — 3-month pilot (no commit)

- KMS calls: $20 per million calls (first 1M calls / month free for pilot).
- Audit logs: 90-day retention included for up to 500GB; additional storage $0.12 / GB / month.
- Data residency: EU-hosting surcharge = +12% on hosted token unit price + $400/mo regional infra fee.
- BAA / HIPAA: standard BAA included; professional services for attestation/controls mapping billed separately (estimate $8k one-time for readiness).

PATH B — 12-month committed production
- HSM seats: $2,450 / seat / month with a 12-mo commit (30% off list). Minimum 2-seat commit required for production initial rollout (can scale after).
- KMS calls: $14 per million calls with volume breakpoints at 5M and 20M calls/month; committed volume pricing available in the spreadsheet.
- Audit logs: 365-day retention included for up to 2TB under the enterprise bundle; >2TB storage billed at $0.09 / GB / month.

Commercial rationale: the path B pricing assumes a modest commitment to dedicated capacity (preferred for predictable latencies and residency). The mixed-credit structure gives Latera a strong pilot economics while protecting our runway to provision HSM seats.
