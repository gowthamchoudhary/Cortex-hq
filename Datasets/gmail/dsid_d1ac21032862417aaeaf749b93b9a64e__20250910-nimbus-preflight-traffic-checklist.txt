Quick first-pass sizing notes (high-level):  

Conservative scenario (preserve short-query p99 during peak):
- Target capacity: handle sustained 4x peak with 10% headroom => design for ~1,400 QPS sustained during peak windows.
- Proposed GPU mix: 8x A100-80GB (or equivalent Ampere SKU) across 4 nodes (2 GPUs/node) with per-GPU concurrency target ~40 short queries/sec at assumed batching latency 10–20ms.
- Long-form handling: dedicate 1 GPU's worth of capacity as spill for long-form so short-queries don't queue; this reduces total available for long jobs but keeps p99 tight.
- KV cache: enable per-session caching for multi-turn chains; expect ~25% token savings noted.

Cost-optimized scenario (allow long-form degradation):
