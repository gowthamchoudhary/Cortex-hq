Hi Emma — great questions, thanks. Answers below (short form) and I've pasted the exact formula line from the spreadsheet at the end.

1) Credits: credits are issued as account-level invoice credits that apply to future invoices; we do not issue retroactive cash refunds except in rare, executive-approved circumstances. Credits will be applied to the next invoice(s) until exhausted.
2) Carryforward: standard policy is one-quarter carryforward only; size is unlimited in absolute terms but we recommend governance that caps carryforward to 50% of the next quarter's commit to avoid distortion of tiered pricing. We can codify a cap if Clearsky prefers.
3) Annual true-up: we can do annual true-up but recommend quarterly for quicker alignment to usage curves; if you prefer annual we can pair it with monthly reporting and a mid-year checkpoint.

Formula (from clearsky_trueup_example.xlsx):
Make-good credit = max(0, (Committed_tokens - Actual_billed_tokens) * effective_per_token_rate_at_committed_tier)
Where "effective_per_token_rate_at_committed_tier" is the blended per-token price after applying the committed-tier discount (see tab 'Pricing').

On tier breakpoints: carryforward does not automatically change a customer's committed tier. Tier changes only occur via an executed amendment to the commit schedule or automatic evaluation at renewal.

Suggested SOW language (short):
"Quarterly Reconciliation: Within 15 days following the end of each calendar quarter, Redwood will reconcile billed tokens against committed tokens. Any shortfall will be resolved by (i) customer credit to future invoices, (ii) one-quarter carryforward capped at 50% of next quarter's commit, or (iii) a one-time invoice adjustment, as selected by the customer and mutually agreed in writing."

If that looks good I'll draft the SOW paragraph and send for your Procurement review.
