Mina / BrightPay,

Thanks — quick technical answers below and attached diagram for the flow items. I'll upload a formal CSV of subprocessors in a follow-up email but included highlights here.

1) Data classification:
- We classify data as: Public, Internal, Confidential, Sensitive (PII). Customer-supplied user data with direct identifiers is marked Sensitive.

2) PII handling & storage:
- Inference requests that include customer PII are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and stored transiently on GPU-backed caches (KV cache) and ephemeral model shards. Persistent storage of tokens is off by default; customers may enable persistent logs for debugging — if enabled, logs are stored in S3 with server-side encryption (AES-256) and are accessible only to a small set of audited roles.

3) Key management & encryption:
- We use a customer-facing KMS integration (customers may provide keys via KMS/HSM) for encryption-at-rest keys. Root keys are rotated quarterly; data encryption keys (DEKs) are rotated automatically and by policy.

4) Access controls & audit:
- RBAC via our IAM system, least privilege by default. Admin access is restricted to named engineers and SREs, all access requires MFA and approval for privilege elevation. Audit logs record all accesses, retained for 365 days by default (configurable).

5) Subprocessors (high level):
- Storage: AWS S3 (us-east-1, eu-west-1)
- Compute orchestration: GCP (regional compute pools) and third-party GPU partners (contracted)
- Logging & observability: Datadog (US/EU regions), Sentry (error reporting)
- Identity & secrets: HashiCorp Vault (hosted), AWS KMS (customer keys)
*Full CSV to follow; vendors are listed with their primary regions. We run annual vendor assessments and have SCCs or equivalent where applicable.*

6) Cross-border transfers & legal mechanisms:
- For EU customer data, we rely on standard contractual clauses (SCCs) and our DPA includes EU transfer addendum. Where customers bring their own keys or host in a regional private deployment, transfers are limited to the configured region and are excluded from generic SCCs.

7) Retention & deletion:
- Defaults: request-level ephemeral cache retention (minutes), logs 30–90 days depending on config, debug logs up to 365 days if enabled. Deletion requests are processed via our privacy portal and are subject to verification.
