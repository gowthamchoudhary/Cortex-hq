Sabrina — thanks for the clear questions. I pulled together a short checklist and attached our Private Offer FAQ + a marked MSA with common redlines. High level: 

- AWS Marketplace purchase: billing goes through AWS (you receive a single AWS invoice). Redwood does not send a separate commercial invoice for Marketplace SKUs. That simplifies PO matching for some customers but can introduce a 30–45 day settlement window depending on AWS payment cycles.

- Private offer / directed invoice: Redwood can accept a PO, issue invoices directly to Orion, and support your exact remit-to and tax handling. Private offers let you avoid AWS settlement lag but require our finance/legal to accept the PO terms (we can move fast once the PO and MSA are agreed).

- Co-sell crediting: for marketplace listings with an AWS partner co-sell registered, co-sell crediting is typically recognized once the listing purchase is visible in AWS systems — that can lag actual customer usage by 1–2 billing periods. For private offers that register the partner (partner-backed private offer), crediting is usually immediate upon close but requires the offer to be processed through the co-sell workflow with AWS (we can coordinate).

- Tax/VAT: If you need Redwood to be the seller of record (for VAT registration reasons or local tax handling), a private offer/direct invoice is cleaner. Marketplace purchases route through AWS as the billing entity which changes tax handling and may require different VAT treatment.

I'll flag a couple of things for us to confirm internally (see Marcus and Vivek cc'd). If it helps, we can drop a one-page note for your AP team summarizing remit-to and timing.

Looping in a couple quick operational notes from finance/partner ops that often matter to customers deciding the lane: 

- Marketplace: SKU consumption is traceable in AWS usage reports; however, co-sell incentive payout to the partner (and internal crediting) follows AWS settlement. If Orion needs partner rebate applied to Redwood invoice, that won't work on a marketplace purchase.

- Private offer: we can set the invoice frequency (monthly/quarterly) to match your PO; Redwood will be the seller of record so VAT/tax is applied on our invoice. We can also include a partner co-sell identifier on the private-offer record so partner incentives are preserved. That setup requires a short co-sell registration step with AWS (2–5 business days).

- Practical tip: many of our enterprise customers sign MSA + private-offer for the first year to satisfy procurement and VAT needs, then evaluate moving to marketplace SKU for self-serve renewal cycles. That hybrid path minimizes procurement friction while preserving marketplace visibility later.
