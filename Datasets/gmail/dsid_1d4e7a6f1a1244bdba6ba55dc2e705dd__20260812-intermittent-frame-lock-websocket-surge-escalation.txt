Sooj / David / Elena —

I dug into the connection dispatcher: we have a rare race in our HTTP/2 stream-creation path when clients open many concurrent multiplexed streams while sending sporadic PINGs/keep-alives. Under load, the dispatcher marked some streams as errored and dropped them back to the connection pool; the runtime then attempted to reschedule token emission which caused frame gaps.

Short-term mitigation (I can apply now):
- Disable aggressive HTTP/2 flow priorities for Soma's routing key (reduces dispatcher churn).
- Enable chunk coalescing at runtime (merge small token frames into fewer frames).

Impact: should reduce observed p99 gaps to <500ms in our staging replay. Downside: slightly increased latency on tiny responses (p50 up ~10–15ms). For the go/no-go, that's acceptable and keeps throughput.

Request: Soojin, recommend we implement the routing change and ask Elena to re-run the 1k session test at 12:30 PDT. I'll be on standby.
