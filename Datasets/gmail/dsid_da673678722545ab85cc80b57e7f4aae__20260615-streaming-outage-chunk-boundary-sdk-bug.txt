Quick update after reproducing locally against FinTechX repro steps. Repro steps used their curl example and our internal streaming harness. Findings:

- Reproduction: when the upstream model service emits a streaming chunk that is followed immediately by non-standard trailing headers (e.g., "x-ftx-trace: 1234" without an intervening CRLF line break), the Redwood client-sdk HTTP/2 framing code in versions <= 1.3.6 will treat that as part of the chunk payload. The JS stream parser waits for the expected chunk boundary and can block the user-level stream until a full CRLF sequence appears.
- Symptom: customer-observed stalled chunks and eventual timeout/502 from our edge when the SDK never consumes the full message. This lines up with the logs.
- Workaround: clients can disable streaming-parsing heuristics by setting header "X-Redwood-Streaming-Mode: lax" or upgrade to the patched client-sdk (1.3.7) which includes a stricter chunk boundary state machine.
- Patch: I have a small diff ready (client-sdk-1.3.7-patch.diff). Will push to canary and put an emergency release if QA passes.

I'll push the patch to our dedicated canary pool and ping once deployed.
