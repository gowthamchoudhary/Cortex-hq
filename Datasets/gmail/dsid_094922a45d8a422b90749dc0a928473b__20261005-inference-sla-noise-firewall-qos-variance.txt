- Conntrack snapshot indicates a 6x increase in ephemeral NAT session churn in the period 08:45–09:05 PT (attached conntrack_stats.csv).
- Packet capture from the outgoing NAT shows multiple SYN retransmits and several RSTs ~matching customer request_id ff8c2a7b.
- No recent model deploys or infra changes in the serving tier at 08:30–09:30 PT on our side.

Preliminary hypothesis: congestion or QoS policing on the peered path (partner transit / customer firewall) is causing head-of-line delays and intermittent resets. This lines up with the customer symptoms (p99 spikes and higher 5xxs).

Short-term mitigation options (tradeoffs):
1) Pin requests to alternate peering edge for Greenlane (expected: lower latency for many flows; risk: potential capacity constraints, requires policy push and quick routing changes).
2) Enable aggressive keepalive and connection reuse in the serving pool for Greenlane's traffic (expected: reduce conntrack churn; risk: may not address upstream QoS shaping).
3) Coordinate with Greenlane to apply a temporary firewall rule to increase connection tracking table size / reduce aggressive QoS class for our source IPs (expected: effective if they control the firewall; risk: requires customer operations time).

- I opened JIRA INF-2147 and attached traceroute & conntrack dumps.
