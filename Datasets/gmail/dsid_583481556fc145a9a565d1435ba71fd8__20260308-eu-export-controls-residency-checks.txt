Marta / team — quick legal/technical note on export controls: 

- OFAC: Redwood enforces transaction-level blocking based on customer-configured allowlists and our sanctions screening for managed exports. For EU-hosted inference, we do not automatically transfer request/response payloads outside eu-west-1 unless explicitly configured to replicate or stream to other regions.

- ITAR: Our models and weights are derived from public and licensed datasets; they are not ITAR-controlled technical defense articles per our counsel’s initial review. If EuroRetail plans to feed ITAR-restricted technical content into inference workflows, that raises a use-case-level restriction and we’ll need to contractually restrict ingestion and possibly recommend an on-premise deployment.

- Next steps: If you can flag any categories of content you consider restricted, send them to legal@euro-retail.com and we’ll map controls. Also happy to draft a 1-page redlineable clause for the SOW to address export controls and data residency guarantees.
