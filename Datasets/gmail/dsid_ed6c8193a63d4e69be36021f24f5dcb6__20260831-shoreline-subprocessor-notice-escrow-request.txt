Hi Grace,

As part of our Q3 vendor updates, Shoreline Payments will be adding a new subprocesssor, EdgeTransit, to our telemetry and analytics stack. EdgeTransit will handle aggregated anonymous telemetry and intermediate log routing for a subset of transactions. The proposed effective date is 2026-09-14.

Key points:
- Purpose: telemetry aggregation, transient log buffering, routing to analytics partners.
- Data types: pseudonymized telemetry, service-level metadata, no full card data.
- Locations: EdgeTransit runs clusters in US and EU; EU clusters are configured for EU residency.

We're attaching the updated subprocessor roster and a high-level dataflow map for your review (shoreline_subprocessor_roster_v2.pdf, shoreline_dataflow_map_v2.pdf). Please let us know within 30 days if you have any objections or material concerns that would require mitigation.

Grace —

Thanks for the quick review and the clear list. Answers inline below, plus we've attached EdgeTransit security artifacts.

1) Retention/TTL: EdgeTransit retains pseudonymized telemetry for 72 hours by default; aggregation outputs kept for 90 days. No persistent storage of raw transaction payloads.
2) EU residency: EdgeTransit operates regionally and EU clusters are configured with hard egress controls. Failover to non-EU only occurs if regional autoscaling capacity is exceeded AND only after Shoreline approval; such failovers are logged and customers with residency blocks are excluded.
3) SOC2: Attached EdgeTransit_SSA_2026Q2.pdf (summary) and EdgeTransit_SA.pdf (security architecture). Network encryption: TLS 1.2+ (strong ciphers), mutual TLS for internal service-to-service traffic, AES-256 at rest for aggregated artifacts.
4) Technical escrow: We can provide a redacted escrow snapshot that includes deployment manifests, KMS integration patterns, and key-handling flow diagrams. For IP reasons we cannot provide full source code, but will provide runnable deployment artifacts for an internal staging environment.
