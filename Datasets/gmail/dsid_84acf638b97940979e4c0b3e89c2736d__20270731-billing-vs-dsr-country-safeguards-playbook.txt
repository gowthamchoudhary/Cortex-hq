Hi Elena, Vanessa —

High-level finance guidance:

- Invoices and month-end statements issued to VeriTax are accounting artifacts and are preserved to meet statutory tax and audit obligations. We do not delete or redact issued invoices solely because of an account deletion request.

- Usage entries in our internal ledger that are required to substantiate billed amounts (time-stamped, aggregated token counts, invoice line items) are retained in a manner that supports audit and reconciliation. Where possible these records are pseudonymized for operational use, but the canonical billing ledger is part of our finance retention scope.

- Country-specific: if local law (e.g., Italian VAT rules) requires a longer preservation window, we will preserve relevant accounting artifacts for that duration. We rely on customer-provided jurisdictional information to apply extended retention.

Operational notes for VeriTax:

1) If a user-level DSR requests erasure of personal data, we will remove PII from operational records where feasible. That does not remove invoice PDFs or high-level invoice metadata needed for tax.

2) If VeriTax needs us to apply a legal hold or mark particular invoices as evidence for a tax audit, we can tag those records; please provide the invoice IDs or date ranges.

3) For exports you share with auditors, we can produce redacted CSVs showing billing totals and tax lines while removing customer-identifying PII where appropriate.
