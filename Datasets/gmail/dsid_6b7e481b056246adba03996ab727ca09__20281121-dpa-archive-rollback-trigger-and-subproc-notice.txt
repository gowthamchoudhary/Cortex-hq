   - "Redwood will commence rollback validation within 3 business days and begin restoration/purge suppression within 7 business days where technically feasible. Backup snapshot suppression may require up to 30 calendar days depending on subprocessor retention policies. Where delays are anticipated, Redwood will provide a remediation timeline and interim evidence package."

4) Evidence and escrow: Redwood will provide an evidence package comprising immutable snapshot metadata, a chain-of-custody report, and a runbook extract. Hi all — operational details and realistic commitments below (MedBridge account, deal RDW-4821, EU region). I’ve attached the backup-purge-timetable.csv and a subprocessor-notice-template.docx for your review.

Summary of steps we will take when a Customer-Initiated Rollback is received and validated:

1) Intake & validation (Day 0–1):
   - Verify authorized signer and deal id; open internal change ticket; mark account retention state.
2) Snapshot tagging & preservation (Day 1–3):
   - Tag all primary and derivative snapshots with rollback flag; move flagged snapshots to immutable tier where supported.
3) Purge suppression (Day 1–7):
   - For running schedules: cancel next purge window if it falls within 72 hours. For older snapshots on cold storage, suppression will be queued and may take up to 30 days depending on subprocessor GC cycles.
4) Subprocessor notification (Within 48 hrs):
   - We will send standardized notice (see template) to affected subprocessors with snapshot ids and requested hold period.
5) Evidence package (Day 3–10):
   - Deliver metadata bundle and chain-of-custody stub; optional read-only escrow snapshot delivery under SOW.

Operational notes / constraints:
- Some third-party backup vendors (list available on request) have immutable retention windows that cannot be shortened; in those cases suppression is best-effort and may force us to provide an evidentiary explanation rather than a full rollback.
- If procurement requires a guarantee of immediate snapshot restore on demand, that should be scoped into the SOW with reserved capacity and extra charges.

Proposed text for the PO appendix (short):
"Redwood will: (i) validate a Customer-Initiated Rollback within 3 business days; (ii) begin snapshot preservation procedures within 7 business days; and (iii) notify affected subprocessors within 48 hours. Backup purge suppression for long-lived third-party backups may require up to 30 days depending on subprocessor policies; Redwood will provide an evidence package and remedial timeline where suppression is not immediately possible."
