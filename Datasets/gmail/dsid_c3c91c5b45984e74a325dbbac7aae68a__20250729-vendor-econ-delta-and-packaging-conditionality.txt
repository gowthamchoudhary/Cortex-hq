Hi Helen —

Thanks for the clear asks. I've attached a quick cost-comparison workbook and a short note on packaging options. High-level takeaways up front:

- For a 1M token/month pilot, hosted per-token pricing is still the lowest friction; estimated billed tokens ~1.1M after rounding for prompts + completions. Base hosted estimate: $0.019 / 1k tokens (after pilot credit) -> ~$21/mo effective.
- If CloudNova wants dedicated capacity for consistent tail latency, the effective per-token depends on utilization. At 40% utilization, expected effective rate is ~$0.012 / 1k tokens; at 70% utilization it's ~$0.008 / 1k tokens. We show these curves in the attached spreadsheet (tab: utilization-model).
- Packaging: we support both throughput-based bundles (vCPU/GPU-hour buckets with burst credits) and reserved-seat bundles (a predictable monthly SKU per concurrent session). For pilots we typically recommend throughput bundles with a small reserved bucket + burst to avoid overcommit.
- One-time: we can provide a $15k onboarding credit for pilots that commit to a 6-month Dedicated pilot and provide a mutual action plan. This credit can be used against first 2 months of usage or onboarding professional services.
- Compliance evidence: SOC2 Type II summary can be appended to an SOW; for full reports we use an NDA + formal request pipeline.

Jordan and Grace — can you confirm the ops carveouts (egress, logs) and whether we can structure the onboarding credit as a bill credit vs. professional services line item?

Hi all —

A few operational clarifications from infra/solutions:

- Egress: Standard policy is $0.09 / GB for cross-region egress; intra-region traffic to common cloud providers is free. For pilots with significant model logging we can offer a capped egress allowance in the first 90 days (example: 500 GB free) as part of the onboarding credit.
- Logs/observability: Basic console logs are included; long-term retention (>30 days) is billed via the observability add-on. We can map the add-on to a fixed addendum in the SOW to avoid surprise billing.
- SLA/credits: Dedicated pools are offered with a 99.9% SLA; credits for missed SLOs are calendar-month bill credits, not cash refunds. We can show example credit calculations in the SOW.
- Migration ops carveouts: Some incumbents price out transfer-assist as a separate service. We can bake a limited migration runbook into the onboarding credit to cover model conversion + validation (up to 20 engineer-hours). Additional hours billed at standard PS rates.

Helen — curious about the expected token mix for the pilot (long-form generation vs. embeddings). The economics swing quite a bit if you plan heavy embedding usage. If you can share the sample traffic in the requirements doc, we can refine the utilization curve.
