We propose specific examples in section 4 of the draft. Note: the key is defining the standard ("resulting solely from" vs "in part from"); we propose "primarily resulting from" to avoid giving customers a blanket escape for mixed-cause claims.

3) Notice and cure: we can accept a 30-day notice and cure period for non-IP claims; for IP claims we request prompt notice "as soon as reasonably practicable" and reserve the right to move to court if claim handling is unreasonably delayed.

4) Mitigation evidence: we ask that Customer provide contemporaneous records and copies of the demand/claim and representative examples. Our counterparty has to be able to investigate; we cannot accept vague notice.

5) Cap and indemnity allocation: our standard MSA ties indemnity to direct damages and deducts offsets for third-party recoveries. If Acme wants broader unlimited indemnity for IP, we'll need to escalate commercially.
