Short answer: yes, we can convert a portion of unused hosted credits into a burst credit pool usable against Dedicated/hosted burst capacity, and we can structure a 24-month term with a staged ramp. High-level proposal below so you can run it by Procurement/Legal: 

- Credit conversion: Convert up to 150k of your unused hosted credits into a named Burst Pool (usable across regions, subject to minor per-minute throttling). Unconverted credits remain as balances for API usage. 

- Term options we recommend:
  Option A — 12 months (fastest): No discount, full access to burst pool, credit conversion as a one-time amendment.
  Option B — 24 months (preferred): 8% list discount on committed units, staged ramp schedule (20% months 1-3, 50% months 4-12, 100% months 13+). We can apply converted credits to offset committed usage during ramp months.

- Operational notes: Burst pool will be rate-limited to protect shared capacity; SLA for Dedicated pools remains the same. We will provide a monthly credit burn report.
