Quick findings from initial triage (logs + telemetry):
- During the 00:50–01:10 UTC window we saw a sudden token-volume burst from LimePay (batched invoice generations). That coincided with a config that increased max_batch_size for a small percentage of requests (A/B rollout: 10% traffic) to reduce per-token costs.
- Increased batching produced a longer queuing tail under peak arrivals. With existing concurrency caps and our autoscale cooldown, inflight queue length rose and we hit the per-tenant concurrency threshold repeatedly -> 429s observed.
- KV prefix cache showed elevated miss rates (cache thrash) for some invoice templates, amplifying per-request compute and extending latency variance.

Fast mitigations (can be done now):
1) Revert the max_batch_size rollout for affected model variant (rollback at routing layer) — immediate effect on queue depth.
2) Temporarily increase LimePay's per-tenant concurrency cap by +30% for the next 3 hours (manual override) while we stabilize.
3) Force smaller batching for identified routes (route-level override) and enable our prefix-aggregation prefetch to reduce compute variance.

I have a candidate emergency patch (proposed-mitigation-pr.patch) that makes the per-route override low-risk and togglable; it requires a 15-min deploy. If we want a hotfix deploy now we can schedule it within the next 40 minutes.

Risk notes: doing a blanket concurrency increase could raise contention in shared GPU pools if other high-volume tenants peak. Prefer the route-level overrides + rollback.
