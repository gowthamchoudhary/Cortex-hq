Emma — quick technical note after reviewing the appendix and expected throughput. We can support the VPC carveout and bespoke logging retention, but there are operational constraints that affect cost:

- VPC + customer-managed logging (S3 endpoint) requires a small ops surcharge to cover secure egress routes and longer retention snapshots (we estimate +8% on Dedicated unit costs).
- If BlueHarbor wants guaranteed cold-storage retention beyond 365 days with retrieval SLAs, that will be a separate line item (we propose a $1k/mo retention add-on).
- Migration playbook: we can commit to a two-phase migration (pilot + bulk cutover) with a dedicated AE + Solutions Eng hours (estimated 80 hours). We can roll 40 hours into onboarding credit for the first 3 months; the rest billed at standard SE rates unless we include an onboarding credit.

Proposed commercial adjustment to hit close to $0.0032/gen-token for months 1-6:
- Base Dedicated price: $0.0036/gen-token
- One-time onboarding credit applied across first 3 invoices: $50k (equivalent to ~0.0004/token over the committed volume)
- VPC ops surcharge: +8% (applies after credits)

That gets the net in-range if BlueHarbor accepts the 12-month commit and the onboarding credit. If we can't take the ops surcharge (BlueHarbor insists on zero surcharge), we can alternatively expand the commitment to 100M gen tokens/month and reduce the base to $0.0034.

I can prepare a short commercial workbook with line-by-line math — Stephanie, if you want this formatted as a three-row scenario table I can drop it in the shared quote.
