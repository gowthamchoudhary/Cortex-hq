Rohan — thanks for the summary. A few internal guardrails from Finance/Commercial:

1) Approval bands: Commercial may offer up to a 7.5% incremental discount via deal desk automation. Anything between 7.5%–15% needs Finance review and a written justification (volume, committed $) and must be escalated to Head of Revenue for sign-off. >15% rarely approved; would require an exec-level exception and offset (e.g., higher commitment or non-refundable deposit).

2) Index vs. pass-through: index-based credit is preferred operationally. Pass-throughs introduce audit overhead and variability in billing. If BrightEdge insists on pass-through, require a reconciliation cadence and capped quarterly true-up.

3) Contract language: we can accept an index trigger with a fixed lookup (provider list + exchange). We should add a stabilization clause: single-trigger events under X% will be handled by a one-time credit not a permanent discount change.

Operational asks for the SOW: please include
- A worked example for the 15% trigger showing math and the resulting credit/discount over the next quarter
- A fallback model: if the selected index is unavailable, use a secondary provider or Redwood internal cost proxy
- Escalation contacts and SLA for decisioning (48 business hours for commercial/finance acknowledgement)
