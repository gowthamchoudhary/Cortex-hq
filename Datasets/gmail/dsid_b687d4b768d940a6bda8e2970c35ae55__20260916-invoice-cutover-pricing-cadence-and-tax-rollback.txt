Grace — notes from Legal / proposed redlines below. I attached a doc with tracked changes (orderform_redline_legal.docx). Key points:

1) Billing entity: we can add an optional field on the Order Form: "Customer Billing Entity (if different from Contracting Entity): ______" and a clause stating that Redwood will invoice the specified billing entity provided the billing entity is a related legal entity of Customer and Customer consents in writing. Add a representation that customer will accept invoices and is responsible for any taxes assessed.

Suggested clause text (for insertion at end of commercial terms):
"Billing Entity. Customer may specify an alternate billing entity on the Order Form provided such entity is a Related Entity of Customer. Customer represents and warrants that it has authority to bind the specified billing entity to this Order Form. Redwood will issue invoices to the specified billing entity and collect amounts due from such entity. Customer remains primarily responsible for payment to Redwood in the event of non-payment by the specified billing entity."

2) VAT / Reverse charge: add language requiring presentation of a valid VAT registration certificate before applying reverse charge. Redwood will include a notation on the invoice: "VAT: Reverse Charge (Article 194) applied — customer VAT registration number: [number]". If VAT rules or evidence are insufficient, Redwood reserves the right to charge VAT at the prevailing rate.

3) Net terms / invoices: recommend we keep the primary Order Form net-30 default and include a negotiated exception box where Sales can insert Net-45 for enterprise customers. If Finance approves Net-45 for this account, include the agreed net term in the Order Form Appendix.

I'll leave finer legal language in the attached redline. Once Finance confirms the invoice flow and net term we can finalize.
