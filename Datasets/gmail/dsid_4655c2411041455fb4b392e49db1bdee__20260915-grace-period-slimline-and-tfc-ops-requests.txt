Jordan / team — reviewed. High level: we can accept a pilot-specific concession but need tightly-scoped language to avoid setting a precedent for production deals. Below is suggested redline text (we prefer inserting into Order Form and referencing the MSA):

Suggested Order Form clause (pilot carve-out):
"""
Pilot Term and Termination: Notwithstanding Section 6.2 (Term and Renewal) of the MSA, the parties agree that the Services provided under this Order Form are a time-limited pilot ("Pilot"). The Pilot Term shall be six (6) months commencing on the Effective Date and shall not automatically renew. Either party may terminate the Pilot for convenience upon forty-five (45) days' prior written notice. If Customer elects to terminate the Pilot for convenience prior to the Pilot Term expiration, Customer shall be entitled to a service credit equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the prorated fees for the remaining Pilot Term, provided Customer returns all Previews and preview data and cooperates with the agreed offboarding plan.
"""

Key caveats:
- This carve-out must be limited to the specific Order Form and labeled as a Pilot concession; it cannot be rolled into the MSA.
- We should exclude auto-renew entirely for pilots to avoid accidental extension into paid production.
- We need an explicit offboarding obligations checklist attached as an SOW annex (short, 3 bullets) to ensure safe data return.

If everyone is aligned, we will produce a redline pdf and an SOW annex. Karthik — please confirm the commercial trade (credit vs discount vs commitment). If Acme wants an earlier effective reduction in notice (e.g., 30 days), legal will need an exec sign-off.
