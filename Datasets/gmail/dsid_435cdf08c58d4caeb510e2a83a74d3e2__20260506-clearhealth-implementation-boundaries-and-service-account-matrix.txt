Amanda — thanks for the clear summary. Quick answers inline below, and action items at the bottom. I'm copying Monica from Customer Success to own the rollout steps and Ben to handle service-account scoping.

1) SAML
- Entity ID: we accept any URI-form entity ID; prefer urn:redwood:<tenant-id> for pinned configs but we can accept your IdP entity.
- ACS URL: https://api.redwood.com/sso/saml/acs/<tenant-id> (we will provide the final ACS once you confirm tenant-id).
- We support SHA256 certs; we rotate metadata on a quarterly cadence or on-demand for emergency rotation.

2) SCIM
- Base path: https://api.redwood.com/scim/v2/<tenant-id>
- Auth: OAuth2 bearer (client credentials). We do not require client certificates by default but can support mTLS for enterprise Private deployments.

3) IP allowlist
- We accept up to three CIDR ranges per environment. Production recommendation: two /20s or equivalent. We can also provide a region-limited proxy endpoint if you prefer a single egress IP.

4) Service accounts
- We recommend one account per integration surface (ingestion, billing_webhook, admin-sync). Names: redwood-clh-ingest, redwood-clh-billing, redwood-clh-sync.
- Key management: Redwood recommends customer-managed KMS for Private deployments; for Hosted we can store keys in our HSM and issue short-lived tokens.
