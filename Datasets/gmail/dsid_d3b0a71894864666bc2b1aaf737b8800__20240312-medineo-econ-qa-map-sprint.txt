Procurement / Jordan — quick operational note on batching and latency risk: 

- Our baseline model used a conservative continuous batching window tuned for p95 latency targets. However, at tail concurrency levels (spikes > 500 rps per region) aggressive batching can increase p99 latency by up to 2x if you do not have autoscale headroom. Mitigations:
  * Canary: route 5% traffic to a dedicated small pool with quantized model (int8) and measure quality & latency for 48–72 hrs.
  * Autoscale buffer: reserve burst capacity or set a soft limit that triggers fallback to a lower-cost but lower-latency model variant.
  * Cache TTLs: prefer prefix-level caching with short TTL (30s–2m) for high-frequency prompts instead of full-response caching for dynamic flows.

I'll attach a short mitigation note (karthik_batching_mitigation.pdf) with a suggested autoscale config and a checklist to include in the MAP.
