Hi Jacob — thanks for the clear list. Short answers up front, then specifics and next steps:

- Service account lease: we recommend an ephemeral service account model for provisioning flows. Our connector supports OAuth2 client credentials with automatic rotation via our control plane. Typical cadence we suggest is 24–72 hours for tokens used in provisioning; for long-running webhook or sync jobs we recommend a refresh token + scheduled rotation every 7 days.

- IP fallback: for hosted customers we publish a stable NAT egress range per region; we also support a region-aware fallback. If a NAT IP changes during cutover, our automatic routing will keep sessions alive and we provide a short maintenance window notice with the new IPs (we push notifications to the drive link below). For customers who require absolute stability we provide a Dedicated or Private option (VPC/static egress) as part of the Dedicated capacity contract.
