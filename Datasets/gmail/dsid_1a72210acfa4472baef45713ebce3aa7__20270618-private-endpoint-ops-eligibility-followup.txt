- Static egress IPs: For Private deployments we can route outbound traffic through a managed NAT gateway and provide a short list of stable IPs (typically a small pool per-region). We can provide the expected pool for your chosen region once you confirm where the Private endpoint will live (us-east-1 vs us-west-2).
- TLS: Redwood supports TLS for all control plane and data plane endpoints. mTLS is supported for dedicated/private connections where required — it's optional but recommended for high-security customers.
- PrivateLink vs Peering: PrivateLink reduces blast radius and doesn't require cross-account routes, but adds an ENI per AZ on our side and requires specific endpoint subnets. Peering is simpler for egress-heavy workloads but requires route propagation and maintenance. We'll review recommended CIDR sizes during the workshop.
